# website/ — the scroll-presentation redesign

This folder is the canon home of the website redesign: the design of
the page the public site will grow into, held as working mockups and a
settled specification. It entered this repository on the owner's word
(2026-08-25, "I want it to live here"), moving from the workshop tree
where the drafts were born; the workshop's earlier drafts (v1–v3)
stayed behind as history.

The ruling behind the whole effort, in the owner's words: the current
site "shares too much info, overwhelming and unhelpful." The answer,
settled 2026-08-23: ONE set of content per step of THE_STEPS.md —
summary on the page, details opening BELOW, in-page, never on a
separate page — inside the live site's exact look and sequential flow.

## What lives here

- `scroll2/index.html` — **THE BUILD** (2026-08-25): the redesign as a
  real page, generated by `tools/build_scroll2.py` from the records —
  the scroll/data/ bundles, the canonical unit YAMLs, and the triage
  index. Genesis 1, all six stamped blocks, every number computed at
  build time; the verse trees are drawn by the deployed site's own
  renderer, imported from tools/export_site.py. Regenerate with
  `python3 tools/build_scroll2.py`; preview with
  `python3 -m http.server 8077` from the repo root at
  /website/scroll2/. Gloss-gated like every shipped file.

- `front_door_mockup_v5_2026-08-25.html` — THE CURRENT MOCKUP. Day 2
  (Genesis 1:6–8, gen_02_raqia_day) as the worked example, carrying
  every settled ruling in SPEC.md, the live site's left rail and site
  tabs, and the STEP 5 teaching layer (rules-intro box, thread-peek
  popovers, the kept-out card).
- `front_door_mockup_v4_2026-08-23.html` — the mockup the owner ruled
  on, kept as history. Its 1:7/1:8 verse trees are simplified
  sketches; the build works to the 1:6 standard.
- `buttons_mockup_2026-08-23.html` — the affordance gallery. The
  owner's pick: option C, the split button with the big arrow — the
  ONLY clickable style, every step.
- `SPEC.md` — every settled ruling, the open item, and what the build
  must and must not decide.

## What this folder is not

It is not the site. The public site is built by `tools/export_site.py`
into `site/` (generated, gitignored) from the real records; these
pages are hand-drawn design documents whose numbers and quotes are
props frozen at drawing time. Nothing here grounds a claim, and
nothing here ships to the public until the redesign is BUILT into the
exporter on the owner's word (THE_STEPS Step 10).

One trap this folder's own history teaches: the repository's
`scroll/index.html` is the UNDRESSED reader (a dark top bar);
`tools/export_site.py` re-dresses it at build time into the site mast
and the 210px left rail the public actually sees. When replicating
shipped chrome, read `site/scroll/index.html` after an export — or the
live site — never the undressed reader alone. The v5 rail was rebuilt
to the deployed dress after exactly that misread.

THE CURSE-SKELETON LUMINARIES [G04-01/02, Pesikta + Yerushalmi Taanit via Minchat Shai; Kitzur Baal HaTurim; the Ramah's census]: מארת ("luminaries," 1:14) is DOUBLY LEAN — no vav at all, the written skeleton of מארה ("curse"): the Pesikta reads "only the sun's orb was created to give light," the Yerushalmi hangs the Wednesday fast over the children's croup on it, and the Kitzur adds that the moon exists only so the sun would not be worshipped alone. The Ramah's census seals it: exactly TWO doubly-lean stations — יהי מארת ("let there be luminaries," 1:14) and את שני המארת ("the two luminaries," 1:16) — and the stream carries both, only these. THE HILLELI'S FULL FIRST VAV [G04-04, handwritten Masorah + the Hilleli codex + the Ramah via MS]: למאורת ("for luminaries," 1:15) full in the first vav, lean in the second — the reading of the 900-year-old Hilleli codex whose spelling list MS copied, matched in-stream. THE RAMAH'S LUMINARY-RULE VERIFIED WHOLE [G04-05/06/07]: "המאור ('the luminary') full, and every luminary-word in the Torah full — except ONE: שמן למאר ('oil for the light'), FIRST of the Terumah portion." SNAPSHOT: all seven article-stations full (the great and small lights of 1:16, the Tabernacle lamp-verses, the lamp-charge of Numbers), all four remaining למאור ("for the light") full, and the single lean למאר exactly at Exod 25:6 — the Ramah's own sign at his own address (the handwritten Masorah's "unique-and-lean" note on 1:16 loses to the Ramah and the precise books, and the stream sides with them). THE DIVIDED DIVIDE-WORD [G04-08/09/10, Et Sofer + Meiri's Kiryat Sefer + the divided books via MS]: ולהבדיל ("and to divide," 1:18) — Et Sofer and the Meiri rule the vav carries a geiya (the small stress-stroke); other books point the lamed with the hataf half-vowel instead of the plain silent half-vowel. SNAPSHOT writes BOTH marks — geiya on the vav AND hataf under the lamed — a mixed station joining the two camps, the half-vowel law's LAMED again (split-attested station 5, the water-troughs mix), and the word's accent is the zaqef qatan (the small two-dot pause) MS names. READINGS (named-only): R.

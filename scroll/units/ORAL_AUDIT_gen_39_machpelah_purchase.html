CROWNS (chain-attested + DB-verified)

  • G39-01/02/03 the miser loses his letter: Ephron's name stands EIGHT times in the purchase chapter — seven plene, ONE defective: לעפרן at the weighing (23:16), and it is the SECOND token in its own verse — full at the listening, lean at the payment. The defective form is unique in the Torah, and the Kitzur's arithmetic rides it: עפרן lean = 400 = the shekels; רע עין ("evil of eye") = 400 likewise.
  • G39-10/10b the ten namings of the sellers: בני חת ("the children of Chet") — exactly TEN times in the whole Torah: eight at the purchase, two deed-echoes (25:10, 49:32); nine bare pairs + the bowing-verse's prefixed tenth (23:7). The Kitzur's ten-utterances reading censused whole.
  • G39-05/06 the midst-word divides as the Ramah rules: בתוככם plene ten times (23:9 among them); defective exactly twice — his two sign-verses letter-exact (Gen 35:2, Num 32:30).
  • G39-07 the bury-word runs lean: every bury-form in the chapter defective (the Ramah's whole-Torah rule that unmasked the printer's false plene note) — and the Torah's ONE vav-written bury-form is the emphatic doubled קבור of "you shall SURELY BURY him" (Deut 21:23).
  • G39-04 the withheld yod of the dead: מתך ("your dead") — four tokens, all in this chapter, all defective; Bereshit Rabbah's "many dead" derash rides the missing yod against Isaiah's one plene resurrection-word (Isa 26:19; edge).
  • G39-11 the sun in the initials: שרה מאה שנה ("Sarah, a hundred years," 23:1) opens ש-מ-ש — the letters of שמש ("sun"): before Sarah's sun set, Rebecca's rose.
  • G39-12 Esau in the deed: ויקם שדה עפרון ("and the field was established," 23:17) — initials ו-ש-ע, the letters of עשו ("Esau") reversed: the future contester hiding backwards in the purchase-seal.
  • G39-08/09 the withhold-verb stands alone: יכלה ("withhold" / "could") — three consonantal tokens in the Torah, split by pointing: the segol-pointed withhold ("none will WITHHOLD his grave," 23:6, unique) against the two qamatz could-verbs (36:7, Exod 2:3); the Kitzur's moral: deny a grave and lack one (Prov 22:8; edge).
  • G39-18 the cave faces Mamre directly: of six Machpelah tokens, only Sarah's burial-verse (23:19) runs the cave straight onto על פני ממרא ("facing Mamre") with no אשר ("which") between — the Masorah's unique, adjacency-verified against Abraham's burial-notice (25:9).
  • G39-13/14/15/16/17/19/20 minutiae as ruled: the lone tarsa (postpositive ornament-accent) on ויען ("and Ephron ANSWERED," 23:10) and on ארץ ("a land of four hundred," 23:15); the dageshed zayin of לאחזת ("burial HOLDING," 23:20); גר ("a STRANGER am I") bare of the lengthener-stroke per Ben Asher's collation, leaning on the sojourner by maqqef; the rise-verb's alternating accent — ויקם doubled-marked (stress drawn back) at 23:3, single-marked at the bowing (23:7), exactly per the mnemonic.

## PART I — What the five derived units established (the machine model)

### I.1 The work-order transaction is real and stable
Five independent derivations converged on one transaction shape: **DECLARE (spec) →
RESULT (receipt) → BUILD → TEST → COMMIT**, executing against six registers
(TIME/WORLD/REGISTRY/SPECS/TESTS/LEDGER) plus a FLAGS side channel. Nothing forced
the text into this shape; it fell out of the verb forms and particles per the frozen
TIR rules. Every day of the creation week closes as a ledger transaction with an
ordinal label the morphology itself tags (Aomsa), except day 1's marked cardinal
(*echad*, "one") — a flag the chain reads richly, still [OPEN] on our track.

### I.2 Hebrew mood morphology is a command-type system
The rulebook mapping (TIR-026..033) survived five units without a counterexample:
- jussive (`j`) → LET (clean fiat: *yehi or*, *yehi raqia*)
- imperative (`v`) → CMD! (first true instances: day 5's *peru u-revu u-milu*)
- imperfect-in-command (`i`) → LET? — the mandatory question mark; the form that
  cannot distinguish "let it" from "it will" (day 2's *vi-yhi mavdil*; both day-5
  demands)
- weqatal (`q`) → THEN (sequenced obligation — later revealed as the LAW genre's
  backbone; see Part III)
- niphal-in-directive → agentless LET (*yiqqavu* — doer slot deliberately empty)
- cohortative → CMD-US (waiting at Gen 1:26)
- participle → ONGOING (standing behavior: *mavdil*, *romeset*)

The type system is machine-checkable: every mood claim traces to a morph code.

### I.3 Receipts are a system with variations, not a formula
- Days 1–3: *va-yehi khen* ("and it was so") pops the demand same-verse (latency 0).
- Day 4: the corpus's first **cross-verse open demand** (pushed 1:14, popped 1:15) —
  and receipt-before-build repeated (1:11 → 1:15 pattern confirmed).
- Day 5: **no receipt token at all** (MT and Onkelos agree). Receipt work relocated
  into the delivery's relative clause — the demand verb returning as a perfect with
  the delegate as subject (*asher shartzu ha-mayim*). The chain (Lekach Tov) asks our
  exact question and offers two answers, including: things called *beriah* need no
  receipt, "for He spoke and it WAS" (Ps 33:6) — *bara* itself as receipt.
- The scan then found the receipt system's continuation (Part III): *ka'asher tzivah*
  ("as [the LORD] commanded") — the conformance receipt of the spec/law genre,
  73 occurrences concentrated at execution seams.

### I.4 The spec-delta method works and scales
Diffing order-form against delivery, letter by letter, yielded verified deltas on
days 3, 4 (three), and 5 (three) — over-deliveries (stars; taninim), agent shifts
(waters → God, with the delegate credited), enrichments (kind-keys, kanaf), and
label-vs-entity restatements. Two governing discoveries:
1. **Flags never block**: the oracle passes deliveries AS DELIVERED. Spec-conformance
   and acceptance are independent gates — three consecutive days confirmed.
2. Deltas are where the Oral chain concentrates: the diminution dossier on day 4's
   delta #1, the retinue on #2, the Leviathan traditions on day 5's taninim — the
   chain reads the same seams the diff method finds.

## PART II — What the full-Torah scan added (structure from within the code)

### II.1 The symbol economy
Genesis declares 1,625 content lemmas from nothing; every later book runs mostly on
inherited symbols: Exod 76% reads, Lev 90%, Num 91%, **Deut 94%** — *Mishneh Torah*
("repetition of the Torah") by measurement. Chapter-grain inside Genesis: ch. 1 = 0%
(the boot), ch. 7 = 90% (un-creation in creation's vocabulary).

### II.2 Eight reuse mechanisms (each with verse evidence in the findings memo)
1. Registry reads (Gen-1 installs read by all five books)
2. Formula re-runs (the mandate ×12 ending in covenant first person, Lev 26:9;
   the genealogy template shared verbatim by Gen 5 ∥ 11)
3. Re-issue after reset (the post-flood reboot re-runs the boot's directives;
   the tov-test conspicuously NOT re-run)
4. Reversal-by-same-symbols (*tehom* reopened/closed; *bara* ending at its own
   cancellation, then rationed to three world-scale returns)
5. Operator migration to creatures (tov-test seized at both fall-points and at
   Moses's birth; naming delegated/reclaimed; the *mashal* dominion trail to Joseph)
6. Symbols hardening into law (*sharatz* → Lev 11 purity category; kind-keys → the
   dietary index; *tzelem* → the bloodshed law; *shabbat* the noun debuting only at
   Exod 16, with Exod 20 CITING the creation week as precedent)
7. Late poetry as symbol-table dump (Gen 49 re-reading chs. 1–4; Exod 15 and
   Deut 32–33 re-reading *tehom*)
8. Self-segmentation (*toledot* headers ×29 across three books)

### II.3 Genre is numerically detectable
Mood-census separations, no human judgment required: narrative = wayyiqtol-dominant;
law = weqatal-dominant with wayyiqtol near zero (Lev 1: 2 vs 27; Lev 4: 1 vs 53);
sermon = imperative-heavy over weqatal with very high read% (Deut); ledger = template
n-grams with minimal verb variety (Gen 5/11). Genre misfits are self-flagging: Lev 8
runs 63 wayyiqtol inside the law book — because it is the *execution narrative* of an
installed spec (see III.3).

## PART III — What "narrative versus law" means now

Before these passes: two literary genres. After them: **two machine modes of one
program, distinguishable by verb morphology alone, connected by shared receipts.**

### III.1 Narrative is the execution trace
Wayyiqtol chains are the machine's event log: things that fired once, in order —
each *va-y…* verb one tick of the formal clock (TIR-029's observation, now measured
corpus-wide). A narrative chapter is a log segment: events, register writes, ledger
commits. High read% is what a log looks like — entries referencing installed symbols.

### III.2 Law is installed code
Weqatal chains are the instructional mirror of that clock: obligations *sequenced
after a trigger* — condition → consequence chains that have not "run" in the text but
are loaded to run indefinitely. A law chapter is a handler-installation block:
protasis (im/ki — "if/when") = trigger condition; apodosis THEN-chain = handler body;
sanction clauses = error paths. Leviticus 1 (2 wayyiqtol / 27 weqatal) installs
procedures; nothing "happens" in it — exactly as nothing "happens" in source code.

### III.3 The two modes share a receipt system — measured this session
Genesis 1's *va-yehi khen* ("and it was so") receipts a **fiat**. The spec/law genre
has its own conformance receipt: ***ka'asher tzivah*** ("as [the LORD] commanded") —
**73 occurrences**, and their distribution is the theory's confirmation:
- Exod 39 (8x) + Exod 40 (7x): the Tabernacle BUILD chapters, executing the
  Exod 25–31 spec — receipt-per-component;
- Lev 8 (7x): the ordination narrative executing Exod 29's weqatal spec — which is
  precisely why 63 wayyiqtol appear inside the law book: it is spec-execution,
  narrative-mode, receipts firing line by line;
- **Gen 7 (2x): Noah executing the ark spec** — the pattern's first instance, already
  inside Genesis;
- Deut 5 (3x): the re-statement citing conformance.
So: *va-yehi khen* and *ka'asher tzivah* are the same operator at two scales —
conformance-of-delivery-to-order — and the spec-delta method we built on the creation
week is, by construction, the right instrument for the Torah's largest structures:
**Exod 25–31 (spec) vs Exod 35–40 (delivery)** and **Exodus law vs Deuteronomy
re-statement** are corpus-scale diff targets with receipts already in the text.

### III.4 The interleaving is the architecture (interpretive, but now evidenced)
The Torah alternates log and code deliberately: narrative frames install law
(Sinai is an install event narrated in the log); law cites narrative as precedent
(Exod 20:11 quoting the creation week); the boot's own vocabulary hardens into legal
categories (*sharatz*, kind-keys, *tzelem*). Between the two modes sit the standing
speech acts — blessing, curse, covenant, oath: log events that install persistent
objects (day 5's BLESS was our first; the scan shows *brit* ×76 as the largest such
object). Genesis reads as boot + early runtime; Exodus as the pivot (log delivering a
nation, then code installation beginning); Leviticus as nearly pure code; Numbers as
log-with-code (execution, failures, case law — e.g. the daughters of Zelophehad as a
runtime exception producing a patch); Deuteronomy as the re-compilation: 94% reads,
imperative-heavy, addressed to the next runtime before the handoff.

## PART V — One-paragraph summary

Five hand-derived, machine-verified units established that the creation week is a
typed transaction system — moods as command types, receipts, diffs, flags, ledgers —
and the whole-Torah scan then showed that this system does not end at Gen 2:3: the
corpus is one program whose later books run on Genesis's symbol table (76→94% reads),
re-invoke its formulas at every reboot and covenant, harden its vocabulary into law,
and split measurably into log (narrative/wayyiqtol) and code (law/weqatal) joined by
a two-scale receipt system (*va-yehi khen* / *ka'asher tzivah*) that the text itself
provides. The machine we built for seven days appears to be the machine the whole
Torah runs on. The next passes exist to try to break that claim — and are designed so
that if it breaks, the break will be letter-visible.

## Onkelos Numbers 5:11-31 (21 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Num 5:11 — CONTEXT. The frame (the fourth of eight).
- Onkelos Num 5:12 — MATERIAL. A MAN, A MAN; SHE LIES A LIE. "אִישׁ אִישׁ כִּֽי תִשְׂטֶה אִשְׁתּוֹ" ("a man, a man, if his wife go astray") — the doubled man's four Torah seats Lev 15:2, Lev 24:15, Num 5:12, Num 9:10 (computed) → גְּבַר גְּבַר אֲרֵי תִסְטֵי אִתְּתֵיהּ ("a man, a man, if his wife go astray"); "וּמָעֲלָה בוֹ מָֽעַל" ("and commit a trespass against him") → וּתְשַׁקֵּר בֵּיהּ שְׁקָר ("and LIE against him a lie") — me'ilah is lying again, the verb's pausal form מָֽעַל ("she trespassed") the verse's last word; the Sifrei's "in the area of illicit relations, not money" (piska 7 row 1).
- Onkelos Num 5:13 — MATERIAL. THE SIX CONDITIONS. "וְשָׁכַב אִישׁ אֹתָהּ שִׁכְבַת זֶרַע" ("and a man lie with her a lying of seed"), "וְנֶעְלַם מֵעֵינֵי אִישָׁהּ" ("and it be hidden from her husband's eyes"), "וְנִסְתְּרָה וְהִיא נִטְמָאָה" ("and she be secreted and she be defiled"), "וְעֵד אֵין בָּהּ" ("and there is no witness against her" — one seat, computed), "וְהִוא לֹא נִתְפָּֽשָׂה" ("and she was not seized" — the Torah's one seat, computed) → וְסָהִיד לֵית בַּהּ וְהִיא לָא אִתַּחָדָת ("and there is no witness in her and she was not taken") — the Sifrei reads each: not a minor, not her sister, the husband not unseeing, the secreting's minimum, "witness" = two, the forced excluded (piska 7 rows 2-3).
- Onkelos Num 5:14 — MATERIAL. THE SPIRIT OF JEALOUSY; DEFILED OR NOT. "וְעָבַר עָלָיו רֽוּחַ קִנְאָה וְקִנֵּא אֶת אִשְׁתּוֹ" ("and a spirit of jealousy pass over him and he be jealous of his wife" — 5:14 and 5:30, computed) → רוּחַ קִנְאָה ("a spirit of jealousy"); "וְהִיא נִטְמָֽאָה" ("and she is defiled") ... "וְהִיא לֹא נִטְמָֽאָה" ("and she is not defiled") — both arms in one verse: the DOUBT is the case (the Sifrei: she drinks only in doubt, and the doubt-rule of purity is built on it — piska 7 row 3).
- Onkelos Num 5:15 — MATERIAL. THE OFFERING: A TENTH OF THE EPHAH, BARLEY MEAL, NO OIL, NO FRANKINCENSE, A MEMORIAL OF INIQUITY. "עֲשִׂירִת הָאֵיפָה קֶמַח שְׂעֹרִים" ("a tenth of the ephah of barley meal") — the plene spelling האיפה ("the ephah", with the yod) here and at Exod 16:36, Num 28:5 against Leviticus' defective האפה ("the ephah", without it — 5:11, 6:13; computed); קמח ("meal") at two Torah seats, Sarah's (Gen 18:6) and this (computed) → חַד מִן עַסְרָא בִּתְלַת סְאִין קִמְחָא שְׂעָרִין ("one of ten in THREE SE'AH of barley meal") — THE EPHAH CONVERTED: the translation writes the ephah as three se'ah, the conversion layer on a dry measure (the exam's Menachot 77a); "לֹֽא יִצֹק עָלָיו שֶׁמֶן וְלֹֽא יִתֵּן עָלָיו לְבֹנָה" ("he shall not pour oil on it nor put frankincense on it") — Lev 5:11's sinner's-offering clause with a different verb (there "he shall not PUT oil", here "POUR" — computed); "מִנְחַת קְנָאֹת הוּא" ("an offering of jealousies it is"), "מִנְחַת זִכָּרוֹן מַזְכֶּרֶת עָוֺֽן" ("an offering of memorial, a reminder of iniquity" — both phrases alone in the Tanakh, computed) → מִנְחַת קִנְאָתָא הוּא ("an offering of jealousy it is"), מִנְחַת דּוּכְרָנָא מַדְכָּרַת חוֹבָא ("an offering of memorial, a reminder of guilt").
- Onkelos Num 5:16 — MATERIAL. BROUGHT NEAR AND STOOD. "וְהִקְרִיב אֹתָהּ הַכֹּהֵן וְהֶֽעֱמִדָהּ לִפְנֵי יְהוָֽה" ("and the priest shall bring her near and stand her before the LORD") → וִיקָרֵב יָתַהּ כַּהֲנָא וִיקִימִנַּהּ קֳדָם יְיָ ("and the priest shall bring her near and stand her before the LORD") — the offering verb on the woman; the Sifrei: not two together, not with her servants, at the Nicanor gate (piska 9).
- Onkelos Num 5:17 — MATERIAL. HOLY WATER IS THE LAVER'S; AN EARTHEN VESSEL; THE FLOOR'S DUST.

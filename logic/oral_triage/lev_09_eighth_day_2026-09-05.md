## Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 — the Lev-9 rows (this unit's share)
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 1 — MATERIAL. "The eighth day"
  disambiguated by verbal analogy to "the third day" at Sinai (Exod
  19:16): third OF THE COUNT there, so eighth OF THE COUNT here —
  the seven-fill clause (Lev 8:33) closing the argument. The
  calendar predicate: eighth to the consecration, not to the month.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 2 — MATERIAL. The honor order:
  the Place honored Aaron first (Exod 19:24), so Moses honors him at
  the end — called before the elders.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 3 — MATERIAL. THE CALF ANSWERS
  THE CALF: Aaron's sin-offering is a CALF — "send a gift ahead
  before you enter"; Israel's goat+calf pair for their two holdings
  (the Joseph goat early, the calf late).
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 4 — MATERIAL. The ox-and-calf
  species map: the offense resembled TWO species (the molten calf;
  "the likeness of an OX that eats grass," Ps 106:20) — let the ox
  atone for the ox-deed and the calf for the calf-deed; "the offense
  you fear has already been slaughtered before the Place."
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 5 — MATERIAL. "They took as
  Moses commanded" — WITH ALACRITY; the whole assembly drew near in
  joy and STOOD (the reconciled-queen parable).
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 6 — context (one awe, one
  counsel; the circumcise-the-heart frame).
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 7 — context (Moses, Aaron,
  Samuel weighed equal — Ps 99:6).
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 8 — MATERIAL. "APPROACH the
  altar" — the shy-bride parable; and the recorded alternative:
  Aaron SAW THE ALTAR AS AN OX-SHAPE and feared it — "my brother,
  is it this you fear?"
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 9 — MATERIAL. Order of
  service: HIS OWN offering first, then the people's; his atones
  for himself AND the people, theirs does not cover priests; "and
  he purified it LIKE THE FIRST" — the four-horn application
  imported from the first offering.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 10 — MATERIAL. KA-MISHPAT THE
  PROCEDURE POINTER: "he did it according to the ORDINANCE" (9:16)
  imports the olah's whole Lev-1 table — flaying and cutting,
  wholly to the fires, standing beside the altar to throw, popped
  limbs returned to the pyre.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 11 — MATERIAL. The
  handful-filling synced to Lev 2:2 by the milui-milui verbal
  analogy — a pebble, a salt grain, or a crumb of frankincense in
  the fist DISQUALIFIES, here as there.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 12 — MATERIAL. "Beside the
  morning burnt offering" — TWO meal offerings that day: one with
  the olah, one on its own.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 13 — MATERIAL. Communal PEACE
  OFFERINGS learned from here (the ox and ram "for the people");
  and the tribute: Aaron's sons learned SEVEN services in one hour
  (slaughter, reception, throwing, sprinkling, purification,
  pouring, atonement).
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 14 — MATERIAL. The eighth =
  1 NISAN: the tabernacle raised on the new moon (Exod 40:17), the
  cloud covering THAT day (Num 9:15) — and the Presence rested on
  AARON'S service, not on Moses' seven filling-days.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 15 — MATERIAL. "Va-yehi" —
  joy before Him as the day heavens and earth were created (the
  creation-diction pairing); Moses' blessing of the finished work
  (Exod 39:43) with R. Meir's wording beside.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 16 — MATERIAL. "The day of
  his espousals... the day of his heart's joy" — the day the
  Presence rested; the day the NEW FIRE came down and licked the
  olah and the fats.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 17 — MATERIAL. At 9:22 Aaron
  EARNED the priestly gifts and the lifting of palms — for himself
  and his generations until the dead live.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 18 — MATERIAL. "He came down
  from making" — descending from his offerings IN JOY.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 19 — MATERIAL. Why Moses
  entered with him: the offerings were done and the Presence had
  NOT descended; Aaron stood distressed — "because of me it did
  not descend" — Moses entered with him, they sought mercy, and
  the Presence came down.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 20 — MATERIAL. The fire
  descends and the people SING — "Rejoice, O righteous" (Ps 33:1).
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 29 — MATERIAL. THE TRANSPOSED
  VERSE recorded at 9:22 (mikra mesuras — the descent belongs
  before the lifting); the blessing STANDING derived by pairing
  blessing to service (Deut 10:8), the Chronicles proof beside.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 30 — MATERIAL. The blessing
  SEALED here and opened at Num 6:24-26 (the cross-book pointer);
  why the joint entry — to teach the incense work, or the
  entry-blessing by the a-fortiori from exit.
- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 31 — MATERIAL. The falling on
  faces; Solomon's parallel (2 Chr 7:3); Malachi 3:4's two eras —
  "as the days of old" = Moses, "as former years" = Solomon.

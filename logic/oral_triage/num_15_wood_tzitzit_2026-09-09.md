## Sifrei Bamidbar 113-115 (4 rows whole — the span's piskaot)
- Sifrei Bamidbar 113:1 — MATERIAL. On 15:32-33. (a) DISPARAGEMENT: בִּגְנוּת יִשְׂרָאֵל ("in disparagement of Israel") —
  they kept only the FIRST Sabbath and desecrated the second (the readback's fact: the manna's Sabbath of Exod 16 the
  first kept, on the tape's exodus story; this the second). (b) THE WORD: מְקֹשֵׁשׁ ("gathering") = תּוֹלֵשׁ מִן הַקַּרְקַע
  ("detaching from the ground") — the LABOR named; not a man whose NAME was Mekoshesh, refuted from 15:33's repetition
  "those who found him gathering wood" (a name is not repeated as a participle with its object). (c) WHO HE WAS —
  Zelophehad: R. Akiva by VERBAL ANALOGY, "wilderness" here (15:32) and "wilderness" there (27:3, "our father died in
  the wilderness"); R. Yehuda b. Beteira: "you will give an account — He who spoke and the world was COVERED him, and
  you UNCOVER him; you libel that righteous man" — he was of THE BOLD ONES (14:44, "and they made bold to go up to the
  top of the mountain"). A recorded two-arm dispute on the gatherer's IDENTITY, unresolved — data on the entity (as the
  unclean men's three arms at 68:1). (d) "AND THEY FOUND him" — Moses appointed WATCHERS (שׁוֹמְרִים, "watchers") and
  they found him gathering. (e) 15:33's repetition "those who found him gathering" — WHY AGAIN, when 15:32 already said
  "they found a man"? It teaches THEY WARNED HIM and he continued (the forewarning, הַתְרָאָה "warning", read off the
  case's own doubled clause); R. Yitzchak: not needed — an A-FORTIORI from idolatry, the gravest, which is not liable
  without warning, so every command of the Torah; then the repetition teaches that the warning must SPECIFY THE LABOR
  (מֵעֵין מְלַאכְתּוֹ, "of the kind of his labor") — whence for ALL the primary labors (אֲבוֹת מְלָאכוֹת, "fathers of
  labors") of the Torah the warning names the labor. (f) "to Moses and to Aaron and to all the congregation" — if Moses
  did not know, would Aaron? — INVERT the verse and expound it (R. Yoshiyah); R. Yonatan in R. Elazar's name (the
  shelf's Hebrew; its English reads "R. Chanan"): they sat in the study house and the finders came and stood before
  them — the same two arms as 68:1 on 9:6: the halt's stage is one stage for both cases.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 114:1 — MATERIAL. On 15:34-36. (a) "AND THEY PLACED HIM IN THE GUARD" — all who are liable to
  KARET (the Hebrew reads כָּל חַיָּבֵי כְּרִיתוֹת, "all liable to excisions", with the shelf's bracketed emendation
  מִיתוֹת "deaths": all liable to death penalties) are CONFINED (נֶחְבָּשִׁים, "are imprisoned") pending judgment — the
  custody rule stated as a general from the case. (b) "FOR IT HAD NOT BEEN DECLARED WHAT SHOULD BE DONE TO HIM" — but
  Exod 31:14 already says "its profaners shall surely die"! What then was not declared? WHICH DEATH (בְּאֵיזוֹ מִיתָה,
  "by which death") — until told from the mouth of the Holy One. THE UNCERTAINTY IS THE MODE, the death itself
  standing law since Exod 31:14 — Sanhedrin 78b:7's distinction from the blasphemer (whose liability was itself the
  question) at its source. (c) 15:35 "DIE SHALL DIE THE MAN" — לְדוֹרוֹת ("for the generations"); "STONE HIM WITH
  STONES" — לְשָׁעָה ("for the hour", the instance). THE GENERATIONS' RULE / THE INSTANCE'S VERDICT — the block's
  charter line read at its own address: the split falls INSIDE ONE SENTENCE, the doubled infinitive the rule and the
  stoning clause the instance. (d) "ALL THE CONGREGATION" — in the PRESENCE of all the congregation (בְּמַעֲמַד, "in
  the standing of"), not literally all: Deut 17:7 "the hand of the witnesses shall be on him first to put him to
  death". (e) 15:36 "AND THEY BROUGHT HIM OUTSIDE THE CAMP" — all liable to death are executed OUTSIDE THE COURT
  (חוּץ לְבֵית דִּין, "outside the house of judgment"). (f) "WITH STONES" against Lev 24:23 "with A STONE" — two
  verses reconciled by THE STONING HOUSE'S PROTOCOL: the site two stories high; one witness pushes him at the loins;
  turned onto his heart, he is turned back onto his loins; if he dies, enough; if not, the second witness takes THE
  STONE and sets it on his heart; if he dies, enough; if not, ALL ISRAEL stone him WITH STONES — "the hand of the
  witnesses first to put him to death, and the hand of all the people after" (Deut 17:7): "stone him with stones"
  fulfilled and "they stoned him with a stone" fulfilled (two verses until a third decides — the thirteenth middah's
  shape; Mishnah Sanhedrin 6:4's rows are this protocol). (g) "AS THE LORD COMMANDED MOSES" — He said "stone", and
  they stoned; "HANG", and they hanged — but we have not heard hanging: Deut 21:22 "if there be in a man a sin of a
  death sentence and he be put to death, and you hang him on a tree" — R. Eliezer (the hanging AFTER the death;
  Mishnah Sanhedrin 6:4's dispute whether every stoned man is hanged, R. Eliezer's arm). (h) R. CHIDKA in the name of
  Shimon HaShikmoni, R. Akiva's disciple: Moses KNEW the gatherer was liable to death; he did not know WHICH death. The
  section was fit to be said through Moses — but the gatherer INCURRED that it be said through him: LIABILITY IS ROLLED
  THROUGH THE LIABLE, as merit through the meritorious (68:1's line on the unclean men, its mirror — the same teacher
  on both halts).
- Sifrei Bamidbar 115:1 — MATERIAL. On 15:37-41. THE FRINGES. (a) WOMEN INCLUDED — R. Shimon EXEMPTS them: a positive
  command that TIME CAUSES (day, not night — 15:39 "you shall see it"), and THE RULE stated here in full: "every positive
  command that time causes, women are exempt from"; R. Yehuda b. Bava: the sages exempted a woman's VEIL outright and
  obligated her WRAP only because her husband sometimes covers himself with it. (b) THE WORD: צִיצִת ("fringe") = a thing
  that PROTRUDES (יוֹצֵא, "goes out") and of ANY AMOUNT; the elders of Beth Shammai and of Beth Hillel in the upper room
  of Yonatan b. Beteira: fringes have NO MEASURE — nor has the lulav. (c) THE COUNT: one thread? — "TWISTED CORDS"
  (גְּדִלִים, Deut 22:12): not fewer than THREE (Beth Hillel); Beth Shammai: three of white wool and the fourth of blue —
  and the law follows Beth Shammai (the shelf's Hebrew carries bracketed emendations at this line, the houses swapped
  and "four" for "three"; the English follows the received reading); the minimum at the making, remnants and cut-offs
  of any size. (d) THE STRUCTURE: all fringe? — "cords"; all cords? — "fringe": the CORD from the corner, the FRINGE
  from the cord (two nouns, two parts). (e) THE CORNERS: even three-, five-, six-, seven-, eight-cornered? — "on the
  FOUR corners of your covering" (Deut 22:12) excludes them; pillows and covers excluded — "wherewith you cover
  yourself"; NIGHT garments excluded — "you shall see it", by day and not by night; a garment for day and night
  obligated; the BLIND man's garment included — "it shall be for you a fringe", in any event. (f) THE BLUE THREAD: SPUN
  AND DOUBLED (שָׁזוּר וְכָפוּל, "twisted and doubled"); the white by INDUCTION from the shared verb "place" (the second
  middah's shape on one verb, the Sifrei's own "you derive it"); placed at the WEAVING (the corner's woven edge), not at
  the GROWING (the loose strands) — placed there, still valid; R. Eliezer b. Yaakov includes both ("on the corners");
  TIED to the garment, not woven with it ("and they shall place", not "make"). (g) THE FOUR: one mitzvah, mutually
  inclusive (one missing, no mitzvah) — R. Yishmael: four mitzvot. (h) THE NAMES: blue (תְּכֵלֶת, "tcheleth") from the
  Egyptians' BEREAVEMENT of the firstborn (נִתְכְּלוּ, "were bereaved") or their DESTRUCTION at the sea (כָּלוּ, "were
  finished"); fringe (צִיצִת) from "He PEERED (הֵצִיץ) over our fathers' houses in Egypt" (Song 2:9); R. Chanina b.
  Antignos: the fulfiller — Zech 8:23's ten men holding the corner; the annuller — Job 38:13's corners shaken. (i) R.
  MEIR: not "you shall see THEM" but "you shall see HIM" — the fulfiller is as one who receives the face of the
  Shekhinah: the blue like the sea, the sea like the firmament, the firmament like the Throne of Glory (Ezek 1:26).
  (j) "SEE AND REMEMBER" — see THIS command and remember ANOTHER that hangs on it: the recitation of the Shema, known
  from "I am the LORD your God" (15:41), written only in the Shema's sections; "remember" = recite the section by
  mouth; THE ORDER OF THE THREE SECTIONS: the Shema (the yoke of the kingdom) before "and it shall be if you hear" (the
  yoke of the commands) before the fringes (by day only) — and not three at night, from "you shall see it"; R. Shimon
  b. Yochai: learning before teaching before doing — "so the Torah was given: to learn, to teach, to keep, to do"; an
  A-FORTIORI: if the fringes' fulfiller, a sign only, is as one who fulfilled all the commands, how much more the doer
  of any one. (k) "AFTER YOUR HEARTS" — heresy (Koh 7:26); "after your eyes" — harlotry (Judg 14:3); "after which you
  go astray" — idolatry (Judg 8:33); R. Natan: not to drink from his own cup with his eye on another's; THE EYES
  FOLLOW THE HEART — or the heart the eyes? the blind commit every abomination, so the eyes follow the heart; R.
  Yishmael: Koh 11:9's "walk in the ways of your heart" bounded by this clause. (l) 15:40 "so that you remember and
  do" — REMEMBERING EQUATED WITH DOING; "and you shall be holy to your God" — the holiness of all the commands, or
  Rebbi: the holiness of the FRINGES (Lev 19:2 already carries the commands' holiness) — the fringes ADD holiness to
  Israel. (m) 15:41 "I AM THE LORD YOUR GOD WHO BROUGHT YOU OUT OF EGYPT" — why here? lest one hang IMITATION DYE (the
  indigo, קְלָא אִילָן "kela ilan") for blue and say "who will know?" — the measure of punishment (the secret sinner
  exposed in public, as Egypt was) and the greater measure of good; THE SERVANT PARABLE: the loved one's son ransomed
  from captivity as a SERVANT, not a son — "put on my sandals, carry my things before me to the bathhouse" — the writ
  produced when he balks; so the Holy One redeemed them as servants and in the wilderness decreed LIGHT commands and
  HEAVY ones — the Sabbath, illicit unions, the fringes, the tefillin — "on that condition I redeemed you: that I
  decree and you fulfill". (n) "I AM THE LORD YOUR GOD" AGAIN — Exod 20:2 already said it; lest Israel say "we will not
  do and not receive the reward" — Ezekiel 20's elders: a servant his master SOLD leaves his domain — sold in order to
  RETURN, he does not; "with a strong hand" pestilence (Exod 9:3), "an outstretched arm" the sword (1 Chr 21:16),
  "outpoured wrath" famine — "after these three I will rule over you perforce" (Ezek 20:32-33).
- Sifrei Bamidbar 115:2 — CONTEXT. R. Natan: no command in the Torah without its REWARD AT ITS SIDE — the fringes' case:

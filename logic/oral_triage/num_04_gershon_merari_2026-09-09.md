## Onkelos Numbers 4:21-49 (29 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Num 4:21 — CONTEXT. The frame: "and the LORD spoke to Moses, saying" — the first of the portion's eight (computed), to Moses alone (the chapter's earlier frames 4:1 and 4:17 were to Moses AND Aaron).
- Onkelos Num 4:22 — MATERIAL. THEM ALSO. "נָשֹׂא אֶת רֹאשׁ בְּנֵי גֵרְשׁוֹן גַּם הֵם" ("lift the head of the sons of Gershon, them also") → קַבֵּל יָת חֻשְׁבַּן בְּנֵי גֵרְשׁוֹן אַף אִנּוּן ("receive the sum of the sons of Gershon, they too") — the census idiom's infinitive form at its second and last seat (computed: 4:2 Kohath, 4:22 Gershon; Merari at 4:29 is counted without the idiom), the accounting rendering held.
- Onkelos Num 4:23 — MATERIAL. THE HOST OF THE WORK, DOUBLED. "כָּל הַבָּא לִצְבֹא צָבָא לַעֲבֹד עֲבֹדָה" ("everyone who comes to host the host, to work the work") → כָּל דְּאָתֵי לְחַיָּלָא חֵילָא לְמִפְלַח פָּלְחָנָא ("everyone who comes to the host a host, to serve a service") — the two cognate doublings kept in the Aramaic; the window thirty-to-fifty repeated (4:23, 30, 35, 39, 43, 47 — computed with 4:3).
- Onkelos Num 4:24 — CONTEXT. "This is the service of the families of the Gershonite, to serve and to carry" → לְמִפְלַח וּלְמַטּוֹל ("to serve and to carry") — the two verbs that name the two services of 4:47.
- Onkelos Num 4:25 — MATERIAL. GERSHON'S LOAD IS THE SOFT. The curtains of the tabernacle, the tent, its covering, "וּמִכְסֵה הַתַּחַשׁ" ("and the tachash covering") on top, and the screen of the door → וְחוֹפָאָה דְסַסְגּוֹנָא ("and the sasgona covering") — the colored-hide word of 4:6 again; the woven inventory of 3:25-26 now assigned as a burden.
- Onkelos Num 4:26 — CONTEXT. The court hangings, the gate screen, the ropes, "all that will be delivered to them" → וְיָת כָּל דִּי יִתְמְסַר לְהוֹן ("and all that will be handed over to them").
- Onkelos Num 4:27 — MATERIAL. BY THE MOUTH OF AARON; APPOINTED BY CHARGE. "עַל פִּי אַהֲרֹן וּבָנָיו" ("by the mouth of Aaron and his sons") shall be all the service of the Gershonites → עַל מֵימַר אַהֲרֹן וּבְנוֹהִי ("by the word of Aaron and his sons"); "וּפְקַדְתֶּם עֲלֵהֶם בְּמִשְׁמֶרֶת" ("and you shall appoint over them by charge") → וּתְמַנּוּן עֲלֵיהוֹן בְּמַטְּרָא ("and you shall appoint over them by watch") — the priests assign, the Levites bear, as at 4:19.
- Onkelos Num 4:28 — MATERIAL. ITHAMAR'S HAND. "בְּיַד אִֽיתָמָר" ("in the hand of Ithamar") → בִּידָא דְאִיתָמָר ("in the hand of Ithamar") — Gershon and Merari under Ithamar (4:28, 4:33 — computed), Kohath's holy things under Eleazar (4:16, the chapter's one Eleazar — computed): the two surviving sons hold the two portfolios, the soft and the hard against the holy.
- Onkelos Num 4:29 — CONTEXT. Merari "by their families, by their fathers' house you shall count them" — no "lift the head" (computed: the idiom at 4:2 and 4:22 only).
- Onkelos Num 4:30 — CONTEXT. The window repeated: from thirty to fifty, "everyone who comes to the host to serve the service of the tent of meeting".
- Onkelos Num 4:31 — MATERIAL. MERARI'S LOAD IS THE HARD. "וְזֹאת מִשְׁמֶרֶת מַשָּׂאָם" ("and this is the charge of their burden"): the frames, the bars, the pillars, the sockets → דַּפֵּי מַשְׁכְּנָא וְעַבְרוֹהִי וְעַמּוּדוֹהִי וְסַמְכוֹהִי ("the boards of the tabernacle and its bars and its pillars and its sockets") — 3:36-37's inventory as a burden; the charge-word on a load.
- Onkelos Num 4:32 — MATERIAL. THE VESSELS BY NAMES. "וּבְשֵׁמֹת תִּפְקְדוּ אֶת כְּלֵי מִשְׁמֶרֶת מַשָּׂאָֽם" ("and by names you shall appoint the vessels of the charge of their burden") → וּבִשְׁמָהָן תְּמַנּוּן יָת מָנֵי מַטְּרַת מַטּוּלְהוֹן ("and by names you shall appoint the vessels of the watch of their burden") — the census's count-of-names (1:2) turned on VESSELS: each socket and pin assigned to a named man.
- Onkelos Num 4:33 — CONTEXT. Merari's closing formula, "in the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest" — the second Ithamar (computed).
- Onkelos Num 4:34 — MATERIAL. THE PRINCES JOIN THE COUNT.

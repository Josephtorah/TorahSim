## Sifra, Kedoshim — Section 1 (12 rows)
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 1 — MATERIAL. Said IN FULL ASSEMBLY
  (בהקהל) — "because MOST OF THE TORAH'S BODIES HANG ON IT";
  "holy" = SEPARATE (פרושים); the sanctify-Me symmetry with its
  refused converse ("in My holiness I am, whether you sanctify Me
  or not"); Abba Shaul — the king's retinue must IMITATE the king.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 2 — MATERIAL. The WOMAN included in
  fearing parents — "you shall fear" plural: two in the clause.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 3 — MATERIAL. Why "a man" then — HE
  has the means in hand; SHE is under another's authority.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 4 — MATERIAL. Fear of parents LINKED
  — הקיש (the linkage operator) — to fear of God.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 5 — MATERIAL. Honor of parents linked
  to honor of God ("honor the LORD from your wealth").
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 6 — MATERIAL. Cursing parents linked
  to cursing God.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 7 — MATERIAL. STRIKING has no upward
  analogue — unsayable toward Heaven; and the reason of the three
  links: THREE PARTNERS in a person (God, father, mother).
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 8 — MATERIAL. R. Shimon: order is not
  rank — lambs before goats, turtledoves before pigeons
  everywhere, yet each pair EQUAL by the reversed verses.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 9 — MATERIAL. Father before mother
  everywhere — yet BOTH EQUAL ("his mother and his father you
  shall fear" reversing the order); the Sages' reason for the
  father's precedence: he and his mother both owe the father's
  honor.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 10 — MATERIAL. FEAR defined (not sit
  in his place, not speak in his place, not contradict him) vs
  HONOR defined (feed, give drink, clothe, cover, bring in, take
  out); and THE OVERRIDE CLAUSE: parent orders a transgression —
  do NOT obey: "you shall keep MY Sabbaths — ALL of you owe MY
  honor." Plus "turn not to the idols" — not even to LOOK
  (R. Yehuda).
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 11 — MATERIAL. THE TEN NAMES of
  idolatry — a census by etymology (hollow ones, hewn, poured,
  stood, jointed, rotting, dung-balls, detested, sun-pillars,
  self-blessed); "turn after them and you MAKE them gods."
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Section 1 12 — MATERIAL. Molten gods — make
  none for others, none made by others for you: TWO warnings on
  the self-maker; R. Yosei — THREE (adding "you shall have none").

- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 2 — MATERIAL. Extended from shelamim
  to ALL two-day offerings ("when you slaughter A SACRIFICE").
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 3 — MATERIAL. "You shall slaughter

- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 4 — MATERIAL. THE RE-ASSIGNMENT MOVE:
  the third-day clause is redundant for wrong-TIME (already
  taught) — "if it does not bear on its own matter, GIVE IT to
  wrong-PLACE": the freed clause reassigned to the neighboring
  gap.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 5 — MATERIAL. "Its eaters bear
  iniquity... shall be cut off" — a building block: whoever eats
  the HOLY in violation earns karet.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 6 — MATERIAL. "When YOU reap" —
  excludes what robbers reaped, ants nibbled, wind or beast broke;
  excludes the gentile's own reaping — so the convert whose field
  was cut before conversion is EXEMPT from the poor gifts; R.
  Yehuda's forgotten-sheaf carve (it begins only at binding).
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 7 — MATERIAL. THE FIVE-PREDICATE

  class = FOOD, GUARDED, LAND-GROWN, GATHERED AS ONE, STORED FOR

  as one), grain and legumes in. A pure specification row.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 8 — MATERIAL. The tree census liable
  to the corner: sumac, carobs, nuts, almonds, vines,
  pomegranates, olives, dates.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 9 — MATERIAL. The corner — פאה (the edge) —
  attaches at the FINISH, needs a NAME, sits at the END; given at
  start or middle it counts, but the last ONE-SIXTIETH must
  still stand. The recorded fraction.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 10 — MATERIAL. R. Shimon's FOUR
  REASONS the corner sits at the end: robbery of the poor,
  idling of the poor, appearances, and "the Torah said so" — a

- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 11 — MATERIAL. "YOUR field" — not

  to sibling unions, "filling the world with the unfit" — זימה
  read as זה מה הוא ("this one — WHAT is he?").
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 6 — MATERIAL. R. Eliezer — the
  verbal analogy on זימה (scheme): punished BEFORE HEAVEN as one
  who took a woman and her mother.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 7 — MATERIAL. TEMPLE BUILDING DOES
  NOT OVERRIDE THE SABBATH — "My Sabbaths KEEP and My sanctuary
  FEAR": keeping to the Sabbath, awe to the sanctuary; and the
  awe redirected — not OF the sanctuary but of HIM WHO COMMANDED
  it, argued by the Sabbath parallel.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 8 — MATERIAL. Sanctuary awe FOREVER
  — standing or destroyed, as Sabbath keeping is forever.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 9 — MATERIAL. THE AWE PROTOCOL: no
  entering the Mount with staff, moneybelt, shoes, dust on the
  feet; no shortcut through; spitting — by the a-fortiori.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 10 — MATERIAL. אוב (the ghost-pit) =
  the פיתום speaking from his armpit; ידעוני (the familiar) —
  speaking from his mouth; THEY are stoned; THE CONSULTER stands
  under the warning only.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 11 — MATERIAL. "Seek them not to be
  defiled" — they come upon a man only when he TURNS HIS MIND to
  them; "know what you exchange — the What for the what!"
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 12 — MATERIAL. "Before the hoary
  head rise" — not the ignorant elder (אשמאי); זקן (elder) = THE
  SAGE ("gather Me seventy men of the ELDERS"); R. Yosei the
  Galilean — זה קנה חכמה ("this one ACQUIRED wisdom") — the word
  itself parsed as an acquisition compound.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 13 — MATERIAL. Rise WITHIN REACH
  (not from afar); honor — NOT with money: as rising costs
  nothing, the honoring costs nothing.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 14 — MATERIAL. The honor protocol —
  not in his seat, not speaking in his place, not contradicting
  him; the eye-shutting dodge — "given to the heart: and you
  shall fear your God" — the heart clause's second firing.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 7 15 — MATERIAL. THE RECIPROCAL DUTY:
  the elder must NOT BURDEN the public ("elder — and fear your
  God" read reflexively); Issi ben Yehuda — "before the hoary
  head rise": EVERY gray head in the clause.

## Sifra, Kedoshim — Chapter 8 (11 rows)
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 1 — MATERIAL. THE EVIDENCE-BURDEN
  GEOGRAPHY: "a convert WITH YOU in your land" — in the Land he
  must bring PROOF of conversion; outside the Land he is
  accepted on his word ("with you" — wherever you are; the
  verses split the burden).
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 2 — MATERIAL. Word-wronging of the
  convert — "yesterday you worshipped idols and today you
  entered under the wings of the Presence" banned.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 3 — MATERIAL. THE INTAKE GATE: "as
  the citizen" — as the citizen accepted ALL the Torah's words,
  so the convert; from here — a convert who accepted all BUT ONE
  THING is NOT accepted; R. Yosei son of R. Yehuda — even one
  detail of the scribes' refinements.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 4 — MATERIAL. "LOVE HIM AS YOURSELF"
  — the neighbor command said again FOR THE CONVERT; "for you
  were strangers": know the stranger's soul from your own.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 5 — MATERIAL. MEASURES ARE JUDGMENT:
  "do no wrong IN JUDGMENT — in measure, weight, capacity": THE
  MEASURER IS A JUDGE — falsify the measure and you bear the
  judge's five names and cause his five effects (defile the
  land, profane the Name, remove the Presence, fell by sword,
  exile).
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 6 — MATERIAL. The gloss row: "in
  measure" — land measure; "weight" — the balance beam
  (טריטני); "capacity" — the large liquid measure, or the small,
  or the strike-rod (מחוק) — the recorded lexicon variants.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 7 — MATERIAL. Calibrate all four —
  scales, weights, dry measure, liquid measure; R. Yosei son of
  R. Yehuda — the hin is within the ephah, so why "a just hin"?
  — READ הין AS SPEECH: your NO (לאו) be just and your YES (הין)
  be just — the speech-honesty rider inside the weights verse.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 8 — MATERIAL. "There SHALL BE to
  you" — APPOINT A MARKET INSPECTOR (אגרדמין) over it; the
  cleaning table: the wholesaler wipes his measures every thirty
  days, the householder every twelve months; Rabban Shimon ben
  Gamliel inverts; the shopkeeper twice a week, weights weekly,
  the balance at every weighing.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 9 — MATERIAL. THE TILT PROTOCOL:
  liquid goods — tip the scale a handbreadth to the buyer;
  weighing exact — give the surcharges — גרומים (the tip-weights): ONE TENTH in
  liquid, ONE TWENTIETH in dry. Transmitted fractions.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 10 — MATERIAL. "Who brought you out
  of Egypt" — ON CONDITION of the measures: whoever CONCEDES the
  measure commands concedes the Exodus; whoever denies them
  denies the Exodus. The commandment tied to the founding datum.
- Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 8 11 — MATERIAL. Keep-and-do to
  statutes and judgments both; "I am the LORD" — faithful to pay.

## Onkelos Lev 19 (37 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Lev 19:1 — CONTEXT. Frame.
- Onkelos Lev 19:2 — CONTEXT. "Speak with ALL the assembly" — the
  full-assembly note the Sifra reads, standing in the verse.
- Onkelos Lev 19:3 — CONTEXT. "The DAYS OF MY SABBATHS" plural
  kept; mother before father kept.
- Onkelos Lev 19:4 — MATERIAL. אלילים (idols) rendered טַעֲוָן
  ("the ERRORS/strayings") — the idol named by the going-astray
  verb, one of the Sifra's ten mocking names performed.
- Onkelos Lev 19:5 — CONTEXT. "To your acceptance" kept.
- Onkelos Lev 19:6 — CONTEXT. Literal two-day clause.
- Onkelos Lev 19:7 — CONTEXT. פגול rendered מְרָחָק ("the
  rejected") — the rejection effect verb.
- Onkelos Lev 19:8 — MATERIAL. "Shall bear his iniquity" rendered
  חוֹבֵיהּ יְקַבֵּל ("shall RECEIVE his debt") — the ledger
  verb again on the holiness charter's own karet clause.
- Onkelos Lev 19:9 — CONTEXT. The corner and gleaning verbs
  literal.
- Onkelos Lev 19:10 — CONTEXT. "The FALLEN of your vineyard"
  — נִתְרָא (the dropped) — to the poor and the converts.
- Onkelos Lev 19:11 — CONTEXT. The three bans literal, "a man
  against his FELLOW."
- Onkelos Lev 19:12 — CONTEXT. Literal — "profane the Name."
- Onkelos Lev 19:13 — MATERIAL. "Rob not" doubled into two verbs
  — לָא תַעֲשׁוֹק ("oppress not") and וְלָא תְאַנֵּס ("and COERCE
  not") — the wage clause literal to the morning.
- Onkelos Lev 19:14 — MATERIAL. The deaf and blind rendered
  LITERAL ("him who hears not," "him who sees not") — the
  translation keeps the ink's own persons while the Sifra
  extends to the blind-in-the-matter: the two layers visible.
- Onkelos Lev 19:15 — CONTEXT. "In TRUTH — בְּקוּשְׁטָא (in truth) —
  judge your fellow."
- Onkelos Lev 19:16 — MATERIAL. THE SLANDER-MORSEL IDIOM: "go not
  talebearing" rendered לָא תֵיכוּל קוּרְצִין ("EAT NO
  SLANDER-MORSELS in your people") — the Aramaic legal idiom for
  informing; "stand not on your fellow's blood" kept literal.
- Onkelos Lev 19:17 — MATERIAL. "Bear no sin upon him" rendered
  "RECEIVE no liability on his account" (וְלָא תְקַבֵּל עַל דִּי
  לֵיהּ חוֹבָא) — the rebuke boundary as debt language.
- Onkelos Lev 19:18 — MATERIAL. "Love your neighbor as yourself"
  rendered וּתְרַחֵם לְחַבְרָךְ כְּוָתָךְ ("show LOVE/COMPASSION
  to your fellow as yourself") — the great rule in its Aramaic
  verb.
- Onkelos Lev 19:19 — CONTEXT. The three mixture bans; שעטנזא
  (the mixture word) kept as the term.
- Onkelos Lev 19:20 — MATERIAL. THE DOCUMENT INSERTED: "freedom
  not GIVEN her" rendered "freedom not given her BY DOCUMENT
  (בִּשְׁטָר)" — the Sifra's document-completes-freedom ruling
  standing inside the verse; בִּקֻּרְתָּא (the inquest) kept as the
  term.
- Onkelos Lev 19:21 — CONTEXT. Literal guilt-ram clause.
- Onkelos Lev 19:22 — CONTEXT. Literal — "it shall be FORGIVEN
  him" — וְיִשְׁתְּבֵק (and it shall be released).
- Onkelos Lev 19:23 — MATERIAL. ערלה ("its foreskin") rendered
  by the DISTANCING verb (וּתְרַחֲקוּן רָחָקָא, "you shall
  surely set apart") — the fruit ban as separation, "for
  destruction" — לַאֲבָדָא (for perishing) — naming the benefit ban.
- Onkelos Lev 19:24 — MATERIAL. קדש הלולים rendered קֹדֶשׁ
  תֻּשְׁבְּחָן ("holy of PRAISES") — the blessing reading the
  Sifra derives, standing as the noun.
- Onkelos Lev 19:25 — CONTEXT. Literal fifth-year clause.
- Onkelos Lev 19:26 — CONTEXT. "Eat not OVER the blood" kept
  literal — the five laws hang on the preserved preposition.
- Onkelos Lev 19:27 — CONTEXT. The corners of head and beard
  literal.
- Onkelos Lev 19:28 — MATERIAL. "Tattoo inscription" rendered
  רוּשְׁמִין חֲרִיתִין ("ENGRAVED MARKINGS") — the
  write-and-engrave conjunction the Sifra requires, present as
  a two-word noun.
- Onkelos Lev 19:29 — MATERIAL. "Lest the land go astray"
  rendered וְלָא תִטְעֵי אַרְעָא ("that the LAND not stray") —
  the land as the straying subject, the Sifra's land-to-its-
  fruits reading carried; זימה again as "counsel of sins."
- Onkelos Lev 19:30 — CONTEXT. Sanctuary awe literal ("My
  SANCTUARY HOUSE you shall fear").
- Onkelos Lev 19:31 — CONTEXT. The ghost-pit and familiar
  rendered בִּדִּין וּדְכוּרוּ (the technical pair).
- Onkelos Lev 19:32 — MATERIAL. THE SCHOLAR RULING INSIDE THE
  VERSE: "before the hoary head rise" rendered מִן קֳדָם דְּסַבַּר
  בְּאוֹרַיְתָא תְּקוּם ("before ONE LEARNED IN THE TORAH you
  shall rise") — Onkelos rules the Sifra's elder-equals-sage
  dispute in the translation itself.
- Onkelos Lev 19:33 — CONTEXT. Convert-wronging literal.
- Onkelos Lev 19:34 — CONTEXT. "As the citizen... love him as
  yourself" — the doubled command kept (the Tazria-era ledger's
  GER contrast re-attested).
- Onkelos Lev 19:35 — MATERIAL. "Do no wrong IN JUDGMENT — in
  measure, weight, capacity" — the JUDGMENT frame kept (בְּדִין)
  over the measures: the measurer-as-judge reading standing in
  the verse's own syntax.
- Onkelos Lev 19:36 — CONTEXT. The four "just" items rendered
  with קשוט (truth) throughout.
- Onkelos Lev 19:37 — CONTEXT. Literal close.

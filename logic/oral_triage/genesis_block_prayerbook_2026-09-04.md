### Berakhot 2a:8-10, 6b:7-9, 26a:16-18, 26b:4-10, 27a:9-11 (19)
- 2a:8 — context (the priests-entering peg for the evening Shema).
- 2a:9 — MATERIAL (docket). The alternative source: יליף מברייתו
  של עולם ("learned from the CREATION OF THE WORLD") — ויהי ערב
  ויהי בקר יום אחד ("and it was evening and it was morning, one
  day," Gen 1:5): evening first in the liturgy because evening is
  first in the world.
- 2a:10 — context (the morning-blessings order challenge riding
  the same question).
- 6b:7 — MATERIAL. Whoever fixes a place is called of Abraham's
  disciples — the frame row.
- 6b:8 — MATERIAL (docket). Whence that Abraham fixed a place? אל
  המקום אשר עמד שם ("to the place where he had STOOD," 19:27) —
  and אין עמידה אלא תפלה ("standing is nothing but prayer,"
  Pinchas's standing-and-praying the analogy verse).
- 6b:9 — context (leaving the synagogue without large steps).
- 26a:16 — context (reward-of-prayer-in-its-time frame).
- 26a:17 — MATERIAL (docket). The dilemma: missed the afternoon —
  pray evening twice? The hinge stated: missed-evening gives
  morning-twice משום דחד יומא הוא ("because it is ONE DAY" — Gen
  1:5 quoted); against: prayer stands in an offering's place and
  the day passed. Or: prayer is MERCY — pray on.
- 26a:18 — MATERIAL. R. Yochanan rules: prays the evening twice —
  no "its day passed, its offering lapsed."
- 26b:4 — MATERIAL. THE DISPUTE: R. Yosei son of R. Chanina — the
  PATRIARCHS instituted the prayers; R. Yehoshua ben Levi —
  instituted AGAINST THE DAILY OFFERINGS.
- 26b:5 — MATERIAL (docket). Baraita for the patriarchs: Abraham
  the morning — the standing-verb of 19:27 (the same analogy as
  6b:8).
- 26b:6 — MATERIAL (docket). Isaac the afternoon — לשוח בשדה
  לפנות ערב ("to MEDITATE in the field toward evening," 24:63),
  and meditation is prayer (the pauper's siach poured out).
- 26b:7 — MATERIAL (docket). Jacob the evening — ויפגע במקום ("he
  ENCOUNTERED the place," 28:11), and encounter is prayer ("do
  not encounter Me," Jer 7:16).
- 26b:8 — MATERIAL. Baraita for the offerings: morning prayer
  until MIDDAY, for the morning daily offering is brought until
  midday; R. Yehuda — until FOUR hours. The sugya keeps BOTH
  baraitot.
- 26b:9 — MATERIAL. The afternoon until evening; R. Yehuda —
  until the afternoon's plag.
- 26b:10 — MATERIAL. The evening prayer has NO FIXED LIMIT — the
  limbs and fats not consumed by evening burn all night.
- 27a:9 — MATERIAL. The four-hours tanna sought: "the sun grew
  hot and it melted" (the manna) = four hours.
- 27a:10 — MATERIAL (docket). The assignment: you say four? when
  it says כחם היום ("as the HEAT OF THE DAY," Gen 18:1) — SIX
  hours is stated there; so "the sun grew hot" = four. The two
  heat-tokens given their hours; our verse carries the six.
- 27a:11 — MATERIAL. Both arms keep it: the sages by בבקר בבקר
  ("morning, morning" — split into two mornings), R. Yehuda by
  the extra morning-token advancing one hour.

### Pesachim 56a:6-8 (3)
- 56a:6 — context (why WE say the line — as Reish Lakish
  expounded).
- 56a:7 — MATERIAL (docket). Jacob's deathbed (49:1): sought to
  reveal the end; the Presence departed; "is my bed flawed?"; the
  sons answered SHEMA — "as your heart holds only One, so ours";
  Jacob answered ברוך שם כבוד מלכותו לעולם ועד ("Blessed is the
  Name of His glorious kingdom forever").
- 56a:8 — MATERIAL. The sages' dilemma: say it — Moses did not;
  omit it — Jacob said it. התקינו שיהו אומרים אותו בחשאי ("they
  instituted saying it SILENTLY"); the princess who smelled the
  gravy — say it and be shamed, omit it and suffer — her servants
  brought it silently.

### Rosh Hashanah 32b:4-6 (3)
- 32b:4 — MATERIAL. No INDIVIDUAL remembrance-verse in the
  liturgy, even for good ("remember me, LORD…").
- 32b:5 — MATERIAL (docket). פקדונות הרי הן כזכרונות ("VISITINGS
  are like remembrances") — וה׳ פקד את שרה ("and the LORD visited
  Sarah," Gen 21:1) — R. Yosei; R. Yehuda: they are not.
- 32b:6 — MATERIAL. For R. Yosei, Sarah's visiting is an
  individual's — but כיון דאתו רבים מינה כרבים דמיא ("since the
  MANY came from her, it is as the many's").

## Sifra, Emor — Section 13 (12 rows)
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 1 — MATERIAL. "Command" = URGING,
  immediately and for generations; R. Shimon: especially where
  there is MONETARY LOSS; "take to you" — YOU are the treasurer;
  "olive oil" — not sesame, nut, or radish oil; "from its olive";
  THE THREE OLIVES AND THREE OILS: the first olive picked at the
  treetop, crushed, basketed — the first flow; re-crushed and
  pressed under the beam — the second; ground again — the third:
  THE FIRST FOR THE MENORAH, the rest for grain offerings.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 2 — MATERIAL. The second olive (picked at
  the roof) — the same three flows; first for the menorah.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 3 — MATERIAL. The third olive (packed in
  the press till it softens, dried on the roof) — three flows; the
  first for the menorah.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 4 — MATERIAL. R. Yehuda's METHOD: not
  ground in a mill but CRUSHED in a mortar; not the beam but
  STONES; not in the basket but AROUND it, drawn from the basket's
  middle.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 5 — MATERIAL. THE NINE-GRADE ORDER: first
  of the first — none above it; second of first = first of second;
  third of first = second of second = first of third; third of
  second = second of third; third of third — none below it. A
  total order over the nine flows.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 6 — MATERIAL. "Pure" = CLEAN; "beaten" —
  R. Yehuda: CRUSHED; beaten oil VALID for grain offerings (Exod
  29:40) — so "beaten FOR THE LIGHT": required for the lamp, not
  for the offerings.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 7 — MATERIAL. THE WESTERN LAMP: "to raise
  a lamp" — the flame must RISE on its own; "to raise a LAMP" — the
  WESTERN lamp burns ALWAYS, from it he begins and at it he ends;
  the tending: two eastern lamps found burning — clean the eastern,
  leave the western burning to light the evening lamps; found out —
  clean it and light it from the OUTER ALTAR; "continually" — even
  on the Sabbath, even in IMPURITY.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 8 — MATERIAL. "Outside the veil of
  testimony" — Exod 40:24 left the menorah's position open (near
  the veil or the entrance?) — "outside the veil... in the tent":
  NEARER THE VEIL than the entrance. A position fixed by
  cross-reference.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 9 — CONTEXT. "Testimony" — a testimony to
  all who enter the world that the Presence is in Israel; the forty
  years without need of a lamp (Exod 40:38).
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 10 — MATERIAL. "Aaron and his sons arrange
  it" (Exod 27:21) — one priest with seven lamps? "to raise a lamp"
  (singular); seven priests with seven lamps? "Aaron and his sons
  ARRANGE" — ONE priest arranges. The staffing fixed by number
  agreement.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 11 — MATERIAL. "Evening to morning" — give
  them their MEASURE to burn evening to morning; NO OTHER SERVICE
  evening to morning but this; "continually" — Sabbath, impurity.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 13 12 — MATERIAL. "On the PURE menorah" — on
  the menorah's own purity: no propping the lamps with chips or
  pebbles; "arrange the lamps BEFORE THE LORD" — not prepared
  outside and carried in; "continually" again.

## Sifra, Emor — Chapter 18 (11 rows)
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 1 — MATERIAL. "Take fine flour and bake
  IT" — even WHEAT may be bought (for this); "it" — this alone, not
  other grain offerings bought as wheat.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 2 — MATERIAL. Twelve loaves EQUAL; two
  tenths EQUAL; "the one loaf" — kneaded and shaped ONE BY ONE; the
  two loaves (Shavuot) likewise; baked TWO AT A TIME ("place them")
  — the two loaves NOT; THE THREE MOLDS: one as dough, one in the
  oven, one on removal so they not break.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 3 — MATERIAL. THE THREE-VERSE ARRANGEMENT:
  two rows — eight and four? "six the row"; six-six-six? "twelve";
  four-four-four? "two rows, six the row" — "until three verses say
  it we have not heard": an arrangement pinned by THREE
  constraints, each closing a reading the others leave open.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 4 — MATERIAL. "On the PURE table" — on the
  table's own purity: the PROPS must not lift the bread off the
  table; FOUR golden props forked at their heads, two per row, and
  TWENTY-EIGHT reeds like half a hollow reed — fourteen per row.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 5 — MATERIAL. "On the row" — one row? —
  "row"-"row" verbal analogy: both rows.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 6 — MATERIAL. "Pure frankincense" — CLEAR;
  "it shall be for the bread" — an OBLIGATION to the bread; it
  INDISPENSABLE, it renders piggul, it disqualifies the bread; NOT
  on the bread but in TWO DISHES with rims placed on the table so
  they not crack the bread.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 7 — MATERIAL. "For a memorial" — R.
  Shimon: "memorial"-"memorial" (Lev 5:12) — a FULL HANDFUL: two
  handfuls, one per row.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 8 — MATERIAL. "Sabbath day, Sabbath day"
  — arrange the NEW and burn the OLD on the Sabbath; "arrange it" —
  no reeds set on the Sabbath: entered on Friday, removed, laid
  along the table's LENGTH — every vessel in the sanctuary lay
  lengthwise with the house EXCEPT THE ARK.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 9 — MATERIAL. After the Sabbath he
  re-enters and sets THREE under each loaf and TWO under the top
  (no load on it); "from the children of Israel" — by their
  consent; "eternal covenant" — from Him whose the covenant is.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 10 — MATERIAL. "Eaten in a holy place";
  KNEADED and SHAPED in a holy place too ("it shall be"); the two
  loaves too ("most holy"); R. Yehuda: all their making INSIDE; R.
  Shimon: be accustomed to say — the two loaves and the showbread
  are valid in the court AND valid at Beit Pagi.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 18 11 — MATERIAL. "IT" — one loaf broken, ALL
  disqualified; "to him" / "to his sons" — to AARON without
  division, to his sons IN division; high priests eat without
  division; "from the fire offerings" — only AFTER the fire's gift;
  "eternal statute" — the eternal house.

## Sifra, Emor — Section 14 (5 rows)
- Sifra, Emor, Section 14 1 — MATERIAL. "Went out" — FROM MOSES'
  COURT: he came to pitch his tent in the camp of Dan; "what is
  your standing here?" "I am of Dan's daughters"; "each by his
  standard by their FATHERS' house" (Num 2:2); he entered Moses'
  court, LEFT CONVICTED, stood and blasphemed. "Son of an Egyptian
  man" — though there were no mamzerim then, he was AS a mamzer;
  "among the children of Israel" — he had CONVERTED; "quarreled in
  the camp" — over camp matters; "the Israelite man" — his opponent.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 14 2 — MATERIAL. "Pronounced the Name" — the
  EXPLICIT NAME heard at Sinai; "and cursed" — it does not say
  "blessed" as at Naboth (1 Kings 21:13, the euphemism) — teaches
  ONE IS NOT KILLED FOR A SUBSTITUTE NAME.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 14 3 — MATERIAL. THE COURT PROCEDURE (R.
  Yehoshua b. Korcha): witnesses examined with the SUBSTITUTE ("let
  Yosi strike Yosi"); verdict reached — not killed on the
  substitute: everyone sent out, the ELDEST witness told "say what
  you heard EXPLICITLY"; he says it; the judges STAND and REND, and
  never mend; the second and third: "I too as he." The witnesses
  need not rend — they rent when they heard.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 14 4 — MATERIAL. "Brought him to Moses" — NOT
  with the wood-gatherer; the mother's name — disgrace to him, his
  mother, his family, his tribe; the contrast Oholiab son of
  Achisamach of Dan (Exod 38:23) — praise to him, his father, his
  family, his tribe.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 14 5 — MATERIAL. "Placed in custody" — not
  with the wood-gatherer; both in the SAME PERIOD; the wood-gatherer
  KNOWN to be death-liable ("its profaners shall die") but not WHICH
  death ("it was not explained what should be done to him," Num
  15:34); here — "to explain to them by the LORD's mouth" — NOT
  KNOWN whether death-liable at all. Two custody cases with two
  different open variables.

  OUTSIDE the camp" — the COURT INSIDE, the STONING PLACE OUTSIDE;
  "those who heard shall lean" = the WITNESSES; "ALL who heard" =
  the JUDGES; "their hands" — each one's.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 19 2 — MATERIAL. "Their hands on his head" —

  execution.)
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 19 3 — MATERIAL. "Stone him" — not his
  CLOTHING; "the whole congregation" — the WITNESSES in the
  presence of all; a one-time ruling? — "speak to the children of
  Israel" — a practice FOR GENERATIONS.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 19 4 — MATERIAL. "Man, man" — GENTILES killed
  for cursing the Name like Israel, but by the SWORD — the Noahide
  has only one death mode.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 19 5 — MATERIAL. "When he curses his God" —
  R. Meir: SUBSTITUTE NAMES too carry death; the Sages: the UNIQUE
  Name — death; the substitutes — a WARNING. A recorded two-arm
  dispute on the predicate's domain.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 19 6 — MATERIAL. "Bear his sin" — R. Yehuda:
  "bearing of sin"-"bearing of sin" (Num 9:13) — KARET (the 50th

- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 19 7 — MATERIAL. "The whole congregation

## Sifra, Emor — Chapter 20 (10 rows)
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 1 — MATERIAL. "A man who strikes" — a
  WOMAN striker too ("one who strikes," Exod 21:12); struck a woman
  or a MINOR ("any soul"); not STILLBORNS ("man" — viable); "strikes
  ALL the soul" — until he strikes his WHOLE soul: one who struck
  with enough to kill and another came and killed — LIABLE (the
  first blow counts).
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 2 — MATERIAL. "Strikes the soul of a beast
  shall pay IT" — the beast, not a man paid with a beast.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 3 — MATERIAL. THE BLEMISH LIST WIDENED:
  "puts a blemish in his fellow" — pierced his ear, pulled his
  hair, spat and it reached him, removed his cloak, uncovered a
  woman's head in the market — "as he did so shall be done to him":
  the SHAME class enters the talion clause.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 4 — MATERIAL. Struck him BACKHAND, with a
  tablet, a ledger, the nails in his hand — "as he DID."
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 5 — MATERIAL. Held him in the SUN and he
  was scorched, in the COLD and he was chilled, set a DOG or a
  SNAKE on him — "did."
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 6 — MATERIAL. But "wait for me here in the
  sun" and he was scorched, or provoked the dog or snake — "blemish"
  EXCLUDES: the amplify-then-restrict — the included are DEEDS BY
  HAND, the excluded are not. The agency boundary drawn at the
  hand.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 7 — MATERIAL. TALION IS PAYMENT: blinded
  his eye — blind his? "one who strikes a beast... one who strikes
  a man" — as the beast-striker PAYS, so the man-striker PAYS; and
  Num 35:31 — no ransom for a MURDERER, ransom for LIMBS. (The
  first-call compile's talion cell, seated from the spine.)
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 8 — MATERIAL. "Strikes a beast... strikes
  a man" — the PARENT-striker liable for ONE of them (not both
  together); only with a WOUND (as the beast); only IN LIFE (as the
  beast): three parameters transferred from the beast clause.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 9 — MATERIAL. "One judgment for you" —
  monetary law like capital law in INQUIRY and EXAMINATION; but not
  in TWENTY-THREE judges — "eye for eye" amplifies (the capital
  bench not required for limbs).
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 20 10 — MATERIAL. 24:23 — the court inside,
  the stoning outside (again); not his clothing; "a STONE" — died by
  one stone, discharged; "as the LORD commanded Moses" — the
  LEANING, the PUSHING, the HANGING, and "his corpse shall not
  remain overnight on the tree" — the whole execution protocol
  imported by the as-commanded frame.

## Onkelos Exodus 38:1-31 — read IN FULL, 31 verses

- Onkelos Exod 38:1-7 — MATERIAL (spec re-run). The burnt-offering
  altar 5 × 5 × 3, horns of one piece, copper overlay; the vessel
  census (pots, scoops, basins, flesh-hooks, fire-pans, all
  copper); the grate under the rim reaching the altar's MIDDLE;
  and חֲלִיל לוּחִין עֲבַד יָתֵיהּ ("HOLLOW, of boards, he made it") —
  the hollow-structure token executed (the earth-fill altar
  doctrine's hardware). Standing verdicts from the Terumah sitting
  (exo_27's rows) credited on quick-look — same numbers.
- Onkelos Exod 38:8 — MATERIAL, the block's dual-track crown. THE
  MIRRORS VERSE: the laver and its base made of copper בְּמֶחְזְיַן
  נְשַׁיָּא דְּאָתְיָן לְצַלָּאָה בִּתְרַע מַשְׁכַּן זִמְנָא ("from the MIRRORS of
  the women WHO CAME TO PRAY at the entrance of the Tent of
  Meeting") — the received translation converts the assembling
  women (Hebrew הַצֹּבְאֹת, "the hosting/assembling ones") into
  PRAYING women — the modesty track; the spine (Midrash Tanchuma,
  Pekudei 9:4 below) runs the OTHER track: the mirrors of DESIRE
  honored by God over Moses' objection. Two recorded readings of
  one word, both carried.
- Onkelos Exod 38:9-20 — MATERIAL (spec re-run). The courtyard:
  south 100 cubits / 20 pillars, north the same, west 50 / 10,
  east 50 with the fifteen-cubit flanks (3 + 3 pillars) and the
  20-cubit embroidered screen at 5 high לָקֳבֵל סְרָדֵי דַרְתָּא
  ("corresponding to the enclosure's curtains"); copper sockets,
  silver hooks and bands, silvered pillar-tops, copper stakes.
  The hundred-by-fifty (the Sabbath carrying-limit exporter,
  Terumah round's constant) confirmed at execution.
- Onkelos Exod 38:21 — MATERIAL, the block's law head. THE AUDIT
  LINE ITSELF: אִלֵּין מִנְיָנֵי מַשְׁכְּנָא ("THESE ARE THE ACCOUNTS of
  the tabernacle"), the tabernacle OF THE TESTIMONY, דְּאִתְמְנֵי
  עַל מֵימְרָא דְמֹשֶׁה ("counted AT THE WORD OF MOSES") — the audit
  self-ordered; the Levites' service under אִיתָמָר בַּר אַהֲרֹן
  ("Itamar son of Aaron") — the second signatory on the books.
- Onkelos Exod 38:22-23 — MATERIAL. The auditors' bylines: Bezalel
  did all the LORD commanded Moses; with him Oholiab — נַגָּר וְאָמָּן
  וְצַיָּר ("carpenter, craftsman, and embroiderer") — the trades
  listed as credentials.
- Onkelos Exod 38:24-26 — MATERIAL, the block's numeric crown. THE
  PUBLISHED BOOKS WITH THE CURRENCY CONVERTED: gold 29 talents 730
  — Hebrew shekel rendered סִלְעִין בְּסִלְעֵי קוּדְשָׁא ("SELAIM, by the
  SELAIM of the Sanctuary") — the conversion-layer at the money
  register AGAIN (EX30-10's equality-invariant machinery, now on
  the audit lines); silver of the census 100 talents 1,775 selaim;
  the rate-line: a beka a head = פַּלְגּוּת סִלְעָא ("HALF A SELA") by
  the Sanctuary standard for every one passing the count from
  twenty up; and the census total 603,550 — the half-shekel
  machine's own roll (EX30-04) closing its books.
- Onkelos Exod 38:27-28 — MATERIAL. The reconciliation lines: 100
  talents → 100 sockets, כִּכְּרָא לְסַמְכָא ("a talent per socket" —
  the unit-cost line); and THE 1,775 — hooks for the pillars,
  overlaying their tops, and banding them — the line item the
  spine's audit story turns on (Pekudei 7:3 below).
- Onkelos Exod 38:29-31 — MATERIAL. The copper books: 70 talents
  2,400 selaim → the entrance sockets, the copper altar and grate
  and vessels, the court and gate sockets, and every stake — the
  disposition of every talent published to the stake-level.

## Midrash Tanchuma anchored in 38:1-31 — 57 sections

- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 1:1 (⇐ Exod 38:21) — MATERIAL (light).
  The Throne of Glory opposite the Sanctuary; Jerusalem above
  opposite Jerusalem below, built for love of the lower one; the
  destruction from "thy destroyers go forth FROM THEE."
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 1:2 — MATERIAL (light). The oath: the
  Presence will not enter the upper city UNTIL the lower is
  rebuilt (the above waits on the below); the midrash itself
  quoting the JERUSALEM TARGUM of Psalm 122:3 ("built in the
  firmament like a city joined together on earth") — the reading
  instrument cited inside the reading, again.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 2:1 — context. "LORD, I love the
  habitation of Thy house" opened.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 2:2 — MATERIAL (light). R. Shimon bar
  Yochai: the Temple below aligned opposite the Temple above (the
  מְכֻוָּן reading).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 2:3 (⇐ Exod 38:21) — MATERIAL, the
  round's schema crown. THE TABERNACLE EQUALS CREATION, DAY BY DAY:
  day 1 heavens-as-curtain ↔ the tent curtains; day 2 the
  dividing firmament ↔ the veil that DIVIDES; day 3 the gathered
  waters ↔ the laver's water; day 4 the lights ↔ the lampstand;
  day 5 the swarming fowl ↔ the winged cherubim (and the bird
  offerings); day 6 Adam ↔ the anointed high priest; day 7
  "finished-blessed-hallowed" ↔ "the work was COMPLETED" (39:32),
  "Moses BLESSED them" (39:43), "anointed and SANCTIFIED it"
  (Numbers 7:1) — the three closing verbs mapped one to one; and
  the reason: heavens and earth are WITNESSES, and the tabernacle
  is THE TESTIMONY — the building as the world's own schema run at
  furniture scale (the Genesis-as-schema doctrine in the
  tradition's own words).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 2:4 — MATERIAL (light). מִשְׁכַּן מִשְׁכַּן
  doubled = seized-as-PLEDGE twice (the two destructions) — the
  repetition read as the double mortgage.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 2:5 — MATERIAL. THE TESTIMONY TO THE
  NATIONS: the tabernacle is the PUBLIC RECEIPT of the calf's
  pardon — Moses: "who will tell the nations You are reconciled?";
  the answer: "let them make Me a sanctuary and I will dwell among
  them" — the indwelling command itself issued as the visible
  proof of forgiveness (EX25-01's grant given its purpose-clause).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 2:6 — enrichment. The witness warning
  re-run (deny Him and it is seized; heaven-and-earth summoned by
  Isaiah).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 2:7 — MATERIAL (light). The
  witness-examination and stoning procedure recited (the square,
  the wine before execution, the witnesses' stone placed BY BOTH
  TOGETHER on the heart — "the hand of the witnesses first"), the
  court-procedure shelf quoted mid-homily; recorded as the
  case-shelf's own machinery appearing in the reading book.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 2:8 — enrichment. The cup-of-blessing
  "savrei maranan / to life" custom traced to the same
  answer-formula; R. Levi's stopped-lying-lips row.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 3:1 — MATERIAL (light). Bezalel praised
  ABOVE ("see, I have called" — 31:2, to the angels) and BELOW
  ("see, the LORD has called" — 35:30, to Israel) — the two רְאֵה
  tokens as two audiences.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 3:2 (⇐ Exod 38:21) — MATERIAL, the
  round's law crown. THE TWO-SIGNATORY RULE: "a faithful man

  מִשְּׁנַיִם ("no AUTHORITY over the public is appointed with fewer

- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 3:3 — MATERIAL (light). The tabernacle

- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 3:5 — MATERIAL (light). The decree

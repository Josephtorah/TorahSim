  one I sanctify this house; I thought it was me or you: your sons

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 24 — context (David accepts

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 25 — MATERIAL. The call to

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 26 — MATERIAL. From here:

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 27 — MATERIAL. "In their

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 33 — MATERIAL. The

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 34 — MATERIAL. The mode

  THEM" — them and not their garments; tunics for PRIESTS, not for

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 35 — MATERIAL. WHERE they

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 36 — MATERIAL. "It is what

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 37 — MATERIAL. "Uncle of
  Aaron" stated to PAIR Uzziel to Aaron: as Aaron pursued peace in

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 38 — MATERIAL. Malachi's

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 39 — MATERIAL. Rabbi's

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 40 — MATERIAL. "Let not your
  heads go loose" — do not GROW HAIR: the pri'ah-pri'ah verbal

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 41 — MATERIAL. No tearing of

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 42 — MATERIAL. The exit ban

- Sifra, Shemini, Mechilta d'Miluim 2 43 — MATERIAL. "For the oil

## Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 — the wine machine (10:8-11)
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 1 — MATERIAL. The MEASURE: not any
  amount, not from the press — 'shekhar' = ENOUGH TO INTOXICATE, a
  quarter-log of forty-day wine (the data channel's quantity);
  'wine' warned even any-amount, even from the press.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 2 — MATERIAL. The recorded arms:
  R. Yehuda — wine carries DEATH, other intoxicants a WARNING;
  R. Eliezer — only drunk in its manner: diluted with any water,
  exempt.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 3 — MATERIAL. "You and your sons WITH
  you" — profaned and blemished priests excluded from the ban's
  scope.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 4 — MATERIAL. Entry AND exit; the
  ALTAR made like the tent; liable only IN SERVICE — Rabbi's
  verbal analogy to Exod 40:32's entering.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 5 — MATERIAL. Extended to Shiloh and
  the eternal House by "an everlasting statute"; the implied
  positive from "that you not die."
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 6 — MATERIAL. ISRAEL not liable to
  death for RULING while drunk — "you and your sons... and not
  die": you in death, Israel not.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 7 — MATERIAL. Nor Aaron's sons for
  ruling — by the a-fortiori from Israel; "Israel from the
  written text, Aaron and his sons from the argument."
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 8 — MATERIAL. "Everlasting statute"
  multiplies the covered SERVICES (pourings, mixings, wavings,
  presentations, fistfuls, burnings, pinchings, receptions,
  sprinklings); DRUNK SERVICE IS INVALID — from "to distinguish
  between the holy and the profane"; missing-garments and
  unwashed synced by the statute-statute verbal analogy (Exod
  29:9).
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 1 9 — MATERIAL. THE CURRICULUM VERSE:
  "to distinguish holy/profane" = the valuations; "impure/pure" =
  the purity laws; "to teach" = the RULINGS; "all the statutes" =
  the expositions; "which the LORD spoke" = the halachot; "by the
  hand of Moses" = SCRIPTURE. "Could even the TRANSLATION?" — the
  verse says "to teach": the Targum's status asked and bounded ON
  THE PAGE — the canon channel enumerating its own layers.

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 1 1 — MATERIAL. "His REMAINING sons" —

  PRECEDES IN INHERITANCE PRECEDES IN HONOR; R. Eliezer: the

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 1 3 — MATERIAL. "The meal offering" =

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 1 4 — MATERIAL. "Eat it UNLEAVENED" —

  no generational parallel.
- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 1 5 — MATERIAL. "Beside the altar" — not
  in the sanctuary, not on the altar; the exclusion row (she /
  her / it) excluding the todah's bread, the nazirite ram's, the

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 1 6 — MATERIAL. "In a HOLY PLACE" —

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 1 7 — MATERIAL. "Your due and your

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 1 9 — MATERIAL. Breast and thigh eaten

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 1 10 — MATERIAL. "You, your sons and

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 1 11 — MATERIAL. Communal peace

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 2 7 — context (dibbur as bold speech —

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 2 11 — MATERIAL. The acute-mourner

  R. Yehuda: for the generations day and night barred; Rabbi: the

- Sifra, Shemini, Chapter 2 12 — MATERIAL. "Moses heard and it was

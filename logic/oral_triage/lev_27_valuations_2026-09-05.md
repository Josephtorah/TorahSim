## Sifra, Bechukotai — Section 3 (14 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 1 — MATERIAL. THE VALUER/VALUED SPLIT:
  "the children of Israel" VALUE, gentiles do not; are they VALUED?
  "a man" (R. Meir): the valued class is wider than the valuers' —
  the deaf-mute, the imbecile, the minor are valued but do not
  value.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 2 — MATERIAL. R. Yehuda: Israel are
  VALUED, gentiles not; do gentiles value? "a man" — yes: the
  valuers' class is wider — the tumtum and the androgynos value but
  are not valued. The same two tokens, the two classes' widths
  swapped.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 3 — MATERIAL. Converts and slaves by
  "say to them"; "a man" — not a minor; a minor who knows to
  articulate? "when he articulates" — included; "vow"-"vow" (Deut
  23:22): as there "do not delay," so here — the vow-delay timer
  imported.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 4 — MATERIAL. "A vow in your
  valuation" — valuations likened to vows: both under do-not-delay.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 5 — MATERIAL. "In your valuation" —
  includes an UNSPECIFIED valuation; or: he gives the valuation of
  the WHOLE, not of limbs.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 6 — MATERIAL. Limbs on which LIFE
  depends? "souls"; "souls" — not the DEAD; the dying? "he shall
  stand... and value" — WHAT CAN STAND CAN BE VALUED; what cannot
  stand cannot. The standing predicate.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 7 — MATERIAL. "Souls" — one valuing
  a hundred; a woman valuing; the disfigured and the boil-stricken,
  outside the worth class, inside the valuation class.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 8 — MATERIAL. "And it shall be" — the
  tumtum, the androgynos, the under-a-month, outside valuation,
  INSIDE worth-vows. The two classes not nested.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 9 — MATERIAL. "Male" — not the tumtum
  and androgynos; not even as female — "if a FEMALE" — a certain
  male and a certain female; THE SIXTIETH YEAR counts BELOW: "from
  sixty years and UPWARD."
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 10 — MATERIAL. The twentieth and the
  fifth year likewise below, by argument from the sixtieth.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 11 — MATERIAL. But sixty-below is a
  STRINGENCY, and twenty-below and five-below would be a LENIENCY
  — so "year"-"year" verbal analogy: as the sixtieth year is
  below, so the fifth and twentieth, whether lenient or strict. R.
  Eliezer: "and upward" — one month and one day AFTER the month, by
  "and upward" (Numbers 3:40). The boundary-year rule with the
  one-day-past threshold.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 12 — MATERIAL. After five and after
  twenty likewise: a month and a day past.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 13 — MATERIAL. "If he is too poor for
  your valuation" — too poor to pay it; "he shall stand him before
  the priest" — excludes the dead; the dying? the standing
  predicate again.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 3 14 — MATERIAL. THE FOUR CLOCKS: "the
  valuation of so-and-so on me" and he died — "he shall value him"
  — liable even if dead; "the priest shall value him" — he gives at
  the VALUATION'S TIME; "according to what the VOWER'S hand attains"
  — the vower's, not the valued's; man, woman, minor; from here:
  MEANS by the VOWER, YEARS by the VALUED, VALUATIONS by the VALUED,
  THE VALUATION AT ITS TIME. "The priest shall value him" — the
  archetype: all valuations by the priest.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 9 (16 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 1 — MATERIAL. "This one's leg is a
  burnt offering" — the whole? "all that he gives of it shall be
  holy" — not the whole; to common? "shall be holy"; sold to those
  owing burnt offerings, its price common except the leg's (R. Meir,
  R. Yehuda); R. Yosei, R. Shimon: even a leg sanctifies the WHOLE
  ("all... shall be holy" includes it). The limb-sanctification
  dispute.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 2 — MATERIAL. Temple-upkeep
  consecration makes no substitute — "offering" excludes it.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 3 — MATERIAL. Public offerings — "he
  shall not exchange it"; partners' — "nor substitute it": the
  INDIVIDUAL makes substitution, not the public, not partners.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 4 — MATERIAL. R. Shimon: the tithe
  was in the class and went out to teach — individual offerings
  only; and lest one learn only the tithe's likes (altar holy,
  cattle and flock, eaten two days) — "beast," "if a beast":
  most-holy and lesser-holy of the individual included.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 5 — MATERIAL. "Nor substitute IT" —
  the consecrated makes a substitute, THE SUBSTITUTE MAKES NONE; R.
  Yehuda: the offspring makes one; the Sages: neither offspring nor
  substitute. Substitution is not transitive.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 6 — MATERIAL. "Good for bad or bad for
  good" — whole for blemished, blemished for whole (bad = blemished
  by Deut 17:1); "if he substitutes" — the WOMAN, the HEIR included;
  "beast for beast" — one for two, two for one, one for a hundred,
  a hundred for one; R. Shimon: "beast for beast," not "beast for
  beasts"; they said: many beasts are called "beast."
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 7 — MATERIAL. Not beast for birds nor
  birds for beast; not beast for meal offerings nor the reverse.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 8 — MATERIAL. Not beast for fetuses,
  nor fetuses for beast; not fetuses for limbs nor limbs for fetuses;
  not limbs for whole offerings nor whole for limbs.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 9 — MATERIAL. R. Yosei: limbs FOR
  whole offerings yes, not whole for limbs — as "this leg is a burnt
  offering" makes the whole a burnt offering, so "this leg for that"
  makes the whole a substitute.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 10 — MATERIAL. "It and its substitute
  shall be holy" — where does holiness fall on it? IN THE OWNER'S
  HOUSE; so the substitute — in the owner's house.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 11 — MATERIAL. THE FIRSTBORN
  ARGUMENT: the firstborn makes a substitute in the owner's house,
  not in the priest's (R. Akiva); R. Yochanan b. Nuri: why not? R.
  Akiva: sin and guilt offerings are the priest's gift and take no
  substitute, so the firstborn; R. Yochanan: those have no ownership
  in life, the firstborn does; R. Akiva: YOU HAVE ANSWERED THE
  ARGUMENT — WHAT DO YOU ANSWER THE VERSE? "It and its substitute" —
  where holiness falls, in the owner's house. Argument overruled by
  ink.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 12 — MATERIAL. Once given to the
  priest, neither it nor its substitute makes substitution.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 13 — MATERIAL. "And it shall be" —
  the consecrated makes a substitute, not the substitute.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 14 — MATERIAL. The a-fortiori (the
  substitute takes holiness even blemished, surely it substitutes)
  refused by "it... shall be holy."
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 15 — MATERIAL. "Shall be holy" —
  holiness falls on the substitute EVEN PERMANENTLY BLEMISHED; the
  reverse a-fortiori refused likewise. The substitute stricter than
  its source in one respect, weaker in another.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 9 16 — MATERIAL. R. Yosei b. R. Yehuda:
  "shall be holy" — THE INADVERTENT LIKE THE DELIBERATE: substitution
  binds even in error.

- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 4 1 — MATERIAL. "If any beast" — the
  impure beast? 27:27 speaks of that; so — DISQUALIFIED CONSECRATED

- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 4 2 — MATERIAL. "Impure" — the DEAD
  included; "this is consecrated" and it died — redeemed? "he shall
  stand... and value" — the standing predicate: no standing, no
  valuation.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 4 3 — MATERIAL. Why include the dead
  beast and exclude the one that died after consecration? learned
  from the impure beast: its redemption hour equals its consecration
  hour — so the dead one included, the died-after excluded.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 4 4 — MATERIAL. Lest one learn only the
  impure beast's likes (first consecration, subject to trespass,
  wholly Heaven's — the burnt bulls and goats): "beast," "if a
  beast," "if any beast" — most-holy and lesser-holy, public and
  individual.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 4 5 — MATERIAL. "Stand the BEAST before
  the priest" — a beast is redeemed; BIRDS, WOOD, FRANKINCENSE,
  SERVICE VESSELS are NOT.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 4 6 — MATERIAL. "Value it good or bad"
  — consecrated things not redeemed BY ESTIMATE; "as the priest's
  valuation so it shall be" — THE BIDDING LADDER: bids of ten,
  twenty, thirty, forty, fifty; the fifty retracts — his property
  seized down to ten; each retractor likewise; the ten retracts —
  sold at its worth and the shortfall taken from him. A retraction
  protocol.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 4 7 — MATERIAL. "If he redeems" — the
  woman, the heir; "add a fifth" — two fifths appear: one adds and
  one does not, owner or anyone? as the redemptions below have the
  OWNER adding a fifth and no one else, so here.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 4 8 — MATERIAL. "A man who consecrates
  his HOUSE" — a dwelling? 27:15 says that; so — one consecrates his
  PROPERTY.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 4 9 — MATERIAL. "Holy to the LORD" —
  UNSPECIFIED CONSECRATIONS GO TO TEMPLE UPKEEP. The default.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 10 (13 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 1 — MATERIAL. Not by estimate; "as
  the priest values so it stands" — THE OWNER'S PRECEDENCE: owner
  and another both at twenty, the owner first because he adds a
  fifth; another at twenty-one, the owner gives twenty-six; at
  twenty-two, twenty-seven... at twenty-five, thirty — the fifth is
  never added on the other's INCREMENT; at twenty-six, the owner may
  pay thirty-one and a dinar or be told "it has reached you." An
  auction rule with the fifth's ceiling.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 2 — MATERIAL. "If the consecrator
  redeems" — the woman, the heir; "add a fifth and it is his" — paid,
  his; unpaid, not his.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 3 — MATERIAL. "His holding-field" —
  from his mother too ("a man consecrates"); "according to its SEED"
  — not its growth; "a homer of barley seed at FIFTY SHEKELS" — A
  KING'S DECREE: the same rate in the sandy district and in the
  orchards of Sebaste. A fixed rate self-labeled as decree — the
  data channel named.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 4 — MATERIAL. Not consecrated in the
  Jubilee year, but if consecrated it holds ("from the Jubilee
  year"); "his field" — crevices ten handbreadths deep and rocks ten
  high are NOT measured with it.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 5 — MATERIAL. "As your valuation it
  stands" — FORTY-NINE SELAS AND FORTY-NINE PUNDIONS; the pundion is
  the small-change premium. The per-year rate derived.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 6 — MATERIAL. "After the Jubilee" —
  near it; far — "if after the Jubilee he consecrates"; the crevices
  and rocks excluded again.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 7 — MATERIAL. Not consecrated less
  than two years before the Jubilee, not redeemed less than a year
  after — "the priest shall reckon the money by the remaining
  years."
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 8 — MATERIAL. Months not reckoned
  against the sanctuary, but the sanctuary may reckon months.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 9 — MATERIAL. "I will pay year by
  year" — not heard; he pays ALL AT ONCE.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 10 — MATERIAL. "Remaining years" —
  years not months; the sanctuary may make months a year; "until the
  Jubilee" — the year itself not entered; "deducted from your
  valuation" — even from the sanctuary's side: if the sanctuary held
  it a year or two before the Jubilee, a sela and a pundion deducted
  per year.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 11 — MATERIAL. "If he redeems" — the
  woman, the heir; "the field" — a beit-kor, a beit-letekh, a
  beit-seah, a beit-kav all alike; paid — his.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 12 — MATERIAL. "If he does not redeem"
  — the owner; "if he sold" — the TREASURER; "to another" — not his
  son; his brother? "to a MAN" — includes the son and excludes the
  brother: the son stands in his father's place for designation and
  the Hebrew slave, the brother does not.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 10 13 — MATERIAL. "Not redeemed again"
  — as it was; but he may BUY it from the treasurer and hold it as a
  purchased field.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 11 (8 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 11 1 — MATERIAL. "The field in its

  to the priests holds it? it goes out to ALL his brother priests:
  not "his holding."
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 11 4 — MATERIAL. "A purchased field not

  consecrated — a holding field (R. Meir); consecrated before the
  father died — R. Yehuda, R. Shimon: fit to become a holding field,

- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 11 5 — MATERIAL. R. Eliezer: "reckon"-

- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 11 6 — MATERIAL. "Give the valuation ON
  THAT DAY" — no delay: even a pearl for the light-handed has only
  its place and its hour.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 11 7 — MATERIAL. "Returns to whom he
  bought it from" — not the treasurer; "to whom the land's holding

  the PRIEST, not the first owners; "every valuation in the shekel"
  — no valuation UNDER A SELA; "the holy shekel" — the firstborn's

- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 11 8 — MATERIAL. "Twenty gerah the
  shekel" — the sela's size; more if he wishes ("shall be"); never
  less ("it," Numbers 18:16). The Onkelos-mediated sela of round 28
  now at its source verse.

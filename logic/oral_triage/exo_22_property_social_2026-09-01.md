## Mekhilta of Rabbi Yishmael on Exodus 22 — read IN FULL, 95 paragraphs

- Mekhilta on Exod 22:1-2 (burglar, 6¶) — MATERIAL. The tunneler's
  doubt-license (doubt-came-to-steal-doubt-to-kill), and FROM HERE the
  chain derives that saving life overrides — the pikuach-nefesh seed in
  our own verse. R. Yishmael: "did the sun rise on him ALONE?" — the sun
  is a figure for PEACE-CLARITY (known to be at peace → killing him is
  liable); day/night explicitly NOT the divider, proven by the
  betrothed-girl analogy (this verse "comes to teach and ends up
  taught"). R. Eliezer b. Yaakov's wine-jars case. Sold-for-his-theft:
  capped six years from Exod 21:2 (cross-unit: exo_21_slave_person);
  sold only for value (R. Yehudah / R. Eliezer dispute on
  more-than-worth).
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:3 (6¶) — MATERIAL. "Found in his hand" — hand =
  DOMAIN (Num 21 + Gen 24 as witnesses). "Alive" extends to wild
  animals; "two" — live payment, not dead. THE SEVEN THIEVES LADDER:
  genevat daat (deceiving people) is the FIRST of thieves — false
  hospitality, false gifts, false measures; Absalom's three thefts; and
  Israel at Sinai "stole the Supreme Mind" with we-will-do-and-hear
  while the heart was not steady (Psalm 78). Stealing Torah by stealth
  earns MERIT (Proverbs' thief not despised) — the one blessed theft.
  Above them: stealing what is forbidden-in-benefit (exempt); above
  all: GONEV NEFESH — kidnapping is capital. Five-for-ox
  four-for-sheep placement.
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:4 (grazing, 6¶) — MATERIAL. THE TOOTH (shen)
  category installed: "the tooth is muad to eat what suits it, the
  beast muad to break as it walks" — liability only once the damager
  LEFT the owner's domain; assessment from the BEST land (meitav):
  R. Yishmael (the victim's best) vs R. Akiva (assessment-in-best as
  general rule, kal va-chomer to hekdesh); field vs vineyard each its
  own liability; handing the flock to son/agent/slave exempts, to
  deaf-mute/deranged/minor is liable; R. Natan's
  stacker-without-permission case.
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:5 (fire, 6¶) — MATERIAL. Fire despite the
  ownership a-fortiori: the verse comes to make ACCIDENT LIKE INTENT,
  unintending like intending, and THE WOMAN LIKE THE MAN for all the
  Torah's damages. Thorns = the measure-giver (river/road/ten-hand
  fence cut liability; R. Elazar b. Azariah's beit-kor stance, R.
  Eliezer 16 cubits, R. Akiva 50); the Jordan-crossing fire case
  (leaping fire liable a mil). Stack includes hidden vessels? — field
  teaches OPEN things only. FOUR GENERAL RULES (R. Shimon b. Elazar in
  R. Meir's name): the DOMAIN MATRIX — damager's domain exempt,
  victim's domain full, joint/public domain: tooth-and-foot exempt,
  the rest muad full / tam half.
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:6-8 (unpaid keeper, 9¶) — MATERIAL. The FIRST
  KEEPER paragraph: deposit needs "here, guard this" ("your eyes on
  it" = exempt, R. Yishmael); claims must be measurable ("any claim
  not by measure, weight and count is no claim"); thief-from-the-thief
  exempt from double; "el ha-elohim" = TO THE JUDGES, for an OATH (not
  the urim ve-tumim — ruled from "asher yarshi'un"); shelichut-yad
  verbal analogy chain (oath by yod-heh, in court, both passages
  mutual); BEIT SHAMMAI vs BEIT HILLEL: liability for INTENT of
  misuse (machshevet ha-lev) vs only from actual misuse; "kol davar
  pesha" run as KLAL-U-FRAT-U-KLAL (middah: movables with no land
  liability); "ki hu zeh" = ADMISSION IN PART of the claim (the
  modeh-be-miktzat seed); THE THREE-JUDGES DERIVATION: dinei mamonot
  in THREE from this passage's three elohim-tokens (R. Yoshiyah count,
  R. Yonatan's no-expounding-beginnings + no-even-court, Rebbi via
  the plural yarshi'un; R. Meir* five-to-finish-in-three); double "to
  his fellow" — not to hekdesh, not to others; the fifth only where
  principal is paid (R. Shimon's reconciliation).
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:9-12 (paid keeper, 12¶) — MATERIAL. THE SECOND
  KEEPER identified: "this one is the PAID keeper, the earlier the
  UNPAID" — argued from benefit-parallel with the hirer; died = by
  Heaven's hand, broken = by beast, captured = by bandits; the
  possible-to-rescue test (R. Eliezer from death's paradigm; R.
  Akiva's counter); "none seeing" = NO WITNESSES (if there are
  witnesses, the keeper is exempt without oath); THE OATH OF THE LORD:
  by the NAME (yod-heh) — and from here ALL Torah oaths are by the
  Name; "between the two of them" excludes heirs, excludes a suspected
  opponent, bars the judge from forcing the oath (R. Natan: the oath
  FALLS ON BOTH — the false-swearer's opposite shares); "and the owner
  ACCEPTS [the oath] and he shall not pay" — from here: ALL WHO SWEAR
  [by Torah law] SWEAR AND DO NOT PAY; the carcass belongs to its
  owner (baal ha-nevelah mitapel be-nivlato); torn-beast evidence:
  bring witnesses / bring the carcass (ed ha-terefah, R. Yonatan/R.
  Yochanan b. Yoshiyah/R. Yoshiyah); WHICH KILLS PAY: preventable
  predators (cat, fox, marten) liable; overpowering ones (wolf, lion,
  bear, leopard, bardelas, snake) exempt — the possible/impossible
  rescue line.
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:13-14 (borrower + hirer, 5¶) — MATERIAL. The
  BORROWER "detached" by the text into his own parashah (nitko
  ha-katuv); liability from leaving the lender's domain even one hour;
  broken-or-died extended to captured (mitah-mitah analogy) and to
  theft/loss (a-fortiori); BE-VE'ALAV: if the owner is WITH him (even
  one hour at the start) — exempt; not with him — liable. THE HIRER
  derived: "if it is hired, it came for its hire" — reasons like the
  paid keeper (swears on accidents, PAYS theft and loss): THE
  FOUR-KEEPERS TABLE closes (unpaid / paid / borrower / hirer).
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:15-16 (seducer, 7¶) — MATERIAL. The seducer pays
  a FINE (needed in writing — the a-fortiori from the rapist fails);
  "not betrothed" excludes the returned widow/divorcee from betrothal
  (R. Yose ha-Gelili) vs R. Akiva (still fined; the phrase freed for
  the gezerah shavah); FIFTY of the rapist read in both directions;
  MOHAR = KETUBAH ("ein mohar ela ketubah"), proven from Shechem's
  'multiply upon me mohar and gift' (Gen 34 — our gen_56 span's ink
  cited as the definition's seat); father's VETO (rather: father may
  keep or refuse — both seducer and rapist); no-father case still
  fined (mikol makom); "fit to him as a wife" excludes the forbidden
  unions (widow to high priest, divorcee to common priest, mamzeret...).
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:17 (sorceress, 1¶) — MATERIAL. Man and woman
  alike; EXECUTION-MODE dispute: R. Yishmael sword (lo-tichyeh to
  lo-tichyeh), R. Akiva stoning (Sinai's beast-or-man), R. Yehudah b.
  Beteira stoning via ov/yidoni's exit-to-teach; azharah from Deut
  18's "there shall not be found."
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:18 (bestiality, 1¶) — MATERIAL. Stoning by the
  Lev 20 harigah-harigah analogy; the lain-with counted as the lier
  (shochev = nishkav); azharot located (Lev 18; Deut 23 kadesh).
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:19 (idol sacrifice, 2¶) — MATERIAL. THE SERVICE
  ARCHITECTURE: zevichah "was in the general rule and left to teach" —
  Temple-style services (slaughter and its kin) are liable for ANY
  idol whether or not that idol's cult; non-Temple-style only in its
  own cult's manner. יחרם read as EXECUTION. Shituf (joining His name
  with another) — R. Shimon b. Yochai: liable to destruction (Kings'
  fearing-the-LORD-and-serving-their-gods); the mountains-verse
  against hidden-idolatry excuses.
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:20 (the sojourner, 1¶ long) — MATERIAL. Dual
  verbs split: ona'ah = WORDS, lachatz = MONEY; "a blemish in you, do
  not say to your fellow" (R. Natan — you were strangers); THE
  BELOVED-GERIM CENSUS: converts carry every title Israel carries
  (servants, ministers, lovers, covenant, acceptance, guarding —
  verse-paired list); Abraham circumcised at NINETY-NINE so the door
  stays open to converts of any age (our gen_33 material cited as the
  door-opening precedent); the four confessing classes (Isaiah 44).
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:21-23 (widow and orphan, 3¶) — MATERIAL. Scope
  dispute: R. Yishmael — ALL persons (the verbs unqualified); R. Akiva
  — widow/orphan named because their way is to be afflicted. One
  affliction, great or small, is liability — WITH THE EXECUTION
  NARRATIVE: R. Shimon and R. Yishmael led out to die, and R. Yishmael
  locates the sin in litigants KEPT WAITING while he finished his cup
  or sandal — "one delay-affliction and one great affliction are one."
  R. Akiva's calamity-reading to his students. The cry: heard always,
  FASTER when he cries; the individual-vs-many a-fortiori to the good
  measure; the punishment mirrored word for word (your wives widows —
  even ALMANOT CHAYOT, living widowhood, courts unable to release;
  your sons orphans — courts keeping them from their father's estate
  on presumption he lives) — measure-for-measure at the ink grain.
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:24 (lending, 5¶) — MATERIAL. "IM = OBLIGATION"
  here — one of the Torah's THREE obligatory if-clauses (with the
  firstfruits meal-offering and the stone altar): lending is CHOVAH,
  proven from Deut's ha'avet ta'avitenu; money-for-money not
  produce-for-produce; THE PRIORITY LADDER: My people before a
  gentile, the poor before the rich, YOUR poor before your city's,
  your city's before another city's ("the poor WITH YOU"); "do not be
  to him as a creditor" — do not show yourself to him at all times;
  INTEREST: the lender violates FIVE prohibitions, and the witnesses,
  guarantor and scribe violate with him (Rebbi permits the scribe; R.
  Meir bars the dictating lender from the resurrection-of-interest
  clause).
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:25-26 (the pledge, 7¶) — MATERIAL. Day-garment
  returned all day, night-garment all night (paired with Deut's
  sunset-return): "they pledge day-clothing by night and
  night-clothing by day, and return each in its time." Kesutah = the
  cloak, simlato = the shirt, bameh yishkav brings the hide-mattress;
  R. Natan's case: a hundred owed in court does not force the sale of
  the two-hundred cloak; "and I will hear, for I am GRACIOUS — with
  compassion I created My world."
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:27 (judge and prince, 3¶) — MATERIAL. Elohim =
  judge (R. Yishmael) vs the azharah for BLASPHEMY (R. Akiva — the
  warning the penalty-verse Lev 24 lacked); the FOUR-LIABILITIES
  utterance: a prince's son cursing his father is liable four ways
  (prince, father, judge, "among your people"); R. Yehudah b.
  Beteira: judge and prince each its own liability; "be-amkha" —
  while they act by your people's way.
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:28 (fullness and flow, 1¶) — MATERIAL.
  Melatkha = BIKKURIM (firstfruits), dim'akha = TERUMAH; "do not
  delay" = do not REORDER the gifts (firstfruits before terumah
  before first tithe before second — argued from the four/three/two/
  one NAMES each gift carries); reordered anyway — the act stands
  though forbidden (mah she-asah asui).
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:29 (firstborn, 3¶) — MATERIAL. The human/animal
  HEKESH runs both ways: stillbirths open the womb-exemption for
  both; redemption/gift valid to any priest anywhere; thirty-day
  care for both; "seven days with its mother" = WITH (not under);
  eighth-day-and-onward valid by the shemini-shemini analogy;
  consecrated animals' young are nursed from CHULLIN funds (the
  sanctified may not be milked).
- Mekhilta on Exod 22:30 (holy men; the torn flesh, 3¶) — MATERIAL.
  "When you are holy, you are Mine" (R. Yishmael); Issi b. Yehudah:
  every new commandment ADDS holiness to Israel; TEREFAH: "in the
  field" = the ORDINARY CASE (dibber ha-katuv ba-hoveh — house
  included via the nevelah hekesh; the method named on three
  parallels); to the DOG as-the-dog (benefit permitted — a-fortiori
  from carcass); and the dog's WAGE: "not a dog shall whet its
  tongue" (Exod 11) — "the Holy One withholds no creature's reward."

## Onkelos Exodus 22:1-30 — read IN FULL, 30 verses

- Onkelos Exod 22:2 — MATERIAL. אִם עֵינָא דְסָהֲדַיָא נְפָלַת עֲלוֹהִי ("if
  the EYE OF WITNESSES fell upon him") — the sun REPLACED by witness
  observation: the translation encodes the Mekhilta's peace-clarity
  reading as law (seen by witnesses → no blood-license).
- Onkelos Exod 22:7-8 — MATERIAL. אלהים rendered דַיָנַיָא ("the
  JUDGES") at every token — the translation decides the judges-not-
  oracle question; "asher yarshi'un" = "whom the judges CONVICT."
- Onkelos Exod 22:19 — MATERIAL. "Who sacrifices to the IDOLS OF THE
  NATIONS (טַעֲוַת עַמְמַיָא) shall be KILLED (יִתְקְטֵל)" — object and
  penalty both resolved in translation (errors-of-the-nations; cherem
  = execution).
- Onkelos Exod 22:28 — MATERIAL. מלאתך rendered בִּכּוּרָךְ ("your
  FIRSTFRUITS") — the Mekhilta's identification carried as the
  translation itself; the verse-tail made a firstborn-separation
  command (תַּפְרֵשׁ, "set apart before Me").
- Onkelos Exod 22:30 — MATERIAL. ובשר בשדה טרפה rendered וּבְשַׂר תְּלִישׁ
  מִן חֵיוָא חַיְתָא ("flesh TORN FROM THE LIVING ANIMAL") — the terefah
  clause translated with the LIMB-FROM-THE-LIVING vocabulary: the
  Israel-side statute rendered in the very terms of the Noahide ban
  our gen_21 seats (Gen 9:4) — the two-era architecture visible
  inside the received translation.

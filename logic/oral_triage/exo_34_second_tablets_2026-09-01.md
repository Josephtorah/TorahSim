## Onkelos Exodus 34:1-35 — read IN FULL, 35 verses

- Onkelos Exod 34:1-4 — MATERIAL. פְּסָל לָךְ ("CARVE FOR YOURSELF")
  — the second tablets human-hewn, God-written: the division of
  manufacture (the spine's marriage-contract parable — you tore the
  document, you supply the parchment and I write; the sapphire
  quarry revealed IN Moses' tent — the Torah feeds its laborer, the
  chips his wealth). And the PRIVACY CONDITION (Midrash Tanchuma,
  Ki Tisa 31): the first tablets given in public thunder were
  touched by the evil eye and broken; the second given with no man
  seen on the whole mountain — "nothing is finer than modesty."
- Onkelos Exod 34:5-7 — MATERIAL, the parashah's crown. THE
  THIRTEEN ATTRIBUTES in the received translation: מַרְחִיק רְגַז
  ("DISTANCING anger"), מַסְגֵּי לְמֶעְבַּד טַבְוָן וּקְשׁוֹט
  ("MAKING goodness and truth ABOUND") — and the paradox-clause
  RESOLVED BY A CONDITION: נַקֵּה לֹא יְנַקֶּה split as סָלַח
  לִדְתַיְבִין לְאוֹרַיְתֵיהּ ("He FORGIVES THOSE WHO RETURN TO HIS
  TORAH") וְלִדְלָא תַיְבִין לָא מְזַכֵּי ("and those who do NOT
  return He does not acquit") — repentance inserted as the branch
  condition; the visiting-of-fathers scoped to בְּנִין מָרָדִין
  ("REBELLIOUS sons" — only continuers), the Decalogue's own
  scoping met again. The spine's frame (Ki Tisa 32): He TAUGHT
  Moses how to plead — the attributes as the standing appeal
  liturgy ("whenever Israel says this order, they are answered" —
  the taught-prayer doctrine). TESTING-shelf pointer: Mishnah Yoma
  4:2 (this exam's docket) anchors at 34:7's iniquity-vocabulary —
  the high priest's confession formula.
- Onkelos Exod 34:9-10 — MATERIAL (light). The Presence asked to go
  within (שְׁכִנְתָּא); the covenant of wonders: אַעֲבֵּיד פְּרִישָׁן
  ("I will do WONDERS not created in all the earth").
- Onkelos Exod 34:18-26 — MATERIAL. The covenant tariff-list
  re-enacted after the pardon: the firstborn laws — the donkey
  redeemed with a sheep or neck-broken (וְתַנְקְפֵיהּ), none appear

  clause with the season-tokens בִּזְרוֹעָא וּבַחֲצָדָא ("in PLOWING

  reads for the sabbatical-eve limit, this docket); the three
  appearances before רִבּוֹן עָלְמָא ("the MASTER OF THE WORLD");
  the boundary-promise (no man will covet your land when you go

  THE SANCTUARY"); and the third meat-milk ban rendered again as

- Onkelos Exod 34:27-28 — MATERIAL, the project's own crown. THE
  COVENANT RUNS ON THE MOUTH: כִּי עַל פִּי הַדְּבָרִים rendered

- Onkelos Exod 34:8, 34:11-17 — context (the swift prostration; the

  Section 9 6-8 expounds the redundancy.

  (ונכרתה) rendered וְיִשְׁתֵּיצֵי ("shall be DESTROYED") here and at
  every karet seat in the span.
- Onkelos Lev 7:12-14, 16-17, 21 balance — read in full: the four
  bread kinds (גְּרִיצָן cakes / אֶסְפּוֹגִין wafers — the sponge-loaf

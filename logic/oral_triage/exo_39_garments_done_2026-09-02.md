## Onkelos Exodus 39:1-43 — read IN FULL, 43 verses

- Onkelos Exod 39:1-7 — MATERIAL (spec re-run + delta). The
  garments made; the gold-thread process spelled out at 39:3:
  וְרַדִּידוּ יָת טַסֵּי דַהֲבָא וְקַצִּיצוּ חוּטִין ("they BEAT the gold
  into plates and CUT threads") to work into each color — the
  execution-only manufacturing verse (no command-side parallel:
  the one process the execution chapters ADD); the ephod, the
  band of one weave with it; and at 39:6 the delta: the shoham
  stones engraved גְּלִיפָן כְּתַב מְפָרַשׁ ("engraved with SCRIPT MADE
  CLEAR/DISTINCT") where Hebrew has "signet engravings" — the
  clear-script token.
- Onkelos Exod 39:8-21 — MATERIAL (spec re-run). The breastplate
  doubled, span by span; the four rows of stones set in gold, the
  twelve names כְּתַב מְפָרַשׁ כִּגְלוֹף דְּעִזְקָא ("in CLEAR SCRIPT, as
  signet engraving") — the token again on the tribal register;
  the chains, rings, and the blue-cord binding לָא יִתְפָּרֵק
  חוּשְׁנָא ("the breastplate shall NOT BE DETACHED from the
  ephod") — the standing negative (EX28's not-detach rule)
  executed as commanded.
- Onkelos Exod 39:22-26 — MATERIAL (spec re-run). The robe wholly
  greenish-blue, the armor-collar opening לָא יִתְבְּזָע ("that it
  NOT TEAR" — the not-tear negative's hardware); the hem's
  pomegranates and gold bells alternating זַגָּא וְרִמּוֹנָא זַגָּא
  וְרִמּוֹנָא ("a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a
  pomegranate") לְשַׁמָּשָׁא ("for SERVICE") — the audible-entry
  spec executed.
- Onkelos Exod 39:27-31 — MATERIAL. The linen undershirts,
  turban, שְׁבַח כּוֹבָעַיָּא ("the SPLENDOR of the hats" — the
  glory-word on the headgear), breeches, sash; and THE PLATE:
  צִיצָא כְּלִילָא דְקוּדְשָׁא ("the diadem, the HOLY CROWN") of pure
  gold, written גְּלִיפָן כְּתַב מְפָרַשׁ ("an engraving in CLEAR
  script"): קֹדֶשׁ לַייָ ("HOLY TO THE LORD") — the jurisdiction
  plate (EX28's seat) manufactured, the clear-script token's
  third strike in one chapter.
- Onkelos Exod 39:32 — MATERIAL, the span's first closing verb:
  וּשְׁלִים כָּל פָּלְחַן מַשְׁכְּנָא ("and all the SERVICE of the
  tabernacle was COMPLETED") — and the obedience formula: the
  sons of Israel did כְּכֹל דִּי פַקִּיד יְיָ יָת מֹשֶׁה ("according
  to ALL that the LORD commanded Moses") — the day-7
  "finished" verb's counterpart (the creation mapping's first
  leg, Pekudei 2:3).
- Onkelos Exod 39:33-41 — MATERIAL. THE DELIVERY MANIFEST: they
  BRING the tabernacle TO Moses — tent, vessels, clasps, planks,
  bars, pillars, sockets, covers, veil, ark-with-poles-and-cover,
  table, lampstand with its lamps IN ORDER, oils, altars, screens,
  laver, court hardware, and the garments — the complete kit
  enumerated line by line at handoff (the spine explains WHY it
  came to Moses: the erection deadlock, below).
- Onkelos Exod 39:42-43 — MATERIAL, the span's crown pair. The
  obedience total: "according to all that the LORD commanded
  Moses, so the sons of Israel did ALL the service"; וַחֲזָא מֹשֶׁה
  יָת כָּל עִבִדְתָּא ("and Moses SAW all the work") — the
  inspection — וְהָא עֲבָדוּ יָתַהּ כְּמָא דִי פַקִּיד יְיָ ("and BEHOLD,
  they had done it as the LORD commanded") — the acceptance test
  passed — וּבָרִיךְ יָתְהוֹן מֹשֶׁה ("and Moses BLESSED them") — the
  day-7 blessing verb (the creation mapping's second leg; the
  case shelf meets the verse at Tosefta Menahot 7:2's completion
  rows).

## Midrash Tanchuma anchored in 39:1-43 — 13 sections

- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 10:1 (⇐ Exod 39:32-33) — MATERIAL,
  the span's law row. THE WAYS-OF-PEACE LIST opened as the
  yelamdenu: "these things were instituted מִפְּנֵי דַרְכֵי שָׁלוֹם
  (for the WAYS OF PEACE) — the PRIEST reads from the Torah
  FIRST, after him the Levite, after him the Israelite" — the
  reading-precedence rule (the case shelf's row at Mishnah
  Gittin 5:8) recited at the completion verse; how precious
  peace is ("contend not with them in battle").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:1 — enrichment. "He withdraws not
  His eyes from the righteous" — Isaac resembling Abraham,
  Joseph Jacob; the realized-plans frame.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:5 (⇐ Exod 39:33) — MATERIAL, the
  block's crown. THE ERECTION DEADLOCK: the work done, all sat
  WAITING for the Presence — and it did not come; the
  wise-hearted tried to raise the tabernacle AND IT FELL apart
  each time; Bezalel and Oholiab tried and failed; the murmuring
  began ("see what the son of Amram did with our money —
  promising the Holy One would dwell in goats-hair curtains").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:6 — MATERIAL. THE CAUSE DISCLOSED:
  Moses grieved that he had NO SHARE in the making (Israel gave,
  the craftsmen made — Moses neither); so the Holy One CONCEALED
  the erection from all hands — and Israel carried the whole kit
  TO MOSES ("and they brought the tabernacle to Moses" — 39:33
  explained: the delivery manifest is the failed erectors'
  hand-back), each part shown against the spec ("did you not
  tell us such-and-such?" — "yes" — item by item).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:7 — MATERIAL. THE RESERVED STEP:
  "because you grieved, none could raise it — I have written the
  RAISING to your name"; Moses: "I do not know how to raise it";
  the answer: עֲסֹק בְּיָדֶיךָ ("BUSY YOUR HANDS with it") — "you
  will APPEAR to raise it, and IT WILL RISE OF ITSELF, and I
  write of you that you raised it" — תָּקִים ("you shall raise"),
  הוּקַם ("it WAS raised" — the passive), וַיָּקֶם מֹשֶׁה ("and Moses
  raised") — the three verb-forms read as the division of labor
  between the man's hands and the self-standing house.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:8 — MATERIAL, numeric dispute. THE
  ASSEMBLY DRILLS: R. Chiyya bar Yosef — through the seven
  investiture days Moses dismantled and re-erected it TWICE
  daily (two verb-forms); R. Chanina — THREE times daily (three
  verb-forms: תָּקִים, הוּקַם, וַיָּקֶם) — a verb-census dispute on
  the drill count, both carried.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:9 — MATERIAL. THE BLESSING'S TEXT
  (39:43's blessing filled in): the sages — "the LORD God of
  your fathers add to you..." (Deuteronomy 1:11); R. Meir —
  יְהִי רָצוֹן שֶׁתִּשְׁרֶה שְׁכִינָה בְּמַעֲשֵׂה יְדֵיכֶם ("may the Presence
  REST IN THE WORK OF YOUR HANDS"), and they answered "let the
  pleasantness of the LORD be upon us" (Psalm 90:17) — the
  liturgy's own erection-psalm seated at the verse; AND THE
  REFRAIN EXPLAINED: "as the LORD commanded Moses" written again
  and again because they suspected Moses — "since you criticized
  him, I WRITE MY NAME on every single item I commanded him" —
  the eighteen-fold refrain as the divine COUNTERSIGNATURE on
  each audit line.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:10 — MATERIAL (light). The
  extension: criticize Moses and you criticize ME — the
  countersignature's warranty clause.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 6:1 (⇐ Exod 39:33) — MATERIAL.
  The Buber timeline: R. Chanina — SIX MONTHS occupied with the
  tabernacle: THREE building and THREE folded away unassembled
  (the murmurers: "is it made? did he not say the Presence would
  dwell with us?"); the erection reserved for the month of
  Isaac's good news; "let lying lips be dumb" — and the very
  murmurers ended CARRYING it to Moses.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 7:1 — enrichment. Deathbed
  reward visions (R. Abbahu's thirteen balsam rivers; the
  departing verses of the three elders; Ben Azzai's precious-
  death row).
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 7:2 — enrichment. R. Shimon's
  valley of gold dinars — "whoever takes now takes from his
  world-to-come portion."
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 7:3 — enrichment. R. Shimon ben
  Halafta's Sabbath pearl returned at his wife's word (no reward
  of Torah in this world).
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 7:4 — MATERIAL (light). "You
  will laugh at the latter day" turned on the mockers: "can the
  Presence rest on the handiwork of MOSES?" — when the raising
  fell to him, he laughed last; the radiant-face verse (34:29)
  worn as the clothing of strength.

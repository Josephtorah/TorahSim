## Onkelos Exodus 40:1-38 — read IN FULL, 38 verses

- Onkelos Exod 40:1-8 — MATERIAL, the erection order. Dated to the
  day: בְּיוֹמָא יַרְחָא קַדְמָאָה בְּחַד לְיַרְחָא ("on the first day of the
  first month") — the placement program in dependency order: ark
  in, veil OVER the ark, table in and SET IN ORDER, lampstand in
  and LIT, gold altar BEFORE the ark, entrance screen, offering
  altar before the door, laver between tent and altar WITH WATER,
  the court around, the gate screen — the install script, inside
  outward.
- Onkelos Exod 40:9-11 — MATERIAL. The anointing program: oil on
  the tabernacle and ALL in it — וּתְקַדֵּשׁ יָתֵיהּ ("and SANCTIFY
  it"); the offering altar anointed to קֹדֶשׁ קוּדְשִׁין ("HOLY OF
  HOLIES") rank; the laver too — the consecration pass that turns
  hardware into sancta.
- Onkelos Exod 40:12-15 — MATERIAL, the personnel install. Aaron
  and sons brought to the entrance, immersed; Aaron dressed,
  anointed, sanctified וִישַׁמֵּשׁ קֳדָמָי ("that he may serve BEFORE
  ME" — the audience-token on the office); the sons dressed; and
  40:15's charter line: their anointing לְכַהֲנוּת עָלָם לְדָרֵיהוֹן
  ("an ETERNAL PRIESTHOOD for their GENERATIONS") — the
  perpetual-office grant (the investiture's EX29 seat closing at
  the execution side).
- Onkelos Exod 40:16-17 — MATERIAL, the date crown. Moses did
  according to ALL; and the timestamp: בְּיַרְחָא קַדְמָאָה בְּשַׁתָּא
  תִנְיֵתָא בְּחַד לְיַרְחָא אִתָּקַם מַשְׁכְּנָא ("in the FIRST month of the
  SECOND year, on the first of the month, the tabernacle WAS
  RAISED") — אִתָּקַם, the PASSIVE raised-of-itself form beside
  40:18's active "Moses raised" — the verb pair the spine reads
  as the self-standing house (Pekudei 11:7).
- Onkelos Exod 40:18-33 — MATERIAL. The execution of the install
  script, each stage sealed כְּמָא דִי פַקִּיד יְיָ יָת מֹשֶׁה ("as the
  LORD commanded Moses" — seven times in sixteen verses, the
  countersignature refrain); the Testimony INTO the ark (40:20);
  the veil hung; the bread SET IN ORDER (40:23); the lamps LIT
  (40:25); the incense BURNED (40:27); the burnt-offering and
  meal-offering OFFERED (40:29) — Moses himself running the
  services at first light (the acting-priest week); the laver
  filled לְקִדּוּשׁ ("for SANCTIFICATION" — Onkelos' rite-word where
  Hebrew has washing), and וִיקַדְּשׁוּן ("they would SANCTIFY")
  hands and feet at every entry and approach (40:31-32) — the
  wash converted to a consecration rite in the translation's
  vocabulary; the court raised; וְשֵׁיצֵי מֹשֶׁה יָת עִבִידָא ("and
  Moses COMPLETED the work") — the third completion verb.
- Onkelos Exod 40:34-35 — MATERIAL, the book's crown. THE
  INDWELLING LANDS: וַחֲפָא עֲנָנָא יָת מַשְׁכַּן זִמְנָא ("and the cloud
  COVERED the Tent of Meeting"), וִיקָרָא דַיְיָ אִתְמְלִי יָת מַשְׁכְּנָא
  ("and the GLORY of the LORD FILLED the tabernacle" — the
  glory-word, Onkelos' reverence layer, where Hebrew has the bare
  Name); and Moses — the one man whose hands raised it — לָא
  יָכִיל... לְמֵעַל ("COULD NOT ENTER"), for the cloud dwelt on it
  and the glory filled it: EX25-01's "I will dwell among them"
  discharged at the last chapter, with the builder himself locked
  out by the very fullness he installed (the
  compression-without-diminution doctrine's proof-state: full is
  FULL).
- Onkelos Exod 40:36-38 — MATERIAL, the standing protocol. THE
  CLOUD AS TRAVEL SIGNAL: וּבְאִסְתַּלָּקוּת עֲנָנָא ("when the cloud
  ROSE") they set out; if it did not rise, they did not — עַד
  יוֹמָא דְּאִסְתַּלָּקוּתֵיהּ ("until the day of its rising"); and the
  book's last verse with its double delta: עֲנָנָא יְקָרָא דַיְיָ
  ("the cloud of the GLORY of the LORD") on the tabernacle by
  day, and וְחֵיזוּ אֶשָּׁתָא ("a VISION of fire") in it by night —
  before the eyes of ALL the house of Israel בְּכֹל מַטְּלָנֵיהוֹן
  ("in all their JOURNEYS") — the runtime signal-protocol the
  book of Numbers will drive on, and Exodus closes with the
  machine LIVE.

## Midrash Tanchuma anchored in 40:1-38 — 13 sections

- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:11 (⇐ Exod 40:2 frame) — MATERIAL.
  THE COMMAND'S OWN DATE: "make Me a sanctuary" was said ON THE
  DAY OF ATONEMENT — Moses' three ascents totaling ONE HUNDRED
  AND TWENTY DAYS from the sixth of Sivan to the tenth of Tishri
  (the Ki Tisa calendar, EX34's 120-day seat, quoted back at the
  erection); that same day: "the LORD repented of the evil," "I
  have pardoned according to thy word," "let them make Me a
  sanctuary," and "on this day shall atonement be made" — the
  four sayings stacked on one date; "and they made the tabernacle
  with joy and gladness."
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:12 — MATERIAL, numeric dispute. THE
  COMPLETION-DATE DISPUTE: R. Samuel bar Nachman — the work took
  THREE months (Tishri, Marcheshvan, Kislev) and lay FOLDED
  through Tevet, Shevat, Adar, erected on the first of Nisan; R.
  Chanina — completed on the FIRST OF ADAR (winter work runs at
  half the summer rate — the labor-rate conversion argument).
  Both carried.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:13 — MATERIAL. WHY THE DELAY: the
  Holy One wished to MINGLE JOY WITH JOY — the erection timed to
  the first of Nisan, THE DAY ISAAC WAS BORN; the scoffers
  grumbled at the idle finished kit, not knowing the scheduler's
  intent ("how great are Thy works... Thy thoughts are VERY
  deep").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:14 — MATERIAL, the closing crown.
  The ten crowns of that day (per the Seder Olam chronology);
  NONE could raise it but Moses (the reserved step re-affirmed);
  and the landing: the moment it stood, the Presence descended —
  "Moses could not enter... for the cloud abode on it and the
  glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle" — and the promise
  forward: in this world the Presence among you and BEFORE YOUR
  EYES; in the time to come it NEVER DEPARTS ("I will dwell among
  the children of Israel and will not forsake My people").
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 8:1 (⇐ Exod 40:17-18) —
  MATERIAL, the self-standing crown (Buber recension). Each
  craftsman came carrying his part — "here is my hook, here is my
  board, here is my bolt" — the many skilled who could NOT erect
  it; the Holy Spirit rested on Moses and he erected it — "yet
  say not that MOSES raised it: THE TABERNACLE ROSE OF ITSELF,"
  as it says הוּקַם הַמִּשְׁכָּן ("the tabernacle WAS RAISED" — the
  passive, 40:17); and the extension: even the Temple —
  "unless the LORD builds the house, its builders labor in vain"
  (Psalm 127:1) — the house that only stands when the Owner
  raises it; the erected tabernacle then read as the CROWN
  ("the crown with which his mother crowned him").
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 8:2 — MATERIAL, ink row. THE
  DAUGHTER-SISTER-MOTHER LADDER (R. Yose ben Halafta via his
  son, R. Shimon bar Yochai kissing his head for it): He called
  Israel MY DAUGHTER ("hear, O daughter"), then MY SISTER ("my
  sister, my beloved"), then MY MOTHER — reading לְאוּמִּי ("my
  nation," Isaiah 51:4) as it is written, לְאִמִּי ("MY MOTHER") —
  a written-form claim on the nation-word, opened in full (ink
  claims never credited unopened); "had I come into the world
  only to hear this, it would have been enough."
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 8:3 — MATERIAL (light). "On his
  WEDDING day" = at the Sea; "the day of his heart's joy" = in
  Jerusalem — the wedding-day ladder's first track.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 8:4 — MATERIAL (light). The
  second track: the wedding = SINAI, the joy = Jerusalem; Zion
  the fig — narrow below, wide above ("wider as it wound about
  higher and higher").
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 8:5 — enrichment. R. Levi's
  sifting pun (the beautiful נוֹף that SIFTS the nations).
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 8:6 — enrichment. The waving
  pun (the Levites waved, the sheaf waved).
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 8:7 — enrichment. The pepper
  merchant's joy-of-all-the-earth story; the darkened joy
  (עָרְבָה read from the evening-word) and the return of gladness
  when Jerusalem is rebuilt.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 8:8 — colophon: "The End of
  Parashah Pekudei." Not-bearing; recorded.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Pekudei 8:9 — colophon: וְסָלִיק סֵפֶר
  שְׁמוֹת ("and the End of the BOOK OF EXODUS") — the spine's own
  book-closing line, read at the sitting that closes the book.
  Recorded.

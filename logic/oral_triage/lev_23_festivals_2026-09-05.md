## Sifra, Emor — Section 9 (8 rows)
- Sifra, Emor, Section 9 1 — MATERIAL. THE CALENDAR IS THE COURT'S:
  intercalate the year for the EXILES who set out and have not
  arrived — "children of Israel... the festivals of the LORD": make
  the festivals so that ALL Israel can keep them.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 9 2 — MATERIAL. Deliberated, decided, but
  did not SAY "intercalated" before Nisan arrived — NOT
  intercalated: "which YOU PROCLAIM... My festivals" — if you
  proclaimed them, My festivals; if not, not My festivals. The
  utterance is the act.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 9 3 — MATERIAL. Not needed but intercalated
  — erring, deliberate, or misled — INTERCALATED: "them... them...
  them" (23:4) — even erring, even misled, even deliberate. The
  court's proclamation binds against the astronomy.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 9 4 — MATERIAL. THE LIMITS: intercalated at
  NIGHT, or from one year to the next, or by less or more than a
  MONTH — NOT intercalated: "these are My festivals" — those are
  not. The proclamation's domain bounded.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 9 5 — MATERIAL. Intercalate for NEED
  ("holy convocations" — the assembly's need).
- Sifra, Emor, Section 9 6 — MATERIAL. Not for IMPURITY: "in the
  first month — Passover" — let the first month not pass without
  a Passover (impurity is handled by the impure-Passover rule, not
  by moving the year).
- Sifra, Emor, Section 9 7 — MATERIAL. Why the SABBATH beside the
  festivals: profaning the festivals = profaning the Sabbaths;
  keeping them = keeping both — the two institutions cross-graded.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 9 8 — MATERIAL. Yom Kippur falling on the
  Sabbath, unwitting work — LIABLE FOR EACH separately ("it is the
  Sabbath," "it is the Day of Atonement" — R. Akiva); R. Yosei
  HaGelili: one. A recorded liability-count dispute.

  night — surely) REFUSED by the meta-rule: "any reasoning that
  begins STRICT and ends LENIENT is no reasoning" (he would sit
  without a sukkah for want of the species) — and the verse.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 17 11 — MATERIAL. Ezra (Neh 8:15) as the

## The finds (this ledger's crowns)
- THE CALENDAR IS THE COURT'S UTTERANCE (Section 9 2-4; Chapter 10
  1-4): the proclamation binds even erring or deliberate; a
  decision unspoken before Nisan or before dark does nothing; the
  bounds — not at night, not across years, not by less or more
  than a month, not before its time — "these are My festivals."
- THE ASYMMETRY (Chapter 10 5): the year stretches for need, the
  moon never — "the month": they follow the month. (The Exodus
  calendar block's "stretch-never-fabricate" recorded from the
  Sifra's side.)
- THE SABBATH YIELDS TO TESTIMONY, NOT TO NOTIFICATION (Chapter 10
  6-7).
- THE OPTIONAL SEVEN AND THE OBLIGATORY NIGHT (Chapter 11 3).
- THE FALLBACK LADDER (Chapter 11 5): bulls → rams → lambs; purity
  → impurity.
- THE OMER MACHINE (Section 10; Chapter 12): first of all reaping,
  the five grains, the two-tenths anomaly with the un-doubled
  libations, the new grain's clock (immediately / midday for the
  distant / the whole day after the destruction), the three
  land-linked laws with three source labels.
- THE MORROW OF THE SABBATH = THE FESTIVAL by FOUR ROUTES (Chapter
  12 1-5) — and ONKELOS PERFORMS IT TWICE (23:11, 23:15).
- THE THREE CLOCKS (Chapter 12 6): reap and count by night, bring
  by day; count 49, sanctify the 50th like the Jubilee.
- THE TWO SETS (Chapter 13 6): Numbers' lambs for the day, Emor's
  for the bread; the WAVING GEOMETRY four ways (Chapter 13 8); the
  BREAD-WITHOUT-LAMBS dispute settled by the wilderness proof
  with R. Shimon replacing the reason (Chapter 13 9-10).
- WHY PEAH SITS AMONG THE FESTIVALS (Chapter 13 11).
- THE FOUR-TOKEN LITURGY (Section 11 1) — R. Akiva's parse of
  shabbaton/zikaron/teruah/mikra kodesh into rest, zikhronot,
  shofarot, and the day; the blessing-order dispute with the Usha
  practice history (Section 11 3-5); the shofar, the plain blasts,
  and three-of-three imported from the Jubilee (Section 11 6-9).
- ONKELOS DEFINES THE TERUAH'S SOUND (23:24): the broken cry.
- THE DAY ATONES ONLY THE PENITENT (Chapter 14 1-2); KARET =
  DESTRUCTION (Chapter 14 4); THE ADDITION from profane to holy on
  every rest, with no karet and no warning on the addition
  (Chapter 14 5-8); THE UNWRITTEN WARNING FOR THE FAST derived
  from a redundant punishment (Chapter 14 9).
- THE INTERMEDIATE DAYS' work law by the demonstrative (Section 12
  5-8); THE ORDER RULE with invalidity (Section 12 9).
- THE DO-NOT-DELAY TIMER (Chapter 15 3-4): expiry after the three
  festivals in order.
- THE FOUR SPECIES: identifications, counts, the whole-taking
  rule, ownership (the ship), and ONKELOS NAMING THEM (23:40).
- THE SUKKAH: dwell as you live; nights-as-days by the Lev 8
  confinement analogy (Chapter 17 5-8); women excluded, minors
  included; ANY material by the strict-to-lenient meta-rule
  (Chapter 17 10); real booths vs clouds — ONKELOS RULES CLOUDS
  (23:43).
- MOSES TAUGHT EACH FESTIVAL'S LAWS IN ITS SEASON, and fixed the
  season for all public offerings to override the Sabbath (Chapter
  17 12-13) — with Onkelos's "order... and taught" (23:44).

- Onkelos Num 3:3 — MATERIAL. THE FILLED HAND AS AN OFFERING. "These are the names of the sons of Aaron, הַכֹּהֲנִים הַמְּשֻׁחִים ("the anointed priests"), אֲשֶׁר מִלֵּא יָדָם לְכַהֵֽן ("whose hand he filled to serve as priest")" → כָּהֲנַיָּא דְּאִתְרַבִּיאוּ ("the priests who were raised/anointed") and דִּי אִתְקָרַב קֻרְבַּנְהוֹן לְשַׁמָּשָׁא ("whose OFFERING was brought near, to serve") — the investiture idiom ("filled the hand", this exact form's one Torah seat computed) rendered by its RITE: the ram of filling of Lev 8 — the translation names the ceremony where the ink names the hand (a REFERENCE to the investiture by Onkelos' own word).

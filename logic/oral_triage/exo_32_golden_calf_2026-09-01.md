## Onkelos Exodus 32:1-35 — read IN FULL, 35 verses

- Onkelos Exod 32:1-6 — MATERIAL. The event: עֲבֵד לָנָא דַחֲלָן
  ("make us FEARED-ONES" — the translation's idol-word, plural);
  the calf formed; אִלֵּין דַּחֲלָתָךְ יִשְׂרָאֵל ("THESE are your
  gods, Israel" — the plural kept); Aaron's redirect: חַגָּא קֳדָם
  יְיָ מְחָר ("a festival BEFORE THE LORD tomorrow" — the
  delay-strategy in the received translation's own audience-token);
  the rising to לְחַיָּכָא ("amuse themselves" — the merriment-word
  the chain reads as the graver crimes).
- Onkelos Exod 32:9-14 — MATERIAL, the block's crown. THE PRAYER
  DISCOVERED INSIDE THE DECREE: הַנַּח בָּעוּתָךְ מִן קֳדָמַי
  ("DESIST FROM YOUR PRAYER before Me") — the translation renders
  'let Me alone' as a prayer already underway: the door-opening
  reading (the decree announces its own restraint is possible).
  Then וְצַלִּי מֹשֶׁה ("and Moses PRAYED"); the patriarchs' oath
  sworn בְּמֵימְרָךְ ("by YOUR WORD"); and וְתַב יְיָ ("and the LORD
  TURNED BACK") from the evil — the first recorded successful
  intercession against a standing decree: prayer as a decree-input.
- Onkelos Exod 32:15-19 — MATERIAL. The tablets written on BOTH
  SIDES; כְּתָבָא כְּתָבָא דַיְיָ הוּא מְפָרַשׁ ("the writing was
  the WRITING OF GOD, DISTINCT/ENGRAVED") — the mefarash-family
  again (the clear-script token of 28:11 and 28:36, now on the
  tablets themselves); the breaking at the mountain's base.
- Onkelos Exod 32:20 — MATERIAL, the machine's crown. THE DRINK
  TEST: burned, ground fine, scattered on the water — וְאַשְׁקֵי
  יָת בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל ("and he MADE THE SONS OF ISRAEL DRINK") —
  the suspected-wife's procedure run at national scale. The spine
  completes it (Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 26): THE THREE-COURT
  TRIAGE — witnesses AND warning → the sword (the Levites'
  gate-to-gate); warning WITHOUT witnesses → THE WATER TEST, as
  the sotah is tested; NEITHER → the plague (Heaven's docket). One
  crime, three evidence-states, three procedures — a complete
  evidentiary decision-tree in the tradition's own words.
- Onkelos Exod 32:25 — MATERIAL (light). The hard verse rendered:
  Aaron בָּטֵילִנּוּן ("had NULLIFIED/exposed them") — לַאֲסָבוּתְהוֹן
  שׁוּם בִּישׁ לְדָרֵיהוֹן ("to make them take a BAD NAME for their
  generations") — the name-consequence reading.
- Onkelos Exod 32:26-29 — MATERIAL. The Levites' loyalty: מָן
  דַּחֲלַיָּא דַיְיָ ("whoever FEARS the LORD, to me"); the
  gate-to-gate execution, three thousand; and the crown-token at
  32:29: קָרִיבוּ יֶדְכוֹן קֻרְבָּנָא ("YOUR HANDS HAVE OFFERED AN
  OFFERING") — the fill-the-hand installation idiom AGAIN (EX28-01's
  decoding): the Levites' ordination effected BY the crisis-loyalty
  itself — office earned at the breach.
- Onkelos Exod 32:32-35 — MATERIAL. THE BOOK: מְחֵנִי כְעַן
  מִסִּפְרָךְ ("blot me now from YOUR BOOK") — and the answer: מָן
  דִּי חַב קֳדָמַי אֶמְחִנֵּיהּ מִסִפְרִי ("whoever sinned before
  Me, HIM I will blot from My book") — the registry surfaces, and
  substitution is refused: each entry answers for itself. Then the
  DEFERRED ACCOUNTING: וּבְיוֹם אַסְעָרוּתִי וְאַסְעַר ("on the day
  of My accounting I will account") — the open-ledger doctrine
  (the spine: I will sit in judgment on the DAY OF ATONEMENT;
  R. Chanina's canon — whoever says the Merciful is LAX, may his
  bowels be loosed: He is long-suffering and COLLECTS). The plague
  closes the chapter.
- Onkelos Exod 32:7-8, 32:21-24, 32:30-31 — context (the go-down
  exchange; Aaron's account — the row the docket's Mishnah Megillah
  4:10 rules is READ BUT NOT TRANSLATED in public, recorded at
  exo_34's ledger with the translation-law; the ascent for
  atonement).

## Midrash Tanchuma — the sections attributed to this block, read IN
## FULL (Ki Tisa 19-26 + Buber 13)

- Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 19 (with Buber 13) — MATERIAL. The
  event's dossier: בּשֵׁשׁ read בָּא שֵׁשׁ ("the SIXTH HOUR came") —
  the deadline miscount; Satan's bier-illusion (Moses shown dead
  between heaven and earth); HUR KILLED for rebuking them (the
  Tetzaveh spine's background now read at its home parashah —
  Aaron's fear made explicit: "he UNDERSTOOD from the slaughtered
  one"); Aaron's delay-tactics (demanding the WIVES' rings — and
  the women REFUSED: "God forbid we renounce the One who did all
  these wonders" — the verse's own ink: the rings came from THEIR
  ears, not their wives'; the women's fidelity recorded in the
  grammar); the magicians (Jannes and Jambres) at the furnace; the
  calf leaping out.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 20 + 22 (with Buber 13) — MATERIAL. THE
  FIVE DESTROYERS: Wrath, Anger, Fury, Destroyer, Annihilation —
  Moses' invocation of the three patriarchs dismisses THREE; "You
  stand against one and I against one" — the breach-stander's
  division of labor (Psalm 106:23's stood-in-the-breach); Moses
  grasping the Throne, sheltered by the cloud (Job 26:9). And the
  ox-defense: "are You jealous of an OX? It eats grass and is
  slaughtered" — the powerlessness argument at the appeal.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 21 — MATERIAL (light). YOUR-PEOPLE
  scoped: עַמְּךָ ("THY people") = the mixed multitude Moses
  accepted on his own word ("better to receive the penitent") —
  "these are THY gods" not OUR gods: the proselytes' calf, the
  intake-decision owned by its maker; the 125-talent gematria of
  masekhah; the four-faced chariot seen at Sinai and the ox-face
  unhitched — the image's source named.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 26 — MATERIAL (the triage section — the
  three-court taxonomy recorded above at 32:20's row). Riders: the
  letters FLEW from the tablets and they grew heavy (the writing
  carried the weight); the excommunication-reading of GO-DOWN
  (rebuked from the heavenly court — with Judah's precedent); the
  restoration by the same word (Go-down / Go-UP, 33:1); and the
  Day-of-Atonement docket at 32:34 (recorded above).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 23 + 24 + 25 — MATERIAL (light). The
  prayer-times yelamdenu (morning prayer until midday vs R.
  Yehudah's four hours — THE PRAYERS CORRESPOND TO THE DAILY
  OFFERINGS: liturgy wired to the tamid clock, the patriarchs'
  three prayers, Daniel's three times, David's evening-morning-
  noon); the remember-the-fathers argument-set (the ten pearls
  against Abraham's ten trials; burning/slaughter/exile answered by
  Abraham's furnace, Isaac's neck, Jacob's exile; the oath sworn BY
  YOURSELF — mountains pass, You do not; the mercy-to-thousands
  arithmetic — only seven generations from Abraham to Moses); the
  eruv-messenger yelamdenu (the fool-messenger row — deaf-mute,
  fool, minor invalid as eruv-carriers) recorded as the section's
  opening.

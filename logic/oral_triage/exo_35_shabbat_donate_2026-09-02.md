## Onkelos Exodus 35:1-29 — read IN FULL, 29 verses

- Onkelos Exod 35:1-3 — MATERIAL, the block's law head. THE ASSEMBLY
  ITSELF: וְאַכְנֵשׁ מֹשֶׁה יָת כָּל כְּנִשְׁתָּא ("and Moses ASSEMBLED the
  whole congregation") — the whole-people convocation BEFORE the
  building orders; Shabbat repeated FIRST, before any tabernacle
  work: שַׁבָּא שַׁבְּתָא קֳדָם יְיָ ("a Shabbat of Shabbat BEFORE the
  LORD" — the audience-token on the day itself), כָּל דְּיַעְבֵּד בֵּיהּ
  עִבִדְתָּא יִתְקְטֵל ("whoever does work on it SHALL BE PUT TO
  DEATH"); and the ONE named labor: לָא תְבַעֲרוּן אֶשָּׁתָא בְּכֹל
  מוֹתְבָנֵיכוֹן ("you shall not KINDLE A FIRE in all your dwellings on
  the Shabbat day") — the precedence rule (rest outranks the
  building) carried in the ink's own order, and the singled-out
  fire-labor the case shelf will meet at Mishnah Sanhedrin 4:1
  (court executions do not burn on Shabbat — the exam docket's row).
- Onkelos Exod 35:4-19 — MATERIAL. The donation call re-issued in
  execution voice: אַפְרָשׁוּתָא קֳדָם יְיָ ("a set-apart offering
  BEFORE the LORD"), כֹּל דְּיִתְרְעֵי לִבֵּיהּ ("everyone whose heart is
  WILLING") — the voluntariness condition on every clause; the full
  materials census (the Terumah list re-enacted verbatim); and at
  35:19 the garments rendered לְבוּשֵׁי שִׁמּוּשָׁא ("garments of
  SERVICE") — the translation's functional term where the Hebrew has
  the plaited/knit word.
- Onkelos Exod 35:20-24 — MATERIAL. The response: 35:22's hard
  preposition solved by the translation — Hebrew "the men came UPON
  the women" rendered וּמַיְתַן גֻּבְרַיָּא עַל נְשַׁיָּא ("and the men
  brought IN ADDITION TO the women") — the ADDITIVE reading (both
  sexes gave; the men's gifts rode on top of the women's), the
  received translation's own solution to the odd עַל; the jewelry
  census (bracelets, nose-rings, finger-rings, buckles); every
  wave-offering of gold קֳדָם יְיָ ("before the LORD").
- Onkelos Exod 35:25-26 — MATERIAL. THE WOMEN'S OWN CRAFT: וְכָל
  אִתְּתָא חַכִּימַת לִבָּא בִּידָהָא מְעַזְלָא ("every WISE-HEARTED woman
  spun WITH HER HANDS") — the wisdom vocabulary applied to women's
  labor; and 35:26 the goats-hair spinners דְּאִתְרְעֵי לִבְּהֶן...
  בְּחָכְמָא ("whose hearts moved them WITH WISDOM") — the spine will
  read the spinning-from-the-backs-of-the-goats claim here (outside
  declared scope; the wisdom-token is on the ink).
- Onkelos Exod 35:27-29 — MATERIAL. The leaders bring the stones
  (the row the spine indicts — see the Tanchuma rows below); the
  closing totalization: every man and woman whose heart was willing
  — נְדַבְתָּא קֳדָם יְיָ ("a FREEWILL GIFT before the LORD"), the
  block's own category word.

## Midrash Tanchuma anchored in 35:1-29 — 3 sections

- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:2 (⇐ Exod 35:22, 35:25) — MATERIAL.
  The eagerness reading: "they came both men and women" read as the
  CROWDING — men and women pressing in one throng to give; the
  donation as joy, not levy.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:3 (⇐ Exod 36:6-7 via 35:27's frame)
  — MATERIAL. TWO MORNINGS sufficed for the whole tabernacle (the
  doubled בַּבֹּקֶר בַּבֹּקֶר "morning, morning" counted as two); after
  two days the willing could no longer give — the halt order stood;
  and the LEADERS' DISTRESS: having missed the general collection
  they asked to give the priestly garments and stones instead.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 11:4 (⇐ Exod 35:27) — MATERIAL, the
  block's ink crown. THE MISSING LETTER: because the leaders were
  slow, וְהַנְּשִׂאִם ("and the LEADERS") is written DEFECTIVE at 35:27
  — one letter yod short of the full spelling — the lateness fined
  one letter in the word's own skin; they repaired it afterward by
  underwriting the stones and joining the work. An ink-level claim
  (spelling census), opened here in full per the credit guards.

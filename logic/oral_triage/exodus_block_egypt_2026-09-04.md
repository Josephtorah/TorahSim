### Pesachim 95a — the second Passover's statute engine
- 95a:1 — THE MISHNAH (Pesachim 9:3): what divides first from
  second Passover — the first bound by "it shall not be SEEN...
  not be FOUND" (the leaven bans), the second has leaven and matza
  with him in the house; the first requires hallel at the EATING,
  the second not; BOTH require hallel at the MAKING; both eaten
  roast on matza and bitter herbs; both override the Sabbath —
  MATERIAL frame: the exam's own diff table.
- 95a:2 — "according to the entire STATUTE of the Passover shall
  they do it" (Num 9:12): the text speaks of commands IN ITS BODY
  (מִצְוָה שֶׁבְּגוּפוֹ, "a command in its body") — MATERIAL: the
  engine's criterion.
- 95a:3 — the structure: ON-body commands from "on matza and
  bitter herbs they shall eat it"; the closing particular "and a
  bone they shall not break IN IT" restricts the general to
  in-body likeness — MATERIAL: general-and-particulars, the
  engine's form.
- 95a:4 — Isi ben Yehuda's variant: "they shall do it" itself
  reads as the in-body commands — MATERIAL, dual-track frame.
- 95a:5-6 — the challenge (would the general ADD everything?) and
  the resolution (the stated particulars keep the include rule at
  likeness, not everything) — MATERIAL rider, terse.
- 95a:7 — Isi's spending of the bone-clause: one bone WITH marrow
  and one WITHOUT — MATERIAL (ANTICIPATED: EX12-27's marrow row,
  F-059, holds both arms).
- 95a:8 — the Rabbis' "they shall do IT": the second Passover is
  not slaughtered for an INDIVIDUAL — wherever company can be
  sought, it is sought — MATERIAL: the seek-company rule.
- 95a:9 — the derivation run opens: is the second bound by
  seen/found like the first? "on matza and herbs they shall eat
  it" — MATERIAL.
- 95a:10 — positive commands only? the negated-particular "they
  shall not leave of it until morning" brings the negative class —
  MATERIAL rider.
- 95a:11 — THE TYPE SYSTEM: the three particulars are a POSITIVE
  (matza-herbs), a NEGATIVE-REPAIRED-BY-POSITIVE (not-leave — the
  morning burn repairs it), and a FULL NEGATIVE (the bone) — the
  general includes each type by its own paradigm — MATERIAL: the
  engine's typed includes.
- 95a:12 — the matza-general INCLUDES roast-with-fire (in-body),
  EXCLUDES leaven-removal (הַשְׁבָּתַת שְׂאוֹר, not on its body);
  "reverse it!" refused: מִצְוָה דְגוּפֵיהּ עֲדִיף — the in-body
  command is preferred — MATERIAL: the include and THE TIE-BREAK.
- 95a:13 — the not-leave general INCLUDES not-carry-out (לֹא
  תוֹצִיא, 12:46) — the pair resembling: this one invalidated as
  leftover, that one as taken-out — MATERIAL.
- 95a:14 — and EXCLUDES seen/found: the resemblance metric runs on
  the LASH CLASS — not-leave is a negative-repaired-by-positive
  (no lashes) and seen/found likewise (no lashes) — yet the
  in-body preference decides — MATERIAL: the metric named.
- 95a:15 — the bone general's include opener (runs into 95b:1's
  credited row: raw in, slaughter-over-leaven out) — context.

### Pesachim 95b — hallel, the override table, the carve-outs
- 95b:3 — hallel at the MAKING, both eras: two recorded routes —
  the verse excludes only the night; and "is it POSSIBLE that
  Israel slaughter their Passovers or take their palm-branches
  without hallel?" — MATERIAL: a liturgical a-priori on the
  record.
- 95b:4 — the mishnah's override row: Sabbath YES, impurity NO —
  and the gemara flags it as NOT R. Yehuda's — MATERIAL frame.
- 95b:5 — the mechanics of the dispute: the first authority —
  "impurity PUSHED IT OFF; shall it return and be done in
  impurity?"; R. Yehuda — "the Torah RETURNED for it, to be done
  in purity; not achieved — it is done in impurity" — MATERIAL:
  both reasons recorded.
- 95b:6 — the baraita's three pairs: first and second alike
  override the Sabbath, override impurity, require LODGING —
  MATERIAL (R. Yehuda's line).
- 95b:7 — "overrides impurity — per whom? R. Yehuda" — context.
- 95b:8 — R. Yehuda's OWN lodging exemption for the second: "you
  shall turn in the morning and go to your tents" + "six days you
  shall eat matza" — what is eaten for six requires lodging; the
  second (one day) does not — MATERIAL.
- 95b:9 — resolution: TWO tannaim within R. Yehuda — MATERIAL
  rider: the dispute's honest shape.
- 95b:10 — THE MISHNAH: a Passover that comes in impurity —
  flux-bearers (men and women), menstruants, and women after
  childbirth may NOT eat of it; if they ate, they are EXEMPT from
  karet (excision) — MATERIAL: the communal-impurity carve-out.
- 95b:11 — R. Eliezer adds: exempt even on Temple ENTRY —
  MATERIAL.
- 95b:12 — the karet derivation: "every PURE one may eat flesh;
  the soul that eats flesh... with his impurity on him — cut off"
  (Lev 7:19-20) — CROSS-BOOK: the Tzav round's own flesh-purity
  verses run the exemption — MATERIAL.
- 95b:13 — the criterion: eaten-BY-THE-PURE → the impure eater is
  liable; NOT eaten-by-the-pure (brought in impurity) → the
  impure eater exempt — MATERIAL: the rule's edge.
- 95b:14 — R. Eliezer's route: "they shall expel from the camp
  every leper, every flux-bearer, every corpse-impure" (Num 5:2)
  — WHEN the corpse-impure are expelled, flux-bearers are
  expelled; when not (a Passover in impurity), not — MATERIAL.
- 95b:15 — Rav Yosef's dilemma: flux-bearers PRESSED INTO the
  sanctuary hall at an impure Passover — permitted-courtyard
  extending to the hall, or only what was permitted permitted? —
  MATERIAL: the recorded question.
- 95b:16 — Rava's answer: "they shall expel from the CAMP — even
  PART of the camp" (the hall stays barred) — MATERIAL.
- 95b:17 — the second version: wherever "outside the camp you
  shall expel them" is read, "they shall expel" is read —
  MATERIAL rider, dual version.
- 95b:18 — Rav Yosef's second dilemma: corpse-impure ATE THE
  SACRIFICIAL PORTIONS of an impure Passover — context (answered
  96a:2).

### Pesachim 96a:1-5 — the portions and Egypt's altars
- 96a:1 — the dilemma's two sides — context.

### Pesachim 96a:13-22 + 96b:2 — the export run, clause by clause
- 96a:13 — "they shall eat the flesh on THIS night": Egypt-only?
  NO — "you shall perform the SERVICE" (13:5) EXPORTS it —
  MATERIAL: the export operator's first firing in the run.
- 96a:14 — then why "THIS"? for R. Elazar ben Azaryah and R.

- 96a:15 — "no uncircumcised may eat OF IT": exported by "you
  shall perform" — MATERIAL.
- 96a:16 — then why "OF IT"? OF IT he does not eat — but he EATS
  MATZA AND BITTER HERBS — MATERIAL (ANTICIPATED: EX12-29 already
  seats the lamb-barred-owe-the-bread corollary).
- 96a:17 — "no foreigner may eat of it": exported — MATERIAL.
- 96a:18 — the token's job: OF IT apostasy disqualifies — and
  apostasy does NOT disqualify from terumah (the priest's due) —
  MATERIAL: the pesach/terumah boundary.
- 96a:19 — why BOTH the uncircumcised and the foreigner clauses:
  one is repulsive (the body), one's heart is not toward Heaven —
  neither could be derived from the other — MATERIAL: the
  non-redundancy argument.
- 96a:20 — "sojourner and hireling": the same run — exported; its
  OF-IT spends on the apostasy/terumah boundary again — MATERIAL,
  terse.
- 96a:21 — "you shall circumcise him — THEN he eats of it": the
  export stands; OF IT — the circumcision of his males and slaves
  bars HIM from the pesach and does NOT bar him from terumah —
  MATERIAL: the gate's scope.
- 96a:22 — "a bone they shall not break IN IT": exported; IN IT —
  in a VALID one, not in a disqualified one — MATERIAL
  (ANTICIPATED: EX12-27's valid-only route, F-059).
- 96a:23 — "eat not OF IT raw": exported; OF IT reassigned to
  Rabbah's teaching in R. Yitzchak's name — MATERIAL (the
  credited LAW row, here opened).
- 96b:2 — the resolution of the seven days: Egypt's leaven ban ran
  ONE NIGHT AND ITS DAY (and the night rule holds for the
  generations too); the generations' ban runs ALL SEVEN —
  MATERIAL: the two-era leaven row sharpened to its final form.

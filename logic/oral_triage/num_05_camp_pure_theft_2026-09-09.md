## Sifrei Bamidbar piskaot 1-6 (17 rows, read whole — the heads by position: 1 on 5:1; 2 on 5:5; 3 on 5:7; 4 on 5:8; 5 on 5:9; 6 on 5:10)
- Sifrei Bamidbar 1:1 — MATERIAL. THE WARNING FOR THE PUNISHMENT. Why is the section stated? Num 19:20 gives the PUNISHMENT for defiling the sanctuary (excision, karet — "cut off") but no WARNING; "command the children of Israel that they send out of the camp... and they shall not defile their camps" (5:2-3) IS the warning that the unclean not enter the sanctuary. The operator: 5:3's clause is the negative command paired to 19:20's penalty — the punishment/warning pairing the courts require (the exam's Sanhedrin 54a; Mishnah Makkot 3).
- Sifrei Bamidbar 1:2 — MATERIAL. FOUR READINGS OF "COMMAND". The imperative is for immediate performance (from the run, 5:4 "and the children of Israel did so") and for the generations (from Lev 24:2-3's "eternal statute" on the same verb); R. Yishmael: all the Torah's commands take both from the one qualified — a paradigm (I3); R. Yehudah b. Beteira: "command" everywhere means impulsion (Deut 3:28); R. Shimon b. Yochai: "command" everywhere entails expense (Lev 24:2, Num 35:2, 28:2 — one exception, 34:2); Rebbi: "command" everywhere is exhortation (Gen 2:16-17). A DISPUTE on one lemma, four settings RECORDED, none chosen.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 1:3 — MATERIAL. THE THREE CAMPS AND THE LIMIT OF THE A-FORTIORI. "Out of the camp" — the Levite camp; "outside the camp" adds the Israelite; "their camps in whose midst I dwell" — the Shekhinah's (the Presence's); the a-fortiori (I1) would have reached the Shekhinah's camp from the lighter Israelite camp — so why write it? WE DO NOT PUNISH BY AN A-FORTIORI (the middot's own case law — MIDDOT.md). R. Yehudah: the a-fortiori does hold and the clause assigns a SEPARATE CAMP to each kind (the leper alone by Lev 13:46 "solitary shall he sit"); Rebbi: the leper departed from the general to teach on the general (I8) — the graver the uncleanness, the farther the sending. Three teachers, one verse — RECORDED.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 1:4 — MATERIAL. THE LADDER OF STRINGENCY. The sages' gradations: wherever the man with a discharge (zav) defiles, the leper defiles, and more (the house-entrant, Lev 14:46) — sent from three camps; wherever the corpse-unclean defiles, the zav defiles, and more (the stone over a cavity, Lev 15:9) — sent from two; wherever one immersed that day (tevul yom) defiles, the corpse-unclean defiles, and more (a man by touch, 19:22) — sent from one; wherever one lacking atonement disqualifies, the tevul yom disqualifies, and more (the heave-offering). A VERDICT TABLE of four ranks from the three camp-words (the exam's Mishnah Kelim 1:5-9).
- Sifrei Bamidbar 1:5 — MATERIAL. WHO IS SENT — THE SCOPE DISPUTED. "From male to female" (5:3): all, not the ark's bearers only; R. Yoshiyah: adults and minors (the punishment criterion refused); R. Yochanan: every kind of uncleanness that affects male or female; the one of unknown sex and the hermaphrodite from "outside the camp you shall send them"; the one who cannot walk (carried); UTENSILS from "their camps"; R. Akiva: men and utensils from "send them"; R. Yishmael by induction (a paradigm, I3, from plague-uncleanness — refuted by the couch-and-seat distinction, restored by the leprous house's stones); R. Yossi HaGelili: what can become a source of uncleanness (the earthenware vessel excluded). The scope's settings RECORDED — the scenes' who-field.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 1:6 — CONTEXT. A tangent on Lev 17:15-16 (the carcass-eater who does not bathe): R. Yitzchak — excision for the unbathed body, not the unwashed clothes, by an a-fortiori (I1) from the graver corpse-uncleanness; "if he does not wash his clothes" an exhortation. Not this span's verse; the form (the exhortation without a penalty) is row 1's.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 1:7 — MATERIAL. THREE CAMPS FROM THREE CAMP-WORDS; THE SHEKHINAH AMONG THE UNCLEAN. "Outside the camp you shall send them" — not a place inside; from the threefold "camp" (computed on the ink: 5:2 "the camp", 5:3 "outside the camp", "their camps") THEY STATED: three camps — Israel's from Jerusalem's entrance to the Temple mount, the Levites' to the courtyard, the Shekhinah's inward (the exam's Zevachim 116b, routed at Bamidbar); "in whose midst I dwell": beloved are Israel — even unclean, the Shekhinah is among them (Lev 16:16; Num 35:34 — the clause's second Torah seat, computed). Onkelos' "My Shekhinah dwells among them" (5:3) is this row's noun.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 1:8 — MATERIAL. THE RUN'S DOUBLING READ. 5:4 "and the children of Israel did so... as the LORD spoke to Moses, so did the children of Israel": R. Yossi HaGelili — before the calf there were no zavim or lepers among them; after, there were (the three kinds on the one day); R. Shimon b. Yochai — the fear that came after the calf (Exod 24:17 against 34:30); "did so" — Israel's praise; the second clause — THE UNCLEAN THEMSELVES DID NOT PROTEST (the doubled formula computed on the ink: "so" twice, "the children of Israel" twice). The reading of the doubling is the claim; the calf's chronology is the tradition's, labeled.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 2:1 — MATERIAL. THE RULE OF REPETITION; ME'ILAH IS LYING; WHO IS GUILTY. Why the section, already at Lev 5:20-26? For THE PROSELYTE'S STOLEN PROPERTY: sworn against falsely, the proselyte dead without heirs — the principal and the fifth to the priests, the guilt offering to the altar. "THIS IS A RULE IN THE TORAH: any section stated in one place, missing one thing, and repeated elsewhere is repeated only for the thing originated" (the middot's case law — MIDDOT.md); R. Akiva: everything in it is expounded; R. Yoshiyah: "a man or a woman" likens a woman to a man for all damages (from the pit, Exod 21:33); R. Yonathan: not needed (Exod 21:34, 22:5 pay regardless) — it teaches its own law for women; "any of the sins of man" — the lie about ANY thing is a lie against the LORD (not Lev 5:21-22's list alone); "ME'ILAH (trespass) IN ALL PLACES IS LYING" — five proof-seats (1 Chr 5:25, Josh 7:1, 1 Chr 10:13, 2 Chr 26:18, Num 5:12) — the lemma Onkelos translates by (5:6, 5:12, 5:27); "that soul shall be guilty": the one of unknown sex, proselytes, servants — minors excluded by an a-fortiori (I1) from idolatry; the heirs exempt from the guilt offering, not from the principal (5:7 "and he shall give it"); THE SABBATH-BURNER PAYS NOTHING — the death penalty absorbs the payment; confession for the guilt offering as for the sin offering; R. Nathan: a paradigm (I3) — all who are executed confess (the exam's Mishnah Sanhedrin 6:2).
- Sifrei Bamidbar 3:1 — MATERIAL. HIS OWN SIN; THE THING ITSELF; THE FIFTH'S BASE DISPUTED; ANY HAND. "Their sin which they did" — not the father's (the deposit denied to the depositor's son); "restore his guilt at its head": the stolen thing itself may be returned, not money only; "its fifth": R. Yoshiyah — a fifth of the WHOLE (so that it and its fifth make five equal parts — a quarter of the principal); R. Yonathan — a fifth of the principal — A DISPUTE on the fifth's base (the exam's Bava Metzia 54a; Mishnah Bava Kamma 9:7); "to whom he is liable": the court's messenger or the heir too; R. Nathan: the court takes the theft from the robber for the robbed man's creditor — "in any manner".
- Sifrei Bamidbar 4:1 — MATERIAL. NO REDEEMER IS THE DEAD PROSELYTE. R. Yishmael: is there a man in Israel without a kinsman? The verse teaches the proselyte who died — the principal and the fifth to the priests, the offering to the altar; R. Nathan: "the man" — a search is made for a man's heirs, none for a minor's (a minor has no sons); Abba Chanan in R. Eliezer's name: the robbed one, not the robber ("to whom to return"); "the guilt restored" = MONEY, the offering being "the ram of atonement"; "the LORD's, to the priest": to the OFFICIATING WATCH — not any priest he chooses ("whereby atonement is made for him" — the men who atone).
- Sifrei Bamidbar 4:2 — MATERIAL. THE PRIEST-THIEF DOES NOT KEEP IT. A priest who robbed the proselyte — does he acquire what is already in his hand? The a-fortiori (I1: if he acquires others' theft, how much more his own) is REFUTED by the distinction — what he acquires only on coming to hand (tithe) others cannot take; the proselyte's theft, in which others have a portion, is taken from him and divided among the watch (R. Nathan's phrasing): the middot's case law again — an a-fortiori answered by a difference.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 4:3 — MATERIAL. THE PROSELYTE DIES FIRST. The robber on his way with the money and the offering, the proselyte dies — the heirs exempt from the offering ("whereby atonement shall be made for him" — his death atoned): so R. Akiva taught BEFORE Zifron; AFTER Zifron: money given to the watch, then the proselyte dies — the heirs do not retrieve it ("whatever a man gives to the priest, to him shall it be", 5:10); the priest may not demand the offering from the heir. A teacher's two settings, dated by his journey — RECORDED.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 4:4 — MATERIAL. THE MONEY BEFORE THE RAM; TWO WATCHES. Money to Jehoiarib's watch and the offering to Jedaiah's — or the reverse: "aside from the ram of atonement whereby atonement shall be made" — no atonement until the money is returned; the living animal is offered by the second watch and another given to the first (the exam's Bava Kamma 110a-111a; Mishnah Bava Kamma 9:12).
- Sifrei Bamidbar 5:1 — MATERIAL. EVERY HEAVE-OFFERING; THE FIRST FRUITS' RECIPIENT. R. Yishmael: a pile dedicated before leveling and redeemed still owes the heave-offering (terumah) — not if redeemed after ("the first of your grain", Deut 18:4); R. Akiva: "all terumah" — a whole granary may be terumah if some is left (Mishnah Terumot 4:5); "of all the holy things": terumah on ALL produce — Issi b. Yehudah's a-fortiori (I1) from the tithe, twice; Issi b. Menachem's; "which they bring near to the priest, to him shall it be": is terumah BROUGHT? No — Exod 23:19 says the FIRST FRUITS (bikkurim) are brought to the house of the LORD but not to whom: this verse gives them to the priests. The row assigns Exodus' unassigned bringing its recipient.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 6:1 — MATERIAL. A MAN'S HOLY THINGS ARE HIS — WHAT STAYS WITH THE OWNER; THE FOURTH-YEAR FRUIT. All holy things were pulled out to the priests, leaving the owners portions of the thank and peace offerings, the paschal lamb, the animal tithe, the second tithe, and the fourth-year planting (neta revai); the officiating priest's skin; NETA REVAI to the owners — R. Meir from this verse; R. Shimon by the three-facet analogy with the second tithe (called holy, brought to the place, redeemed) — refuted by terumah (holy, not brought), refuted by first fruits (brought, not redeemed), and restored: "I learn a thing of three facets from a thing similar in three, not from one similar in one or two" (the middot's case law on the paradigm's strength); R. Yossi from Lev 19:25 "to increase for YOU". Onkelos' inserted "THE TITHE of his holy things" (5:10) names one item of this row's list. The exam: Mishnah Maaser Sheni 5:1-5.
- Sifrei Bamidbar 6:2 — MATERIAL. NO SEIZING; THE OWNER'S CHOICE; THE FIRSTBORN'S THIRTY DAYS. From 18:19 a priest might seize the gifts — "a man's holy things are his": he gives to whom he wishes; measured out on the ground for some priests and others joined — the later ones share ("his holy things are his"); in a basket — the first keep it ("whatever a man gives to the priest"); R. Yossi: A SON REDEEMED WITHIN THIRTY DAYS WHO DIES — the money returns ("his holy things are his"); AFTER thirty days — it stays with the priest ("whatever a man gives to the priest, to him shall it be") — the firstborn engine's thirty-day row at a Naso seat (Bekhorot 49a, routed at Bamidbar; the compiled cold_run_bamidbar levites(age)).

- Onkelos Num 5:4 — MATERIAL. THE RUN, DOUBLED. - Onkelos Num 5:5 — CONTEXT. The frame (the third of eight).
- Onkelos Num 5:6 — MATERIAL. A MAN OR A WOMAN; ANY SIN OF MAN; TO LIE A LIE. "אִישׁ אֽוֹ אִשָּׁה כִּי יַעֲשׂוּ מִכָּל חַטֹּאת הָֽאָדָם" ("a man or a woman, if they do of any of the sins of man" — the phrase's one seat, computed) → גְּבַר אוֹ אִתְּתָא אֲרֵי יַעְבְּדוּן מִכָּל חוֹבֵי אֱנָשָׁא ("a man or a woman, if they do of any of the sins of man"); "לִמְעֹל מַעַל בַּיהוָה" ("to commit a trespass against the LORD") → לְשַׁקָּרָא שְׁקָר קֳדָם יְיָ ("TO LIE A LIE before the LORD") — the translation renders trespass (me'ilah) as LYING: the Sifrei's own lemma rule, "me'ilah in all places is lying" (piska 2 row 1; piska 7 row 1), is the Aramaic word. - Onkelos Num 5:9 — MATERIAL. EVERY HEAVE-OFFERING TO THE PRIEST.

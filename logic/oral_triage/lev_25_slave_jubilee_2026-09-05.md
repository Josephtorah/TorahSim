## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 7 (4 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 7 1 — MATERIAL. THE SELF-SALE GATE: no one
  may sell HIMSELF to keep the money, buy a beast, tools, a house —
  only when impoverished ("becomes poor and is sold"); sold only TO
  YOU; the court sells him only to you (Deut 15:12); "your brother"
  — treat him with BROTHERHOOD; he treats himself as a SLAVE (Exod
  21:2): the asymmetric protocol — you as brother, he as servant.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 7 2 — MATERIAL. "Do not work him a slave's
  work" — he does not carry your things behind you nor your gear
  to the bathhouse; "in HIM" — you may work a FREE man that way.
  The ban is keyed to status, not to the task.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 7 3 — MATERIAL. THE HIRELING TABLE: "as a
  hireling" — paid the same day (Deut 24:15); "as a resident" —
  "where it is good for him" (Deut 23:17); "with you" — WITH you in
  food, drink, clean clothing: not you white bread and he coarse,
  you old wine and he new, you on cushions and he on straw; "serve
  with you" — his craft not handed to another: a public bath-keeper,
  barber, baker does not do it (R. Yosei: if it was his craft
  before, yes; the master may not teach it first); the Sages: he
  cuts his hair, washes his clothes, bakes his dough; "he goes out
  from you" — not you in a village and he in a city; "he and his
  children with him" — the master feeds his WIFE AND CHILDREN — but
  not a wife taken without the master's knowledge ("he and his wife
  and children" — as he with knowledge, so they).
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 7 4 — MATERIAL. "Return to his family and to
  his fathers' holding" — R. Eliezer b. Yaakov: the one sold a year
  or two before the Jubilee, whom the Jubilee frees; R. Meir: to
  what his family held; R. Yehuda: to his holding, NOT to his
  former office; so too the exile; "he shall return" — THE
  MURDERER (the exile) included: the return clause reaching the
  refuge-city exile.

## Sifra, Behar — Section 6 (6 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Section 6 1 — MATERIAL. "For they are My servants" —
  MY DEED CAME FIRST; "whom I brought out of Egypt" — on condition
  they not be sold as slaves are sold; "not sold as slaves are sold"
  — not stood in the alley nor on the auction stone. The prior-deed
  argument and the market ban.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 6 2 — MATERIAL. "Do not rule him with
  rigor" — not "warm this cup" when he needs none, "cool this cup"
  when he needs none, "hoe under the vine until I come"; "I do it
  for my own need" — GIVEN TO THE HEART, so "fear your God": the
  heart-clause rule's second seat in the parashah (needless work =
  rigor).
- Sifra, Behar, Section 6 3 — MATERIAL. "Then what may we use?" —
  "your slave and maidservant from the nations": an Israelite's son
  by his slavewoman may be kept a slave ("who shall be to you"); "the
  nations AROUND you" — not the Canaanites in the land; "from the
  nations" — their sons and daughters; themselves — "from them you
  may buy"; "slave and maidservant" — the slave likened to the
  maidservant: as she has NO BETROTHAL, he has none.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 6 4 — MATERIAL. "The resident aliens'
  children" — sons and daughters; themselves by "from them"; "their
  families with you" — a foreigner who fathered a child by a
  Canaanite woman may be bought; a Canaanite who fathered by a
  foreign woman? "born in your land." "A holding for you" — as a
  holding is acquired by MONEY, DEED, and POSSESSION, so slaves.
  The three acquisition modes imported.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 6 5 — MATERIAL. "Inherit them to your SONS"
  — not your daughters to your sons: a man does not bequeath his
  daughter's rights to his sons; whoever is presumed his son IS his
  son ("your sons after you"); "to inherit as a holding" — as a
  holding has no overreaching, slaves have none (the Section 3 2
  link from this side).
- Sifra, Behar, Section 6 6 — MATERIAL. "Forever you may work them"
  — WORK ONLY, nothing more; "your brothers the children of Israel,
  each his brother" — a man over a woman, a woman over a man too
  ("in any case"); "do not rule him with rigor" — HIM not; a free
  man you may. Rigor keyed to status again.

## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 8 (8 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 8 1 — MATERIAL. "When a stranger or resident
  with you attains" — what made him rich? cleaving to you; "your
  brother becomes poor WITH HIM" — what made him poor? cleaving to
  him; "sold to the stranger" — the righteous convert; "resident" —
  the carcass-eating convert; "the stranger's family" — the idol
  worshipper; "or to the stock" — sold to the IDOL ITSELF; he comes
  saying "I am being sold" — obliged? "AFTER he is sold" — only
  once sold; "he shall have redemption" — AT ONCE, do not let him
  be absorbed; "one of his brothers" — the paternal brother precedes
  the maternal.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 8 2 — MATERIAL. "His uncle" — the father's
  brother; "his uncle's son" — that one's son; "of his flesh from
  his family" — the NEAREST FIRST (the redeemer ordering repeated
  for persons).
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 8 3 — MATERIAL. "Or his hand attains" — his
  own; "and he is redeemed" — by ANY man; "shall redeem him" three
  times — ALL redemptions by this order.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 8 4 — MATERIAL. "Reckon with his buyer" —
  not the buyer's HEIRS; "from the year of his sale until the
  Jubilee" — he does NOT go out at six; "the money of his sale by
  the number of years as a hireling's days" — redeemed by MONEY,
  not by produce or tools.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 8 5 — MATERIAL. THE DEDUCTION: goes out by
  deduction of money ("if many years remain, he returns his
  redemption from the money of his purchase"); sold at a maneh and
  improved to two hundred — reckons at the maneh (25:51); sold at
  two hundred and worth a maneh — at the maneh (25:52): the lesser
  figure, R. Dostai's field rule now on persons.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 8 6 — MATERIAL. Sold to a gentile — the
  slave's hand is on top when redeemed; sold to an Israelite? the
  "hireling"-"hireling" verbal analogy (25:53, 25:40) carries the
  upper hand to him. The two-way transfer between buyer types.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 8 7 — MATERIAL. "As a yearly hireling he
  shall be with him" — what the master eats he eats, what he drinks
  he drinks (the hireling table for the gentile-owned slave).
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 8 8 — MATERIAL. "He shall not rule him with
  rigor" — must you enter his house to see what he does to him? "IN
  YOUR SIGHT" — commanded only for what is before your eyes. The
  enforcement bounded by visibility.

## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 9 (6 rows — the book's close)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 9 1 — MATERIAL. "If he is not redeemed BY
  THESE" — R. Yosei HaGelili: by these (relatives) for freedom, by
  others for servitude; R. Akiva: by these for servitude, by others
  for freedom. Two open variables with the two arms crossed.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 9 2 — MATERIAL. R. Shimon: robbing a gentile
  is ROBBERY — "after he is sold"; may he drag him out? "he shall
  have redemption"; may he overreach him? "reckon with his buyer" —
  EXACTLY with him.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 9 3 — MATERIAL. A gentile not under your
  hand? then what could you do — "he goes out in the Jubilee year,
  he and his children": the verse speaks of a gentile UNDER
  Israel's hand; if the Torah so guards a gentile's property under
  your hand, how much more one not under it; and if a gentile's, how
  much more an Israelite's. The a-fortiori ladder on robbery.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 9 4 — MATERIAL. "For the children of Israel
  are servants to Me" — on condition they not be enslaved; "I am
  the LORD your God" — whoever enslaves them below is counted as
  enslaving Above.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 9 5 — MATERIAL (seats in lev_26_bless_curse,
  26:1). "Make no אֱלִילִים (elilim, 'idols')" — one of the
  contemptuous names for idolatry; "graven image and pillar" — each
  disqualified when made, each under "you shall not set up"; "you
  shall not set up" — the Mercury-heaps on the roads (Rabban Shimon
  b. Gamliel: the sun-images on the roofs); "a figured stone you
  shall not place IN YOUR LAND to bow upon it" — in your land you
  bow on no stone, but on the stones OF THE SANCTUARY you do. The
  place clause carving the sanctuary out of the ban.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 9 6 — MATERIAL (seats in lev_26_bless_curse,
  26:2). "My sabbaths keep, My sanctuary revere" — addressed to THE
  ONE SOLD TO A GENTILE: let him not say "my master worships idols,
  I too; my master uncovers nakedness, I too; my master profanes
  the Sabbath, I too" — the whole chapter's tail warns the sold man
  on every commandment; "I am the LORD" — faithful to pay reward.
  The two closing verses read as the sold slave's charter.

## Onkelos Lev 25:39-55 + 26:1-2 (19 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Lev 25:39 — MATERIAL. פָּלְחַן עַבְדִּין (the service of
  slaves) — slave's work as a service class.
- Onkelos Lev 25:40 — MATERIAL. כַּאֲגִירָא כְתוֹתָבָא (as a hireling,
  as a resident) — the two statuses the hireling table imports from.
- Onkelos Lev 25:41 — MATERIAL. לְזַרְעִיתֵיהּ (to his clan);
  אַחֲסָנַת אֲבָהָתוֹהִי (his fathers' holding).
- Onkelos Lev 25:42 — MATERIAL. לָא יִזְדַּבְּנוּן זְבוּן עַבְדִּין
  (they shall not be sold a slaves' sale) — the market-sale noun.
- Onkelos Lev 25:43 — MATERIAL. בְּקַשְׁיוּ (with harshness) for
  rigor — the same word Onkelos uses for "with hostility" at
  26:21-28: rigor and the covenant's hostility share one Aramaic
  root, an ink link across the parashah boundary.
- Onkelos Lev 25:44 — CONTEXT. From the nations around you; plain.
- Onkelos Lev 25:45 — MATERIAL. תוֹתָבַיָּא עָרְלַיָּא (the
  UNCIRCUMCISED residents) — "the residents" glossed as
  uncircumcised: Onkelos marks the resident alien's status by the
  covenant sign.
- Onkelos Lev 25:46 — MATERIAL. לִירוּתַת אַחֲסָנָא (for an
  inheritance of holding); בְּקַשְׁיוּ (with harshness) again.
- Onkelos Lev 25:47 — MATERIAL. עָרֵל וְתוֹתָב (an uncircumcised
  resident); לְאַרְמַי זַרְעִית גִּיּוֹרָא (to a GENTILE of a
  stranger's stock) — the idolater arm of Chapter 8 1
  chosen: the family of the stranger read as a gentile.
- Onkelos Lev 25:48 — MATERIAL. פֻּרְקָנָא תְּהֵי לֵיהּ (redemption
  he shall have); חַד מֵאֲחוֹהִי (one of his brothers).
- Onkelos Lev 25:49 — MATERIAL. The uncle, the uncle's son, the near
  of flesh, his own hand — the redeemer ladder kept whole.
- Onkelos Lev 25:50 — MATERIAL. כְּיוֹמֵי אֲגִירָא (as a hireling's
  days) — the reckoning by hireling-days.
- Onkelos Lev 25:51 — CONTEXT. Many years remaining; plain.
- Onkelos Lev 25:52 — CONTEXT. Few years remaining; plain.
- Onkelos Lev 25:53 — MATERIAL. לְעֵינָיךְ (in your sight) — the
  visibility bound kept.
- Onkelos Lev 25:54 — MATERIAL. בְּאִלֵּין (by these) — the token of
  Chapter 9 1's crossed dispute, untranslated into either arm.
- Onkelos Lev 25:55 — CONTEXT. My servants, the Exodus; plain.
- Onkelos Lev 26:1 — MATERIAL. טַעֲוָן (idols — "errors") for
  elilim: the contemptuous rendering the Sifra names (Chapter 9 5);
  צֶלֶם וְקָמָא (an image and a standing-stone); אֶבֶן סָגְדָא (a
  stone of bowing) for the figured stone — the stone defined by its
  use, not its carving.
- Onkelos Lev 26:2 — MATERIAL. יוֹמֵי שַׁבַּיָּא דִילִי (the days of
  My sabbaths); לְבֵית מַקְדְּשִׁי תְּהוֹן דָּחֲלִין (to the house of
  My sanctuary you shall be in awe) — the sanctuary as a house.

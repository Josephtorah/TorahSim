## Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin — the Noahide sugya frame (read 2026-09-01)

- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 56a:24 — MATERIAL. The seven-laws baraita
  itself: courts, blasphemy, idolatry, forbidden relations, bloodshed,
  robbery, limb from the living. The exam's master list.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 56b:7 — MATERIAL. Completes R. Yochanan's
  word-by-word: לאמר ("saying") = forbidden relations (Jeremiah 3:1
  cross-ref); "of every tree of the garden" = not robbery; "you may eat"
  = not limb from the living.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 56b:8 — MATERIAL. R. Yitzchak TEACHES THE
  INVERSION: ויצו ("and He commanded") = idolatry, אלהים ("God") =
  courts — the token-assignment dispute the exam tests (F-012).
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57a:2 — context. "The other: it reveals
  their way" — dialectic leg on the flood indictment.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57a:8 — MATERIAL. Rav Yosef, the school of
  Rav: a Noahide is EXECUTED for THREE — forbidden relations, bloodshed,
  blasphemy (the גש"ר letter-mnemonic).
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57a:10 — context. Dialectic on extending
  the three (learning from bloodshed / from איש איש 'any man').
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57a:11 — MATERIAL. Rav Sheshet, the school
  of Rav: executed for FOUR (idolatry joins).
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57a:12 — MATERIAL. Challenge from the
  baraita (warned but not killed for idolatry); Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak:
  אזהרה שלהן זו היא מיתתן ('their prohibition is their death') — THEIR PROHIBITION IS THEIR DEATH-LIABILITY.
  The principle 58b:25 reuses for the Sabbath row.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57a:13 — MATERIAL (credit: already read at
  the triage as G21-11's source). Rav Huna, Rav Yehudah, all Rav's
  students: executed for ALL SEVEN — "the Merciful revealed one and the
  same holds for all."
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57a:14 — context. Robbery challenge: the
  baraita says "forbidden," not "liable."
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57a:15 — context. Resolution: the tail
  ("an Israelite from a gentile — permitted") forced the head's phrasing.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57b:2 — MATERIAL. R. Yaakov bar Acha's
  aggadah-book (the lore book) baraita: a Noahide is executed by ONE judge, on ONE
  witness, WITHOUT forewarning, on a MAN's testimony and not a woman's,
  EVEN A RELATIVE's; by R. Yishmael — even for a fetus.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57b:6 — MATERIAL. The school of Menashe:
  every Noahide death is STRANGULATION — באדם ("in the man") read onto
  the verse-tail as blood kept inside the body.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57b:8 — MATERIAL. Resolution of the
  women-in-courts objection: "his SONS — to judgment; his HOUSEHOLD — to
  charity" (Gen 18:19 split).
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57b:9 — MATERIAL. Rav Avya the elder's
  objection (a woman who killed — exempt from "from the hand of a MAN"?)
  and Rav Yehudah's resolution: שופך דם האדם — "WHOEVER sheds," in any
  case. The woman-killer is liable.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 57b:11 — context. איש איש baraita (Lev
  18:6) — gentiles warned on relations like Israel; Leviticus-side leg.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 58a:9 — context. Dialectic pressing R.
  Eliezer's "his father" (why not literal — that is "cleave, not male").
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 58b:6 — MATERIAL. The Gen 20:12 proof
  DEFLECTED: "she was his brother's daughter" — Abraham spoke of kinship
  degree, not sister-law. Our verse's role: attempted proof, deflected.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 58b:7 — context. Kayin married his sister —
  "the world is built on kindness" (Psalm 89:3); the concession frame.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:1 — context. Courts as positive duty in
  a negative-duty list — dialectic on the census's shape.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:2 — MATERIAL. R. Yochanan: a gentile
  who occupies himself with Torah is liable — מורשה ("inheritance," Deut
  33:4) read as ours-not-theirs (or as מאורסה, "betrothed").
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:3 — context. Why not count it among the
  seven — robbery-of-the-inheritance framing.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:4 — MATERIAL. R. Meir's counter: a
  gentile who studies Torah is LIKE A HIGH PRIEST — האדם ("the man," Lev
  18:5), not priests-Levites-Israelites.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:5 — MATERIAL. The resolution: like a
  high priest IN THEIR OWN SEVEN laws.
- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:7 — MATERIAL. R. Chanina ben Gamliel's
  reading mechanics (blood-from-the-living split of Gen 9:4); the rabbis

- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:8 — context. The Deuteronomy 12:23

- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:9 — context. The rabbis' counter-route:

- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:10 — MATERIAL. The framework question:

- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59a:11 — MATERIAL. R. Yose son of R.
  Chanina, THE FRAMEWORK: every commandment said to the sons of Noach and

- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59b:5 — context. Dialectic: then let every

- Babylonian Talmud Sanhedrin 59b:15 — MATERIAL. Rav Huna: דמו ("its

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Section 1 (5 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 1 1 — MATERIAL. "If you walk in My
  statutes" — the Omnipresent DESIRES that Israel LABOR IN TORAH
  (Psalm 81, Isaiah 48, Deut 5 quoted): the condition's content is
  study, stated before the commandments' keeping.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 1 2 — MATERIAL. THE TWO-CLAUSE SPLIT:
  "walk in My statutes" cannot mean the commandments — "keep My
  commandments and do them" already says that; so "walk" = LABOR IN
  TORAH; mirrored at 26:14: "if you do not listen to Me" is not the
  commandments ("not do all these commandments" says that) — it is
  not laboring in Torah. Both conditions split the same way.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 1 3 — MATERIAL. THE REMEMBER-VERB RULE:
  "remember the Sabbath" — in the heart? "keep" covers the heart, so
  "remember" = RECITE WITH THE MOUTH; the same for the desert
  provocation (Deut 9:7), Miriam (Deut 24:9), Amalek (Deut 25:17) —
  each pair of remember-and-guard assigns the mouth to remember. A
  meta-rule across four seats.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 1 4 — MATERIAL. "I will make your cities
  a waste" — of people? "I will desolate the land" says that; so —
  of PASSERS-BY; "I will desolate your sanctuaries" — of offerings?
  "I will not smell your sweet savor" says that; so — of the
  PILGRIM BANDS. Two doublets each assigning the second clause its
  own object.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 1 5 — MATERIAL. "Keep and DO them" —
  one who learns IN ORDER TO DO, not one who learns not to do: for
  him it were better not to have been created.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 1 (8 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 1 1 — MATERIAL. "Your rains in their
  season" — on Wednesday nights (not Sabbath eves — even in Elijah's
  years rain on Sabbath eves is a curse sign); in Herod's days rain
  fell by night and the sun dried the ground by morning; in Shimon
  b. Shetach's days, in Queen Salome's reign, rain from Sabbath
  night to Sabbath night until wheat grew like kidneys, barley like
  olive stones, lentils like gold dinars — the Sages stored samples
  "to show how much sin costs" (Jeremiah 5:25). The blessing's
  schedule with two recorded runs.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 1 2 — MATERIAL. "YOUR rains" — not all
  lands'; then "all families shall be blessed in you" = plenty in
  Israel and famine elsewhere, the nations buying from you (Joseph's
  silver-gathering; Deut 33:25). The blessing scoped to the land.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 1 3 — ENRICHMENT. "The land shall give
  its produce" — as in Adam's days, bearing the day it is sown
  (Psalm 111:4; Genesis 1:11).
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 1 4 — ENRICHMENT. "The tree of the field
  its fruit" — as in Adam's days, bearing the day it is planted.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 1 5 — ENRICHMENT. "A fruit tree" — the
  tree itself edible like its fruit.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 1 6 — ENRICHMENT. Even barren trees will
  bear fruit.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 1 7 — MATERIAL. "Threshing shall reach
  vintage" — busy threshing until vintage comes, busy at vintage
  until sowing comes; "eat your bread to satiety" — a little, blessed
  in the belly (Exod 23:25); "dwell securely IN YOUR LAND" — in your
  land, not outside it.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 1 8 — MATERIAL. "I will give PEACE in
  the land" — lest you say "food and drink, but without peace it is
  nothing": peace is weighed against everything (Isaiah 45:7).

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 2 (5 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 2 1 — MATERIAL. "None shall make you
  afraid" — of no creature; "I will remove the evil beast" — R.
  Yehuda: removed from the world; R. Shimon: made to CEASE HARMING
  (the Sabbath psalm's "who ceases the harmers"): God's praise is
  harmers present and not harming.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 2 2 — ENRICHMENT. Isaiah 11's wolf and
  lamb; the infant's hand in the viper's eye.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 2 3 — MATERIAL. "No sword shall pass
  through your land" — not only no war, but no ARMIES PASSING
  THROUGH from one province to another (as in Josiah's days); "they
  fall before you by the sword" — each by his fellow's sword.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 2 4 — MATERIAL. "Five of you chase a
  hundred, a hundred ten thousand" — "of you," the WEAK among you;
  and the ratio is not arithmetic (five to a hundred would give a
  hundred to two thousand) — the many who do Torah are not as the
  few who do Torah: a non-linear scaling read off the verse's own
  numbers.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 2 5 — MATERIAL. "I will turn to you" —
  the king's many laborers and the one who worked long: a great
  reckoning; "turn to you" — in good; "make you fruitful" — in
  offspring; "multiply you" — in upright stature; "establish My
  covenant" — not like the first, which you broke (Jeremiah 31's
  new covenant).

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 3 (7 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 3 1 — MATERIAL. "Old, old-stored" — the
  older, the better; "old-stored" — wine only? "old, old-stored" —
  everything that ages; "the old you shall clear before the new" —
  granaries full of new, storehouses full of old, and you anxious
  how to clear the old.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 3 2 — MATERIAL. "My dwelling among you"
  — THE TEMPLE; "My soul shall not abhor you" — once I redeem you I
  do not reject you again.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 3 3 — ENRICHMENT. The king strolling
  with his tenant in the orchard — "I am like you."
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 3 4 — CONTEXT. "I will be your God" —
  My awe still on you; "who brought you out" — who did the wonders
  in Egypt will do these.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 3 5 — MATERIAL. "From being slaves to
  them" — not slaves to slaves: they were slaves to KINGS.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 3 6 — ENRICHMENT. The lent cow and the
  ten sons — the yoke broken at once (Psalm 129).
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 3 7 — MATERIAL. "I led you upright" —
  R. Shimon: two hundred cubits; R. Yehuda: a hundred, like Adam;
  women by Psalm 144:12 — the Temple's pattern, a hundred cubits;
  or: upright stature, fearing no creature. A measure disputed.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Section 2 (3 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 2 1 — MATERIAL. "If you do not listen
  to Me" — to the SAGES' EXPOSITION; not the written Torah — "not
  do all these commandments" says that. The curse's first condition
  is contempt for the oral layer.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 2 2 — MATERIAL. "To ME" — one who
  KNOWS his Master and INTENDS to rebel (Nimrod "before the LORD,"
  Sodom "against the LORD exceedingly"): the preposition marks
  knowing rebellion.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Section 2 3 — MATERIAL. THE SEVEN-STEP DESCENT:
  not learning → not doing ("not do"); → despising others who do
  ("despise My statutes"); → hating the Sages ("abhor My
  judgments"); → preventing others ("so as not to do"); → denying
  the commandments were from Sinai ("all My commandments"); →
  denying the Root ("to break My covenant"). Seven clauses read as
  seven successive states, each entailing the next — a state
  machine of decline.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 4 (5 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 4 1 — MATERIAL. "I too" — in ANGER; "I"
  who built will destroy, "I" who planted will uproot (Jeremiah
  45:4); "will do THIS" — the hardest: My great Name upon you as a
  CREDITOR; "to you" — from your own hand; evil never issues from
  before Me (Lamentations 3:38 read: not the evils but the good).
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 4 2 — MATERIAL. "I will appoint
  בֶּהָלָה (behalah, 'terror') over you" — the plagues VISIT you one
  upon another, the next arriving while the first still stands;
  terror = the plague that terrifies: the plague of death.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 4 3 — MATERIAL. THE ILLNESS LADDER:
  "consumption" — wasting; "fever" — burning; "that consume the
  eyes" — he despairs of life; "and make the soul pine" — OTHERS
  despair of him; "sow in vain" — the first year no growth, the
  second growth and the enemies eat; or: sons and daughters raised
  and consumed by sin (Lamentations 2:22). Four clauses as four
  worsening states.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 4 4 — MATERIAL. "I will set My face
  against you" — as "turn to you" in good, so "set My face" in
  evil: the king who leaves all his business to attend to you — for
  harm. The good-evil mirror on one verb.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 4 5 — MATERIAL. "Smitten before your
  enemies" — death inside and enemies around outside; "those who
  hate you rule you" — I raise over you only FROM YOU AND AMONG YOU
  (nations take the open, your own dig for the hidden — Judges 6,
  Micah 3); "you shall flee" — from terror; "none pursues" — for
  lack of strength.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 5 (7 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 5 1 — MATERIAL. "If UNTIL these" — R.
  Eliezer: the Omnipresent brings no punishment without first
  WARNING ("if until these" = after warning); R. Yehoshua: lest
  Israel say the plagues are exhausted — I have more; "seven for
  your sins" — seven transgressions, seven punishments: THE
  SEVENFOLD RULE.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 5 2 — MATERIAL. "I will break the pride
  of your strength" — the TEMPLE (Ezekiel 24:21); R. Akiva: the
  mighty of Israel like Joab; others: the proud, Israel's glory,
  like Pappus b. Yehuda and Lulianus of Alexandria. Three referents.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 5 3 — MATERIAL. "Heavens as iron, earth
  as brass" — HARDER than Deuteronomy's "heavens as brass, earth as
  iron": there the sky sweats like brass (some dew) and the earth
  keeps its fruit like iron; here the sky does not sweat and the
  earth sweats and rots its fruit. The two curse texts ordered by
  severity from the metals' physics; "your strength spent in vain"
  — Rabbi: the vineyard; some: flax; some: the strength; or the
  father who dowered his daughter and buried her within the wedding
  week.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 5 4 — MATERIAL. "Spent in vain" — one
  who never plowed, weeded, hoed and blight struck at harvest — no
  loss; one who labored at all of it and blight struck — his teeth
  are set on edge; "the land shall not give its yield" — even what
  you carry to it; "the tree not give its fruit" — it sets and then
  drops.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 5 5 — MATERIAL. "If you walk with Me
  קֶרִי (keri, 'casually/in hostility')" — you made My judgments
  CASUAL in the world, I make you casual in the world; "seven
  according to your sins" — the sevenfold rule again.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 5 6 — MATERIAL. "The beast of the
  field" — the bereaving beast; the non-bereaving by Deut 32:24's
  "tooth of beasts"; biting without killing? "with the venom of
  crawlers" — as those bite and kill, so these; years were known in
  the land when a donkey bit and killed, an ox bit and killed.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 5 7 — MATERIAL. "Bereave you" — the
  little ones; "cut off your cattle" — outside; "diminish you" —
  inside; "your roads desolate" — highways and byways, great and
  small.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 6 (7 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 6 1 — MATERIAL. "A sword avenging the
  covenant's vengeance" — vengeance IN the covenant and vengeance
  NOT in it (the blinding of Zedekiah); "gathered into your cities"
  — the siege; "pestilence among you" — death inside, enemies
  outside; "given into the enemy's hand" — the rule that no corpse
  stays overnight in Jerusalem, and the buriers carrying it out fall
  into the enemy's hand. A recorded law producing the curse's
  mechanism.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 6 2 — MATERIAL. "Break your staff of
  bread" — every support of bread; "ten women bake in one oven" —
  for lack of wood; "return your bread by weight" — crumbling bread
  weighed in the oven; R. Yosei b. Dormaskit: the man with an issar
  weighing bread against dates — "eat and not be satisfied" either
  way; or: the hunger of panic.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 6 3 — ENRICHMENT. Doeg b. Yosef's
  widow and the siege (Lamentations 2:20); fathers eat sons and sons
  fathers (Ezekiel 5:10).
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 6 4 — MATERIAL. "Destroy your high
  places" — plain; "cut off your sun-images" — the sorcerers and
  diviners; "your carcasses on your idols' carcasses" — Elijah and
  the famine-swollen last of three thousand who kisses his idol till
  his belly bursts; "My soul abhor you" — exile, or the Presence's
  withdrawal; "your cities a waste" — cities twice: the fortified
  towns and provinces; "your sanctuaries" — the doubled sanctuary
  token includes SYNAGOGUES AND STUDY HOUSES; "not smell your sweet
  savor" — plain.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 6 5 — MATERIAL. "I will desolate the
  land" — A GOOD MEASURE: lest Israel say the enemies now enjoy our
  land — "your enemies who dwell in it shall be desolate upon it":
  the later comers find no ease.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 6 6 — MATERIAL. "Scatter you among the
  nations" — a HARSH measure: a province exiled together consoles
  itself; you I scatter as one winnows barley, no grain cleaving to
  its fellow (Jeremiah 15:7).
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 6 7 — MATERIAL. "Draw out a sword after
  you" — a sword sent does not soon return; as spilled water does
  not return to the vessel.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 7 (6 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 7 1 — MATERIAL. "Your land desolate,
  your cities waste" — a HARSH measure: one exiled who will return
  counts his vineyard as not ruined; you — "your land shall be
  desolate": because you will not return (in that exile).
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 7 2 — MATERIAL. THE SABBATH-DEBT: "then
  the land shall be paid its sabbaths" — I told you to sow six and
  release one for Me, that you know the land is Mine; you did not;
  rise and be exiled, and it will release BY ITSELF every release it
  OWES Me (26:34-35). The unkept releases as a LEDGER DEBT the land
  collects during the exile — the simulator's own vocabulary in the
  Sifra's mouth.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 7 3 — MATERIAL. "Faintness in their
  heart" — not softness but מֹרֶךְ (morekh, 'faintness'): terror,
  dread, worry, trembling, fear.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 7 4 — MATERIAL. "The sound of a driven
  leaf shall chase them" — R. Yehoshua b. Karcha's memory: sitting
  among trees, the wind knocked the leaves, we ran crying "the
  horsemen!" and looked back and saw no one, and wept that the
  verse was fulfilled in us; "flee as from the sword" — from terror;
  "fall with none pursuing" — from lack of strength.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 7 5 — MATERIAL. THE SURETY RULE: "each
  shall stumble over his brother" — not over his brother but IN HIS
  BROTHER'S SIN: ALL ISRAEL ARE SURETIES FOR ONE ANOTHER. A
  collective-liability principle read off the preposition.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 7 6 — CONTEXT. "No standing before your
  enemies" — the hour Jerusalem was taken.

## Sifra, Bechukotai — Chapter 8 (10 rows)
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 1 — MATERIAL. "You shall perish among
  the nations" — R. Akiva: the TEN TRIBES exiled to Media; others:
  perishing = EXILE, not death — "the enemies' land consumes you"
  says real perishing. One verb assigned two referents.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 2 — MATERIAL. THE FATHERS' SINS
  RECONCILED: "pine away in their sin, and in their fathers' sins
  with them" — but "fathers shall not die for sons" (Deut 24:16)?
  only when they HOLD THEIR FATHERS' DEEDS, generation after
  generation, are they judged by them. A conflict resolved by a
  condition.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 3 — MATERIAL. "They shall confess
  their sin and their fathers' sin" — words toward REPENTANCE: as
  soon as they confess, I return and have mercy.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 4 — MATERIAL. "I too will walk with
  them in hostility" — casual for casual; "bring them into the
  enemies' land" — a GOOD measure: lest they say "we will be as the
  nations" — I set My prophets over them and return them under My
  wings (Ezekiel 20:32-33): against your will I make My kingship
  reign over you.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 5 — MATERIAL. "Or then their
  uncircumcised heart is humbled" — toward repentance: as soon as
  they humble their heart, I have mercy.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 6 — MATERIAL. "My covenant with
  Jacob... Isaac... Abraham" — why the fathers BACKWARD? if Abraham's
  deeds do not suffice, Isaac's; if not Isaac's, Jacob's: each
  suffices for the world to hang on him. A fallback chain read off
  the reversed order.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 7 — MATERIAL. Why "remember" for
  Abraham and Jacob but not Isaac? his ashes are seen as heaped on
  the altar (no remembering needed); why "also" for Abraham and
  Isaac but not Jacob? Jacob's bed was whole. Token presence and
  absence each given a reason.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 8 — MATERIAL. "And" (the particle
  אֶת, et) — the MATRIARCHS included (Genesis 49:31 "Abraham AND
  Sarah"); "the land I will remember" — the covenant is cut to the
  LAND too. The particle middah on the covenant list.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 9 — MATERIAL. "The land shall be
  forsaken of them and be paid its sabbaths" — the sabbath-debt
  restated (Chapter 7 2); "because and because" — not head for
  head: I exacted one in a hundred; "because they despised My
  judgments" — the LAWS; "abhorred My statutes" — the EXPOSITIONS.
- Sifra, Bechukotai, Chapter 8 10 — MATERIAL. "This" — the desert's
  season; "also this" — Baal Peor's; "and also this" — the Amorite
  kings'; "not rejected nor abhorred to destroy them" — every good
  gift taken, and but for the Torah scroll left them they would not
  differ from the nations; "not rejected" — in Vespasian's days;
  "abhorred" — in Greece's; "to destroy them, to break My covenant"
  — in Haman's; "for I am the LORD their God" — in Gog's. THE
  COVENANT TO THE TRIBES from "the covenant of the ancestors whom I
  brought out." "Statutes, judgments, and TORAHS" — statutes = the
  expositions, judgments = the laws, torahs = TWO TORAHS, one
  written and one oral (R. Akiva: many torahs — the burnt offering's,
  the meal offering's...); "between Him and the children of Israel"
  — Moses the agent; "on Mount Sinai by Moses' hand" — the Torah
  with its laws, details, and explanations given through Moses from
  Sinai. The book's closing self-description: two channels.

## Onkelos Lev 26:3-46 (44 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Lev 26:3 — MATERIAL. בִּקְיָמַי תְּהָכוּן (in My statutes
  you shall walk) — the walking verb kept; the Sifra's "labor in
  Torah" is not written in.
- Onkelos Lev 26:4 — MATERIAL. מַטְרֵיכוֹן בְּעִדָּנְהוֹן (your rains in
  their seasons) — the schedule token.
- Onkelos Lev 26:5 — MATERIAL. דְּיָשָׁא יָת קְטָפָא (threshing the
  vintage); לְאַפּוֹקֵי בַר זַרְעָא (to the going-out of the sower) —
  the seasons chained.
- Onkelos Lev 26:6 — MATERIAL. וֶאֱבַטֵּל חַיְתָא בִשְׁתָּא (I will
  make the evil beast CEASE) — R. Shimon's arm, cease not remove;
  וּדְקָטְלִין בְּחַרְבָּא לָא יְעִדּוּן (and those who kill by the
  sword shall not PASS THROUGH) — the sword read as sword-bearers
  passing through: the Sifra's armies-in-transit (Chapter 2 3).
- Onkelos Lev 26:7 — CONTEXT. Fall before you by the sword; plain.
- Onkelos Lev 26:8 — MATERIAL. חַמְשָׁא מְאָה וּמְאָה מִנְּכוֹן
  לְרִבּוֹתָא (five a hundred, and a hundred of you a myriad) — the
  non-linear numbers kept exactly.
- Onkelos Lev 26:9 — MATERIAL. וְאֶתְפְּנִי בְמֵימְרִי לְאוֹטָבָא לְכוֹן
  (I will turn BY MY WORD to do you good) — the Sifra's "in good"
  (Chapter 2 5) written into the verse; the turning through the
  Word.
- Onkelos Lev 26:10 — CONTEXT. Old of old; clear the old before the
  new; plain.
- Onkelos Lev 26:11 — MATERIAL. מַשְׁכְּנִּי (My dwelling); וְלָא
  יְרַחֵק מֵימְרִי יָתְכוֹן (My Word shall not put you far) — the
  abhorring rendered as distancing through the Word.
- Onkelos Lev 26:12 — MATERIAL. וְאַשְׁרֵי שְׁכִינְתִּי בֵּינֵיכוֹן (I
  will make My Presence dwell among you) for "I will walk among you"
  — the walking replaced by the Presence's dwelling; קֳדָמַי לְעָם
  (before Me for a people).
- Onkelos Lev 26:13 — MATERIAL. נִיר עַמְמַיָּא (the yoke of THE
  NATIONS) — "your yoke" specified as the nations' yoke;
  לְחֵרוּתָא (to freedom) for "upright" — the Jubilee's freedom word
  reused for the Exodus posture.
- Onkelos Lev 26:14 — MATERIAL. לָא תְקַבְּלוּן לְמֵימְרִי (you do not
  ACCEPT My Word) for "not listen to Me" — hearing as acceptance of
  the Word.
- Onkelos Lev 26:15 — MATERIAL. תְּקוֹצוּן (loathe); תְּרַחֵק נַפְשְׁכוֹן
  (your soul puts far); לְאַשְׁנָיוּתְכוֹן יָת קְיָמִי (to CHANGE My
  covenant) for "break" — breaking as alteration.
- Onkelos Lev 26:16 — MATERIAL. בַּהֶלְתָּא (terror); שַׁחֶפְתָּא
  וְקַדַּחְתָּא (consumption and fever); מְחַשְּׁכָן עַיְנִין וּמַפְּחַן
  נְפָשׁ (darkening the eyes and making the soul breathe out) — the
  ladder's four kept.
- Onkelos Lev 26:17 — MATERIAL. וְאֶתֵּן רוּגְזִי בְּכוֹן (I will set
  My ANGER against you) for "My face" — the face de-anthropomorphized
  to anger (Chapter 4 1's "in anger" written in).
- Onkelos Lev 26:18 — MATERIAL. לְמִרְדֵּי יָתכוֹן (to chastise you);
  שְׁבַע עַל חוֹבֵיכוֹן (seven upon your sins) — the sevenfold kept.
- Onkelos Lev 26:19 — MATERIAL. יְקַר תָּקְפְּכוֹן (the glory of your
  strength); תַּקִּיפִין כְּפַרְזְלָא מִלַּאֲחָתָא מִטְרָא (hard as
  iron FROM SENDING DOWN RAIN) and חֲסִינָא כִנְחָשָׁא מִלְּמֶעְבַּד

  translation.

  hardness; plain repetition.
- Onkelos Lev 26:28 — MATERIAL. בִּתְקוֹף רְגָז (with strength of

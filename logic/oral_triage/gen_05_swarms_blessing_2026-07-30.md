  Exod 7:28 (the frogs) — Tanchuma Pekudei 9:2 is the chain witness pairing the first two.
- ye'ofef polel: Gen 1:20 ↔ Isa 6:2 (u-vi-shtayim ye'ofef, the seraphim) — the
  angels-on-day-5 tradition (BR 1:3 et al.) is built on exactly this shared rare form.
- ha-gedolim pair: Gen 1:16 ↔ 1:21 (unit export; within-corpus).

## Onkelos Exodus 26:1-37 — read IN FULL, 37 verses

- Onkelos Exod 26:1 + 26:31 + 26:36 — MATERIAL. The craft-grade
  taxonomy carried in translation: מעשה חשב ("the work of the
  designer") rendered עוֹבַד אֳמָן ("the work of the MASTER-CRAFTSMAN"
  — the cherubim woven INTO the fabric, 26:1 and the veil 26:31),
  against מעשה רקם ("the work of the embroiderer") rendered עוֹבַד
  צַיָּר ("the work of the EMBROIDERER/designer" — the door-screen
  26:36): two named grades of sacred craft, the higher for the inner
  fabrics, the lower for the outer screen — a rank-order the
  translation keeps distinct.
- Onkelos Exod 26:15 — MATERIAL (light). עצי שטים עמדים ("acacia
  wood, STANDING") rendered קָיְמִין ("standing/enduring") — the
  standing-word the chain reads doubly: boards that stand the way the
  tree grew, and boards that ENDURE forever (the Talmudic reading of
  the token; the wood that never decays). TESTING-shelf pointer:
  Mishnah Sukkah 3:14's row anchors on this verse in the enumeration.
- Onkelos Exod 26:30 — MATERIAL, the chapter's crown. ותקים את המשכן
  כמשפטו ("you shall raise the tabernacle according to its FASHION")
  rendered וּתְקִים יָת מַשְׁכְּנָא כְּהִלְכְּתֵיהּ ("you shall raise
  the tabernacle ACCORDING TO ITS HALAKHAH" — its RULE) — the
  received translation applies the law-word itself to the building's
  assembly order: the architecture has a halakhah. The pattern-clause
  closes it: דִּי אִתְחֲזֵיתָא בְּטוּרָא ("which you were SHOWN in the
  mountain").
- Onkelos Exod 26:33 — MATERIAL. The veil (פָּרֻכְתָּא) hung under
  the clasps, the ark of the testimony brought within, וְתַפְרֵשׁ
  פָּרֻכְתָּא לְכוֹן בֵּין קוּדְשָׁא וּבֵין קֹדֶשׁ קוּדְשַׁיָּא ("and
  the veil shall SEPARATE FOR YOU between the Holy and the Holy of
  Holies") — the partition as LAW: one curtain, one boundary, the
  graded-sanctity architecture in a single clause. TESTING-shelf
  pointer: Mishnah Yoma 5:1 (the Second Temple's two curtains and the
  cubit between them) is this clause's case row.
- Onkelos Exod 26:34-35 — MATERIAL (light). The placement table: the
  cover on the ark IN the Holy of Holies; the table outside the veil
  on the NORTH, the menorah opposite on the SOUTH — the floor-plan as
  standing law (each vessel's station named).
- Onkelos Exod 26:2-14, 26:16-29, 26:32, 26:37 — context (the
  curtain dimensions and loops, fifty clasps of gold and of bronze
  — the tabernacle becoming ONE (וִיהֵי מַשְׁכְּנָא חָד, 26:6, the
  unity clause), the goat-hair tent, the ram and sasgona covers, the
  boards' measures, the forty silver sockets, the corner boards, the
  five bars with the MIDDLE BAR running end to end (26:28), the gold
  overlay, the four and five pillars).

- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 9 + Buber 7 + Buber 8 — MATERIAL. The
  goat-hair curtains as the descent's destination: "I left the upper

  יריעות עזים ('curtains of GOAT HAIR') and I will come dwell with
  you"; the ministering angels' objection (Psalm 8:2) and the answer;
  and THE CONTAINED VOICE: before the tabernacle stood, the Voice
  would go out and burst into the nations' tents (Deuteronomy 5:23's
  "who has heard... and lived" read against them) — the tabernacle
  CONTAINS the speech that previously overflowed: the oracle-
  interface reading (EX25-05) from the other side. Rider: Solomon's
  prayer-asymmetry — the foreigner's petition answered
  unconditionally, the Israelite's per his known heart (1 Kings
  8:39-43) — the petition-handling delta recorded.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 9 (end) + Buber 9 — MATERIAL, the boards'
  provenance. יעקב אבינו נטע אותם ("JACOB OUR FATHER PLANTED THEM"):
  going down to Egypt he told his sons they would be redeemed and
  asked for a tabernacle — plant cedars NOW, so the boards stand
  ready; the cedars SANG (Psalm 96:12's "then shall all the trees of
  the wood sing" — אז read as song); twenty-four cedar species, seven
  named (Isaiah 41:19), the shittah alone chosen. And the tradition's
  own detail wired to this chapter's ink: הבריח התיכון ירד ביד יעקב
  למצרים ("THE MIDDLE BAR went down to Egypt IN JACOB'S HAND") — the
  end-to-end bar of our 26:28 given a Genesis provenance. The
  shittim-name aetiology rides: sinned at Shittim (Numbers 25),
  struck at Shittim, healed by shittim-wood — לרפאות ("to HEAL") what
  was done there; the future healing spring waters the very ravine
  (Joel 4:18).

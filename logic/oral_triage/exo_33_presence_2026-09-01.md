## Onkelos Exodus 33:1-23 — read IN FULL, 23 verses

- Onkelos Exod 33:3-6 — MATERIAL. The withdrawal negotiation run
  through the Presence-layer: the ascent-clause rendered on
  שְׁכִנְתִּי ("MY PRESENCE") with the one-hour destruction
  rationale at 33:5 (שָׁעָה חֲדָא — "were My Presence among you ONE
  HOUR I would destroy you"); and THE ORNAMENTS: תִּקּוּן זֵינֵיהוֹן
  ("their ORNAMENTS/ARMOR") stripped from Mount Chorev — the spine
  identifies them as the Sinai CROWNS (the two-angels crowning of
  the Tetzaveh reading) and Buber as the WEAPONRY with the Name
  engraved; stripped unwillingly. The we-will-do-and-hear grant
  revoked at the calf.
- Onkelos Exod 33:7 — MATERIAL, the chapter's crown. THE HOUSE OF
  INSTRUCTION: אהל מועד rendered מַשְׁכַּן בֵּית אוּלְפָנָא ("the
  tent of the HOUSE OF INSTRUCTION") — and every seeker: כָּל
  דְּתָבַע אוּלְפַן מִן קֳדָם יְיָ ("all who sought INSTRUCTION from
  before the LORD went out to it") — seeking God decoded as seeking
  TEACHING; the interim interface is a school, open to everyone who
  seeks (against the priestly oracle's access list of EX28-09 — two
  interfaces, two authorization models). The spine's riders: the
  mile distance (from Joshua 3:4's two thousand cubits); the
  excommunication inference (the student honors the Master's ban —
  Moses' own a-fortiori); "everyone who receives an elder is as one
  who receives the Presence"; even the angels seeking Him say "He
  is at Moses' tent."
- Onkelos Exod 33:9-11 — MATERIAL. SPEECH WITH SPEECH: face-to-face
  rendered מַמְלַל עִם מַמְלַל ("WORD WITH WORD, as a man speaks
  with his friend") — the reflexive מִתְמַלֵּל ('was spoken with') guarding
  transcendence; Joshua the resident student who does not leave the
  tent. The spine's protocol law (Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 27):
  THE ANGER-PARTITION — "when I am angry you appease, when you are
  angry I appease; BOTH faces must not be angry at once" — the
  two-party wrath-protocol, with the return-to-camp command and the
  Joshua-replacement warning (the matron and the orphan-girl
  parable: return, or the ward inherits the palace).
- Onkelos Exod 33:12-17 — MATERIAL (light). The favor negotiation:
  רַבִּיתָךְ בְּשׁוּם ("I have EXALTED you by name" — the same
  by-name verb as Bezalel's 31:2: the office-grant vocabulary);
  שְׁכִנְתִּי תְהַךְ ("MY PRESENCE will go," 33:14); the
  distinction-request rendered וְיִתְעַבְּדַן לָנָא פְרִישָׁן
  ("WONDERS will be done for us" — the separateness effected by
  wonders); the nations-use-intermediaries argument ("what
  difference between us?" — the direct-access demand, Midrash
  Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 27's frame). TESTING-shelf pointer: Pirkei Avot
  4:18 (this exam's docket) reads 33:14's פָּנַי יֵלֵכוּ ('My presence will go') as the
  appeasement-timing law — do not placate your fellow IN THE HOUR
  OF HIS ANGER: wait until the face of anger passes.
- Onkelos Exod 33:18-23 — MATERIAL, the epistemology crown. The
  vision protocol: לָא תִיכוּל לְמֶחֱזֵי יָת אַפֵּי שְׁכִנְתִּי
  ("you CANNOT SEE the FACE of My Presence — no man sees Me and
  lives"); הָא אָתַר מַתְקַן קֳדָמַי ("behold, a place PREPARED
  BEFORE ME" — the spine: My place is secondary to Me, I am not
  secondary to My place — space-independence, R. Yosi bar
  Chalafta); the rock's cave; וְאָגֵין בְּמֵימְרִי עֲלָךְ ("I will
  SHIELD you WITH MY WORD" — the hand rendered as the Word's
  shelter); and 33:23's abstractions — יָת דְּבַתְרָי ("that which
  is BEHIND Me") seen, וְדִקֳדָמַי ("that which is BEFORE Me") not
  seen: the knowable-from-behind doctrine. The spine's treasuries
  scene rides: the reward-stores shown, and the GREAT store given
  חִנָּם ("GRATIS — I am gracious to whom I will be gracious"):
  grace beyond merit as a standing store; Moses' question on the
  wicked's ease deferred to the world to come.
- Onkelos Exod 33:1-2, 33:8 — context (the angel-escort promise; the
  people rising as Moses passes — the stand-before-the-elder law
  the spine derives here).

## The fresh read — verdict rows (20 segments, 6 tractates)

### Sanhedrin 5a:5-7, 46b:20-22 (6)
- 5a:5 — MATERIAL. The license geography: here a scepter, there a
  lawgiver; authority from here serves there.
- 5a:6 — MATERIAL (docket). לא יסור שבט מיהודה ("the SCEPTER shall
  not depart from Judah," 49:10) — אלו ראשי גליות שבבבל ("these
  are the EXILARCHS in Babylon who rule Israel with the staff");
  the lawgiver — Hillel's descendants teaching Torah publicly.
- 5a:7 — MATERIAL. Rabbah bar Chana judged and erred — "if they
  accepted you, do not pay" — and he HELD a license: the license
  from there does not serve here.
- 46b:20 — MATERIAL. The question: eulogy — honor of the LIVING
  or of the DEAD? (the practical stakes: "do not eulogize that
  man"; taking from the heirs).
- 46b:21 — MATERIAL (docket). The first proof is our verse: ויבא
  אברהם לספד לשרה ולבכתה ("Abraham came to EULOGIZE Sarah and to
  weep for her," 23:2) — deflected: Sarah is pleased that Abraham
  is honored through her.
- 46b:22 — MATERIAL. The second proof (all Israel eulogized) with
  its deflection — the righteous are pleased that people are
  honored through them.

### Makkot 9a:9-11, 11b:1-2 (5)
- 9a:9 — MATERIAL. Rava's challenge: הנך מת על האשה ("you shall
  DIE for the woman you took," 20:3) — by man's hand?
- 9a:10 — MATERIAL (docket). No — by HEAVEN'S: דיקא נמי דכתיב
  מחטוא לי ("precisely so, for it is written: from sinning
  AGAINST ME," 20:6) — the dative token assigns jurisdiction.
- 9a:11 — MATERIAL. The Joseph counter ("I would sin against
  GOD" — yet man's court) resolved: judgment given over to man.
- 11b:1 — MATERIAL (docket). Rav Yehuda citing Rav: נידוי על
  תנאי צריך הפרה ("a ban on CONDITION requires release") — from
  Judah's "if I do not bring him to you" (43:9); R. Shmuel bar
  Nachmani's narrative proof follows.
- 11b:2 — MATERIAL. Judah's bones rolled in their coffin all
  forty wilderness years — though he BROUGHT Benjamin — until
  Moses prayed: the conditional self-ban bound absent release.

### Ketubot 30a:5-7 (3)
- 30a:5 — MATERIAL. R. Nechunya ben HaKanah's rule: Yom Kippur as
  Shabbat for payments.
- 30a:6 — MATERIAL (docket). The reason (Abaye): נאמר אסון בידי
  אדם ונאמר אסון בידי שמים ("calamity said by the hand of MAN,"
  Exod 21:23, "and calamity said by the hand of HEAVEN" — "lest
  a calamity befall him," Gen 42:38): as one exempts payment, so
  the other — the verbal analogy on the two ason tokens.
- 30a:7 — MATERIAL. Rav Adda's challenge (perhaps Jacob feared
  lions and thieves — man's hand?) answered: Jacob warned about
  everything.

### Moed Katan 18a:3-5 (3)
- 18a:3 — MATERIAL. The frame: Shmuel's nail-episode — "is there
  no covenant cut to the lips?"
- 18a:4 — MATERIAL (docket). R. Yochanan: whence? — "sit here
  with the donkey, and I and the lad will go... and worship AND
  RETURN to you" (22:5) — ואיסתייעא מלתא דהדור תרוייהו ("and it
  came to pass: BOTH returned"): the spoken plural shaped the
  outcome.
- 18a:5 — context (hand-nails vs foot-nails — the practice rider).

### Avodah Zarah 53b:12-14 (3)
- 53b:12 — MATERIAL. The Mishnah: abandoned in PEACETIME —

- 53b:13 — MATERIAL (docket). Rav: בית נמרוד ("the HOUSE OF

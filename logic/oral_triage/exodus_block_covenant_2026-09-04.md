### Yoma 3b:12-4b:11 — the sequestering principle
- 3b:12-13 — Reish Lakish presses R. Yochanan's installation
  source (everything written there being indispensable) —
  MATERIAL frame.
- 3b:14 — THE SOURCE: "the glory dwelt on Sinai and the cloud
  covered him SIX DAYS, and He called to Moses on the SEVENTH"
  (24:16) — a BUILDING PRINCIPLE (בנין אב): whoever enters the
  camp of the Presence requires SIX days' sequestering —
  MATERIAL: the credited row; the High Priest's week derived
  from Moses' six.
- 3b:15 — the mishnah's seven = R. Yehuda ben Beteira's
  precaution — MATERIAL rider.
- 4a:8 — the in-this-shall-Aaron-come spending — context.

  rider.

  was COVERED in the cloud, was SANCTIFIED in the cloud, to
  receive the Torah for Israel IN SANCTITY — R. Yosei HaGelili:
  this was AFTER the ten utterances, opening the forty days —
  MATERIAL.
- 4a:11 — R. Akiva: the glory dwelt from the NEW MOON — MATERIAL:
  the second arm.
- 4b:1 — the covering's purpose: honor to Moses (the first

- 4b:5 — R. Akiva's count closing on the SEVENTEENTH OF TAMMUZ:

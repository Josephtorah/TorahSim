## Onkelos Exodus 24:1-18 — read IN FULL, 18 verses

- Onkelos Exod 24:5 — MATERIAL. נערי בני ישראל ("the YOUTHS of the
  children of Israel") rendered בּוּכְרֵי בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל ("the
  FIRSTBORN of the children of Israel") — the translation DECIDES who
  officiated at the covenant altar: the firstborn, the pre-Levitical
  priesthood; the offerings made נִכְסַת קוּדְשִׁין ("holy slaughterings")
  before the LORD.
- Onkelos Exod 24:10 — MATERIAL. "They SAW the God of Israel" rendered
  "they saw the GLORY (יְקַר) of the God of Israel," and under His feet
  becomes "under the THRONE OF HIS GLORY (כָּרְסָא יְקָרֵהּ)" — the
  vision double-buffered (glory-layer + throne inserted); the sapphire
  brickwork made "a work of PRECIOUS STONE," the heavens' clearness
  kept (לְבָרִירוּ).
- Onkelos Exod 24:11 — MATERIAL. "He laid not His hand on the nobles"
  rendered "there was NO INJURY (נִזְקָא) to the great ones"; "they
  beheld God, and ate and drank" rendered "they SAW the glory of the
  LORD and REJOICED IN THEIR OFFERINGS WHICH WERE ACCEPTED WITH
  FAVOR, AS IF eating and drinking" — the meal transposed to accepted
  sacrifice; the eating made a simile (כְּאִלוּ, 'as if') inside the
  received text.
- Onkelos Exod 24:1-2 — context-plus. "Come up BEFORE the LORD"
  (לִקֳדָם, the audience-preposition); the graded-nearness ladder
  rendered plainly (Moses alone approaches; they do not; the people
  do not ascend).
- Onkelos Exod 24:3 — MATERIAL. The two payloads distinguished: "all
  the WORDS (פִּתְגָמַיָא) of the LORD and all the JUDGMENTS (דִינַיָא)"
  — the narrative + the ordinances of this very parashah named as two
  deliverables; the people answer with ONE VOICE (קָלָא חַד).
- Onkelos Exod 24:7 — MATERIAL. ספר הברית = סִפְרָא דִקְיָמָא ("the BOOK
  OF THE COVENANT") read ALOUD before the people; נעשה ונשמע rendered
  נַעְבֵּד וּנְקַבֵּל ("we will DO and we will RECEIVE") — acceptance
  vocabulary, the receiving-verb the Targum uses for obedience
  throughout.
- Onkelos Exod 24:8 — MATERIAL. The blood thrown on the altar
  לְכַפָּרָא עַל עַמָא ("TO ATONE FOR THE PEOPLE") — the covenant blood
  glossed as atonement inside the verse; "the blood of the covenant
  which the LORD CUT (דִגְזַר) with you upon all these words."
- Onkelos Exod 24:12 — MATERIAL. The tablets-verse itemized: "the
  TABLETS of stone, and the TORAH and the COMMANDMENT which I have
  written לְאַלָפֵיהוֹן (TO TEACH THEM)" — the teaching-purpose made
  explicit in the grant itself.
- Onkelos Exod 24:14 — MATERIAL. "Whoever has WORDS" rendered "whoever
  HAS A CASE (מָן דְאִית לֵהּ דִינָא) shall APPROACH THEM" — Aaron and
  Hur installed as the standing COURT during the forty days; the
  judiciary continuity clause rendered as law.
- Onkelos Exod 24:4, 24:6, 24:9, 24:13, 24:15-18 — context (the
  twelve pillars for the twelve tribes, the basins' half-blood, the
  ascent roster, Joshua the attendant, the six days' cloud, the
  devouring-fire appearance ["like devouring fire" kept], the forty
  days and forty nights).

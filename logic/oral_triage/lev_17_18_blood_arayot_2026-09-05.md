## Sifra, Acharei Mot — Section 6 (8 rows)
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 6 1 — MATERIAL. OUTSIDE SLAUGHTER:
  Israel liable; GENTILES NOT LIABLE — and moreover PERMITTED to
  build an altar-platform anywhere and offer to Heaven. Converts
  and slaves included ("and say to them"); Aaron and his sons
  included (the raising-inside argument refused by the verse's own
  list); heads of the tribes included by the two-way verbal analogy
  on "this is the thing."
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 6 2 — MATERIAL. "This is the thing
  which the LORD commanded" — a building block: ALL the passages
  were said with "thus said."
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 6 3 — MATERIAL. THE CAMP GEOGRAPHY
  CARVE: "in the camp / outside the camp" resolved — liability
  attaches where the spot is unfit for ANY offering's slaughter;
  the south-of-the-altar slaughterer of a sin offering EXEMPT
  (fit for light offerings).
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 6 4 — MATERIAL. Bird slaughter LIABLE
  ("or who slaughters"); bird-pinching (מליקה, the inside rite) NOT
  — the a-fortiori refused by "who SLAUGHTERS."
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 6 5 — MATERIAL. Common animals
  slaughtered INSIDE not liable under this law — "an offering."
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 6 6 — MATERIAL. Temple-upkeep
  consecrations excluded — they never come to the tent door.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 6 7 — MATERIAL. The unfit list
  excluded (the mounted and mounting beast, the set-aside, the
  worshipped, the harlot's hire, the dog's price, the crossbreed,
  the torn, the side-born, over-age doves, permanent blemishes);
  and "to the LORD" excludes THE DISPATCHED GOAT — not designated
  to the Name on the altar.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 6 8 — MATERIAL. Time-deferred
  offerings excluded ("before the tabernacle" = fit NOW); R.
  Shimon: the deferred still stand under a plain prohibition.

## Sifra, Acharei Mot — Chapter 9 (9 rows)
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 9 1 — MATERIAL. "Ox, sheep, goat" —
  each from any source; "blood shall be reckoned TO THAT MAN" — not
  to his senders (agency refused); "he has shed blood" — ONE who
  slaughters is liable, TWO together are exempt.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 9 2 — MATERIAL. The two-slaughterers
  rule defended against the raising a-fortiori; "that man shall be
  cut off" — the individual, not the community; not the coerced,
  the erring, or the misled; "from among his people" — his people
  remain at peace.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 9 3 — MATERIAL. The WARNING for
  offering outside found at Deut 12 "guard yourself lest you offer."
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 9 4 — MATERIAL. The altar-platform
  eras split: consecrated in the ban era vs the permit era;
  "offers in the open FIELD" — every platform offering counts as
  field slaughter.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 9 5 — MATERIAL. "They shall bring
  them" — a positive command; the negative from "no more to the
  demons"; the platform-permitted classes derived: shelamim, then
  the burnt offering amplified.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 9 6 — MATERIAL. The classes bounded:
  R. Meir — whatever comes by vow and freewill (grain offerings and
  nazirite birds too); the Sages — ONLY burnt offerings and
  shelamim on a platform.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 9 7 — MATERIAL. THE PLATFORM
  DIFFERENCE TABLE: priestly throwing at the altar only — on a
  platform even converts, women, slaves serve; R. Meir — even a
  rock or stone raises liability as an altar; R. Yishmael — only a
  BUILT altar-form; and "pleasing fragrance" — NOT on a platform.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 9 8 — MATERIAL. שעירים (the hairy
  ones) = the DEMONS ("and demons dance there"); "after whom they
  stray" amplifies all foreign worship.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 9 9 — MATERIAL. "Eternal statute" —
  the eternal house; "shall be" — this era too; "this to them" —
  no karet for the platform offense itself; "for their
  generations" — binding down the line.

## Sifra, Acharei Mot — Chapter 10 (11 rows)
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 1 — MATERIAL. The inclusion frame
  for RAISING outside: Israel, converts, their wives, women and
  slaves.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 2 — MATERIAL. TWO WHO RAISE: R.
  Shimon — "any man, any man" includes two; R. Yosei — "THAT one"
  exempts two; each defends against the slaughter parallel.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 3 — MATERIAL. What counts: the
  burnt offering, then "sacrifice" amplifies the fat portions of
  all grades — and the BLOOD by "or sacrifice."
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 4 — MATERIAL. The handful, the
  frankincense, the incense, the priests' grain offering, the
  anointed priest's, the libation offering, the three logs of
  water and of wine — all in: "whatever comes to the tent door."
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 5 — MATERIAL. THE DISQUALIFIED
  raised outside — the lodged, the exited, the impure, the
  wrong-time and wrong-place slaughtered, received or thrown by
  the unfit, below-given-above, above-given-below,
  inside-given-outside, outside-given-inside, the misnamed
  Passover and sin offering — liable IF the class is receivable
  inside ("to perform it").
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 6 — MATERIAL. The cross-cases: R.
  Yosei the Galilean — slaughtered outside raised INSIDE liable;
  outside-outside exempt (he raised the disqualified); the Sages'
  rejoinder — inside-then-outside also became disqualified at
  exit.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 7 — MATERIAL. Rabbi and R. Elazar
  son of R. Shimon defend the asymmetry (a fit moment existed;
  the altar receives it); the impure eater parallel (R. Yosei the
  Galilean's carve and its refutation) recorded in full.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 8 — MATERIAL. Less than an
  olive-bulk of the burnt offering, the fat portions, the
  handful; less than three logs — EXEMPT: "it" = the whole.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 9 — MATERIAL. Only FIRE-WORTHY
  classes: the sin-offering flesh, guilt flesh, most-holy and
  light-holy flesh, the omer's remainder, the two loaves, the
  showbread, grain-offering remainders — all exempt ("burnt
  offering" — what is destined for the flames).
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 10 — MATERIAL. THE
  COMPLETION-OF-SERVICE RULE: the pourer, blender, breaker,
  salter, waver, presenter, table-arranger, lamp-tender, handful
  taker, blood receiver — all EXEMPT outside: only an act that
  COMPLETES a service ("who raises") is liable.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 10 11 — MATERIAL. Individual not
  community; one-vs-two (R. Yosei vs R. Shimon again); raised and
  raised again — per raising (R. Shimon) vs one liability (R.
  Yosei); the altar-top vs even-a-rock dispute rejoined.

## Sifra, Acharei Mot — Section 7 (7 rows)
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 7 1 — CONTEXT. The inclusion frame
  (Israel, converts, wives, women, slaves).
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 7 2 — MATERIAL. "Any man, any man" —

  woman by a gentile or a slave.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 7 3 — MATERIAL. WHICH BLOOD: R.
  Yehuda — lifeblood and exuded blood, common and consecrated,
  all from "any blood"; the Sages — karet only on LIFEBLOOD.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 7 4 — MATERIAL. "I will set My face"
  — "I turn (פונה) from all My affairs and deal with him"; the
  soul not the community; the EATER not the feeder; "from among
  her people" — her people at peace.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 7 5 — MATERIAL. "I have given it to
  you on the altar" — ONE gift atones after the fact; "to you" not
  for others; anywhere ON the altar atones — not on its ground;
  "for the blood atones BY THE LIFE" — exuded blood achieves
  NOTHING.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 7 6 — MATERIAL. "No soul of you shall
  eat blood" — WARN THE GREAT OVER THE SMALL (no feeding
  children); but the children not cut off through them.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 7 7 — MATERIAL. And the great not cut
  off through the small nor through other great — karet rides only
  one's own eating ("all who eat it shall be cut off").

## Sifra, Acharei Mot — Chapter 11 (12 rows)
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 1 — CONTEXT. The inclusion frame
  again for the hunt clause.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 2 — MATERIAL. Covering applies to
  the bought, inherited, gifted, and the self-trapped (geese and
  fowl) — "hunted" of any kind ("ציד, a catch — from any source");
  and Rabbi's CONDUCT RULE: the Torah taught דרך ארץ (proper
  conduct) — one eats flesh only by THIS PREPARATION, the hunt's
  effort.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 3 — MATERIAL. The kosher-species
  carve for the covered bird: likened to the beast that defiles
  garments, then re-carved by "which may be EATEN" — the impure
  bird excluded, the eaten-mother-bird kept.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 4 — MATERIAL. "Beast or bird" —
  each of any number: a hundred beasts in one place, ONE covering;
  beast and bird together, one covering; R. Yehuda — cover the
  beast's before slaughtering the bird; the rejoinder from "for
  the life of ALL flesh, its blood."
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 5 — MATERIAL. "And pour out" —
  excludes the slaughter that failed in his hand, the stabber, the
  wrencher; INCLUDES slaughter for gentiles' food and for dogs.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 6 — MATERIAL. Found torn,
  slaughtered for idolatry, common inside, consecrated outside,
  the stoned bird and beast: R. Meir — liable to cover ("poured...
  shall cover"); the Sages — only slaughter FIT FOR EATING is
  covered.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 7 — MATERIAL. Cover WITH WHAT HE
  POURED — and NOT WITH THE FOOT, "that the commandments not be
  despised upon him"; the pourer himself covers, not a delegate;
  yet all others also warned ("for the life of all flesh").
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 8 — MATERIAL. THE COVER STATE
  RULES: covered and re-exposed — exempt from re-covering; the
  WIND covered it — he is liable to cover (the act must be his).
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 9 — MATERIAL. ALL its blood —
  splashed blood and knife blood covered; R. Yehuda — only when
  no other blood is there.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 10 — MATERIAL. THE COVERING
  MATERIALS: not vessels or stones ("in dust"); the amplified
  list (ground stone, ground pottery, fine flax tow, fine
  sawdust, fine manure, fine sand, lime, brickdust, crushed
  stopper) vs the excluded (metal filings, flour, bran) — amplify
  then restrict, dust-kind only.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 11 — MATERIAL. Rabban Shimon ben
  Gamliel's CLOSING RULE: whatever GROWS PLANTS covers; what
  grows none, covers not — the class predicate replacing the list.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 11 12 — MATERIAL. "For the life of
  all flesh" as cause; warning the great over the great [the
  received emendation]; karet through neither small nor great.

## Sifra, Acharei Mot — Chapter 12 (15 rows)
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 1 — MATERIAL. The carcass-eating
  clause binds the covenant member — citizen and convert, NOT the
  resident alien ("as the citizen a son of the covenant, so the
  convert").
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 2 — MATERIAL. Eating = an
  olive-bulk minimum; "carcass" excludes beak, claws, wings,
  feathers, eggs.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 3 — MATERIAL. THE SWALLOW-HOUSE
  LAW: the bird carcass defiles garments NEITHER in the bowels NOR
  in the mouth — only at בית הבליעה (the swallow-house, the
  throat): "soul" = where the soul-house is.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 4 — MATERIAL. Nor on the way OUT
  (vomited) — "which EATS": by the way of eating it defiles.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 5 — MATERIAL. The BEAST carcass
  has no swallow-house rule — it defiles BEFORE eating (from Lev
  22:8's carve: that whose only impurity is its eating).
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 6 — MATERIAL. "By it" — by the
  bird carcass alone the garments defile at the throat; the
  a-fortiori for the beast refused.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 7 — MATERIAL. The IMPURE-species
  bird excluded: R. Yehuda — its ban is not "carcass"-based; the
  Sages — "carcass AND torn": a species that HAS a torn-state
  (i.e. is otherwise eatable) — the impure bird has none.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 8 — MATERIAL. R. Yehuda: "torn"
  is never redundant — alive it is not carcass, dead it is
  carcass; so "carcass" comes to EXCLUDE THE SLAUGHTERED.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 9 — MATERIAL. MIDDAH CASE LAW —
  DAYO AGAIN: R. Meir's a-fortiori (slaughter purifies the beast's
  torn-state; bird-pinching should purify the bird's) against R.
  Yosei — דיה (ENOUGH for it) to be like the beast carcass:
  slaughter purifies, pinching does not. The bounded-conclusion
  rule cutting an expansion, third exemplar on the walk.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 10 — MATERIAL. The inside-pinched
  bird excluded (it PERMITS the forbidden); the outside-pinched
  consecrated and any common pinched included — the
  permits-the-forbidden test.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 11 — MATERIAL. אחרים (the
  anonymous "Others") — the citizen-and-convert EQUALITY test:
  what binds both equally is in; inside-pinching binds unequally,
  out.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 12 — MATERIAL. Chullin inside and
  consecrated in-or-out slaughtered — excluded by "carcass AND
  torn: equal inside and outside"; then the garment amplification
  chain (large white, colored, small, the hairnet and cap) and
  the boundary — garments and vessels-as-garments defile, MAN and
  EARTHENWARE do not.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 13 — MATERIAL. So the eater with
  one hand on the oven and one on his fellow — BOTH STAY PURE.
  And THE EFFECT SPLIT: failing to wash the BODY = karet; failing
  to launder the GARMENTS = forty lashes — one verse, two organs,
  two sanctions.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 14 — MATERIAL. The whole clause
  speaks of SANCTUARY defilement — warning and punishment aligned
  with the offering-liability parallel.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 12 15 — MATERIAL. The Others' verbal
  analogy: "shall bear his iniquity" here as there — KARET.

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 8 5 — CONTEXT. R. Shimon: the guilty

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 8 6 — CONTEXT. The forty-seven years

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 8 8 — MATERIAL. "In their STATUTES

  as THEIR civil law.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 8 9 — MATERIAL. THE CANON CHANNEL,

  "My statutes" = the MIDRASHOT (the expositions); "you shall
  keep" = the MISHNAH; "to walk in them" = the DEED — and the deed

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Section 8 10 — MATERIAL. Keeping and doing
  given to statutes AND judgments both; "and LIVE by them" — TO
  THE WORLD TO COME ("in this world his end is death").

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 13 13 — MATERIAL. "AND LIVE BY THEM"

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 13 15 — MATERIAL. Approach and

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 13 16 — MATERIAL. "Defile not

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 13 17 — CONTEXT. "You keep them

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 13 18 — MATERIAL. "Do none of these

- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 13 20 — MATERIAL. "The SOULS that do

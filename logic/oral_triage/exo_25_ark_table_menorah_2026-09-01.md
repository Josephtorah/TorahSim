## Onkelos Exodus 25:1-40 — read IN FULL, 40 verses

- Onkelos Exod 25:2 — MATERIAL. ויקחו לי תרומה ("let them take Me an
  offering") rendered וְיַפְרְשׁוּן קֳדָמַי אַפְרָשׁוּתָא ("let them
  SET APART before Me a SEPARATION") — the gift defined by the
  separation-verb (the same vocabulary as the priestly dues): the
  donation is a formal act of setting-apart, and it is made בְּ״קֳדָמַי״
  ("BEFORE Me"), the audience-preposition, not "to" Me. The willing
  heart (דְּיִתְרְעֵיהּ לִבֵּיהּ — "whose heart is willing") kept as
  the sole trigger.
- Onkelos Exod 25:5 — MATERIAL (light). The תחש-skins rendered
  סַסְגּוֹנָא ("sasgona") — the untranslatable creature carried as a
  NAME, not identified; the received translation preserves the
  species-puzzle the Talmud itself debates.
- Onkelos Exod 25:8 — MATERIAL, the parashah's crown. ושכנתי בתוכם
  ("and I will DWELL among them") rendered וְאַשְׁרֵי שְׁכִנְתִּי
  בֵּינֵיהוֹן ("and I will make MY PRESENCE dwell AMONG THEM") — the
  indwelling routed through the שְׁכִנְתָּא ("Shekhinta," the
  Presence-layer), the same double-buffering (two layers held at once) exo_24 recorded for the
  vision; and the AMONG-THEM stands in translation as in the ink: the
  dwelling lands in the PEOPLE, not in the building the verse just
  commissioned. The sanctuary is made קֳדָמַי ("before Me").
- Onkelos Exod 25:9 — MATERIAL. וכן תעשו ("and SO shall you make") —
  the closing clause the case shelf reads as the CONSTITUTIONAL rule
  for all later sanctuary work: extensions of the city and the courts
  follow the shown pattern under full authority (the TESTING-shelf
  rows Mishnah Shevuot 2:2 + Mishnah Sanhedrin 1:5 anchor HERE at
  25:9 — king, prophet, and the 71 required; the 71-docket is already
  seated at EX18-14). The pattern itself: תבנית rendered דְּמוּת
  ("likeness") — Moses is SHOWN (מַחֲזֵי 'shown') a likeness, twice.
- Onkelos Exod 25:15 — MATERIAL, the parashah's first standing
  prohibition. לא יסרו ממנו ("they shall not be removed from it")
  rendered לָא יְעִדּוּן מִנֵּיהּ — the ark's poles NEVER leave their
  rings: a permanent law inside the architecture, the one carry-duty
  clause of the chapter.
- Onkelos Exod 25:22 — MATERIAL. ונועדתי לך שם ("and I will MEET with
  you there") rendered וֶאֱזַמֵּן מֵימְרִי לָךְ ("and I will APPOINT
  MY WORD for you there") — the meeting routed through the מֵימְרָא
  ("Memra," the Word-layer): the ark-cover between the two cherubim
  becomes the standing ORACLE INTERFACE — the fixed address from which
  every future command is spoken (יָת כָּל דִּי אֲפַקֵּד — "all that I
  shall command you to the sons of Israel").
- Onkelos Exod 25:30 — MATERIAL. לחם פנים ("bread of the Face")
  rendered לְחֵם אַפַּיָּא ('the bread of the face') … תְּדִירָא ("the bread of the FACE before
  Me CONTINUALLY") — the תדיר ('continual') continuity-token: the table carries a
  standing duty, not a one-time setting. TESTING-shelf pointers: the
  showbread's dimensions and exchange procedure are the case rows of
  Mishnah Menachot 11:4 + 11:5 + 11:7 on our 25:23-30.
- Onkelos Exod 25:31-36 — MATERIAL. מקשה ("hammered work") rendered
  נְגִיד ("DRAWN out" — beaten from one piece), and 25:36 closes the
  law: כֻּלַּהּ נְגִידָא חֲדָא ("ALL OF IT ONE DRAWN PIECE of pure
  gold") — the menorah's one-piece constraint held in translation;
  the six branches counted from its two sides (25:32) with the shaft
  the seventh. TESTING-shelf pointer: Mishnah Menachot 3:7 — the
  seven branches invalidate one another — is the case row on this
  very count.
- Onkelos Exod 25:37 — context-plus. The seven lamps lighting לָקֳבֵל
  אַפָּהָא ("toward its face") — the orientation clause.
- Onkelos Exod 25:40 — context-plus. וַחֲזֵי וַעֲבֵיד ("SEE and MAKE")
  according to their likeness דִּאַתְּ מִתְחֲזִי בְּטוּרָא ("which YOU
  ARE SHOWN in the mountain") — the shown-pattern clause repeated as
  the chapter's close.
- Onkelos Exod 25:1, 25:3-4, 25:6-7, 25:10-14, 25:16-21, 25:23-29,
  25:33-35, 25:38-39 — context (the materials list, the ark's
  dimensions and rings, the testimony deposited within, the cherubim
  facing each other over the cover, the table's vessels, the
  menorah's cups-knobs-flowers, the talent of gold).

## SPINE AMENDMENT, same day (owner: "you decide" — the delegated
## ruling): for the Mekhilta-silent stretch (Exod 25-40) the substitute
## spine is MIDRASH TANCHUMA (both recensions where they exist), beside
## the standing Onkelos. Primaries over anthologies. Applied to Terumah
## retroactively, append-only. Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 1-8 + Midrash
## Tanchuma Buber, Terumah 1-6 read IN FULL and attributed to this
## block (the curtain and altar sections to exo_26/exo_27).

- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 1 — MATERIAL, law opener (the yelamdenu
  form). מותר תרומה מה היו עושין בה ("the SURPLUS of the offering —
  what was done with it?") — gold leaf, plating for the Holy of
  Holies: the leftover-consecrated-funds law (the enumerated bare row
  Mishnah Shekalim 4:6 anchors at our 25:8). TWO offerings
  distinguished — the tabernacle's and the priests'; the priests'
  conditioned on Torah-learning (R. Yannai's line); the tabernacle's
  called by His own name (לי — "for Me").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 3 (end) + Buber 2 — MATERIAL, THE
  FLAGSHIP LAW FIND. Reish Lakish derives the FIVE WHO MAY NOT
  SEPARATE the offering word by word from OUR 25:2: חרש ("the
  deaf-mute") excluded from דבר אל בני ישראל ("SPEAK to the sons of
  Israel" — he neither hears nor speaks); שוטה ("the incompetent")
  from ידבנו לבו ("whose heart makes him willing" — his heart cannot
  volunteer); קטן ("the minor") from כל איש ("every MAN"); one who
  separates what is not his from מאת כל איש ('from every man') … תקחו ("from each
  man... TAKE" — from your own); the gentile separating an
  Israelite's from בני ישראל ("the sons of Israel"). The
  disqualification table of the terumah laws (Mishnah Terumot 1:1's
  own list) read token by token off this verse — the same
  word-by-word shape as the Noahide crown and the three-cohorts
  census.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 2 + 3 + Buber 1 — MATERIAL (light). The
  good-acquisition reading: ויקחו read with לקח טוב ("a GOOD TAKING I
  gave you" — Proverbs 4:2, the Torah): the traders' parable (goods
  exchanged leave one each; teachings exchanged leave two each), the
  scholar on the ship; and the point sharpened: "My Torah you took —
  TAKE ME WITH IT" (קחו אותי עמה) — the offering as taking the Giver.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 3 + Buber 3 — MATERIAL (light). The
  לי-census: everything Scripture calls "MINE" endures in this world
  and the coming one — the land, the firstborn, the Levites, and this
  offering; and the blessing-token: the whole donation arrived in TWO
  MORNINGS (בבקר בבקר, the doubled morning of 36:3 — a forward
  pointer to the Vayakhel ink).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 5 + Buber 4 — MATERIAL, a counted
  correspondence. R. Yehudah bar Simon: the offering-list's THIRTEEN
  items answer the THIRTEEN things done for Israel in Egypt (the
  garment-list of Ezekiel 16:10-13), and thirteen will be repaid in
  the world to come (Isaiah 4:5-6). The 25:3-7 list as a repayment
  ledger — the count is the midrash's own; the ink enumeration is
  checkable and grouping-dependent, carried dual-track.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 6 + Buber 5 + Buber 9 (end) — MATERIAL,
  recorded dispute. The תחש (tachash) creature: R. Yehudah — a real
  great pure beast of the wilderness, one horn, six colors; R.
  Nechemiah — מעשה נסים ("a miracle-work"), created for its hour and
  hidden away (proof: whence a thirty-cubit hide?). Pairs with the
  Onkelos sasgona row above — translation and midrash both carrying
  the species-puzzle open; the created-for-the-hour class joins the
  twilight-creation family (EX16-15's table).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 7 + Buber 6 — enrichment. The four
  materials as the four kingdoms (gold Babylon, silver Media, bronze
  Greece, red ram-skins Edom), the lamp-oil as the anointed king;
  Daniel's statue mapped alongside.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Terumah 8 — MATERIAL, the parashah's TIMESTAMP.
  אין מוקדם ומאוחר בתורה ("there is NO EARLIER-AND-LATER in the
  Torah"): this section was spoken to Moses ON THE DAY OF ATONEMENT
  itself — AFTER the calf, though written before it — and the
  purpose-clause follows: יבא זהב שבמשכן ויכפר על זהב שנעשה בו העגל
  ("let the GOLD of the tabernacle come and ATONE for the gold of
  which the calf was made"); hence the name משכן העדות ("the
  tabernacle of TESTIMONY") — testimony to all that the calf was
  forgiven. A recorded dispute between narrative order and event
  order, carried with the ordering doctrine named.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Terumah 1-6 — read in full; content
  parallel to the standard recension's 2-7 as noted above (the
  five-disqualified table, the same one, verbatim at Buber 2; the thirteen-count at
  Buber 4; the tachash at Buber 5; the kingdoms at Buber 6).

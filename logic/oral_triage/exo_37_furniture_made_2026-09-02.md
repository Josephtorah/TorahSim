## Onkelos Exodus 37:1-29 — read IN FULL, 29 verses

- Onkelos Exod 37:1-5 — MATERIAL, the block's head. וַעֲבַד בְּצַלְאֵל
  יָת אֲרוֹנָא ("and BEZALEL made THE ARK") — the ONLY vessel in the
  whole account carrying the craftsman's own name (every other
  clause an anonymous "he made"); the 2.5 × 1.5 × 1.5 cubit box,
  pure gold within and without, the gold rim, four cast rings, the
  poles inserted לְמִטַּל יָת אֲרוֹנָא ("to CARRY the ark").
- Onkelos Exod 37:6-9 — MATERIAL. The cover and its cherubim:
  נְגִיד עֲבַד יָתְהוֹן ("HAMMERED work he made them") — from the two
  ends OF THE COVER ITSELF (one piece, not attached); wings spread
  upward sheltering the cover, faces one to the other, inclined
  toward the cover — the meeting-point geometry executed to
  EX25-05's addressed spec (the call-site's furniture built).
- Onkelos Exod 37:10-16 — MATERIAL (spec re-run). The table 2 × 1 ×
  1.5, gold crown-rim, the handbreadth ledge with its own rim, four
  rings against the ledge as pole-houses; the vessels — dishes,
  incense-spoons, supports, and the half-open tubes דִּי יִתְנַסַּךְ
  בְּהוֹן ("by which the breads are covered") — the bread-stack
  hardware. Standing verdicts from the Terumah sitting credited on
  quick-look (same numbers, same vessel census).
- Onkelos Exod 37:17-24 — MATERIAL. The lampstand: נְגִיד ("hammered
  work") whole — base, shaft, cups, knobs, flowers מִנַּהּ הֲווֹ ("WERE
  OF IT," one piece); six branches, the almond-pattern cups (three
  per branch, four on the shaft), the knob under each branch-pair;
  seven lamps, tongs, scoops; כִּכְּרָא דְּהַב דְּכֵי ("a TALENT of pure
  gold") for the whole — the one-talent mass-budget line executed.
- Onkelos Exod 37:25-29 — MATERIAL. The incense altar 1 × 1 × 2
  cubits, horns of one piece, gold overlay and rim, two rings under
  the rim for the poles; and 37:29: the anointing oil and the
  incense made דְּכֵי עוֹבַד בּוּסְמָנוּ ("PURE, the work of the
  PERFUMER") — the protected formulas (EX30-06's karet-guarded
  recipes) manufactured by the licensed craft only.

## Midrash Tanchuma anchored in 37:1-29 — 40 sections

- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 6:1 (⇐ Exod 37:1) — MATERIAL. The
  creation frame: the world was made BEGINNING WITH LIGHT (He
  wrapped Himself in light as a garment, then stretched the heavens
  like a curtain — R. Samuel bar Nachman's answer); R. Judah and R.
  Nehemiah's recorded dispute on darkness-first vs world-first
  carried as a dispute.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 6:2 — MATERIAL. The king lit lamps in
  the dark place and THEN built the palace; "from the Holy One the
  righteous learned to BEGIN WITH LIGHT" — and so when Moses told
  Bezalel to build, what opened the work? THE ARK (the Torah's
  seat, the light).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 6:3 — MATERIAL. The reason stated:
  Moses said BEGIN WITH THE TABERNACLE; Bezalel began instead with
  the ark — because the ark is the place of the Torah, THE LIGHT OF
  BOTH WORLDS ("give to a wise man and he will be yet wiser").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 6:4 — MATERIAL (light). Noah's
  inference exhibit: commanded seven pairs of the clean, he DERIVED
  the offering license — the righteous extend commands by
  inference ("teach a righteous man...").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 6:5 (⇐ Exod 37:1) — MATERIAL, the
  block's dispute crown. THE BUILD-ORDER DIALOGUE VERBATIM: Bezalel
  asks WHAT IS THE TABERNACLE FOR; Moses — "that He may dwell in it
  and teach Torah to Israel"; Bezalel — "and WHERE WILL THE TORAH
  BE PLACED?"; Moses — "after we make the tabernacle we will make
  the ark"; Bezalel — "that is not the way: the ARK FIRST, then
  the tabernacle." The spine's recension makes Bezalel the
  ark-first (dependency-driven) scheduler AGAINST Moses'
  house-first order — the Babylonian Talmud (Berakhot 55a) records
  the ROLES REVERSED (there Moses orders vessels first and Bezalel
  argues house-first, "the custom of the world"); a genuine
  two-recension dispute over WHICH scheduling argument the famous
  exchange carried. Both tracks carried; the ink of chapter 36-37
  (structure executed before ark) sits beside the byline at 37:1.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:1 (⇐ Exod 37:1) — MATERIAL, the
  block's metaphysics crown. BEZALEL'S HANDS ONLY ON THE ARK: no
  other vessel carries his making — the rest were made בַּאֲמִירָתוֹ
  וּבַעֲצָתוֹ ("at his word and counsel") — because the ark is where
  the Holy One מְצַמְצֵם שָׁם שְׁכִינָתוֹ ("COMPRESSES His Presence
  there"), and the name says it: Bezalel made the SHADE (צֵל,
  "shadow") of God between the two cherubim ("and there I will
  meet with thee," Exod 25:22 — EX25-05's address).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:2 — MATERIAL. THE CAVE AND THE SEA
  (R. Joshua of Sikhnin in R. Levi's name): a cave at the sea's
  edge — the sea rushes in, THE CAVE FILLS, AND THE SEA LACKS
  NOTHING; so "the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle" (Exod
  40:34) while "His glory is above heaven and earth" — the
  compression-without-diminution doctrine, stated on the very verse
  where EX25-02's indwelling lands; and further: the Presence
  compressed even INTO THE ARK ("the ark of the covenant of the
  LORD OF ALL THE EARTH," Joshua 3:11).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:3 (⇐ Exod 37:2) — MATERIAL, numeric.
  R. Chanina of Sepphoris: THREE CHESTS — two of gold, one of wood;
  wood inside gold, gold inside wood, lips covered gold — executing
  "within and without thou shalt overlay it"; and the derived rule:
  a scholar's INSIDE MUST MATCH HIS OUTSIDE (תּוֹכוֹ כְּבָרוֹ, "his
  inside like his outside").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:4 — MATERIAL. The broken tablets
  ALSO in the ark ("thou shalt put them in the ark" — tablets and
  the fragments of tablets) → honor scholars poor and broken, the
  Torah is within them; and THE CROWNS: three (Torah, priesthood,
  kingship) with the crown of a GOOD NAME above them all.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:5 — enrichment. The ark beloved as
  the Throne of Glory; the fiery law at His right hand.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:6 — MATERIAL (light). The material
  decoded: acacia because the Torah is the TREE OF LIFE; gold
  because her words are "more to be desired than gold"; and the
  carrying paradox — they bear the ark, AND IT BEARS AWAY their
  sins (נָשָׂא, "carry/forgive", one root).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:7 — MATERIAL (light). R. Nathan: the
  ark aligned opposite the Throne of Glory; read not מָכוֹן
  ("foundation") but מְכֻוָּן ("directly opposite") — the
  above-below alignment law.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:8 — context. The cover for the
  seraphim-above; cherubim corresponding to heaven and earth.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:9 — enrichment. Cherub at each end
  as the heavens' treasure-doors; the tablets written on both
  sides.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:10 — enrichment. Faces one to
  another, opposite the Throne; "out of Zion, the perfection of
  beauty."
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:11 — MATERIAL (light). The whole
  tabernacle exists FOR the ark (the Presence within it); the
  ark's road-miracles — three days' scout, snakes and scorpions
  killed, thorns burned (Numbers 10:33's frame).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:12 — enrichment. The two sparks from
  between the cherubim; the nations' incense question ("who is this
  coming up like pillars of smoke"); rise-up-O-LORD at the
  journeys.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:13 — MATERIAL, numeric. THE CARRYING
  PROTOCOL: the Kohathites bear it; the veil borne before it on
  poles by two senior priests — a handbreadth thick, woven on
  SEVENTY-TWO strands, TWENTY-FOUR threads each, THREE HUNDRED
  priests to handle it; then the sealskin cover ON so that none SEE
  the ark — "they shall not go in to see the holy things as they
  are being covered, lest they die" (Numbers 4:20). Opened in full
  (numeric claims never credited unopened); the same veil-numbers
  the Buber text recites at 36:35 — the two recensions' tables
  verified against each other, agreeing on 72 × 24 and the 300.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:14 — MATERIAL (light). כְּבַלַּע ("as
  they are being covered") read by R. Levi as the BLINK: a glance
  into the ark for an eye-blink kills; the Philistines' returning
  cows knew what they carried and faced it.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 7:15 — enrichment. וַיִּשַּׁרְנָה read as
  song (the cows SANG); the Beth-shemesh seventy thousand who
  looked; wherever the Torah is, the Presence is with it.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 8:1 (⇐ Exod 37:1-2) — MATERIAL, the
  provisioning crown. SHITTIM FOR SHITTIM: it was revealed before
  Him-who-spoke that Israel would SIN AT SHITTIM (the place), so He
  ordained שִׁטִּים (acacia) in the ark — the atonement STOCKED
  BEFORE THE SIN; and the plural command decoded: for every vessel
  He said "THOU shalt make," for the ark וְעָשׂוּ ("THEY shall
  make") — ALL Israel commanded, so that no man can tell his fellow
  "I gave more to the ark, the Torah is more mine than yours";
  whence Torah = WATER: free, no shame in asking a lesser to teach
  you, no price ("come, buy without money").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 8:2 — MATERIAL. The desert grant: the
  Torah given in an OWNERLESS place — free to all comers; the
  convert who studies equals the high priest ("which if a MAN do" —
  not priest, Levite, or Israelite); Jethro's line become the
  scribes at the Temple gate; Shemaiah and Abtalion, descendants of
  Sisera, taught Torah in public.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 8:3 — MATERIAL, ink crown. THE
  WRITTEN-AND-READ RIM: R. Shimon bar Yochai's three crowns mapped
  to vessels — ark = Torah, incense altar = priesthood, table =
  kingship; and the rim's own spelling: written זָר ("stranger"),
  read זֵר ("crown") — worthy, it is his crown; unworthy, he is a
  stranger to it. An ink-level written-vs-read claim, opened in
  full.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 8:4 — MATERIAL (light). Only at the
  ark "upon it" — worthy of Torah, worthy of all; the
  charge-census verse (ark, table, lamp, altars) closing on the
  Torah-worker's rank.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 10:1 (⇐ Exod 37:1, 37:6) — MATERIAL.
  THE EXECUTED ORDER AS RUN: boards joined → goats-hair tent spread
  → veil hung before the ark → (the ark) → the gold cover on it —
  each stage prerequisite to the next.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 10:2 — MATERIAL, eyewitness row. R.
  Eleazar the son of R. Yose: "I SAW THE ARK COVER IN ROME, and on
  it drops of blood" — told there: from the Day of Atonement
  sprinklings — a tanna's first-person museum testimony to the
  vessel this chapter builds, carried as testimony.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 10:3 — MATERIAL. כַּפֹּרֶת decoded: it
  ATONES (מְכַפֶּרֶת) for Israel; then table (bread before the ark),
  then the lampstand TO LIGHT THE TABLE — "the practice of kings"
  (the palace protocol as the ordering rationale); and THE
  ATTRIBUTION LAW: did Bezalel personally make everything, that
  every clause says "Bezalel made"? — he GAVE HIS SOUL to the work,
  so the Holy One did not withhold his wage: HIS NAME ON EVERY
  STAGE.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 10:4 — MATERIAL (light). The Ezra
  parallel (Jonathan ben Asahel named first for giving his soul;
  the helpers helped) — the byline goes to the soul-giver.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 10:5 — enrichment. The reward
  promises: every tabernacle deed repaid; even the high priest's
  garments each atone.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 10:6 — MATERIAL (light). The
  reward-mapping table: goats-hair tent ↔ the cloud-canopy of Isa
  4:5; the cover ↔ "when I have forgiven thee"; the ark ↔ the
  world-to-come's light.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 10:7 — enrichment. The stored good for
  the righteous; light sown.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Vayakhel 7:1 — dup (light-first creation
  + king-lit-lamps + ark-first, std 6:1-6:2 merged). Opened;
  verified same doctrine with the same two parables.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Vayakhel 8:1 — dup (Noah's inference,
  std 6:4). Opened.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Vayakhel 8:2 — MATERIAL (light),
  Buber-only variant: "give to a wise man" applied to MOSES — given
  the Torah, he went and TAUGHT it and enhanced its words.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Vayakhel 8:3 — dup of the build-order
  dialogue (std 6:5) with the Buber wording: Moses answers "when we
  make a tabernacle we shall make an ark," AND HE BEGAN WITH THE
  ARK — same recension-track as the standard, the reversal vs the
  Babylonian Talmud noted above. Opened in full (dispute rows never
  credited blind).
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Vayakhel 9:1 (⇐ Exod 37:1) — MATERIAL,
  Buber-only crown. HE HEALS WITH THE WOUNDING INSTRUMENT: a king
  wounds with a knife and heals with a bandage; the Holy One heals
  WITH THE VERY THING THAT WOUNDED — bitter wood sweetened the
  bitter water at Marah, and the sin at Shittim is healed by
  shittim-wood in the ark ("through that in which you sinned, you
  shall be healed") — the same-instrument doctrine that the
  Vayakhel std 8:1 pre-provisioning row states from the other end.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Vayakhel 10:2 — dup (build order as run +
  the Rome eyewitness + kapporet-atones + kings' array, std
  10:1-10:3 merged). Opened; the Rome testimony verified word for
  word (drops of blood, Day of Atonement answer).
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Vayakhel 11:1 — dup (the attribution law
  + Jonathan ben Asahel, std 10:3-10:4). Opened.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Vayakhel 11:2 — dup (the reward mapping
  with the Buber pore'im/porea' wordplay — "though you REBEL
  against me I REWARD you," the rebel/repay pun). Opened; the pun
  is Buber-only, noted.
- Midrash Tanchuma Buber, Vayakhel 11:3 — colophon ("The End of
  Parashah Vayakhel"), not-bearing; recorded.

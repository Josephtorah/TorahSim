## Onkelos Numbers 1:1-19 (19 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Num 1:1 — MATERIAL. THE DATE STAMP. "And the LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting, בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ הַשֵּׁנִי בַּשָּׁנָה הַשֵּׁנִית ("on the first of the second month, in the second year") of their going out of Egypt" → בְּחַד לְיַרְחָא תִנְיָנָא בְּשַׁתָּא תִנְיֵתָא ("on the first of the second month in the second year") — literal, the day and the month and the year all named: the tape's FORWARD marker (Exod 40:17 the erection on the first of the FIRST month of this year; Num 9:1 the Passover command in the first month — a verse AFTER this one in the text and BEFORE it in time: the retrograde the tradition names at Pesachim 6b:7, "there is no earlier and later in the Torah"). בְּמַשְׁכַּן זִמְנָא ("in the tent of appointed time") for the tent of meeting — the standing rendering.
- Onkelos Num 1:2 — MATERIAL. THE IDIOM OF THE COUNT. שְׂאוּ אֶת רֹאשׁ ("lift the head") → קַבִּילוּ יָת חֻשְׁבַּן ("receive the sum") — the idiom flattened to accounting (the phrase's two Torah seats computed: here and 26:2, the two censuses of the book); "by their families, by their fathers' house" → לְזַרְעֲיַתְהוֹן ("by their seed-families"); לְגֻלְגְּלֹתָֽם ("by their skulls" — per head) → לְגֻלְגְּלַתְהוֹן (kept) — the count is of NAMES per head, "the number of names, every male": not a heap counted but names written (Onkelos: "in the number of names").
- Onkelos Num 1:3 — MATERIAL. THE THRESHOLD. מִבֶּן עֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה וָמַעְלָה ("from twenty years old and upward") — the ink's first threshold in the book (computed: twenty here, a MONTH at 3:15, thirty-to-fifty at 4:3); כָּל יֹצֵא צָבָא ("everyone going out to the host") → כָּל נָפֵק חֵילָא בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל ("everyone going out in the host in Israel"); "you shall count them by their hosts, you and Aaron" → תִּמְנוּן יָתְהוֹן ("you shall count them") — the count assigned to two.
- Onkelos Num 1:4 — MATERIAL. ONE MAN PER TRIBE. אִישׁ אִישׁ לַמַּטֶּה ("a man, a man for a tribe" — the distributive doubling) → גַּבְרָא גַבְרָא לְשִׁבְטָא (kept doubled); "each the head of his fathers' house" — the twelve aides named before the count.
- Onkelos Num 1:5 — MATERIAL. THE LIST OPENS. "These are the names of the men אֲשֶׁר יַֽעַמְדוּ אִתְּכֶם ("who shall stand with you")" → דִּי יְקוּמוּן עִמְּכוֹן ("who shall arise with you"); "for Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur" — the princes' order begins (computed: Reuben, Simeon, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin, Dan, Asher, Gad, Naphtali — Gad ELEVENTH here, THIRD in the count of 1:20-43, SIXTH in the camp of chapter 2).
- Onkelos Num 1:6 — CONTEXT. Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai — literal; the name-list continues.
- Onkelos Num 1:7 — CONTEXT. Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab — literal; the name-list continues.
- Onkelos Num 1:8 — CONTEXT. Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar — literal; the name-list continues.
- Onkelos Num 1:9 — CONTEXT. Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon — literal; the name-list continues.
- Onkelos Num 1:10 — CONTEXT. "For the sons of Joseph: for Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; for Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur" — Joseph counted as TWO tribes while Levi is left out: twelve stay twelve (Onkelos literal).
- Onkelos Num 1:11 — CONTEXT. Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni — literal; the name-list continues.
- Onkelos Num 1:12 — CONTEXT. Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai — literal; the name-list continues.

- Onkelos Num 1:15 — CONTEXT. Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan — literal; the name-list continues.
- Onkelos Num 1:16 — MATERIAL. THE WRITTEN AND THE READ. אֵלֶּה קריאי ("these are the CALLED of" — the written form, unpointed in the store) / קְרוּאֵי ("the summoned of" — the read form, the store's own pointed token): the stretch's ONE ketiv-qere pair (computed: the only unpointed token in Numbers 1:1-4:20, in the Tanakh DB and in the snapshot store alike — the store carries both tokens side by side, the written first) → מְעַרְעֵי כְנִשְׁתָּא ("the summoned of the congregation") — Onkelos renders the READ form; "the heads of the thousands of Israel" → רֵישֵׁי אַלְפַיָּא ("the heads of the thousands") — a title the register keeps.
- Onkelos Num 1:17 — MATERIAL. THE RUN OPENS. "And Moses and Aaron TOOK these men אֲשֶׁר נִקְּבוּ בְּשֵׁמֽוֹת ("who were designated by names")" → דִּי אִתְפָּרָשׁוּ בִּשְׁמָהָן ("who were specified by names") — the designating verb is the ROOT OF THE BLASPHEMER'S "he pronounced" (Lev 24:11, 24:16 — computed on the shared root: to pierce, to specify, to pronounce); here it names the princes (a REFERENCE by shared lemma, no inference drawn).
- Onkelos Num 1:18 — MATERIAL. THE PEDIGREES AND THE DATE REPEATED. "They assembled all the congregation בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ הַשֵּׁנִי ("on the first of the second month") and וַיִּתְיַֽלְדוּ ("they declared their pedigrees")" — the verb's ONE seat in the Tanakh (computed) → וְאִתְיַחֲסוּ ("and they were registered by genealogy") — the pedigree as an ACT of the count (the exam's Talmud: they brought their pedigree scrolls, Yevamot 54b — at Step 5); THE COMMAND'S DATE IS THE RUN'S DATE: 1:1 and 1:18 carry the same day — the census done on the day it was commanded.
- Onkelos Num 1:19 — MATERIAL. THE RUN CLOSES. "כַּאֲשֶׁר צִוָּה יְהוָה אֶת מֹשֶׁה ("as the LORD commanded Moses"), and he counted them in the wilderness of Sinai" → כְּמָא דִי פַקֵּיד יְיָ יָת משֶׁה וּמְנִנּוּן ("as the LORD commanded Moses, and he counted them") — the SPEC/RUN pair's formula (the exact clause at Num 1:19, Num 2:33, Num 3:42, Num 3:51; the wider "according to ALL that the LORD commanded" at Num 1:54, Num 2:34 — computed): the first of five commanded-and-done pairs in the portion.

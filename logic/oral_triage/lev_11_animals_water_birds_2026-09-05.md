## Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 — the land permission (11:1-3)
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 1 — MATERIAL. "To say TO THEM" — to
  the SONS, Elazar and Itamar (the dietary code's addressees
  include the surviving priests).
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 2 — MATERIAL. THE OSTENSIVE REGISTRY:
  Moses would HOLD each creature and SHOW Israel — "this you eat
  and this you do not" — stated for all four classes: the land
  beast (11:2), the water kinds (11:9), the birds (11:13), the
  swarmers (11:29). The type registry taught by exemplar display.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 3 — MATERIAL. The permission's
  history: Gen 9:3's "like green herb I gave you ALL" narrowed by
  Deut 14:4-5's enumerated ten.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 4 — MATERIAL. THE CROSS-BOOK DIETARY
  LEDGER assembled, each ban with its source verse: limb from the
  living (Gen 9:4), the sciatic sinew (Gen 32:33), the fat (Lev
  7:23), the blood (Lev 7:26), and slaughter required (Deut
  12:21's "you shall slaughter... and eat").
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 5 — MATERIAL. Hybrids covered —
  'chayah' from anywhere, 'behemah' from anywhere.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 6 — MATERIAL. "That is ON THE LAND" —
  the sea-raised excluded from this machine.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 7 — MATERIAL. And the sign system
  itself gated to land ("THIS... which is on the land" — you have
  none but on the land).
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 8 — MATERIAL. Class inclusion both
  ways: behemah within chayah, chayah within behemah (the Deut 14
  roster the proof).
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 9 — MATERIAL. THE FETUS: the shalil
  permitted through its mother's slaughter; the fetus that PUT
  OUT ITS HAND and drew it back — PERMITTED (not like born); put
  out its HEAD and drew it back — like BORN, forbidden ("akh"
  cuts it).
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 2 10 — MATERIAL. The calibrations: the
  nine-month live fetus in common animals; consecrated and
  tithe-offspring rows; the placenta included.

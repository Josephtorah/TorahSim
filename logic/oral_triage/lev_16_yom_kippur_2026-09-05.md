  = SHEVUT (rest-fences): no climbing trees, riding, swimming,
  clapping, dancing; and mitzvah shevut — no consecrating,
  valuating, banning, separating gifts, marrying, divorcing,
  refusing, releasing, levirate — the whole fence census; citizen,
  convert, their wives, women and slaves all included.

## Sifra, Acharei Mot — Chapter 8 (10 rows)
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 1 — MATERIAL. THE DAY ITSELF ATONES
  — "for on THIS DAY he shall atone": even without offerings, even
  without the goat. And THE FELLOW GATE: sins between man and God
  the day atones; between man and his fellow — NOT until he
  appeases his fellow.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 2 — MATERIAL. R. Elazar ben
  Azariah's derivation — "from all your sins BEFORE THE LORD you
  are purified": the before-the-LORD class alone.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 3 — MATERIAL. The FIVE AFFLICTIONS
  as law: eating, drinking, washing, anointing, sandals, marital
  relations — from shevut; "IT is a rest" — the day alone bears
  them all, the weekly Sabbath does not.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 4 — MATERIAL. Succession: the
  anointed, the many-garmented, ANY appointed priest atones —
  "who shall be anointed... whose hand shall be filled... the
  priest."
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 5 — MATERIAL. "In his father's
  place" — THE SON PRECEDES all, IF he fills his father's place;
  if not, another serves.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 6 — MATERIAL. MIDDAH CASE LAW — THE
  INDUCTION LIMIT'S SECOND EXEMPLAR: R. Yehuda — as a substitute
  priest is readied, a conditional wife is readied ("his house" =
  his wife); R. Yosei — "if so, THE MATTER HAS NO END" (אין לדבר
  סוף): the regress-stopping objection, the same form that stopped
  Munbaz at Tazria.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 7 — MATERIAL. The afternoon linen
  garments — sacred funds, the building block for all byssus wear.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 8 — MATERIAL. The FOUR-zone
  atonement census (innermost, hall, altar, courts) + priests,
  Israel, LEVITES equalized in one atonement — by the dispatched
  goat (R. Yehuda); R. Shimon's parallel: the bull's blood is to
  the priests what the goat's is to Israel, confession to
  confession.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 9 — MATERIAL. "ONCE a year" — this
  day atones WITHOUT its goats where no other day does; Passover,
  the Feast, Assembly, New Year each excluded in turn.
- Sifra, Acharei Mot, Chapter 8 10 — MATERIAL. "He did as the LORD
  commanded Moses" — the whole passage IN ORDER; Aaron dons
  greatness as one obeying the King's decree; Aaron hears from
  Moses as from the Holy One; the Malachi praise cited.

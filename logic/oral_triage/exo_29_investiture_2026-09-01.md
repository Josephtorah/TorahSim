## Onkelos Exodus 29:1-46 — read IN FULL, 46 verses

- Onkelos Exod 29:1 + 29:4-9 — MATERIAL, the recipe. וְדֵין
  פִּתְגָּמָא ("and THIS IS THE WORD/PROCEDURE you shall do for them")
  — the first PERSONNEL installation: immersion FIRST (וְתַסְחֵי
  יָתְהוֹן בְּמַיָּא — "immerse them in water," 29:4), then the
  dressing ORDER (tunic, robe, ephod, breastplate, belt, turban,
  diadem), the anointing oil poured on the head — and at 29:9 the
  office's own perpetuity: וּתְהֵי לְהוֹן כְּהֻנְתָּא לִקְיָם עָלָם
  ("and the priesthood shall be theirs as an EVERLASTING STATUTE").
  The fill-the-hand idiom again rendered as OFFER THEIR OFFERING
  (וּתְקָרֵב קֻרְבָּנָא 'and you shall bring near the offering' — 29:9, 29:29, 29:33, 29:35): a seven-day
  sacrament creating an office.
- Onkelos Exod 29:10 + 29:15 + 29:19-21 — MATERIAL. THE HANDS AND THE
  BODY IN THE RITE: וְיִסְמוֹךְ ('and he shall lean') … יָת יְדֵיהוֹן עַל רֵישׁ ("and they
  shall LEAN their hands on the head") — the leaning-rite's first
  corpus appearance, three times, Aaron AND his sons together; the
  blood-map on the persons — right EAR-TIP, right THUMB, right BIG
  TOE (29:20); and oil-plus-blood sprinkled on the garments
  themselves (29:21) — the man and his clothing consecrated as one
  unit.
- Onkelos Exod 29:12-14 + 29:16-18 — MATERIAL, the taxonomy boots.
  חַטָּאתָא הוּא ("it is a SIN-OFFERING," 29:14) with its whole
  protocol pre-installed: blood on the altar's horns BY THE FINGER,
  the rest poured at the BASE, the fats up, the flesh burned OUTSIDE
  THE CAMP; then עֲלָתָא הוּא ("it is a BURNT-OFFERING," 29:18) —
  whole on the altar. And THE ACCEPTANCE TRANSFORM: ריח ניחוח
  ("a pleasing fragrance") rendered לְאִתְקַבָּלָא בְרַעֲוָא ("TO BE
  ACCEPTED WITH FAVOR" — 29:18, 29:25, 29:41): the received
  translation de-anthropomorphizes the smelling into acceptance.
  Leviticus' vocabulary is installed here, before Leviticus.
- Onkelos Exod 29:22-28 — MATERIAL. The installation ram's
  wave-offering — אֲרָמוּתָא ("LIFTING," the tenufah) on the open
  hands of Aaron and his sons; the breast to Moses as his portion;
  and THE PERPETUAL DUES: breast and thigh לִקְיָם עָלָם ("an
  everlasting statute") from the sons of Israel from their
  peace-offerings — אַפְרָשׁוּתָא ("a SEPARATION," 29:28): the same
  separation-word as EX25-01's gift — the priestly-dues economy wired
  with the terumah vocabulary. TESTING-shelf pointer: Mishnah
  Menachot 5:6 (what requires waving) anchors at 29:27 in this
  exam's docket.
- Onkelos Exod 29:29-30 — MATERIAL. THE OFFICE TRANSFERS BY GARMENT:
  Aaron's sacred garments לִבְנוֹהִי בַּתְרוֹהִי ("to his sons AFTER
  HIM") — anointed and installed IN THEM; SEVEN DAYS the successor
  son wears them. Succession law wired into clothing: the garments
  are the office's continuity medium.
- Onkelos Exod 29:31-34 — MATERIAL. The sacred meal: cooked בַּאֲתַר
  קַדִּישׁ ("in a HOLY PLACE"), eaten by THOSE IT ATONED (דִּי
  יִתְכַּפַּר בְּהוֹן — "those through whom atonement was achieved");
  וְחִלּוֹנַי לָא יֵיכוּל ("and a COMMON MAN shall not eat, for they
  are holy") — the stranger-ban; leftover to morning BURNED, not
  eaten (the notar law repeating the Passover's 12:10 shape).
  TESTING-shelf pointer: Mishnah Pesachim 8:8 anchors at 29:33 in
  this exam's docket.
- Onkelos Exod 29:35-37 — MATERIAL, a crown. Seven days of
  installation and altar-atonement (a bull each day), the altar
  anointed and sanctified — becoming קֹדֶשׁ קוּדְשַׁיָּא ("most
  holy") — and then THE CONTAGION LAW: כָּל דְּיִקְרַב בְּמַדְבְּחָא
  יִתְקַדָּשׁ ("WHATEVER TOUCHES THE ALTAR BECOMES HOLY") — sanctity
  as a transfer function installed in one clause. The case shelf's
  scoping waits at Mishnah Zevachim 9:1 ("the altar sanctifies what
  is FIT for it") — testing-shelf pointer, not in this docket's bare
  rows.
- Onkelos Exod 29:38-42 — MATERIAL, the heartbeat. THE DAILY OFFERING
  INSTALLED: two yearling lambs לְיוֹמָא תְּדִירָא ("each day,
  CONTINUALLY"), one at morning, one בֵּין שִׁמְשַׁיָּא ("between the
  suns"), with the flour-oil-wine measures (the crushed oil כָּתִישָׁא
  again at 29:40) — עֲלָתָא תְדִירָא לְדָרֵיכוֹן ("a CONTINUAL
  burnt-offering for your generations"): the system's standing daily
  process, installed at the ordination's close.
- Onkelos Exod 29:42-46 — MATERIAL, the parashah's closing crown. THE
  SECOND ANSWER ADDRESS: דִּאֱזַמֵּן מֵימְרִי לְכוֹן תַּמָּן ("where I
  will APPOINT MY WORD for YOU there") — the same appointment-verb
  and Word-layer as EX25-05's ark-cover address, but at the TENT
  ENTRANCE, the daily offering's own location, and addressed to the
  PLURAL — extended at 29:43 to the sons of Israel, וְיִתְקַדַּשׁ
  בִּיקָרִי ("and it shall be sanctified with My GLORY"). Then the
  purpose clause: וְאַשְׁרֵי שְׁכִנְתִּי בְּגוֹ בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל ("I
  will REST MY PRESENCE among the sons of Israel") — and the exodus'
  own stated purpose: brought out of Egypt לְאַשְׁרָאָה שְׁכִנְתִּי
  בֵּינֵיהוֹן ("TO REST MY PRESENCE AMONG THEM," 29:46) — EX25-02's
  among-the-people crown confirmed by the parashah's last verses: the
  exodus was FOR the indwelling.
- Onkelos Exod 29:2-3 + 29:11 — context (the unleavened breads,
  loaves, and wafers of fine wheat flour in one basket; the
  slaughter before the LORD at the Tent entrance).

## Midrash Tanchuma Tetzaveh — the ordination sections attributed to
## this block, read IN FULL (standard 9-13; Buber 7-10, closing "the
## end of Parashat Tetzaveh")

- Midrash Tanchuma, Tetzaveh 10 (with Buber 10's purchase clause) —
  MATERIAL, the block's crown. WHY AARON WAS ELEVATED: the calf
  background — Hur killed for rebuking the mob (proved from 24:14's
  Hur never appearing again), Aaron acting from FEAR (וירא read as
  ויירא — "and he FEARED"); the priesthood grant is a PUBLIC AMNESTY
  ANNOUNCEMENT — "this is the thing you shall do... to hallow them,"
  to make known that nothing is held against Aaron. The species
  keyed to the sin: the BULL atones for the CALF (the gold-for-gold
  atonement's sibling — EX25-09's pattern at the animal register);
  the TWO RAMS answer his two sons destined to die (all four
  deserved death — Deuteronomy 9:20 — Moses' prayer saved two). And
  Buber 10's ownership clause: the bull taken בְּמִקָּח וְלֹא מִן
  הַגָּזֵל ("by PURCHASE and not from PLUNDER") — the
  stolen-offering ban stated at the ordination itself.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Tetzaveh 13 + Buber 9 — MATERIAL. THE LI-FOREVER
  TOKEN: R. Mana in R. Eliezer's name — כָּל מָקוֹם שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר לִי
  ("WHEREVER Scripture says LI, 'for Me'"), it stands FOREVER — and
  Buber 9:1 gives the full census: the firstborn, the silver and
  gold, the kingship, the land, Israel, the Levites, and THE
  PRIESTHOOD (לְכַהֵן לִי 'to serve Me as priest' — our 29:1) each with its verse. The
  token-law behind the everlasting-statute renderings. Riding: the
  EXCLUSIVITY clause (to THEM and not to others, this tribe and no
  other) and the purity curriculum ("he began teaching them how they
  purify, immerse, and eat in holiness"); the mound arrangement
  (ram-bull-ram = the three patriarchs); matzah in Sarah's merit.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Tetzaveh 13 (the consequence table) — MATERIAL.
  KIND BLESSES KIND: while the offerings ran, each species offered
  blessed its species (the seah yielding multiple measures); and the
  cessation table — Haggai 1:6 and Habakkuk 3:17 mapped clause by
  clause: sown-much-brought-little = the showbread ceased;
  drink-not-filled = the wine libation; CLOTHED-AND-NOT-WARM = THE
  PRIESTLY GARMENTS ceased; wages-into-a-holed-bag = the daily
  offerings; the olive's labor failed = the LIGHT OIL. The garments
  and the daily offering carried as load-bearing in the world's
  economy — with Mishnah Sotah 9:12 quoted inside the spine ("since
  the Temple was destroyed there is no day without its curse"). The
  altar-name acrostic rides (pardon-memory-blessing-life — the
  Terumah 10 family, credited).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Tetzaveh 9 — MATERIAL (light). The succession
  frame for 29:29-30: Joshua INHERITED honor from Moses — the
  point-by-point parallel table (shoes off, the song, the sun
  halted, the altar built, the Torah written) — and THE
  STUDENT-BEFORE-MASTER LAW: Joshua's ten years shortened for the
  ten words spoken before his teacher (Numbers 11:28) — whoever
  rules in his master's presence, his years are shortened. The
  office-transfer claim's spine rider. Closing: "the honor of the
  wise — this is Aaron and his sons, through whom the priesthood was
  established."
- Midrash Tanchuma, Tetzaveh 11-12 + Buber 7-8 — context-plus,
  read: the Sinai crowns (sixty myriads of angels, two per
  Israelite — armor and crown), the nations' shame from Sinai
  (the chorev wordplay); Dathan and Abiram named as the
  manna-leftover sinners (the worms from THEIR tents — exo_16's
  ledger already carries the leftover law; enrichment); the
  word-stands-forever frame (Abraham's furnace — God Himself
  descended, the angels deferred to Chananiah, Mishael, and Azariah)
  applied to the priesthood-word. Buber 7-8 dup-credited against
  the standard.

## Mishnah Kilayim 1 — the kind table (seeds, trees, beasts); grafting; the minimum act (9 rows)
- Mishnah Kilayim 1:1 — LAW. wheat and darnel are NOT kilayim with each other; barley and oats, spelt and rye, beans and chickpeas, purkedan and tofeach, white beans and haricots — not kilayim. THE KIND TABLE: 19:19's 'kilayim' (a mixture of two kinds) presupposes a KIND predicate the ink never defines — the pairs that count as one kind are the data channel.
- Mishnah Kilayim 1:2 — LAW. cucumber and melon — not kilayim (R. Yehuda: kilayim); lettuce and wild lettuce, endive and wild endive, leek and wild leek, coriander and wild coriander, mustard and Egyptian mustard, the Egyptian gourd and the bitter gourd, the Egyptian bean and the carob — not kilayim. The cultivated and wild forms of one species = ONE kind; R. Yehuda's dissent carried.
- Mishnah Kilayim 1:3 — LAW. turnip and rape, cabbage and cauliflower, beet and orach — not kilayim; R. Akiva added garlic and wild garlic, onion and wild onion, lupine and wild lupine. The kind table's recorded additions (data).
- Mishnah Kilayim 1:4 — LAW. trees: pears and crustumenian pears, quinces and hawthorn — not kilayim; the apple and the crab-apple, the peach and the almond, the jujube and the lote — THOUGH THEY RESEMBLE ONE ANOTHER, kilayim with each other. THE SIMILARITY TEST REJECTED: resemblance is not kind — the grafting ban of Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 4 17 runs on this table.
- Mishnah Kilayim 1:5 — LAW. radish and rape, mustard and charlock, the Greek gourd with the Egyptian and the bitter — though similar, kilayim. The same rule for seeds.
- Mishnah Kilayim 1:6 — LAW. THE BEAST TABLE: the wolf and the dog, the village dog and the fox, goats and gazelles, ibex and ewes, the horse and the mule, the mule and the donkey, the donkey and the wild ass — though similar, KILAYIM WITH EACH OTHER. 19:19's 'your beast you shall not breed as a mixture' graded on pairs; Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 4 15 (beast-on-wild, pure-on-impure all included from 'My statutes').
- Mishnah Kilayim 1:7 — LAW. no bringing tree on tree, vegetable on vegetable, tree on vegetable, vegetable on tree (R. Yehuda permits vegetable on tree). Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 4 17 VERBATIM — GRAFTING banned all three ways from 'My statutes you shall keep': the field verb's reach beyond sowing.
- Mishnah Kilayim 1:8 — LAW. no planting vegetables in a sycamore stump; no grafting rue on white cassia (vegetable on tree); no fig shoot in the squill to shade it; no vine branch thrust into the melon (tree on vegetable); no gourd seed in the mallow (vegetable on vegetable).

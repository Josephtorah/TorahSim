## The Talmud, per gap (motion 4 — thirteen segments opened)
- Bava Kamma 83b:10 — MATERIAL. THE JUXTAPOSITION LEVER, verbatim:
  "Which 'strikes'? If you say מַכֵּה בְהֵמָה יְשַׁלְּמֶנָּה
  וּמַכֵּה אָדָם יוּמָת (strikes a beast — pays; strikes a man —

  there shall be for you) — מִשְׁפָּט הַשָּׁוֶה לְכוּלְכֶם (a law
  EQUAL for all of you)." One-law read as EQUAL-ACROSS-BODIES:
  only money equalizes; the literal reading fails the span's own

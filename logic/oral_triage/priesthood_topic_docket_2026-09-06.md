- Mishnah Terumot 6:2 — LAW. an Israelite woman who ate terumah and then married a priest — terumah no priest had acquired, she pays principal and fifth TO HERSELF; terumah a priest had acquired — principal to the owner and the fifth to herself, 'for they said: the erring eater pays the principal to the owner and the fifth to whomever he wishes'.

## Sifra, Behar — Section 1 (9 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Section 1 1 — MATERIAL. THE SINAI-DETAIL RULE: why
  is the release named beside Sinai when every commandment was
  given there? As the release's GENERALITIES AND DETAILS were stated
  at Sinai, so were EVERY commandment's generalities and details —
  the chapter's opening frame is a rule about the whole code's
  provenance, read off one verse's place-name.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 1 2 — MATERIAL. THE ONSET CONDITIONS: "when
  you come to THE land" — the designated land, not Transjordan, not
  Ammon and Moab ("which I give you"); conquered but not divided,
  divided to families but not to fathers' houses, divided but no one
  yet knows his portion — NOT yet obligated: "your field," "your
  vineyard" — each must RECOGNIZE his own. The obligation's trigger
  is individual recognition of a plot, not the crossing.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 1 3 — MATERIAL. THE ONSET TIMELINE, computed
  from the crossing: at the Jordan — dough offering, orlah, new
  grain; 16 Nisan — the omer; fifty days on — the two loaves;
  FOURTEEN YEARS — tithes, and the count for the release begins;
  year TWENTY-ONE — the first release; year SIXTY-FOUR — the first
  Jubilee. A dated schedule the machine can run.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 1 4 — MATERIAL. THE LABOR CENSUS opens: "the
  land shall rest" — not from digging pits, cisterns, caves, or
  fixing ritual baths; "your field you shall not sow, your vineyard
  you shall not prune" — sowing and pruning named; plowing, hoeing,
  weeding, cutting, splitting from the doubled "your field NOT" and
  "your vineyard NOT" — every labor of the field and vineyard.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 1 5 — MATERIAL. More of the census:
  fertilizing, clearing stones, smoking the leaves, dusting — from
  "your field NOT"; pruning shoots, trimming, lopping trees — from
  "your vineyard NOT."
- Sifra, Behar, Section 1 6 — MATERIAL. THE CLASS RULE: loosening
  under olives, filling the holes beneath them, making basins
  between trees — PERMITTED: sowing and pruning were inside the
  class and went out to teach it — only what is LABOR IN THE LAND
  AND IN THE TREE is banned (the "went out to teach" middah). And
  the Jubilee year does NOT count in the seven-year cycle: "six
  years sow, six years prune" — the sown years count, the Jubilee
  does not.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 1 7 — MATERIAL. Rice, millet, poppy, sesame
  that ROOTED before the New Year — gathered in the seventh: "you
  shall gather its produce" — in the seventh. The rooting threshold.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 1 8 — MATERIAL. Not rooted — no: "six years
  sow... and gather" — SIX sowings and SIX gatherings, not six
  sowings and seven gatherings. Arithmetic of the verse's pairing.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 1 9 — MATERIAL. R. Yonatan b. Yosef: grain
  that reached A THIRD before the New Year — gathered in the
  seventh. The one-third threshold: a quantity, the DATA channel.

## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 1 (11 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 1 — MATERIAL. THE THIRTY-DAY ADDITION:
  thirty days before the New Year count as the whole year ("in the
  seventh year a sabbath of rest for the land"); from here: the
  three-year figs (בנות שוח, benot shuach, "sycamore-figs") have
  their seventh in the SECOND year after, since they ripen in three
  years; R. Yehuda: the Persian figs in the year after; they said:
  only the three-year figs. A produce-lag rule keyed to ripening time.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 2 — MATERIAL. "A sabbath to the LORD" — as
  said of creation's Sabbath, so of the seventh year; the doubled
  clause: every labor of field and vineyard.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 3 — MATERIAL. THE CHANGED-MANNER RULE: "the
  aftergrowth of your harvest you shall not reap" — the Sages'
  support for the aftergrowth ban; "the grapes of your separated
  vine you shall not gather" — from the GUARDED you do not gather,
  from the OWNERLESS you do; "you shall not gather" — not in the
  harvesters' manner: figs not with the fig-knife but in a ruin,
  grapes not in the winepress but in a trough, olives not in the
  press and beam but crushed into a small press (R. Shimon: even
  ground in the press, then into the small press). Consumption
  permitted, commerce-style processing barred.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 4 — MATERIAL. "A year of rest for the
  land" — once the seventh ends, labor on the TREE'S BODY is
  permitted though its fruits are still seventh-year; the fruits
  forbidden until the fifteenth of Shevat. Two clocks: the land's
  and the fruit's.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 5 — MATERIAL. "The sabbath of the land
  shall be for you" — from what RESTS you eat, not from the GUARDED;
  from here: a field improved — Beit Shammai: its fruits not eaten;
  Beit Hillel: eaten; seventh-year fruit eaten as a favor — Beit
  Shammai no, Beit Hillel yes; R. Yehuda REVERSES the attributions
  — a lenient Shammai, a strict Hillel. Dispute with a recorded
  attribution dispute above it.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 6 — MATERIAL. "For you" — not for others;
  "for eating" — not for meal offerings, not for libations; "for
  you, your slave, your maidservant" — the RICH owners and their
  slaves eat too, against "the poor of your people shall eat" (Exod
  23:11); so why the poor? R. Yehuda: the poor eat AFTER THE
  REMOVAL, not the rich; R. Yosei: both after the removal. The
  removal's eater class disputed.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 7 — MATERIAL. Fit for man to man, fit for
  beast to beast; "your hireling and your resident" — from the
  gentiles; "sojourning with you" — the lodgers.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 8 — MATERIAL. THE FIELD-CLOCK (the removal
  trigger): "your beast and the wild animal" — the a-fortiori (the
  beast in your charge surely eats if the wild does) refused because
  it would let the beast eat FOREVER; the verse likens beast to
  wild: as long as the wild eats in the field, the beast eats at
  home; GONE from the field for the wild — remove from the house
  for the beast. The house's stock is keyed to the field's state.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 9 — MATERIAL. "Which is in your land" —
  what is in your land you eat, not what Aquila shipped to his
  slaves in Pontus; R. Shimon: to Syria yes, abroad no. The export
  ban with its border.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 10 — MATERIAL. "Shall be" — even for the
  lamp, even for dye; "all its produce" — only what is PRODUCE is
  eaten; from here THE RIPENESS STAGES: unripe figs — when they
  glisten, eat with bread in the field; ripened — bring home.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 1 11 — MATERIAL. THE SEASON TABLE: grapes —
  when the sour grape drips, eat in the field; softened — home;
  olives — three log a seah: a quarter reached — crack and eat in
  the field; half a log — crush and anoint in the field; a third —
  crush and bring home; every other tree fruit: its TITHE season is
  its seventh-year season; "to eat" — not sprinkling, not a
  poultice, not a plaster, not an emetic. The quantities are DATA;
  the tithe-season import is the rule.

## Sifra, Behar — Section 2 (5 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Section 2 1 — MATERIAL. "You shall count" — IN THE
  COURT; seven sabbaths of YEARS, not days; not seven consecutive
  releases then a Jubilee — "seven years seven times": until both
  verses are said we would not know. The two-verse pin on the count.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 2 2 — MATERIAL. THE DEPENDENCY DISPUTE:
  count the sabbatical years; count the Jubilee years; R. Yehuda —
  do the seventh though there is no Jubilee, do the Jubilee though
  there is no seventh; the Sages — the seventh applies without the
  Jubilee, the Jubilee ONLY WITH the seventh. Two modules, coupled
  one way or none.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 2 3 — MATERIAL. THE SHOFAR EXPORT: "you shall
  pass a shofar" — the Jubilee; the New Year? "in the seventh month"
  — unneeded for Yom Kippur's date, so it teaches: the seventh
  month's teruah is LIKE THIS ONE — with a shofar. The source side
  of the moadim engine's imported instrument (Sifra Emor Section 11
  6 was the receiving side).
- Sifra, Behar, Section 2 4 — MATERIAL. A plain blast BEFORE the
  teruah ("a shofar of teruah") and AFTER ("you shall pass a
  shofar") — and to the New Year by the same token. The
  three-part blast structure.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 2 5 — MATERIAL. "On the day" — not at night;
  "on Yom Kippur" — even on the Sabbath; "pass a shofar throughout
  your land" — EVERY INDIVIDUAL obligated; the Jubilee blast
  overrides the Sabbath everywhere, the New Year's only in the
  court ("on the tenth" unneeded — so it limits).

## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 2 (5 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 2 1 — MATERIAL. THE TEN-DAY WINDOW: "sanctify
  the fiftieth year" — sanctified from the New Year, not the tenth;
  R. Yochanan b. Beroka: slaves were not released to their homes nor
  fields returned — they ate, drank, rejoiced, crowns on their heads
  — UNTIL Yom Kippur; the shofar sounded, fields returned, slaves
  went home. A year-timer whose release fires ten days in.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 2 2 — MATERIAL. "Proclaim דרור (deror,
  'liberty')" — liberty is FREEDOM; R. Yehuda: as one who dwells
  anywhere and trades in any province — freedom of movement and
  trade as the word's content.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 2 3 — MATERIAL. THE JUBILEE'S PRECONDITION:
  "to all its inhabitants" — when its inhabitants are ON it, not
  when exiled; present but MIXED (Judah in Benjamin's lot) — not;
  when Reuben, Gad, and half Manasseh were exiled, the Jubilees
  CEASED. A state gate on the whole population's placement.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 2 4 — MATERIAL. THE VALIDITY CONDITIONS
  DISPUTE: R. Yehuda — a Jubilee even without the release of land or
  the shofar, but NOT without sending the slaves ("it"); R. Yosei —
  even without sending the slaves, but NOT without the shofar; his
  two reasons: a Jubilee without sending is possible, without the
  blast impossible; and the blast depends on the COURT, the sending
  on EVERY MAN. A module's validity keyed to one of two acts.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 2 5 — MATERIAL. "Return each to his holding"
  — R. Eliezer b. Yaakov: the PIERCED slave two or three years before
  the Jubilee, whom the Jubilee frees (the self-seller and the
  court-sold are stated elsewhere); "to his family" — R. Meir: to
  what his family held; R. Yehuda: to his holding and family, NOT to
  his former office (so too the exile); "you shall return" — the
  woman included.

## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 3 (6 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 3 1 — MATERIAL. Jubilee holiness runs ONLY
  to the next New Year — not extended to Yom Kippur at its close as
  one might add from common to holy: "the fiftieth year shall be for
  you" — the timer's end is the year's end.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 3 2 — MATERIAL. Not sow, not reap the
  aftergrowth, not gather the separated — as said of the seventh,
  so of the Jubilee: the labor ban imported whole.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 3 3 — MATERIAL. THE SUBSTITUTION CHAIN: "for
  it is holy" — as consecrated property SEIZES its payment, so the
  seventh-year fruit seizes its price; but unlike consecrated
  property it does not exit to common ("it" — stays in its holiness):
  THE LAST IS SEIZED AND THE FRUIT ITSELF STAYS FORBIDDEN — meat
  bought with seventh-year fruit: both removed; fish with the meat:
  meat out, fish in; oil with the fish: fish out, oil seized. A
  status that transfers forward along a chain without leaving its
  source.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 3 4 — MATERIAL. "From the field you shall
  eat its produce" — while you eat from the field you eat from the
  house; gone from the field — finish from the house (the
  field-clock restated at the Jubilee).
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 3 5 — MATERIAL. Three pickled kinds in one
  jar — R. Eliezer: one kind gone from the field, remove the whole
  jar; R. Yehoshua: eat until the last is gone; Rabban Gamliel:
  remove each kind as its kind is gone — THE RULING; R. Shimon: all
  vegetables one for removal; purslane eaten until the Beit Netofa
  ones are gone. A four-way dispute with the ruling recorded.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 3 6 — MATERIAL. THE TWO RELEASES MATRIX: "in
  this Jubilee year" — the Jubilee frees SLAVES, the seventh does
  not; the a-fortiori each way REFUSED by the two texts: "this is
  the matter of the release" (Deut 15:2) — the seventh releases
  MONEY not slaves, the Jubilee frees SLAVES not money. "Return each
  to his holding" — the son who redeemed the field his father sold:
  it returns to the FATHER at the Jubilee.

## Sifra, Behar — Section 3 (10 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 1 — MATERIAL. THE OVERREACHING DOMAIN:
  sell only to your fellow, buy only from your fellow; movables
  included by "sale"; NO overreaching for LAND — "or buy FROM THE
  HAND of your fellow... do not wrong": what passes hand to hand has
  overreaching, land does not.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 2 — MATERIAL. No overreaching for SLAVES —
  likened to a holding ("inherit them as a holding," 25:46).
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 3 — MATERIAL. None for CONSECRATED property
  ("his brother" — not the sanctuary); none for DOCUMENTS ("sale" —
  what is itself sold and bought; documents only carry evidence) —
  but documents sold to a spice-dealer as paper DO carry it. The
  four exclusions with the counter-case.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 4 — MATERIAL. "Do not wrong each his
  brother" (25:14) = MONETARY wronging; 25:17 carries the VERBAL
  wronging. The two verbs assigned by verse.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 5 — MATERIAL. THE SIXTH: how much is
  overreaching — four silver of the twenty-four to a sela, A SIXTH
  of the purchase; the return window: until he can show it to a
  merchant or an expert; R. Tarfon at Lod ruled EIGHT silver, a
  THIRD, and the merchants rejoiced — until he said the whole day is
  the window, and they returned to the Sages. A parameter with its
  recorded setting and a recorded rival setting.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 6 — MATERIAL. Buyer and seller both have
  it; the merchant like the layman (R. Yehuda: not the merchant);
  THE WRONGED PARTY HAS THE UPPER HAND: "give me my money" or "give
  me what you overcharged."
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 7 — MATERIAL. The worn coin: R. Meir —
  four issar to the dinar; R. Yehuda — four pundion; R. Shimon —
  eight pundion. Three settings of one parameter.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 8 — MATERIAL. Return window for the coin:
  cities — until shown to a money-changer; villages — until the
  Sabbath eves (market rhythm); if he recognizes it, accepts it even
  after twelve months with only a grievance; gives it to second
  tithe without fear — an ill will only.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 9 — MATERIAL. "Each his brother" — a
  woman too, either direction; R. Yehuda: merchant to layman has it,
  layman to merchant not; "his brother" — in any case. Both parties
  and both directions.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 3 10 — MATERIAL. THE TWO-YEAR FLOOR: sold in
  the Jubilee year itself — may not redeem in less than TWO years
  ("years... you shall buy"); "after the Jubilee" — near it; far from
  it — "according to the many years increase, the few decrease";
  "years of PRODUCE" — a blight year, a mildew year, the seventh do
  NOT count; fallowed or left uncultivated DO; R. Elazar: sold before
  the New Year full of fruit — he cannot demand it back full: "a
  number of HARVESTS he sells you" — sometimes three harvests in
  two years.

## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 4 (rows 1-7 of 9 — the remaining two rows,
## on 25:23-24, are read in the redemption ledger)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 4 1 — MATERIAL. "Do not wrong each his
  fellow" (25:17) — VERBAL wronging, the monetary already stated at
  25:14 (the mirror of Section 3 4).
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 4 2 — MATERIAL. THE HEART CLAUSE RULE: the
  verbal cases — to a penitent "remember your former deeds"; to a
  convert's son "remember your fathers' deeds"; to the sufferer
  burying his children, Job's friends' words; donkey-drivers seeking
  grain sent to one who never sold wheat; R. Yehuda: not asking a
  price without meaning to buy; and if you say "I meant good
  counsel" — the matter is GIVEN TO THE HEART, and of everything
  given to the heart it says "you shall fear your God." A meta-rule:
  the fear clause marks unobservable-intent cases.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 4 3 — CONTEXT. Statutes and judgments each
  with keeping and doing; "dwell on the land" — not exiled;
  "securely" — not frightened.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 4 4 — ENRICHMENT. "Eat to satiety" — R.
  Yehuda: eat much and be satisfied; Rabban Shimon b. Gamliel: that
  is no sign of blessing; nothing lacking from his table.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 4 5 — MATERIAL. "If you say what shall we
  eat in the eighth year" — R. Akiva: the Sages' AFTERGROWTH BAN
  rests here (Torah); the Sages: the aftergrowth ban is RABBINIC —
  the verse is the people's complaint: "you said do not sow, and
  what we gather we may not store; you said remove it — what do we
  eat after the removal?" The ban's provenance disputed, Torah
  against scribes.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 4 6 — MATERIAL. "I will command my blessing
  in the sixth year" — each year for its neighbor; "produce for
  THREE years" — sixth, seventh, and the year after; or: the
  seventh, the Jubilee, and the year after the Jubilee. The
  blessing's arithmetic in two readings.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 4 7 — MATERIAL. THE YEAR LADDER: "sow the
  eighth" — the eighth after a release; "year" — the ninth; "eat
  old" — not spoiled; "until the ninth" — the ninth after the eighth
  after the Jubilee; "year... year" — the tenth after the Jubilee
  after the release; "until its produce comes, eat old" — the
  ELEVENTH for olives. Two consecutive rest years counted out.

## Onkelos Lev 25:1-22 (22 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Lev 25:1 — CONTEXT. בְּטוּרָא דְסִינַי (in the mountain of
  Sinai) — plain.
- Onkelos Lev 25:2 — MATERIAL. שְׁמִטְּתָא קֳדָם יְיָ (a release

- Onkelos Lev 25:6 — MATERIAL. לַאֲגִירָךְ וּלְתוֹתָבָךְ (your hireling

- Onkelos Lev 25:7 — CONTEXT. לִבְעִירָךְ וּלְחַיְתָא (your beast and
  the wild animal) — the pair the field-clock runs on, plain.
- Onkelos Lev 25:8 — MATERIAL. שְׁבַע שְׁמִטִּין דִּשְׁנִין (seven
  RELEASES of years) — "seven sabbaths of years" written as seven

  years).
- Onkelos Lev 25:9 — MATERIAL. שׁוֹפַר יַבָּבָא (a shofar of wailing)

  23:24; בְּיוֹמָא דְכִפּוּרַיָּא (on the day of atonements);
  בְּכָל אַרְעֲכוֹן (in all your land).
- Onkelos Lev 25:10 — MATERIAL. חֵרוּתָא (freedom) for the liberty
  word — Onkelos IS the Sifra's "liberty is freedom" (Chapter 2 2);
  לְכָל יָתְבָהָא (to all its inhabitants); יוֹבֵלָא (Jubilee) kept;

- Onkelos Lev 25:11 — CONTEXT. The three bans repeated with the same

- Onkelos Lev 25:12 — MATERIAL. קוּדְשָׁא תְהֵי לְכוֹן (holy shall it

- Onkelos Lev 25:14 — MATERIAL. לָא תוֹנוּן גְּבַר יָת אֲחוֹהִי (do not

- Onkelos Lev 25:15 — MATERIAL. בָּתַר יוֹבֵלָא (after the Jubilee);
  בְּמִנְיַן שְׁנֵי עֲלַלְתָּא (by the number of produce years) —

- Onkelos Lev 25:21 — MATERIAL. וֶאֱפַקֵּד יָת בִּרְכָתִי (and I will

## The finds (this ledger's crowns)
THE SINAI-DETAIL RULE (Section 1 1) — one place-name generalizing
provenance to every commandment. THE ONSET TIMELINE (Section 1 3) —
a dated schedule from the crossing to year sixty-four. THE CLASS
RULE (Section 1 6) — labor in the land and in the tree, by the

## Sifra, Emor — Section 4 (8 rows)
- Sifra, Emor, Section 4 1 — MATERIAL. וינזרו ("they shall
  separate") — נזירה = SEPARATION (Ezek 14:7, Isa 1:4 as the lexical
  witnesses); "from the holy things of the children of Israel" —
  liable for פיגול/נותר/טמא (wrong-intent, leftover, impure) on
  ISRAEL'S holy things, not on GENTILES' holy things; their OWN
  holy things included by "which they sanctify to Me."
- Sifra, Emor, Section 4 2 — MATERIAL. THE CHILLUL TRIPLE-IMPORT:
  "profanation"-"profanation" (Lev 19:8) carries three things at
  once — TIME (the disqualification-by-time class), DEATH, and
  ACCEPTANCE-dependence; R. Yehuda by "I the LORD"-"I the LORD" —
  KARET. One verbal analogy, three parameters.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 4 3 — MATERIAL. "Say to them" — those at
  Sinai; "to your generations" — forever; fathers AND sons both
  written because each has laws the other lacks (Num 36:8 the
  fathers' inheritance-marriage rule; many commandments in the
  sons) — the non-redundancy of the two addressee tokens.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 4 4 — MATERIAL. "Man" — the woman by "of
  all your seed"; ALL ISRAEL by "every man."
- Sifra, Emor, Section 4 5 — MATERIAL. THE KARET-FOR-IMPURITY
  EXTENSION: Lev 7:20 named only SHELAMIM; 22:3 extends the karet to
  ALL holy things — one-day offerings ("flesh"), offerings whose
  remainder is not eaten ("sacrifice"), birds and grain offerings
  down to the leper's log of oil ("which they sanctify to Me"); the
  piggul-to-tumah a-fortiori REFUSED by the four-way difference
  (piggul: fixed offering, one knowledge, no exception; tumah: two
  knowledges, sliding scale, permitted in exception); the handful,
  frankincense, incense, anointed priest's and libation grain
  offerings by "the holy things which they sanctify."
- Sifra, Emor, Section 4 6 — MATERIAL. "Cut off" — not from one
  side to another: "from before Me, I the LORD" — EVERYWHERE I am.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 4 7 — MATERIAL. THE READINESS THRESHOLD: not
  liable immediately — "which they OFFER"; R. Elazar: is a toucher
  liable?! "who approaches" = UNTIL IT IS FIT TO APPROACH: what has
  permitters — after the permitters are offered; what has none —
  after sanctified in a vessel. The liability clock starts at
  fitness, not at consecration.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 4 8 — MATERIAL. "His impurity on him" =
  impurity of the BODY, not of the flesh — four routes recorded:
  the verbal analogy; Rabbi from "and eat" + "his impurity"; R.
  Chiya from plural-holy-things/singular-impurity; Others — only
  one FROM WHOM impurity departs.

## Onkelos Lev 22 (33 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Lev 22:1 — NOT-BEARING. Speech frame.
- Onkelos Lev 22:2 — MATERIAL. וְיִפָּרְשׁוּן ("they shall SEPARATE")
  — the Sifra's "nezirah = separation" (Section 4 1) performed as
  the translation; "which they sanctify to Me" → מְקַדְּשִׁין קֳדָמַי
  ("sanctify BEFORE Me").
- Onkelos Lev 22:3 — CONTEXT. "That soul" → אֲנָשָׁא ("that PERSON");
  cut off "from before Me."
- Onkelos Lev 22:4 — CONTEXT. The leper → סְגִיר ("shut-in," the
  standard rendering); literal otherwise.
- Onkelos Lev 22:5 — CONTEXT. Literal.
- Onkelos Lev 22:6 — CONTEXT. Literal; "washes his flesh in water."
- Onkelos Lev 22:7 — CONTEXT. "The sun sets" → וּבְמֵעַל שִׁמְשָׁא
  ("at the ENTERING of the sun"); "his bread" kept as bread here.
- Onkelos Lev 22:8 — CONTEXT. Terefah → תְּבִירָא ("broken").
- Onkelos Lev 22:9 — MATERIAL. THE LEDGER VERBS: "My charge" →
  מַטְּרַת מֵימְרִי ("the charge of My WORD"); "bear sin" →
  יְקַבְּלוּן עֲלוֹהִי חוֹבָא ("RECEIVE upon it a DEBT") — the
  liability rendered as a received debt, the same verb the Lev 17
  and Lev 18 ledgers recorded.
- Onkelos Lev 22:10 — CONTEXT. Stranger → חִלּוֹנַי ("common
  person"); toshav → תּוֹתָבָא ("resident"), sachir → אֲגִירָא ("hireling").
- Onkelos Lev 22:11 — CONTEXT. Literal.
- Onkelos Lev 22:12 — MATERIAL. "The terumah of the holy things" →
  בְּאַפְרָשׁוּת קוּדְשַׁיָּא ("the SEPARATED PORTION of the holy
  things") — terumah as the separated part; the Sifra's "border
  holy things" (Chapter 4 16) named by the separation act.
- Onkelos Lev 22:13 — CONTEXT. "As in her youth" → כְּרַבְיוּתָהָא ("as in her youth").
- Onkelos Lev 22:14 — CONTEXT. "In error" → בְּשָׁלוּ; "its fifth."
- Onkelos Lev 22:15 — CONTEXT. Literal.
- Onkelos Lev 22:16 — MATERIAL. ONKELOS INSERTS THE CONDITION:
  "when they eat their holy things" → בְּמֵיכָלְהוֹן בְּסוֹאַבָא ("when
  they eat IN IMPURITY their holy things") — the guilt clause
  bound to impure eating; the Sifra reads the same clause as
  excluding the sower and the defiler (Chapter 6 10) — the
  translation narrows the offense to eating-in-impurity.
- Onkelos Lev 22:17 — NOT-BEARING. Speech frame.
- Onkelos Lev 22:18 — CONTEXT. The convert → גִּיּוֹרָא ("convert"); literal.
- Onkelos Lev 22:19 — CONTEXT. "For your acceptance" → לְרַעֲוָא
  לְכוֹן ("for favor for you").
- Onkelos Lev 22:20 — CONTEXT. Literal.
- Onkelos Lev 22:21 — MATERIAL. לְפַלֵּא ("to set apart") →
  לְפָרָשָׁא נִדְרָא ("to SPECIFY a vow") — the Sifra's "to set
  apart = the yoledet's and nazirite's offerings" (Section 7 8)
  read as vow-specification; "sacrifice of holy things" for
  shelamim.
- Onkelos Lev 22:22 — MATERIAL. THE BEAST LIST RULED: charutz →
  פְסִיק ("SPLIT") — the Sifra's "pierced, notched, SPLIT eyelid"
  (Section 7 12) at the third arm; yabbelet → יַבְּלָן; the man-
  list words garav/yalefet → גַרְבָן/חֲזָזָן identical to 21:20 —
  the cross-list transfer (Section 7 13) visible in the
  translation's shared vocabulary.
- Onkelos Lev 22:23 — MATERIAL. ONKELOS'S קלוט: שרוע וקלוט →
  יַתִּיר וְחַסִּיר ("EXTRA and LACKING") — sarua as a surplus limb,
  kalut as a DEFICIENT one; the Sifra reads kalut as the uncloven
  hoof (Chapter 7 6). A recorded fork on the token.
- Onkelos Lev 22:24 — CONTEXT. The four castration terms rendered
  — מְרִיס (crushed), רְסִיס (bruised), שְׁלִיף (drawn out), גְזִיר
  (cut) — no arm chosen.
- Onkelos Lev 22:25 — CONTEXT. "Foreigner" → בַּר עַמְמִין ("son of
  the peoples"); "their corruption" → חִבּוּלְהוֹן ("their corruption").
- Onkelos Lev 22:26 — NOT-BEARING. Speech frame.
- Onkelos Lev 22:27 — CONTEXT. "Accepted" → יִתִּרְעֵי; offering
  "before the LORD."
- Onkelos Lev 22:28 — MATERIAL. ONKELOS RULES THE FEMALE: "an ox or
  a sheep, it and its young" → וְתוֹרְתָּא אוֹ שֵׂיתָא לַהּ וְלִבְרַהּ ("a
  COW or a EWE — HER and HER young") — every noun and pronoun
  FEMINIZED: the Sifra's argued verdict (Chapter 8 1 — the law
  binds the mother, not the male) performed inside the verse by
  grammar alone.
- Onkelos Lev 22:29 — CONTEXT. "For your acceptance" → favor.
- Onkelos Lev 22:30 — CONTEXT. Literal.
- Onkelos Lev 22:31 — CONTEXT. Literal.
- Onkelos Lev 22:32 — CONTEXT. "I shall be sanctified among the
  children of Israel" literal — the public threshold kept.
- Onkelos Lev 22:33 — CONTEXT. Literal.

## The fresh read — verdict rows (65 segments, one row each)

### The witch (22:17)
- Sanhedrin 67a:18 — context (Rav Yehuda's city-enticers frame).
- Sanhedrin 67a:19 — MATERIAL (docket). THE GENDER SCOPE: מכשפה
  ("a witch") — "whether man or woman; why then the feminine?
  because MOST women are found in sorcery" — the noun's gender
  is a FREQUENCY datum, not a scope limit.
- Sanhedrin 67a:20 — MATERIAL (docket). THE MODE — ARM ONE: R.
  Yosei HaGelili — "here 'you shall not let a witch LIVE'; there
  'you shall not let any soul LIVE' (Deut 20:16)": as there by
  the SWORD, so here.
- Sanhedrin 67a:21 — MATERIAL rider — ARM TWO: R. Akiva — "here
  lo techayeh; there 'whether beast or man it shall not LIVE'
  (Exod 19:13)": as THERE BY STONING, so here — the witch's
  death mode derived from THE SINAI BOUNDARY VERSE, the very
  clause whose machine the covenant block seated (EX19-10,
  EX19-16).
- Sanhedrin 60a:1 — MATERIAL (docket). The Mishnah's own
  juxtaposition: whoever is in "all who lie with a beast" is in
  "the witch shall not live" — the two clauses run as one class.
- Sanhedrin 60a:2 — context (R. Elazar's kilayim extension —
  foreign span, noted).
- Yevamot 4a:4 — MATERIAL (frame). Rav Yosef's meta-rule: even
  one who does NOT expound adjacency in the whole Torah expounds
  it IN DEUTERONOMY — R. Yehuda's own usage split.
- Yevamot 4a:5 — MATERIAL (docket). THE METHOD DISPUTED AT OUR
  VERSE: ben Azzai — the witch beside the beast-lier was PLACED
  there to teach: as the beast-lier is stoned, so the witch.
- Yevamot 4a:6 — MATERIAL rider: R. Yehuda — "because they
  placed the matter beside it, shall we take this one OUT to
  stoning?!" — his route instead: ov and yidoni (the
  necromancers) were inside the sorcerers' class and were
  SINGLED OUT to compare — as they are stoned, so the witch:
  same verdict, rival derivation, the adjacency middah itself
  the dispute.
- Berakhot 21b:9 — MATERIAL (dup frame). Rav Yosef's
  Deuteronomy rule at its Berakhot seat.
- Berakhot 21b:10 — MATERIAL (docket; dup of Yevamot 4a:5 — one
  verdict, both seats in the cite index).
- Berakhot 21b:11 — MATERIAL rider (dup of 4a:6; the
  necromancer-class route spelled out).

### Bestiality (22:18)
- Sanhedrin 54b:5 — MATERIAL (frame). The mode: "he shall
  surely die" fixed as STONING — "here 'you shall kill' (Deut
  13:10's taharog at the enticer), there…" — the kill-verb

- Sanhedrin 54b:6 — MATERIAL (docket). THE PASSIVE READ IN:
  punishment for the active lier is heard elsewhere — so כל שכב
  עם בהמה מות יומת ("ALL who lie with a beast shall surely

  Leviticus 18:23 — punishment here, azhara there: the two-seat
  division of labor again.

### The sacrifices clause and the service paradigm (22:19)
- Sanhedrin 60b:7 — context (whence these words).
- Sanhedrin 60b:8 — MATERIAL (docket). THE CLAUSE PARSED: had it
  said "one who sacrifices shall be destroyed" alone, I would
  say it speaks of slaughtering SACRED OFFERINGS OUTSIDE — לאלהים
  ("TO THE GODS") teaches: it speaks of idol-service slaughter.
- Sanhedrin 60b:9 — MATERIAL rider. THE EMPTYING: only slaughter?
  incense, libation? בלתי לה' לבדו ("save to the LORD alone") —
  it EMPTIED all the services to the Unique Name (ריקן העבודות
  כולן לשם המיוחד): every inside-service, done to an idol, is
  this clause's capital act — the paradigm EX22-09's zevichah
  row already carried.
- Sanhedrin 60b:11 — MATERIAL (frame). The bow's punishment by
  the Deuteronomy juxtaposition (went-and-served-and-bowed
  beside the execution clause).
- Sanhedrin 60b:12 — MATERIAL (docket). THE WARNING FOUND: עונש
  שמענו אזהרה מניין ("the punishment we heard — the warning from
  where?") — כי לא תשתחוה לאל אחר ("you shall not bow to another
  god," 34:14): the second tablets' clause is the punished bow's
  azhara.
- Sanhedrin 60b:13 — MATERIAL rider: the boundary — could I
  include the hugger, the kisser, the shoe-fitter? "zoveach"
  teaches: capital only for inside-style service and the bow.
- Avodah Zarah 51a:12 — context (Ulla's frame — vines trimmed for
  the idol's use).
- Avodah Zarah 51a:13 — MATERIAL (docket). THE BLEMISH EXEMPTION:
  R. Abahu citing R. Yochanan — slaughtering a BLEMISHED animal
  to idols is EXEMPT: "the Torah forbade only INSIDE-STYLE"
  (כעין פנים) — the paradigm's own negative case: what the
  Temple would not receive, the clause does not punish.
- Avodah Zarah 51a:14 — MATERIAL rider: Rava's probe — which
  blemish? an eye-speck is valid on a Noahide's own altar — the
  parameter argued at the edge.
- Sanhedrin 63a:17 — MATERIAL (docket). THE JOINING BAN: R.
  Shimon ben Yochai — whoever JOINS Heaven's name with another
  thing is uprooted from the world: בלתי לה' לבדו ("save to the
  LORD alone"); and the calf's אשר העלוך (plural "which brought
  you up," 32:4) read: they craved MANY gods.
- Sanhedrin 63a:15 — MATERIAL (frame). R. Yochanan: but for the
  vav of he'elukha, Israel's wicked would have deserved
  annihilation — one letter holding the verdict.
- Sanhedrin 63a:16 — MATERIAL rider: the same as a recorded
  tannaitic split (Acherim's arm) — the letter-grain row
  dual-carried.

### The bowing census and the unification (20:3-5, 23:24, 34:14)
- Sanhedrin 63a:1 — MATERIAL (frame). R. Ami: sacrificed,
  incensed, and poured in ONE LAPSE — one liability; Abaye's
  reason: לא תעבדם ("you shall not serve them," 20:5) MADE THEM
  ALL ONE SERVICE.
- Sanhedrin 63a:2 — MATERIAL (docket). THE CENSUS: Abaye —
  THREE bowing verses in the idolatry file — why?
- Sanhedrin 63a:3 — MATERIAL rider: one for the idol whose way
  is worship-by-bowing, one for the idol whose way is NOT, one
  TO DIVIDE (bowing carved as its own liability inside the
  one-service rule) — each verse assigned its office; 34:14's
  office is the azhara (60b:12).
- Sanhedrin 61b:19 — context (Rava's alternative frame).
- Sanhedrin 61b:20 — MATERIAL (docket). LOVE OR FEAR: Abaye —
  "to THEM you shall not bow" but you may bow to a man like
  yourself; even one worshiped like Haman? ולא תעבדם ("and you
  shall not serve them") — and Haman WAS worshiped from fear: worship from love or fear is LIABLE.
- Sanhedrin 61b:21 — MATERIAL rider: Rava — like-Haman
  -but-not-like-Haman: Haman was himself an idol, but there from
  fear — worship from love or fear EXEMPT: the recorded dispute.
- Sanhedrin 61a:14 — context (Rav Yosef's teacher-split frame on
  the self-deifier).
- Sanhedrin 61a:15 — MATERIAL (docket). THE SELF-DEIFIER: where
  people actually WORSHIPED him, all agree liable — לא תעשה לך
  פסל ("you shall not make FOR YOURSELF," 20:3-4 read to include
  self-deification); the dispute is over mere SPEECH ("come
  worship me"): R. Meir — speech is a thing; R. Yehuda — not.
- Sanhedrin 61a:16 — MATERIAL rider: Rav Yosef retracts — even
  R. Yehuda convicts on the enticer's own saying "I will
  worship" — the speech fork narrowed.
- Sanhedrin 63a:13 — context (the excision-comparison frame).
- Sanhedrin 63a:14 — MATERIAL (docket). ACCEPTING AS GOD: the
  calf verse chains its three verbs — וישתחוו לו ויזבחו לו
  ויאמרו ("they BOWED to it, they SACRIFICED to it, they SAID:
  these are your gods") — the SAYING equated by hekesh to the
  bowing and the sacrifice: accepting an idol as one's god IS
  worship.
- Sanhedrin 63a:18 — context (the hugger/kisser lashes row — the
  festivals block's 63b:5 file adjoins).

### The blasphemy verb and the ruler clause (22:27)
- Sanhedrin 56a:5 — MATERIAL (frame). Shmuel: "one who is NOKEV
  the Name shall die" (Lev 24:16) — the punishment seat.
- Sanhedrin 56a:6 — MATERIAL (docket). THE VERB FIXED: whence
  that nokev means CURSING? מה אקב לא קבה אל ("how shall I CURSE
  whom God has not cursed" — Balaam's own verb, Num 23:8); and
  its WARNING from here: אלהים לא תקלל ("you shall not curse
  elohim," 22:27).
- Sanhedrin 56a:7 — MATERIAL rider: or is it PIERCING (vayikov
  a hole, 2 Kings 12:10)? — the alternative parsed and its
  warning traced, the cursing read holding.
- Sanhedrin 66a:11 — MATERIAL (frame). The parent-curser's mode:
  stoning by the damav-bo analogy.
- Sanhedrin 66a:12 — MATERIAL (docket). THE WARNING BUILT: the
  parent-curser's punishment is heard (21:17) — the azhara? If
  the father is a JUDGE — he is inside "you shall not curse
  elohim"; a PRINCE — inside "a ruler in your people you shall
  not curse"; neither —
- Sanhedrin 66a:13 — MATERIAL rider: בנין אב from the two — not
  the prince's height nor the judge's office but their COMMON
  "in your people": the warning reaches every parent of your
  people.
- Sanhedrin 66a:22 — MATERIAL (frame). The deaf-man's verse
  spared for the father via the reassignment middah
  (if-not-needed-for-its-own-matter).
- Sanhedrin 66a:23 — MATERIAL (docket). THE REFERENT DISPUTED:
  "this works for the one who says elohim here is PROFANE
  (judges) — for the one who says SACRED, what is there to
  say?"
- Sanhedrin 66a:24 — MATERIAL rider: the baraita — elohim
  PROFANE, R. Yishmael; elohim SACRED, R. Akiva; and R. Eliezer
  ben Yaakov's row: the Name-blesser's warning is this clause —
  one token, two offices, the dispute recorded.
- Sanhedrin 85a:13 — context (the son's liability frame).
- Sanhedrin 85a:14 — MATERIAL (docket). THE GATE: ונשיא בעמך לא
  תאור ("a ruler of YOUR PEOPLE you shall not curse") — only
  בעושה מעשה עמך ("one who DOES THE DEEDS of your people"): the
  protection is conditional on conduct.
- Sanhedrin 85a:15 — MATERIAL rider: striking learned from
  cursing by hekesh — the gate carries over.
- Yevamot 22b:6 — context (the barred-union son's inheritance
  frame).
- Yevamot 22b:7 — MATERIAL (docket). THE GATE'S EDGE: the mamzer
  son liable for striking his father — but is the father "doing
  the deeds of your people"?
- Yevamot 22b:8 — MATERIAL rider: as Rav Pinchas citing Rav
  Pappa — WHEN HE REPENTED (עושה תשובה — "doing
  repentance"): the deeds-of-your-
  people gate runs on repentance status, not pedigree.

### The satellites (2:12-13, 6:20, 32:8)
- Sanhedrin 56b:9 — MATERIAL (frame). The Noahide reader's
  premise beside it: elohim = JUDGES from 22:7's own
  approach-the-elohim (EX22-04's seat).
- Sanhedrin 56b:10 — MATERIAL (docket). THE CALF AS NOAHIDE
  SOURCE: Rav Chisda and Rav Yitzchak bar Avdimi — one from
  "they turned quickly from the way I COMMANDED them: THEY MADE
  themselves a calf" (32:8), one from Hosea's walked-after-tzav.
- Sanhedrin 56b:11 — MATERIAL rider: THE FORK — a gentile who
  MADE an idol and did not worship it: by the calf-verse arm
  liable FROM THE MAKING; by the Hosea arm, only when he
  follows and WORSHIPS — the arms recorded unassigned (חד אמר —
  "one said").
- Sanhedrin 58b:2 — context (R. Akiva's father's-wife frame).
- Sanhedrin 58b:3 — MATERIAL (docket). AMRAM'S AUNT: "and Amram
  took Jochebed HIS AUNT" (6:20) — objection: surely his
  MOTHER'S-side aunt (barred to Noahides)?
- Sanhedrin 58b:4 — MATERIAL rider: no — his FATHER'S-side aunt
  (outside the pre-Sinai ban): the marriage's ink resolved — and
  the frozen unit's own translation layer (Onkelos at EX06's
  6:20 step) already renders "his father's sister."
- Sanhedrin 58b:16 — context (Rava's Noahide-intimacy frame).
- Sanhedrin 58b:17 — MATERIAL (docket). THE GENTILE WHO STRIKES:
  R. Chanina — a gentile who strikes an Israelite is liable to
  death: "he turned this way and that and saw there was NO MAN,
  and he STRUCK the Egyptian" (2:12) — the narrative verse
  carried as the law's source.
- Sanhedrin 58b:18 — MATERIAL rider: the slap likened to
  slapping the Presence — the homiletic escort noted.
- Sanhedrin 58b:19 — MATERIAL (docket). THE RAISED HAND: Reish
  Lakish — one who RAISES his hand though he did not strike is
  called WICKED: למה תכה ("why WOULD you strike," 2:13) — not
  "why did you strike": the verse's own imperfect verb carries
  the law.
- Sanhedrin 58b:20 — MATERIAL rider: Zeiri citing R. Chanina —
  called SINNER too (the Samuel proof-pair).

### The investiture machine (29:1, 29:9, 29:12, 29:18, 29:30)
- Sanhedrin 83b:12 — context (the profanation-death analogy
  frame).
- Sanhedrin 83b:13 — MATERIAL (docket). THE UNVESTED PRIEST:
  whence that lacking-vestments serving dies? וחגרת אתם אבנט
  ("you shall gird them with the sash," 29:9) —
- Sanhedrin 83b:14 — MATERIAL rider: while their vestments are
  ON them, their priesthood is on them; vestments off,
  priesthood off — THEY ARE STRANGERS, and a stranger who
  served dies by Heaven: the office literally worn.
- Yoma 5a:9 — context (the for-generations frame).
- Yoma 5a:10 — MATERIAL (docket). THE SEVEN DAYS: שבעת ימים
  ילבשם הכהן תחתיו מבניו ("seven days shall the son wear them,"
  29:30) — robed seven and anointed seven; robed seven anointed
  one, robed one anointed seven whence? "who shall be ANOINTED
  and who shall be INVESTED" (Leviticus 16:32) — either way
  qualifies: the succession's two clocks decoupled.
- Yoma 5a:11 — context (the ideal-seven question).
- Yoma 5b:3 — context (Moses' three commanded-sayings — the
  Tzav family, noted).
- Yoma 5b:4 — MATERIAL (docket). THE TROUSERS' GAP: R. Yosei b.
  Chanina — the TROUSERS are not written in the investiture
  portion; וזה הדבר אשר תעשה להם ("and THIS is the THING you
  shall do," 29:1) — the this-the-thing clause IMPORTS the
  trousers and the tenth-ephah: the ink's own gap and the
  clause that supplies it.
- Yoma 5b:5 — MATERIAL rider: the trousers stand in the
  garments' context; the tenth-ephah by the zeh-zeh analogy —

- Zevachim 13a:11 — MATERIAL (frame). Collection named: "and
  they shall PRESENT" = receiving the blood — by the sons of

- Zevachim 13a:12 — MATERIAL (docket). R. AKIVA'S ROUTE: "sons
  of Aaron" here, "these are the names of the sons of Aaron,
  the ANOINTED priests" (Numbers 3:3) there — as there fit and
  vested, so here: collection bound to the fit, vested priest.
- Zevachim 13a:13 — context (R. Tarfon's collection/sprinkling
  distinctions — the heard-and-cannot-explain confession).
- Zevachim 24b:2 — MATERIAL (frame). R. Elazar b. R. Shimon's
  finger rule: wherever "finger" is said of COLLECTION, a
  change disqualifies; of PLACING, likewise.
- Zevachim 24b:3 — MATERIAL (docket). THE PARSE DIRECTION:
  where is "finger" said of PLACING? ולקחת מדם הפר ונתת על
  קרנת המזבח באצבעך ("...and PLACE on the horns of the altar
  with your FINGER," 29:12) — and he holds מקרא נדרש לפניו ולא
  לפני פניו ("a verse is expounded on what PRECEDES it, not on
  what precedes-the-preceding, and not on what follows"): the
  finger binds the placing, the collection stays free — a
  PARSING-DIRECTION meta-rule stated as law on this verse's own
  syntax.
- Zevachim 24b:4 — MATERIAL rider: R. Yochanan — wherever
  "finger" or "priesthood" is said, only the RIGHT hand: the
  laterality default.

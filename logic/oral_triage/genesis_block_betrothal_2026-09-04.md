### Kiddushin 2a:3-5, 4b:2-4, 11b:4-6 (9)
- 2a:3 — context (why "the woman is ACQUIRED" here and "the man
  betroths" there — because silver is coming).
- 2a:4 — MATERIAL (docket). וכסף מנא לן — גמר קיחה קיחה משדה עפרון
  ("and silver, whence? — taking-taking derived from the FIELD OF
  EPHRON"): 'when a man TAKES a wife' beside נתתי כסף השדה קח
  ממני ("I have given the silver of the field — TAKE from me,"
  Gen 23:13).
- 2a:5 — MATERIAL (docket). וקיחה איקרי קניין ("and taking is
  called ACQUISITION") — השדה אשר קנה אברהם ("the field that
  Abraham ACQUIRED," Gen 25:10).

- 4b:2 — context (the write-it-anyway rule for deflectable
  a-fortiori arguments).
- 4b:3 — MATERIAL (docket). The baraita: אין קיחה אלא בכסף ("there
  is no TAKING except with silver") — and so it says: "I have
  given the silver of the field, take from me."
- 4b:4 — MATERIAL. The a-fortiori that made the verse necessary:
  the Hebrew maidservant not acquired by intimacy yet acquired by
  silver — deflectable, hence the verse.
- 11b:4 — context (consecrated property's silver-silver analogy).
- 11b:5 — MATERIAL (docket). The challenge: betrothal learned
  kichah-kichah from Ephron's field — yet Beit Hillel say בפרוטה
  ובשוה פרוטה ("a peruta or a peruta's worth")! Shall we say Rav
  Asi spoke as Beit Shammai?
- 11b:6 — MATERIAL. The restatement: כל כסף קצוב האמור בתורה כסף
  צורי ("every FIXED silver stated in the Torah is TYRIAN
  silver") ושל דבריהם כסף מדינה ("and that of the sages,
  provincial") — Ephron powers the MEDIUM, never the amount.

### Ketubot 57b:1-4, 61a:2-4, 67b:1-3 (10)
- 57b:1 — context (the Mishnah's terumah clause).
- 57b:2 — MATERIAL (docket). Rav Chisda: תשב הנערה אתנו ימים או
  עשור ("let the maiden remain with us DAYS or ten," Gen 24:55).
- 57b:3 — MATERIAL (docket). What is "days"? Not two (people do
  not speak so — the negotiation ladder read into the clause);
  A YEAR — ימים תהיה גאלתו ("DAYS shall its redemption be," Lev
  25:29).
- 57b:4 — MATERIAL. And not a month (Num 11:20's "a month of
  days") — plain "days" is derived from plain "days" only.
- 61a:2 — context (the nursing dispute frame).
- 61a:3 — MATERIAL (docket). עולה עמו ואינה יורדת עמו ("she RISES
  with him and does not descend"): Rav Huna — והיא בעלת בעל ("she
  is married to a MASTER," Gen 20:3): in the master's rising, not
  his descent; R. Elazar — כי היא היתה אם כל חי ("she was the
  mother of all LIVING," Gen 3:20): for life given, not for pain.
- 61a:4 — context (the maidservant clause).
- 67b:1 — MATERIAL. The orphan girl is married off FIRST — a
  woman's shame is greater.
- 67b:2 — MATERIAL (docket). The orphan groom: rent him a HOUSE,
  arrange a BED and his utensils, THEN marry him a wife — "his
  lack" = the house, "which he lacks" = bed and table, לו ("for
  him") = a wife; וכן הוא אומר אעשה לו עזר כנגדו ("and so it
  says: I will make FOR HIM a helper," Gen 2:18) — our verse
  closes the parse.
- 67b:3 — context (sufficient-for-his-lack: even a horse and a
  runner — Hillel's recorded run).

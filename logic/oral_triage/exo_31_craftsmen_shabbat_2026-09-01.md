## Onkelos Exodus 31:1-18 — read IN FULL, 18 verses

- Onkelos Exod 31:2-6 — MATERIAL. THE NAMED ENGINEER: חֲזֵי
  דְרַבֵּיתִי בְשׁוּם בְּצַלְאֵל ("see, I have EXALTED BY NAME
  Bezalel") — the by-name appointment verb; filled with רוּחַ מִן
  קֳדָם יְיָ ("spirit from before the LORD") in wisdom,
  understanding, and knowledge; לְאַלָּפָא אוּמַנְוָן ("to TEACH
  craftsmanship" — the received translation reads the
  devising-of-designs as TEACHING: the master is a teacher);
  Oholiab of Dan beside him, and wisdom placed in every
  wise-hearted. The competence-layer is personnel now, granted from
  before the LORD.
- Onkelos Exod 31:13 — MATERIAL, the chapter's crown. בְּרַם ("BUT/
  HOWEVER") — the scoping particle: My Sabbaths you shall keep —
  אָת הִיא בֵּין מֵימְרִי וּבֵינֵיכוֹן ("it is a SIGN between MY
  WORD and you") for your generations, TO KNOW that I the LORD
  sanctify you. The juxtaposition the tradition reads as the
  labors' own boundary: the tabernacle work does NOT override the
  Sabbath — the very join from which the case shelf's labor
  catalog hangs (Mishnah Shabbat 7:1, this exam's docket, anchors
  here). The Memra as covenant party.
- Onkelos Exod 31:14-15 — MATERIAL. The double sanction: the
  profaner אִתְקְטָלָא יִתְקְטֵל ("shall surely be PUT TO DEATH");
  the worker וְיִשְׁתֵּצֵי ("shall be CUT OFF") — death and karet
  side by side (the deliberate-with-witnesses vs without split the
  case shelf runs); שְׁבַת שַׁבְּתָא ("a Sabbath of complete rest"),
  holy before the LORD.
- Onkelos Exod 31:16-17 — MATERIAL (light). לְדָרֵיהוֹן קְיַם עָלָם
  ("for their generations, an EVERLASTING COVENANT"); the sign
  between MY WORD and the sons of Israel FOREVER; שְׁבַת וְנָח
  ("He CEASED and RESTED") — the day-seven vocabulary, the corpus'
  rest-as-creation claim met in translation.
- Onkelos Exod 31:18 — MATERIAL, the crown. The two tablets of
  TESTIMONY (לוּחֵי סַהֲדוּתָא), stone, כְּתִיבִין בְּאֶצְבְּעָא
  דַיְיָ ("WRITTEN WITH THE FINGER OF GOD") — the anthropomorphism
  KEPT in translation, the rare literal survival: the writing's
  divine authorship is load-bearing and the translator will not
  soften it.
- Onkelos Exod 31:1, 31:7-12 — context (the work-list recited: tent,
  ark, cover, vessels, garments — לְבוּשֵׁי שִׁמוּשָׁא ("the
  garments of SERVICE") — oil and incense; the speech formulas).

## Midrash Tanchuma — the sections attributed to this block, read IN
## FULL (Ki Tisa 12-18 + Buber 9-12)

- Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 12 + 13 — MATERIAL. CALLED FROM THE
  BEGINNING: Bezalel's name announced from Adam's days (the
  generations pre-called — קֹרֵא הַדֹּרוֹת מֵרֹאשׁ, "He calls the
  generations from the beginning"; Adam shown every righteous
  descendant, the whole builder-chain from Bezalel through Solomon
  to Darius); the six-names table (Reaiah, son of Shobal, Jahath,
  Ahumai, Lahad — each an office of the tabernacle's work); and THE
  PAIRING DOCTRINE: the greatest tribe with the lowliest — Judah's
  Bezalel with Dan's Oholiab, and the Temple likewise (Solomon of
  Judah with Hiram of Dan) — "the great and the lowly are equal
  before Him"; no man may grow proud of his commission.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 14-16 (with Buber 9-12) — MATERIAL, the
  tablets' dossier. THE GIFT-READING: forty days Moses learned and
  FORGOT — at the end the Torah was GIVEN AS A GIFT (the learning
  is grace, not conquest); כְּלָלִים ("PRINCIPLES") were taught, not
  the whole (the Torah longer than the earth — the kernel-and-
  derivation doctrine in the spine's own words); the mountain
  curriculum split: WRITTEN by day, ORAL by night (Mishnah and
  Talmud on the mountain — the two-channel law before the tablets);
  the bride-wordplay on ke-khaloto (the Torah given adorned like a
  bride, its ornaments the twenty-four books; the scholar's modesty
  rules); stone because most penalties are stoning; the two tablets
  equal (heaven-and-earth, groom-and-bride, the two worlds); and
  the freedom-reading of the engraving — חָרוּת read חֵרוּת
  ("FREEDOM": from the empires / from the angel of death / from
  sufferings — the recorded three-way dispute; the token Pirkei
  Avot 6:2 carries, this exam's docket).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Ki Tisa 17-18 — context-plus: the
  captured-Torah reading (Psalm 68's "you took captivity captive" —
  Moses took what was in the mouth); Moses called merely-human while
  wielding decree-reversal; the praise-exchange frames. One line
  each, enrichment.

## THE 13 ROWS

- Mishnah Shekalim 4:6 (⇐ Exod 25:8) — MATERIAL, dispute. המקדיש נכסיו
  ("one who consecrates his property") that includes items fit for
  communal offerings: R. Akiva — given to the craftsmen as their
  wages; Ben Azzai — אינה היא המדה ("that is not the method"): the
  craftsmen's wages are first SEPARATED, the items desacralized onto
  the craftsmen's money, given as wages, then bought back from the new
  fund. The temple-economy mechanism dispute under the sanctuary-
  building duty of 25:8. EX25-08's rider holds the surplus law and
  OPENS this row by name (the Tanchuma's own opening question) — the
  mechanism dispute itself is engine content, compiled with both
  verdicts (module temple_funds); no verse-side seat needed.
- Mishnah Shevuot 2:2 (⇐ Exod 25:9) — MATERIAL, class B on EX25-03.
  אין מוסיפין על העיר ועל העזרות ("the city and the courts are not
  extended") except by king, prophet, אורים ותומים ("the Urim and
  Tummim"), a Sanhedrin of seventy-one, two thanksgiving offerings,
  and song — the court walks, the offerings behind, all Israel behind;
  inner loaf eaten, outer burned; and where ANY element is missing,
  one who enters the addition is NOT liable. This is EX25-03's
  constitutional clause (וכן תעשו — "and SO shall you make") running
  as live law: every extension re-enacts the original constitution or
  confers no sanctity. Module sanctuary_extension.

- Mishnah Menachot 11:4 (⇐ Exod 25:30) — MATERIAL. The form table:
  שתי הלחם ("the two loaves") seven by four handbreadths, horns four
  fingers; לחם הפנים ("the showbread") ten by five, horns seven
  fingers — R. Yehudah's mnemonic זד"ד יה"ז (the letter-numerals
  7-4-4 and 10-5-7). And BEN ZOMA derives from our verse's own words —
  ונתת על השלחן לחם פנים לפני תמיד ("and you shall set on the table
  bread of the face before Me continually") — שיהא לו פנים ("that it
  must have FACES"): the bread's name is a form-requirement. EX25-06
  holds the face-token and names this row as its docket; the
  form-requirement and dimension table are the new verse-side leg →
  finding F-032, seated EX25-11.
- Mishnah Menachot 11:5 (⇐ Exod 25:23) — MATERIAL, the conversion
  dispute. R. Yehudah: the table ten by five handbreadths; R. Meir:
  twelve by six — BOTH are our verse's own אמתים ארכו ואמה רחבו ("two
  cubits its length and a cubit its width") under the two recorded
  cubit constants (five vs six handbreadths to the cubit). The loaves'
  fold geometry follows each way (R. Yehudah: two-and-a-half back each
  side; R. Meir: two back each side and a two-handbreadth wind-gap);
  Abba Shaul: the frankincense bowls stood in the gap — answered from
  ועליו מטה מנשה ("and BESIDE him the tribe of Manasseh," Numbers
  2:20): על can mean adjacent. A recorded dispute whose two positions
  are one verse times two constants — computable. Finding F-032 →
  EX25-11 (with 11:4). Module showbread_form.

- Mishnah Menachot 11:7 (⇐ Exod 25:30) — MATERIAL, quotes our ink as
  its proof. The exchange procedure: two tables in the hall (marble
  for the incoming bread, gold for the outgoing — שמעלין בקדש ולא
  מורידין, "we ascend in holiness and do not descend"), the gold table
  within on which the bread lies continually; four priests enter as
  four withdraw, וטפחו של זה כנגד טפחו של זה ("this one's handbreadth
  beside that one's handbreadth") — שנאמר לפני תמיד ("as it is said:
  before Me CONTINUALLY" — our 25:30 verbatim); R. Yose: even if
  these withdraw and THEN these place, that too is תמיד
  ("continually") — the token's operational meaning is a recorded
  dispute (strict simultaneity vs no vacant night). EX25-06 holds the
  token; the dispute and the ascent principle are the new leg →
  finding F-033, seated EX25-12. Module showbread_tamid.
- Mishnah Menachot 3:7 (⇐ Exod 25:32, 25:36) — MATERIAL, class B on

- Mishnah Sukkah 3:14 (⇐ Exod 26:15) — row opened in full; its OWN

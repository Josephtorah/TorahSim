## Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet — Section 1 (14 rows)
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 1 — MATERIAL. The scope
  frame: "the children of Israel" in this matter — and the
  convert-woman and the maidservant included from "a WOMAN"; the
  sanctuary-defilement frame imported from Lev 15:31 ("that they
  die not... defiling My dwelling"), with the birthing mother's own
  clause ("and to the sanctuary she shall not come") fixing that
  she defiles it only FROM INSIDE.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 2 — MATERIAL. The
  lenient birther generalized: ALL the impure defile the sanctuary
  only from inside, as she does — the a-fortiori blocked and the
  equation read instead.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 3 — MATERIAL. The
  lighter impurities (corpse-toucher, the menstruant's bed-partner)
  swept into the sanctuary ban by "that they die not... defiling My
  dwelling" — the inclusion rider.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 4 — MATERIAL. "And
  bears" excludes a birth BEFORE THE SPEAKING (the law's own
  effective date); conception before / birth after — "the matter
  depends only on the BIRTH"; תַזְרִיעַ ("she seeds") = through the
  seeding place — the CAESAREAN EXCLUDED, with R. Shimon's dissent
  (caesarean counts as born for the offering, exempt from the five
  selas).
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 5 — MATERIAL. MIDDAH
  CASE LAW: R. Yehuda's a-fortiori for the stillbirth is REJECTED
  on form — "any inference that begins to be stringent and ends to
  be lenient is NO inference" — and the stillbirth's
  mother-defilement re-derived from the word "male" instead.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 6 — MATERIAL. All
  gestation lengths count ("whatever she bears"); the miscarried
  forms — sandal, placenta, formed sac, dismembered emergence —
  included from "when she seeds and bears: ANY seed she bears."
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 7 — MATERIAL. The
  fish/locust/swarm-shaped miscarriage NOT impure: "male" = what
  has HUMAN FORM.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 8 — MATERIAL. Forms
  unfit for the breath of life excluded (from the eighth-day
  circumcision rider: "what is FIT for soul-life"); and SHE is
  impure, THE CHILD IS NOT — the scapegoat cited as the
  causes-impurity-yet-pure witness, the cycle closed by the verse.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 9 — MATERIAL. Twins:
  count the seven days from the LAST child — the corpse-impurity
  analogy (count from the latest impurity).
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 10 — MATERIAL. The
  rival analogy: bloods count from the FIRST — the two ways argued.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 11 — MATERIAL. The
  tiebreak weighed (impurity-from-elsewhere vs impurity-from-her)
  and SETTLED BY THE VERSE: seven for the last child.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 12 — MATERIAL. The
  seven days are CONSECUTIVE — from "as the days of her menstrual
  state."
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 13 — MATERIAL. Birth
  days equated to menstrual days: neither fit for the
  discharge-count — the seven clean days cannot run inside them.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Section 1 14 — MATERIAL. "Her
  infirmity — she is impure": the bed-partner included; the NIGHTS
  included; the woman who births IN the discharge state needs seven
  clean days.

## Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet — Chapter 1 (8 rows)
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 1 1 — MATERIAL.
  Circumcision BY DAY, not night ("on the day"); and every later
  circumcision (ninth, tenth, eleventh day) by day too — from the
  vav ("AND on the day").
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 1 2 — MATERIAL. The
  student-and-R. Akiva exchange: the student's clever need for the
  vav ("without it I could read seven PLUS eight = fifteen!") —
  Akiva: "you dove into mighty waters and brought up a potsherd:
  Gen 17:12 already says the eighth day."
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 1 3 — MATERIAL. The whole
  day is valid but THE DILIGENT ARE EARLY (Abraham rose early,
  Gen 22:3); and THE EIGHTH-DAY CIRCUMCISION OVERRIDES SHABBAT —
  derived here from "AND on the day," the profaners-die clause
  (Exod 31:14) carved to all other labors — the round-17 Shabbat
  machinery's own import at its Sifra seat.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 1 4 — MATERIAL.
  Circumcision proceeds even over a bright spot ("FLESH of his
  foreskin") — the guard clause "take heed of the affliction"
  (Deut 24:8) carved out for circumcision.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 1 5 — MATERIAL. CERTAIN
  foreskin overrides Shabbat; the doubtful does NOT; the
  androgynous child does not (R. Yehuda: does, and karet applies);
  twilight-born does not; born-circumcised does not — Beth Shammai
  require the covenant drop, Beth Hillel not.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 1 6 — MATERIAL. R. Shimon
  b. Elazar reframes the recorded dispute: on the born-circumcised
  BOTH require the drop (pressed foreskin); they divide on the
  ALREADY-CIRCUMCISED CONVERT.
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 1 7 — MATERIAL. The 33
  days consecutive (day-analogy); the doubled-purity a-fortiori
  rebutted by the written numbers; "she shall SIT" — the
  hard-labor case inside the eleven days is clean of the discharge
  status; and blood-of-purity stands "though she sees."
- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 1 8 — MATERIAL. "No holy
  thing shall she touch": TERUMAH included ("ANY holy thing"),
  TITHE excluded — the sanctuary analogy runs on soul-taking; and
  the entry ban is not a touch ban — two clauses, two gates.

## Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet — Chapter 3 (6 rows)

- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 3 3 — MATERIAL. The lamb

- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 3 4 — MATERIAL. She

- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 3 5 — MATERIAL. ONE

- Sifra, Tazria Parashat Yoledet, Chapter 3 6 — MATERIAL. "And she

## Onkelos Lev 12 (8 verses — read whole with the chapter)
- Onkelos Lev 12:1-8 — MATERIAL as a set. The renderings plain and
  load-bearing at three points: 12:2 תַזְרִיעַ rendered תְעַדִּי
  ("when she conceives"), and נִדַּת דְּוֺתָהּ rendered רִחוּק
  סוֹבְתַהּ ("the SEPARATION of her infirmity"); 12:4 "she shall
  not come to the sanctuary" rendered וּלְמִקְדְּשָׁא לָא תֵעוֹל
  (shall not ENTER — the entry-not-touch gate in the translation);
  12:5 שְׁבֻעַיִם rendered אַרְבְּעָה עֲסַר כְּרִחוּקַהּ
  ("FOURTEEN, as her separation") — THE TRANSLATION IS THE
  VOCALIZATION'S WITNESS: two weeks, not seventy, exactly the
  reading Chapter 2 2 defends.

## The finds (this unit's share)
(1) THE VOCALIZATION SEAT: the two-weeks/seventy fork argued on
12:5's own word with the meta-rule "the reading has a mother" —
and Onkelos' "fourteen days" standing as the reading tradition's
oldest witness. (2) MIDDAH CASE LAW twice: the stringent-to-
lenient inference voided on form (Section 1 5); DAYO held against
every rephrasing (Chapter 2 4). (3) The Shabbat-override of
eighth-day circumcision AT ITS SOURCE (Chapter 1 3) — the seat the
Shabbat machinery's round-17 import cites. (4) THE GOLD-DINAR
STORY: the recorded market effect of a taught leniency (Chapter 3
6). (5) The order swap with its practical kind-matching rule
(Chapter 4 3).

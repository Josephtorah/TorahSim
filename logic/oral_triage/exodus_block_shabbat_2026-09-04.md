### The forgetters and the division of labors (Shabbat 69b-70a)
- Shabbat 69b:6 — MATERIAL. Rava's desert protocol: one who lost
  track of Shabbat counts six days and keeps a seventh — and works
  the MINIMUM every day including his counted day (else he dies);
  the day is distinguished by kiddush ("sanctification") and
  havdala ("separation") alone. A running state machine for a lost
  clock.
- Shabbat 69b:7 — MATERIAL. Partial knowledge: he who recalls the
  COUNT of days since departure may work freely on that weekday
  each week — he certainly did not set out on Shabbat; the
  convoy-on-Friday rider blocks extending it a second day.
- Shabbat 69b:8 — MATERIAL (docket). The forgetter split's own
  verse pair: Rav Nachman citing Rabba bar Avuh — ושמרו בני ישראל
  את השבת ("and the children of Israel shall keep the Shabbat,"
  Exod 31:16, singular) = one observance for many Shabbatot;
  את שבתתי תשמרו ("you shall keep My Shabbatot," Lev 26:2, plural)
  = one per Shabbat.
- Shabbat 69b:9 — MATERIAL. Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak REVERSES the
  assignment — the opposite reading of the same pair; both agree
  the mishnah's grid hangs on these two verses. Recorded dispute
  over which verse carries which arm, carried dual-track.
- Shabbat 69b:10 — context (the mishnah's next clause quoted).
- Shabbat 70a:1 — MATERIAL. Why one-per-Shabbat vs one-per-labor:
  Rav Safra's desist-cause test rejected by Rav Nachman; the
  standing answer — the offering follows the UNWITTINGNESS COUNT:
  unwitting about one matter (the day) = one; unwitting about many
  matters (the labors) = many.
- Shabbat 70a:2 — MATERIAL (docket). DIVISION OF LABORS, Shmuel's
  route: מות יומת ("he shall surely die," Exod 31:14) — the doubled
  death-verb amplifies MANY DEATHS for one desecration; displaced
  from deliberate (35:2 already covers it) to unwitting, and
  "die" there = die by MONEY (multiple offerings).
- Shabbat 70a:3 — MATERIAL. R. Natan's route: kindling (35:3)
  singled out; and the assembly verse's own arithmetic — אלה
  הדברים ("these are the things," 35:1): devarim two, ha- three,
  aleh = 36 by letter values; 39 labors stated to Moses at Sinai.
- Shabbat 70a:4 — MATERIAL. The ladder needs its middle rung:
  בחריש ובקציר תשבת ("in plowing and in harvest you shall rest,"

  categories to itself — each liable on its own.
- Shabbat 70a:5 — MATERIAL. THE KINDLING FORK verbatim: R. Yosei —

- Shabbat 70a:6 — MATERIAL. R. Yosei's own division source: מאחת

## Lev 25:1-22 — the land's sabbath and the Jubilee (7 rows)
- Mishnah Makkot 3:9 — LAW. One furrow, eight prohibitions — "and in
  the seventh year": PLOWING in the seventh among the banned labors —
  LV25A-04's census (Sifra Behar Section 1 4: "plowing, hoeing...
  from 'your field NOT'").
- Mishnah Rosh Hashanah 1:8 — LAW. The disqualified witnesses: "those
  who lend at interest... and the traders in seventh-year produce" —
  LV25A-05's "for eating, not commerce" (Sifra Behar Chapter 1 6) and
  LV25B-14's interest ban.
- Mishnah Sanhedrin 3:3 — LAW (VERBATIM). The same list; R. Shimon:
  first called "gatherers of the seventh," renamed "traders" when the
  oppressors multiplied; R. Yehuda: only when it is his sole trade —
  LV25A-05 with the recorded renaming.
- Mishnah Shevuot 7:4 — LAW. The suspect on oath — the interest
  lender, the seventh-year trader — his opponent swears and collects:
  the same two classes, LV25A-05 and LV25B-14.
- Mishnah Rosh Hashanah 3:2 — LAW (VERBATIM). All shofars valid but
  the cow's, "because it is a horn"; R. Yosei: all are called horn —
  "at the drawing of the HORN OF THE JUBILEE" (Joshua 6:5): the
  Jubilee's own instrument named by the exam, LV25A-09.
- Mishnah Rosh Hashanah 4:9 — LAW (VERBATIM). The order of blasts,
  three of three of three; "as the prayer leader is obligated, so
  EVERY INDIVIDUAL is obligated" — the Sifra's "every individual is
  obligated" from "throughout your land" (Sifra Behar Section 2 5),
  and the plain-broken-plain structure of Section 2 4 — LV25A-09.
- Mishnah Avot 3:7 — CONTEXT. "Give Him of His own, for you and yours
  are His" (1 Chronicles 29:14) — the aggadic neighbor of "the land
  is Mine" (25:23); not a case.

## Lev 25:14-17 — overreaching (4 rows)
- Mishnah Bava Metzia 4:3 — LAW (VERBATIM). "Overreaching is four
  silver of twenty-four to the sela, a sixth of the purchase; until
  when may he retract — until he can show it to a merchant or his
  kinsman; R. Tarfon ruled at Lod eight silver, a third, and the
  merchants of Lod rejoiced... and they returned to the Sages'
  words" — Sifra Behar Section 3 5 word for word, LV25A-14.
- Mishnah Bava Metzia 4:9 — LAW (VERBATIM). "These have no
  overreaching: slaves, documents, land, consecrated things" —
  Section 3 1-3's four exclusions, LV25A-14; R. Shimon's and R.
  Yehuda's additions recorded.
- Mishnah Bava Metzia 4:10 — LAW (VERBATIM). "As there is
  overreaching in buying and selling, so in words: 'how much is this
  thing' with no wish to buy; to a penitent 'remember your former
  deeds'; to a convert's son 'remember your fathers' deeds'" — Sifra
  Behar Chapter 4 2 word for word, LV25A-16.
- Mishnah Bava Metzia 4:2 — CONTEXT. Pulled and did not pay, paid and
  did not pull — the acquisition-by-pulling rule; the Talmud's
  derivation from "from the hand of your fellow" (Bava Metzia 47b)
  lies outside the spine; not a case for the seats.

## Lev 25:23-34 — the land's and the houses' redemption (7 rows)
- Mishnah Arakhin 9:1 — LAW (VERBATIM). "One who sells his field in
  the Jubilee year may not redeem in less than two years — 'by the
  number of harvest years'; a blight, mildew, or seventh year does
  not count; fallowed or uncultivated counts; R. Elazar: sold before
  the New Year full of fruit — three harvests in two years" — Sifra
  Behar Section 3 10 word for word, LV25A-15.
- Mishnah Arakhin 9:2 — LAW (VERBATIM). "Sold to the first at a
  maneh, resold at two hundred — reckons with the first; ... with the
  last, 'to the man in it'; not sell far to redeem near, bad to
  redeem good, not borrow, not by halves; the sanctuary permitted in
  all — a stringency in the layman over the sanctuary" — Sifra Behar
  Chapter 5 2-3, 6 word for word, LV25B-04, LV25B-05, LV25B-06.
- Mishnah Arakhin 9:3 — LAW. The walled-city house: redeemed AT ONCE
  and through twelve months — "like interest, not interest"; the
  seller's son redeems, from the buyer's son; the year counted from
  the sale ("until a full year is completed for him"); "full" —
  including the intercalary month; Rabbi: a year and its
  intercalation — LV25B-07, LV25B-08 (Sifra Behar Section 4 2-4).
- Mishnah Arakhin 9:4 — LAW (VERBATIM). "The twelfth month's day
  arrived unredeemed — irrevocably his, the buyer and the gift
  recipient alike, 'in perpetuity'; at first the buyer would hide on
  the twelfth month's day; Hillel the Elder ordained he deposit his
  money in the chamber, break the door, and enter" — Sifra Behar
  Section 4 8 word for word, LV25B-08.
- Mishnah Arakhin 9:7 — LAW (VERBATIM). "The village houses — given
  the strength of the walled-city houses and the strength of the
  fields: redeemed at once, redeemed through twelve months like the
  houses, out at the Jubilee and by deduction like the fields; two
  courtyards of two houses each, even walled since Joshua" — Sifra
  Behar Chapter 6 1-3, LV25B-09.
- Mishnah Arakhin 9:8 — LAW (VERBATIM). "An Israelite who inherited
  his Levite maternal grandfather does not redeem by this order...
  until he is a Levite and in the Levites' cities (Rabbi); the Sages:
  only in the Levites' cities; no field made pasture...; R. Eliezer:
  in Israel's cities field to pasture and pasture to city, not the
  reverse; priests and Levites sell and redeem forever" — Sifra
  Behar Chapter 6 8-9 word for word, LV25B-11, LV25B-12.
- Mishnah Shekalim 4:9 — LAW. "The sanctuary's hand is on top" — the
  supplier bears the price's move: the sanctuary-favoring asymmetry
  of LV25B-06 (Sifra Behar Chapter 5 6-7: the sanctuary permitted
  what the layman is not) and LV27-12 (the sanctuary may reckon
  months, the layman not).

## Lev 25:35-55 — interest, the Hebrew slave, the gentile slave (10
## rows)
- Mishnah Bava Metzia 5:1 — LAW (VERBATIM). "What is bite-interest
  and what is increase? Bite — lending a sela for five dinars, two
  seah of wheat for three; increase — increasing in produce: bought
  wheat at a gold dinar the kor... 'your wheat is reckoned at thirty
  and you have wine with me' — and he has no wine" — Sifra Behar
  Section 5 2 word for word, LV25B-14.
- Mishnah Bava Metzia 5:11 — LAW. "These transgress: the lender, the
  borrower, the guarantor, the witnesses; the Sages: the scribe too —
  by 'do not give' (25:37), 'do not take from him' (25:36)..." — the
  ban's addressee list on LV25B-14's clauses; the Sifra's "you may
  become his guarantor" (Section 5 2) is the other case — surety for
  the poor man toward a third party, not surety inside an interest
  loan — both recorded.
- Mishnah Nedarim 9:4 — LAW (VERBATIM). R. Meir opens a vow from
  Scripture — "'that your brother live with you' (Lev 25:36): perhaps
  he will become poor and you cannot support him" — the clause of
  LV25B-14 as a vow's opening.
- Mishnah Bava Metzia 1:5 — LAW. "The find of his Hebrew slave or
  maidservant... is theirs" — the hireling status of LV25C-02 (a
  hireling's finds are his own); the Canaanite slave's find is the
  master's — LV25C-08's "work only" reads the other way: the
  Canaanite is a holding.
- Mishnah Kiddushin 1:3 — LAW (VERBATIM). "A Canaanite slave is
  acquired by money, deed, and possession" — Sifra Behar Section 6 4
  word for word, LV25C-07.
- Mishnah Kiddushin 1:5 — LAW. "Property with responsibility (land)
  is acquired by money, deed, and possession; without — only by
  pulling" — the premise LV25C-07 imports from the land to the slave.
- Mishnah Makkot 2:8 — LAW (VERBATIM). "The exile returns to the
  office he held — R. Meir; R. Yehuda: he does not return" — Sifra
  Behar Chapter 7 4 ("R. Yehuda: not to his former office — and so
  the exile"; R. Meir: to what his family held), LV25C-03.
- Mishnah Makkot 3:8 — CREDIT. Defiling all day, warned each time —
  graded at round 45 (priest_purity_file); no Lev 25-27 cell.
- Mishnah Kiddushin 2:8 — CONTEXT. Betrothal with consecrated
  property or second tithe — a betrothal case, not this span's.
- Mishnah Kiddushin 2:9 — CONTEXT. Betrothal with forbidden items;
  not this span's case.

  SANCTITY EVEN WHEN DESOLATE" — the Sifra's synagogues-in-
  sanctuaries (Bechukotai Chapter 6 4) applied to the ruin, LV26-20.

- Mishnah Taanit 3:5 — LAW. Alarms sounded everywhere for blight,

  even for an army in transit — LV26-06's armies-in-transit (Sifra
  Bechukotai Chapter 2 3, Onkelos 26:6).
- Mishnah Eduyot 6:2 — CONTEXT. A limb from the dead; not this

- Mishnah Ketubot 5:2 — CONTEXT. Twelve months to a virgin; not this
  span's case.
- Mishnah Berakhot 7:1 — CONTEXT. Zimmun over redeemed second tithe

## Lev 27:1-25 — valuations, substitution, the house and the field
## (14 rows)
- Mishnah Arakhin 1:1 — LAW (VERBATIM). "All value and are valued,
  vow and are vowed — priests, Levites, Israelites, women, slaves;
  the tumtum and androgynos vow, are vowed, and value, but are not
  valued — only a certain male and a certain female; the deaf-mute,
  the imbecile, the minor are vowed and valued but neither vow nor
  value; under a month — vowed, not valued" — Sifra Bechukotai
  Section 3 1-2, 8-9 word for word (both R. Meir's and R. Yehuda's
  widths seated as the Mishnah's two clauses), LV27-01, LV27-02.
- Mishnah Arakhin 3:1 — LAW. "Valuations lenient and strict: the
  handsomest and the ugliest in Israel — fifty selas each; 'his
  worth on me' — his worth" — the fixed sums of LV27-02 against the
  worth-vow class.
- Mishnah Arakhin 3:2 — LAW (VERBATIM). "The sandy district and the
  orchards of Sebaste alike — a homer of barley seed at fifty
  shekels; a purchased field — its worth; R. Eliezer: both alike;
  the difference — the fifth" — Sifra Bechukotai Chapter 10 3 word
  for word, LV27-10; LV27-16 (R. Eliezer's reckon-reckon).
- Mishnah Arakhin 4:1 — LAW (VERBATIM). "Means by the vower, years
  by the valued, valuations by the valued, the valuation at its
  time; a poor man valuing a rich — the poor valuation" — Sifra
  Bechukotai Section 3 14 word for word, LV27-03.
- Mishnah Arakhin 4:4 — LAW (VERBATIM). "A child valuing an old man
  — the old valuation; a man valuing a woman — the woman's; crossed
  the age before payment — at the valuation's time; the thirtieth
  day as below; the fifth and twentieth year as below — 'year'-
  'year'; R. Elazar: until a month and a day past" — Sifra
  Bechukotai Section 3 9-12, 14 word for word, LV27-02, LV27-03.
- Mishnah Arakhin 7:1 — LAW (VERBATIM). "Not consecrated less than
  two years before the Jubilee, not redeemed less than a year after;
  months not reckoned against the sanctuary, the sanctuary reckons
  months; fifty shekels the homer; crevices ten handbreadths deep
  and rocks ten high not measured; a sela and a pundion per year;
  'year by year' — refused, all at once" — Sifra Bechukotai Chapter
  10 4-10 word for word, LV27-11, LV27-12.
- Mishnah Arakhin 7:5 — LAW (VERBATIM). "Bought from his father, the
  father died, then consecrated — a holding field; consecrated then
  the father died — a purchased field (R. Meir); R. Yehuda and R.
  Shimon: a holding field; a purchased field does not go to the
  priests — one consecrates only his own; priests and Levites
  consecrate and redeem always" — Sifra Bechukotai Chapter 11 4 word
  for word, LV27-16; Sifra Behar Chapter 6 4-5, LV25B-10.
- Mishnah Sanhedrin 1:3 — LAW. Consecrated things by three; movable
  valuations by three (R. Yehuda: one a priest); land nine and a
  priest; a person likewise — the priest as archetype, LV27-03,
  LV27-07.
- Mishnah Bekhorot 1:7 — LAW. "The redemption command: the OWNER
  precedes everyone — 'if not redeemed, sold at your valuation'"
  (27:27) — LV27-19's "not redeemed — by the owner; sold — to
  anyone," LV27-09's precedence.
- Mishnah Bava Metzia 4:8 — LAW. "Five fifths: ... the redeemer of
  his consecrated property adds a fifth; the redeemer of his second
  tithe adds a fifth" — LV27-08 (owner only), LV27-22 (the tithe's
  fifth).
- Mishnah Temurah 1:1 — LAW (VERBATIM). "All substitute, men and
  women — not that one may, but if he did it holds, and he is
  lashed; priests substitute their own; not the sin offering, guilt
  offering, or firstborn — R. Yochanan b. Nuri: why not the
  firstborn? R. Akiva: ... 'it and its substitute shall be holy' —
  where holiness falls, in the owner's house" — Sifra Bechukotai
  Chapter 9 6, 10-11 and Chapter 13 4 word for word, LV27-05,
  LV27-24.
- Mishnah Temurah 1:2 — LAW (VERBATIM). "Cattle for flock, sheep for
  goats, males for females, whole for blemished — 'good for bad';
  'good for bad' = blemished whose consecration preceded the
  blemish; one for two, one for a hundred; R. Shimon: one for one"
  — Sifra Bechukotai Chapter 9 6 word for word, LV27-05.
- Mishnah Temurah 1:6 — LAW (VERBATIM). "Birds and meal offerings
  make no substitute — 'beast'; the public and partners — 'he shall
  not substitute IT'; Temple-upkeep offerings none; R. Shimon: the
  tithe went out to teach — individual, altar" — Sifra Bechukotai
  Chapter 9 2-4, 7 word for word, LV27-04, LV27-05.
- Mishnah Beitzah 5:2 — CONTEXT. On a festival one does not
  consecrate, value, or devote — a festival restriction on the
  chapter's acts, not this span's case.

## Lev 27:26-34 — the firstborn, devotion, the tithes (12 rows)
- Mishnah Arakhin 8:5 — LAW (VERBATIM). "One who devotes his son,
  his daughter, his Hebrew slaves, his purchased field — not
  devoted, one devotes only his own; priests and Levites do not
  devote (R. Yehuda); R. Shimon: priests no — devotions are theirs;
  Levites yes; Rabbi: R. Yehuda for land, 'a perpetual holding'; R.
  Shimon for movables" — Sifra Bechukotai Chapter 12 3, 6 word for
  word, LV27-20.
- Mishnah Arakhin 8:6 — LAW. "Priests' devotions have no redemption
  — given to the priests; R. Yehuda b. Betera: unspecified devotions
  to Temple upkeep ('most holy to the LORD'); the Sages: to the
  priests ('as the devoted field, the priest's holding')" — LV27-20
  carries R. Yehuda b. Betera's arm from the Sifra (Chapter 12 5);
  the Sages' arm enters from the answer sheet.
- Mishnah Arakhin 8:7 — LAW. "One devotes his consecrated things,
  most-holy and lesser-holy; the firstborn, whole or blemished, is
  devoted; R. Yishmael: 'consecrate' and 'do not consecrate' —
  consecrate it for VALUE, not for the altar" — Sifra Bechukotai
  Chapter 12 6 and Section 5 2 word for word, LV27-18, LV27-20.
- Mishnah Challah 4:9 — LAW. "Given to any priest: the devotions,
  the firstborns..." — 27:21's "to the priest" as the devotion's
  destination in the Sages' arm, LV27-20.
- Mishnah Bekhorot 9:1 — LAW (VERBATIM). "The animal tithe: cattle
  and flock not tithed on each other; sheep and goats yes; new and
  old not — the a-fortiori refused by 'and flock' — all flock is
  one" — Sifra Bechukotai Chapter 12 11-13 word for word, LV27-23.
- Mishnah Bekhorot 9:7 — LAW (VERBATIM). "Counted by the rod...
  the tenth marked red; not marked, not counted by the rod, counted
  lying or standing — tithed; had a hundred and took ten, ten and
  took one — no tithe; R. Yosei b. R. Yehuda: a tithe" — Sifra
  Bechukotai Chapter 13 1 word for word, LV27-23.
- Mishnah Nazir 5:3 — LAW (VERBATIM). Beit Shammai to Beit Hillel:
  "one who erred and called the ninth tenth, the tenth ninth, the
  eleventh tenth — sanctified"; Beit Hillel: "not the rod sanctified
  it — the verse that sanctified the tenth sanctified the ninth and
  the eleventh" — Sifra Bechukotai Chapter 13 2-3, LV27-23; both
  houses agree on the error rule's result.
- Mishnah Nazir 1:2 — CONTEXT. Nazirite formulas; not this span's
  case.
- Mishnah Nazir 5:1 — CONTEXT. Mistaken consecration, the black ox;

- Mishnah Bekhorot 1:4 — CONTEXT. The firstborn donkey redeemed by a

- Mishnah Eduyot 4:5 — CONTEXT. The fourth-year vineyard's fifth and

- Mishnah Shabbat 18:1 — CONTEXT. Moving redeemed consecrated

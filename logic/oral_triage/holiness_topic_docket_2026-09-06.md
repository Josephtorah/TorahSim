## Mishnah Peah 1 — the no-measure list, the corner's floor, the classifier (6 rows)
- Mishnah Peah 1:1 — LAW. THINGS WITHOUT A MEASURE — the corner, first fruits, the appearance, kindness, Torah study; the things whose fruit is eaten here and whose principal stands for the world to come. THE CORNER HAS NO MEASURE IN THE INK: 19:9 writes 'you shall not finish the corner of your field' and no fraction — the quantity is a PARAMETER left open by the code (the 1/60 floor is the next row's, self-labeled 'though they said'); the other four items are other spans' (Deut 26, Exod 23:17, the ethics).
- Mishnah Peah 1:2 — LAW. NOT LESS THAN ONE-SIXTIETH for the corner, 'though they said the corner has no measure'; all by the field's size, the number of the poor, and the humility. The floor is the scribes' recorded fraction (Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 9: 'the last one-sixtieth must still stand'); the three scaling factors are data.
- Mishnah Peah 1:3 — LAW. the corner given from the field's start and its middle; R. Shimon: provided he gives the measure AT THE END; R. Yehuda: if he left one stalk he attaches to it as corner, otherwise it is only renunciation. Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 9 VERBATIM: the corner attaches at the FINISH ('you shall not FINISH'), needs a name, sits at the end — given at start or middle it counts, but the last sixtieth must stand.
- Mishnah Peah 1:4 — LAW. THE FIVE-PREDICATE CLASSIFIER: whatever is FOOD, GUARDED, GROWS FROM THE LAND, GATHERED AS ONE, and BROUGHT IN FOR KEEPING owes the corner; grain and legumes are inside. Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 7 VERBATIM — the pure specification row: vegetables out (not stored), figs out (not gathered as one); the ink's 'the harvest of your land' (19:9) read by five predicates.
- Mishnah Peah 1:5 — LAW. among trees — the sumac, carobs, nuts, almonds, vines, pomegranates, olives, dates owe the corner. Sifra, Kedoshim, Chapter 1 8 VERBATIM — the tree census (each passes the five predicates; the fig fails 'gathered as one').
- Mishnah Peah 1:6 — LAW. he gives the corner and is exempt from tithes UNTIL HE SMOOTHS the pile; renounces, feeds beasts, sows from the floor — exempt until the smoothing (R. Akiva); a priest or Levite who bought the floor — the tithes theirs until the smoothing; consecrated and redeemed — liable until the treasurer smooths. The corner's window: from the reaping (19:9 'when you reap') to the smoothing — the tithe threshold is Maasrot's [ROUTED edge]; the corner can still be given from the pile.

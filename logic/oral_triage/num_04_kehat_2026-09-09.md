## Onkelos Numbers 4:1-20 (20 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Num 4:1 — CONTEXT. The frame: "to Moses AND to Aaron, saying" — the second double-addressed frame (computed).
- Onkelos Num 4:2 — MATERIAL. THE HOUSE'S HEAD LIFTED. "נָשֹׂא אֶת רֹאשׁ בְּנֵי קְהָת ("lift the head of the sons of Kohath") from among the sons of Levi, by their families, by their fathers' house" → קַבִּילוּ יָת חֻשְׁבַּן בְּנֵי קְהָת ("receive the sum of the sons of Kohath") — the census idiom on ONE HOUSE (computed: the infinitive form at 4:2 and 4:22; the imperative at 1:2 and 26:2); the same accounting rendering.
- Onkelos Num 4:3 — MATERIAL. THE WORK'S AGES. "מִבֶּן שְׁלֹשִׁים שָׁנָה וָמַעְלָה וְעַד בֶּן חֲמִשִּׁים שָׁנָה ("from thirty years old and upward, and until fifty years old"), כָּל בָּא לַצָּבָא ("everyone who comes to the host") to do work in the tent of meeting" → מִבַּר תְּלָתִין שְׁנִין וּלְעֵלָּא וְעַד בַּר חַמְשִׁין שְׁנִין ("from a son of thirty years and upward, and until a son of fifty years"), כָּל דְּאָתֵי לְחֵילָא לְמֶעְבַּד עִבִדְתָּא ("everyone who comes to the host to do the work") — the THIRD THRESHOLD, a window: thirty to fifty (computed: the pair's seats in chapter 4 — 4:3, 23, 30, 35, 39, 43, 47); "the host" is the WORK-host, the army-word of 1:3 on the Levites' labor (Onkelos one word for both). THE SIFREI'S ROW (piska 62 — read fresh below) reconciles this thirty with 8:24's twenty-five: twenty-five to learn, thirty to serve.
- Onkelos Num 4:4 — MATERIAL. THE HOLY OF HOLIES. "This is the service of the sons of Kohath in the tent of meeting: קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִֽׁים ("the holy of holies")" → קֹדֶשׁ קוּדְשַׁיָּא ("the holy of holies") — the phrase at 4:4 and 4:19 in the span (computed): Kohath's load is the innermost.
- Onkelos Num 4:5 — MATERIAL. THE PRIESTS COVER FIRST. "And Aaron and his sons shall come in בִּנְסֹעַ הַֽמַּחֲנֶה ("when the camp journeys") and take down אֵת פָּרֹכֶת הַמָּסָךְ ("the veil of the screen") and cover with it the ark of the testimony" → בְּמִטַּל מַשְׁרִיתָא ("at the journeying of the camp"), וִיפָרְקוּן יָת פָּרֻכְתָּא דִפְרָסָא ("and they shall take down the veil of the screen"), וִיכַסּוּן בַּהּ יָת אֲרוֹנָא דְסַהֲדוּתָא ("and they shall cover with it the ark of the testimony") — THE VEIL BECOMES THE ARK'S COVER on the march; the priests, not the Kohathites, do the covering (4:15 the sequence: the covering first, the carrying after).
- Onkelos Num 4:6 — MATERIAL. THE ARK'S THREE LAYERS. "They shall put on it a covering of עוֹר תַּחַשׁ ("tachash hide") and spread בֶֽגֶד כְּלִיל תְּכֵלֶת ("a cloth wholly of blue") on top, and put in its poles" → חוֹפָאָה דִּמְשַׁךְ סַסְגּוֹנָא ("a covering of SASGONA hide") and לְבוּשׁ גְּמִיר תִּכְלָא ("a garment entirely of blue") — the tachash rendered by a colored-hide word (the exam: Shabbat 28a-b on what the tachash was); "wholly of blue" is the ROBE'S phrase (computed: Exod 28:31, Exod 39:22, Num 4:6 — the high priest's robe at Exod 28:31 and 39:22, the ark's outer cloth here): the ark travels dressed as the priest is; ONLY THE ARK has blue OUTSIDE the hide (the table, lampstand and altars have the hide outermost — 4:8, 10, 11, 12, 14 below), the ink's own ordering.
- Onkelos Num 4:7 — MATERIAL. THE TABLE. "On שֻׁלְחַן הַפָּנִים ("the table of the face" — the presence bread) they shall spread a blue cloth and put on it the dishes and the spoons and הַמְּנַקִּיֹּת ("the jars") and קְשׂוֹת הַנָּסֶךְ ("the jugs of libation"); and וְלֶחֶם הַתָּמִיד ("and the continual bread") shall be on it" → פָּתוֹרָא דִלְחֵם אַפַּיָּא ("the table of the bread of the face"), מָגִיסַיָּא ("the dishes"), בָּזִיכַיָּא ("the pans" — the frankincense bowls of Menachot 11), מְכִילָתָא ("the measures"), קַסְווֹת נִסּוּכָא ("the jugs of libation"), וּלְחֵם תְּדִירָא עֲלוֹהִי יְהֵי ("and the continual bread shall be on it") — THE BREAD TRAVELS ON THE TABLE: the ink keeps it "continually" on the table even on the march (the exam: Menachot 99b, "before Me continually" — never removed).
- Onkelos Num 4:8 — CONTEXT. "They shall spread on them a cloth of תּוֹלַעַת שָׁנִי ("crimson worm") and cover it with a covering of tachash hide and put in its poles" → צְבַע זְהוֹרִי ("crimson dye") — the table's second cloth; the hide outermost.
- Onkelos Num 4:9 — MATERIAL. THE LAMPSTAND. "They shall take a blue cloth and cover מְנֹרַת הַמָּאוֹר ("the lampstand of the light") and its lamps and its tongs and its fire-pans and all its oil vessels with which they serve it" → מְנַרְתָּא דְאַנְהוֹרֵי ("the lampstand of the lights"), בּוֹצִינָהָא ("its lamps"), צִבְתָהָא ("its tongs"), מַחְתְּיתָהָא ("its fire-pans") — the lampstand and its whole service kit under one cloth.
- Onkelos Num 4:10 — CONTEXT. "They shall put it and all its vessels into a covering of tachash hide and put it עַל הַמּֽוֹט ("on the bar")" → עַל אֲרִיחָא ("on the pole") — carried on a bar, not by rings (the lampstand has no poles of its own).
- Onkelos Num 4:11 — CONTEXT. "On מִזְבַּח הַזָּהָב ("the golden altar") they shall spread a blue cloth and cover it with a covering of tachash hide and put in its poles" → מַדְבְּחָא דְדַהֲבָא ("the altar of gold") — blue under, hide over.
- Onkelos Num 4:12 — CONTEXT. "They shall take all the vessels of service with which they serve in the holy and put them in a blue cloth and cover them with a covering of tachash hide and put them on the bar" → מָנֵי שִׁמּוּשָׁא ("the vessels of service") — the loose vessels bundled, on a bar.
- Onkelos Num 4:13 — MATERIAL. THE ALTAR ASHED. "וְדִשְּׁנוּ אֶת הַמִּזְבֵּחַ ("and they shall remove the ashes of the altar") and spread on it a cloth of אַרְגָּמָֽן ("purple")" → וְיִסְּפוּן יָת קִטְמָא דְמַדְבְּחָא ("and they shall clear away the ashes of the altar"), לְבוּשׁ אַרְגְּוָן ("a garment of purple") — THE BRONZE ALTAR IS THE ONE THING NOT DRESSED IN BLUE: ashed and covered in purple (the exam: the fire kept burning under the cloth on the march — Yoma 21b; the Sifrei at 4:13 is off this span); the ash-verb of Exod 27:3's ash-pots.
- Onkelos Num 4:14 — CONTEXT. "They shall put on it all its vessels with which they serve on it: the fire-pans, the forks, the shovels, the basins, all the vessels of the altar; and spread on it a covering of tachash hide and put in its poles" → מַחְתְּיָתָא ("the fire-pans"), צִנּוֹרְיָתָא ("the forks"), מַגְרוֹפְיָתָא ("the shovels"), מִזְרְקַיָּא ("the basins") — the altar's kit; the hide outermost (computed: tachash at 4:6, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14).
- Onkelos Num 4:15 — MATERIAL. THE SEQUENCE AND THE FIRST DEATH CLAUSE. "And Aaron and his sons shall finish covering the holy and all the vessels of the holy when the camp journeys, and וְאַחֲרֵי כֵן יָבֹאוּ בְנֵי קְהָת לָשֵׂאת ("after that the sons of Kohath shall come to carry"); וְלֹֽא יִגְּעוּ אֶל הַקֹּדֶשׁ וָמֵתוּ ("and they shall not touch the holy, lest they die") — these are מַשָּׂא בְנֵֽי קְהָת ("the burden of the sons of Kohath") in the tent of meeting" → וִישֵׁיצֵי אַהֲרֹן וּבְנוֹהִי לְכַסָּאָה יָת קוּדְשָׁא ("and Aaron and his sons shall finish covering the holy"), וּבָתַר כֵּן יֵעֲלוּן בְּנֵי קְהָת לְמִטַּל ("and after that the sons of Kohath shall enter to carry"), וְלָא יִקְרְבוּן לְקוּדְשָׁא וְלָא יְמוּתוּן ("and they shall not APPROACH the holy, and they shall not die") — the touching rendered as approaching (a wider guard); the ORDER is the law: the priests finish, then the carriers enter; the death clause the first of three in 4:15-20 (computed).
- Onkelos Num 4:16 — MATERIAL. ELEAZAR'S CHARGE. "And the appointment of Eleazar son of Aaron the priest: שֶׁמֶן הַמָּאוֹר ("the oil of the light") and וּקְטֹרֶת הַסַּמִּים ("the incense of spices") and וּמִנְחַת הַתָּמִיד ("the continual meal-offering") and וְשֶׁמֶן הַמִּשְׁחָה ("the anointing oil"); the charge of all the tabernacle and all that is in it, in the holy and in its vessels" → וְדִי מְסִיר לְאֶלְעָזָר ("and what is handed over to Eleazar"), מִשְׁחָא דְאַנְהָרוּתָא ("the oil of lighting"), וּקְטֹרֶת בּוּסְמַיָּא ("and the incense of spices"), וּמִנְחָתָא תְדִירָא ("and the continual meal-offering"), וּמִשְׁחָא דִרְבוּתָא ("and the oil of greatness" — the anointing oil) — the four consumables carried by the priest himself (the exam: Eleazar bore them, the Sifrei Zuta / Yerushalmi Shabbat 10:3 — outside scope), his charge over the whole.
- Onkelos Num 4:17 — CONTEXT. The frame: "to Moses AND to Aaron, saying" — the third double-addressed frame (computed).
- Onkelos Num 4:18 — MATERIAL. CUT NOT OFF. "אַל תַּכְרִיתוּ אֶת שֵׁבֶט מִשְׁפְּחֹת הַקְּהָתִי ("do not cut off the tribe of the families of the Kohathite") from among the Levites" → לָא תְשֵׁיצוּן יָת שִׁבְטָא זַרְעֲיַת קְהָת ("do not destroy the tribe of the family of Kohath") — the cutting-off verb's one seat in this form (computed): the guard laid on MOSES AND AARON — their negligence would cut the house off; the cutting-off word (karet, "excision") turned on the leaders.
- Onkelos Num 4:19 — MATERIAL. THE REMEDY. "This do for them, וְחָיוּ וְלֹא יָמֻתוּ ("that they may live and not die") when they approach the holy of holies: Aaron and his sons shall come in and set them אִישׁ אִישׁ עַל עֲבֹדָתוֹ וְאֶל מַשָּׂאֽוֹ ("each man, each man to his service and to his burden")" → וְיֵחוּן וְלָא יְמוּתוּן ("and they shall live and not die"), וִימַנּוּן יָתְהוֹן גְּבַר גְּבַר עַל פּוּלְחָנֵיהּ וּלְמַטּוּלֵיהּ ("and they shall APPOINT them, each man, each man to his service and to his burden") — the assignment is per man (the doubled "man" kept); the second death clause; the priests assign, the Levites bear.
- Onkelos Num 4:20 — MATERIAL. NOT TO SEE THE SWALLOWING. "And they shall not come in to see כְּבַלַּע אֶת הַקֹּדֶשׁ ("as the holy is swallowed") lest they die" → לְמֶחֱזֵי כַּד מְכַסָּן יָת מָנֵי קוּדְשָׁא ("to see WHEN THEY COVER the VESSELS of the holy") — the swallowing verb (the word's ONE seat in the Tanakh, computed) rendered as covering and the object expanded to "the vessels": the moment forbidden to the eye is the packing (the exam's Talmud reads the swallowing as the taking-down — Sanhedrin 81b; the Sifrei off this span); the third death clause: touching (4:15), approaching unassigned (4:19), seeing (4:20).

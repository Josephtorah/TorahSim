### Rosh Hashanah 3a:1-13 — Nisan for kings: the verse ladder
- 3a:1 — the Canaanite-heard frame (Aaron's death and the clouds)
  — context.
- 3a:2 — R. Abbahu's re-read ("they SAW" read "they were SEEN")
  with Reish Lakish's four senses of כִּי ("for/when/that/
  perhaps") — MATERIAL: the credited row, the chain's re-reading
  tool.
- 3a:3 — the Sihon/Arad/Canaan identification (one king, three
  names, each expounded) — MATERIAL rider.
- 3a:4 — could the year-head be IYAR? — the challenge — context.
- 3a:5 — NO: "in the FIRST month of the SECOND year the
  tabernacle was ERECTED" (40:17) + "in the SECOND year in the
  SECOND month the cloud lifted" (Num 10:11) — standing in Nisan
  it is year two, standing in Iyar it is STILL year two: the
  year-label did not flip between them, so Iyar heads no year —
  MATERIAL: the erection date as the chain's load-bearing rung.
- 3a:6 — could it be SIVAN? NO: "in the THIRD month of the
  going-out" (19:1) — were Sivan a year-head the verse must say
  "in the third month IN THE SECOND YEAR" — MATERIAL: the second
  Exodus rung, an argument from the verse's own silence.
- 3a:7 — Tammuz, Av, Adar raised and dismissed — context.
- 3a:8-10 — R. Elazar's Chronicles rung ("the second month in the
  fourth year of his reign") with Ravina's two objections
  answered (the second-second parallel) — MATERIAL rider.
- 3a:11-13 — the baraita per R. Yochanan: the FULL LADDER — the
  480th-year verse, Aaron's ascent to Hor, the fortieth-year
  eleventh-month verse, after-Sihon, the Canaanite heard, the
  congregation saw, the erection verse, the cloud verse, the
  third-month verse, the Chronicles verse — kings are counted
  only from NISAN — MATERIAL: the chain complete, ten rungs, two
  of them this book's.

### Rosh Hashanah 7a:15-18 — the shekels' year-head
- 7a:15-16 — the intercalation-announcement frame — context.
- 7a:17 — the new-terumah rule: "this is the burnt-offering of
  each month in its month for the months of the YEAR" (Num
  28:14) — bring the month's offering from the NEW terumah; and
  year-year is learned from NISAN: "it is FIRST for you for the
  months of the YEAR" (12:2) — MATERIAL: the credited row.
- 7a:18 — why not year-year from Tishrei's verse? — derive a
  YEAR-WITH-MONTHS from a year-with-months; the Tishrei verse
  carries no months — MATERIAL: the analogy-SELECTION rule (the
  source must share the feature carried), a middah-governance
  row.

### Rosh Hashanah 9a:1-5 — the harvest rest and the holy margin
- 9a:1 — the jubilee count frame — context.
- 9a:2 — whence adding from the profane onto the holy? — frame.
- 9a:3 — "in plowing and in harvest you shall rest" (34:21) — R.
  AKIVA: the seventh year's own plowing and harvest are already
  banned (Lev 25:4) — so this is the EVE-year's plowing entering
  the seventh and the seventh's harvest exiting it: sanctity
  EXTENDED at both edges — MATERIAL: the credited row.
- 9a:4 — R. YISHMAEL: as plowing is optional so the harvest
  meant is optional — EXCLUDING the omer harvest, which is a
  mitzva (and overrides): the verse assigned to the Sabbath, the
  omer carved out — MATERIAL: the dispute's second arm.
- 9a:5 — R. Yishmael's own margin-source (the ninth-at-evening
  fast) — MATERIAL rider.

### Rosh Hashanah 13a:1-4 — the year seam
- 13a:1 — the frame question — context.
- 13a:2 — "the festival of the INGATHERING at the going-OUT of

### Rosh Hashanah 20a:12-16 + 20b:8-12 — month mechanics

- 20a:14 — the baraita: could the court intercalate the MONTH at

- 25a:3 — R. Dosa's forged-witnesses protest and R. Yehoshua's

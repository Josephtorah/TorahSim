### The ark: funding, verbs, crown, staves, jar (25:8-15)
- Yoma 3b:2 — MATERIAL (frame). THE FUNDING DISPUTE: קח לך
  ("take YOURSELF") = from your own, ויקחו אליך ("they shall
  take to you") = from the public — R. Yoshiyah; R. Yonatan:
  both from the public, and lekha is ENDEARMENT — "as if from
  yours: I want yours more than theirs."
- Yoma 3b:3 — MATERIAL (docket). THE VERBS HARMONIZED: Abba
  Chanan per R. Elazar — one verse "you shall make YOURSELF an
  ark" (Deut 10:1), another "THEY shall make an ark" (25:10):
  here when Israel does the Presence's will (the public makes),
  here when they do not (the leader alone) — a WILL-INDEXED
  dispatch on the making verb.
- Yoma 3b:4 — MATERIAL rider: the split bounded — general
  takings/makings disputed; the installation's take-lekha proven
  from-your-own by the goat/calf verse pair (the two commands'
  redundancy).
- Temurah 31b:6 — context (the offering-body frame).
- Temurah 31b:7 — MATERIAL (docket). THE TREASURY SPLIT:
  craftsmen are not paid from ALTAR consecrations but are from
  MAINTENANCE consecrations — R. Abahu: ועשו לי ("and they
  shall make ME," 25:8) read "from what is MINE" — the
  sanctuary's two purses divided at the sanctuary verse itself.
- Temurah 31b:8 — context (unspecified consecrations default to
  maintenance — R. Yehoshua's frame).
- Yoma 72b:6 — MATERIAL (frame). THE THREE CROWNS: altar
  (Aaron's), table (David's), ark — STILL LYING: whoever wishes,
  let him come and take (the Torah's crown open).
- Yoma 72b:7 — MATERIAL (docket). ZER/ZAR: written זר
  ("stranger"), read זֵר ("crown") — merited: it becomes his
  CROWN; not merited: it becomes ESTRANGED from him — a
  ketiv/qere verdict on the ark's own molding (the ink channel
  ruling inside the law channel, the script-witness family).
- Yoma 72b:8 — MATERIAL rider: the two making-verbs again — the
  scholar's townspeople do his work for him (a second dispatch
  of the same verse-pair, carried beside Abba Chanan's).
- Yoma 72a:8 — MATERIAL (frame). The garment-tearer flogged (לא
  יקרע, 28:32) — with Rav Acha bar Yaakov's probe: perhaps the
  verse is DESIGN SPEC ("make a hem SO THAT it not tear")? — "is
  it written 'so that it not tear'?"
- Yoma 72a:9 — MATERIAL (docket). THE TWO STANDING PROHIBITIONS:
  R. Elazar — detaching the breastplate from the ephod (לא יזח,
  28:28) and removing the ark's staves (לא יסרו, 25:15) — lashes
  both; Rav Acha's design-spec probe fired at both and answered
  both times by the bare negative's ink.
- Yoma 72a:10 — MATERIAL rider: R. Yosei b. R. Chanina's
  contradiction — "the staves SHALL BE in the rings, they shall
  not be removed" against the insertion verses — resolved:
  מתפרקין ואין נשמטין ("they loosen but do not slip out") — the
  rings hold loose staves: a mechanical spec from two verses.
- Yoma 52b:13 — context (Josiah hid the ark — the Chronicles

- Yoma 52b:14 — MATERIAL (docket). THE MANNA JAR'S PLACE: R.

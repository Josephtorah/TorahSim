## Onkelos Exodus 35:30-36:38 — read IN FULL, 44 verses

- Onkelos Exod 35:30-33 — MATERIAL, the span's head. The appointment
  re-announced TO THE PEOPLE: חֲזוֹ דְּרַבִּי יְיָ בְּשׁוּם בְּצַלְאֵל ("SEE,
  the LORD has EXALTED BY NAME Bezalel") — the public-name token
  again at the execution side (EX31-01's seat confirmed); the
  endowment upgraded: רוּחַ נְבוּאָה מִן קֳדָם יְיָ ("a spirit of
  PROPHECY from before the LORD") — the translation names the craft
  spirit prophecy; and at 35:32 the delta: Hebrew "to devise
  designs" rendered וּלְאַלָּפָא אוּמַנְוָן ("and to TEACH
  craftsmanship") — the teaching duty read INTO the devising verb.
- Onkelos Exod 35:34-35 — MATERIAL. THE TEACHING GRANT EXPLICIT:
  וּלְאַלָּפָא יְהַב בְּלִבֵּיהּ ("and TO TEACH He put in his heart") —
  he AND Oholiab of Dan; 35:35 closes with וּמַלְפֵי אוּמַנְוָן ("and
  TEACHERS of craftsmanship") where Hebrew has "designers" — the
  master-as-teacher doubled in one span (EX31's seat, now with the
  translation converting even the design-words to teaching-words).
- Onkelos Exod 36:1-4 — MATERIAL. The handoff: Bezalel, Oholiab,
  and EVERY wise-hearted man דִּיהַב יְיָ חָכְמָא וְסוּכְלְתָנוּתָא בְּהוֹן
  ("in whom the LORD had put wisdom and understanding") take the
  materials FROM BEFORE MOSES; each craftsman comes גְּבַר גְּבַר
  מֵעִבִדְתֵּיהּ ("each man from his task") — the per-task
  organization of the workforce.
- Onkelos Exod 36:5-7 — MATERIAL, the span's crown. THE OVERFLOW
  PROTOCOL: the craftsmen report upward — מַסְגַּן עַמָּא לְאַיְתָאָה
  ("the people bring TOO MUCH, more than the work needs"); Moses
  orders a herald through the camp — גְּבַר וְאִתְּתָא לָא יַעַבְדוּן עוֹד
  עִבִדְתָּא לְאַפְרָשׁוּת קוּדְשָׁא ("let no man or woman do any more
  work for the sacred offering") — וּפְסַק עַמָּא מִלְאַיְתָאָה ("and
  the people STOPPED bringing"); and 36:7 the paradoxical audit
  line: מִסַּת לְכָל עִבִדְתָּא ('enough for all the work') … וִיתָרָת ("ENOUGH for all the
  work — AND SOME LEFT OVER") — the input-halt on a full buffer,
  with the restraint itself recorded as obedience. The case shelf
  meets this verse at Mishnah Shevuot 1:1 (the carryings-out of
  Shabbat, two that are four — the Talmud derives the CARRYING
  labor from this camp-wide stop order: bringing from private tents
  to the public collection is 'work' the herald could forbid).
- Onkelos Exod 36:8-19 — MATERIAL (spec re-run). The curtains
  executed to the Terumah spec: ten linen-and-wool drapes 28×4
  cubits, five joined to five, fifty loops opposite fifty, fifty
  gold clasps — וַהֲוָה מַשְׁכְּנָא חָד ("and the tabernacle WAS ONE");
  eleven goats-hair drapes 30×4, five and six, fifty copper clasps
  לְמֶהֱוֵי חָד ("to BE ONE"); the ram-skin and multicolored-skin
  (סַסְגּוֹנָא 'the multicolored skin') covers. Numbers verified against the command chapter —
  the execution repeats the spec verse-shape for verse-shape,
  standing verdicts EX26-01..04 credited (quick-look passed: same
  counts, same joins).
- Onkelos Exod 36:20-34 — MATERIAL. The frame: דַּפַּיָּא ('planks') … קָיְמִין
  ("PLANKS... STANDING upright") — the standing-boards token again
  (the commandment-objects-held-as-they-grow rule, Terumah round's
  seat, confirmed at the execution); twenty south, twenty north,
  six west, two corner-planks coupled below and ringed above; forty
  + forty + sixteen silver sockets, two per plank; five bars per
  wall and the MIDDLE BAR through the planks מִן סְיָפֵי לִסְיָפֵי
  ("from end to end"); gold overlay, gold rings.
- Onkelos Exod 36:35-38 — MATERIAL. The veil made: תִּכְלָא
  וְאַרְגְּוָנָא וּצְבַע זְהוֹרִי וּבוּץ שְׁזִיר ("greenish-blue wool, dark
  red wool, crimson wool, and twined fine linen"), cherub-figured,
  עוֹבַד אָמָּן ("craftsman's work"); its four gold-clad pillars on
  four silver sockets; the entrance screen עוֹבַד צַיָּר
  ("embroiderer's work") on five pillars with copper sockets — the
  two-grade textile law (craftsman inside, embroiderer at the door)
  executed as commanded.

## Midrash Tanchuma anchored in 35:30-36:38 — 41 sections

- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 1:1 (⇐ Exod 35:30) — MATERIAL, the
  portion's yelamdenu. THE MIXED-CLOTH LAW AT THE WOOL-AND-LINEN
  BUILDING: R. Shimon bar Yochai — a garment of wool-and-linen
  (שַׁעַטְנֵז, "mixed stuff") may not be WORN even over a hundred
  garments, but may be SPREAD BENEATH one (the verse says עָלֶיךָ
  "upon you" — worn, not under you); opened at the very portion
  whose fabrics lawfully mix wool and linen — the sanctuary as the
  licensed exception's home. Rides with Ben Azzai's
  mitzvah-drags-mitzvah (one commandment draws another) and R. Meir's angel-per-commandment; and
  THE THREE NAMES: the one from parents, the one from peers, and
  the one a man EARNS — the earned one best of all.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 1:2 (⇐ Exod 35:30) — MATERIAL. The
  commission as the earned name's receipt: Bezalel got the work
  BECAUSE of his good name ("a good name is rather to be chosen
  than great riches").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 1:3 — enrichment. Good name vs the day
  of death (Solomon's answer: at birth none knows; at death the
  deeds are known).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 1:4 — enrichment. Why OIL of all
  fluids: oil floats where others mix — the good-name bearer rises.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 1:5 — enrichment. Name ascends, oil
  descends; name forever, oil transient.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 1:6 — enrichment. Oil for the rich,
  name for poor and rich; the corpse comparisons (Elisha, Jonah,
  the furnace).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 1:7 — MATERIAL (light). The
  anointed-vs-named table: Nadab and Abihu entered ANOINTED and
  came out burned; Hananiah, Mishael, Azariah entered the furnace
  un-anointed and came out alive — the good name outperforms the
  anointing oil; Miriam's well and Aaron's cloud-pillar named as
  the death-day disclosures.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 2:1 (⇐ Exod 35:31) — MATERIAL, the
  cluster's crown. WISDOM IS GIVEN ONLY TO THOSE WHO ALREADY HAVE
  IT: the lady's question to R. Yose ben Halafta ("should not He
  give wisdom to FOOLS?") answered from her own lending practice —
  one lends to the rich (who can repay), so the Holy One fills with
  wisdom him in whom wisdom already sits ("He giveth wisdom to the
  wise," Daniel 2:21) — the initialization precondition on the
  craft-spirit grant.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 2:2 — MATERIAL (light). The shopkeeper
  parable (he smells the bottle and refills in kind); Joshua the
  second exhibit ("FULL of the spirit of wisdom" — already having).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 3:1 (⇐ Exod 35:30) — MATERIAL. Moses
  believed HE would build (every command said "THOU shalt make");
  when the list closed he asked "who will do all this?" — the
  answer: "I have called BY NAME Bezalel." The architect learns the
  builder's identity only at handoff.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 3:2 — MATERIAL, the governance crown.
  THE NEPOTISM SUSPICION AND THE PUBLIC ANSWER: Israel murmured —
  Moses is king, Aaron high priest, his sons deputy priests,
  Eleazar chief of the Levites, the Kohathites bear the tabernacle,
  and now the works contract to his own kinsman! Moses answers: "I
  did nothing of my own; the Holy One said it" — and SHOWS THEM THE
  VERSE ("See, the LORD has called by name"). The appointment
  published against the conflict-of-interest charge — grace in the
  eyes of God AND man.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 3:3 — MATERIAL (light). The name
  decoded: Bezalel = "he made SHADE for God" (בְּצֵל אֵל, "in the
  shadow of God"; Psalm 91:1).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 4:1 — context. "To whom will you liken
  Me" opened.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 4:2 — enrichment. The night-light
  gratitude argument; the these-verses merit chain.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 4:3 — MATERIAL (light). The two verses
  reconciled — "He gave them all NAMES" vs "He calls them all BY A
  NAME": when He wishes, one name summons all as one; when He
  wishes, each by his own name (Michael, Gabriel) — the addressing
  scheme above.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 4:4 — MATERIAL (light). The same
  naming law shown BELOW from our verse: "called BY NAME Bezalel."
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 4:6 (⇐ Exod 35:30) — MATERIAL. WHY
  HUR IS IN THE PEDIGREE: Hur gave his life opposing the calf (the
  commander-in-chief parable — money could be repaid, a life is
  repaid in descendants raised to fame) — the grandfather's
  martyrdom paid in the grandson's commission; the calf-crisis
  seat (exo_32's sitting) now carrying its recorded reward.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 4:7 (⇐ Exod 36:1) — MATERIAL. Wisdom
  granted to ALL the workers, not Bezalel alone — and the ink
  pressed further: בָּהֵמָּה ("in whom") read as the animal-word —
  even the beasts doing the work were made wise; only Bezalel was
  PUBLISHED by name.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 4:8 — MATERIAL (light). The merit
  chain: the tribe of Judah, and the wisdom from HIS MOTHER'S merit
  — Miriam.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 4:9 — MATERIAL (light). The rehearsal
  dispute: the sages — every utterance said TWICE to Himself before
  Israel hears it; R. Acha — FOUR times (the four verbs of Job
  28:27) — the oracle's own revision counts; learned from the
  midwives episode (the astrologers, "every son that is born"
  including the Egyptians).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 4:10 — MATERIAL. THE MIDWIVES' REWARD
  TABLE: the houses = priesthood and kingship to Jochebed (Aaron
  and Moses), and WISDOM to Miriam — whence Bezalel; the
  fear-of-the-LORD-is-wisdom equation closing on our verse.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 5:1 (⇐ Exod 35:31) — MATERIAL, the
  round's architecture crown. THE THREE ATTRIBUTES ARE THE WORLD'S
  OWN TOOLCHAIN: wisdom, understanding, knowledge — the very three
  by which the world was created ("the LORD by WISDOM founded the
  earth; by UNDERSTANDING He established the heavens; by His
  KNOWLEDGE the depths were broken up," Proverbs 3:19-20) — and
  with the same three Bezalel built the tabernacle: the microcosm
  doctrine stated as a toolchain identity.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 5:2 — MATERIAL. The identity extended
  down the timeline: the Temple built with the same three (Hiram,
  1 Kings 7:14), and the FUTURE house too ("through wisdom is a
  house builded," Proverbs 24:3-4) — one toolchain, four builds
  (world, tabernacle, Temple, the one to come).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 5:3 — enrichment. Judah's blessing as
  the tribe's spirit-source (Othniel).
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 5:4 — MATERIAL (light). Bezalel's
  triple decoded curricularly: wise IN TORAH, understanding IN
  HALAKHAH (the law), knowledge FULL OF TALMUD (the learning) — and
  "in all workmanship" as its plain sense; the craftsman's stack
  read as the scholar's stack.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 9:1 (⇐ Exod 36:20) — MATERIAL. THE
  FRUIT-TREE RULE AT THE BOARDS: the King to whom all belongs built
  His own house from NON-fruit-bearing acacia — how much more must
  a man spare fruit trees when he builds (R. Tachalifa of
  Caesarea) — the waste-prohibition a-fortiori seated on the
  planks' species.
- Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel 9:2 — MATERIAL. מִשְׁכָּן read as
  מַשְׁכּוֹן ("PLEDGE"): if Israel incurs guilt the tabernacle is
  SEIZED AS COLLATERAL for them; Moses' question — when there is
  neither tabernacle nor Temple, what then? — answered: the
  RIGHTEOUS are taken as the pledge ("He slew all that were
  pleasant to the eye").
- Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei 5:4 (⇐ Exod 36:3-7) — MATERIAL
  (attributed here by anchor). R. Yohanan's count: TWO MORNINGS
  brought everything — בַּבֹּקֶר בַּבֹּקֶר ("morning, morning") counted
  as exactly two — then the halt order and the enough-and-left-over
  line; a token-census claim on the doubled word, opened in full.

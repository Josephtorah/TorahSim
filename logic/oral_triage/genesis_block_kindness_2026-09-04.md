### Bava Kamma 92a:15-17, 93a:2-4 (6)
- 92a:15 — MATERIAL. Rava asks Rabbah bar Mari the source: who
  prays for his fellow while needing the same thing is ANSWERED
  FIRST — Job's captivity turned when he prayed for his friends.
- 92a:16 — MATERIAL (docket). Rabbah bar Mari answers from THERE;
  Rava says מהכא ("from HERE"): ויתפלל אברהם ("Abraham PRAYED,"
  20:17 — for Abimelech's household) and וה' פקד את שרה כאשר אמר
  ("the LORD VISITED Sarah AS HE HAD SAID," 21:1) — כאשר אמר
  אברהם אל אבימלך ("as ABRAHAM HAD SAID over Abimelech"): the
  as-He-said clause read as the answered-first receipt. Beside
  G37-30's standing remembrance-dispute seat on the same verse.
- 92a:17 — context (the next folk-saying in the chain).
- 93a:2 — context (property follows its owner — Lot with Abram).
- 93a:3 — MATERIAL (docket). Rav Chanan: המוסר דין על חבירו הוא
  נענש תחילה ("who HANDS his fellow to Heaven's JUDGMENT is
  punished FIRST"): Sarai's חמסי עליך ("my WRONG be upon you,"
  16:5) — and the record answers at ויבא אברהם לספד לשרה ("Abraham
  came to EULOGIZE Sarah," 23:2). The condition rides the row:
  only where earthly recourse exists.
- 93a:4 — MATERIAL. R. Yitzchak's rider: woe to the crier more
  than the cried-against; the baraita — both are in the decree,
  but they hasten to the crier first.

### Berakhot 18a:2-4, 54b:7-11, 64a:9-11 (11)
- 18a:2 — context (what divides the two rulings).
- 18a:3 — MATERIAL (docket). The mourner before burial is EXEMPT
  from the Shema, the prayer, the tefillin, and every obligation
  in the Torah; Rav Ashi's ground: כיון שמוטל עליו לקוברו כמוטל
  לפניו דמי ("since the burial LIES UPON HIM, it is as if the
  dead lies BEFORE HIM") — ויקם אברהם מעל פני מתו ("Abraham ROSE
  from before his DEAD," 23:3) with ואקברה מתי מלפני ("that I may
  BURY my dead from BEFORE ME," 23:4): the duty-status read off
  the doubled from-before-me token.
- 18a:4 — context (his own dead yes, a watchman no).
- 54b:7 — context (the blessing-station list: Moses' stone).
- 54b:8 — MATERIAL (docket). ותבט אשתו מאחריו ותהי נציב מלח ("his
  wife LOOKED BACK behind him and became a PILLAR OF SALT,"
  19:26) — the pillar in the must-bless station list beside
  Jericho's wall.
- 54b:9 — MATERIAL. The objection: the others are miracles — this
  is a PUNISHMENT! Answer: on her one says ברוך דיין האמת
  ("Blessed... the TRUE JUDGE").
- 54b:10 — MATERIAL (docket). The resolved teaching: on Lot and
  his wife TWO blessings — the true Judge on her; on Lot, ברוך
  זוכר את הצדיקים ("Blessed... who REMEMBERS the righteous");
  R. Yochanan: even in His anger He remembers — ויזכר אלהים את
  אברהם ("God REMEMBERED Abraham," 19:29) and sent Lot out of the
  overthrow.
- 54b:11 — context (Jericho's wall swallowed — the geometry
  answer).
- 64a:9 — MATERIAL. R. Avin HaLevi: to the LIVING never לך בשלום
  ("go IN peace") but לך לשלום ("go TO peace") — Jethro's
  to-peace sent Moses up in success; David's in-peace sent
  Absalom to the tree.
- 64a:10 — MATERIAL (docket). And from the DEAD the reverse: only
  לך בשלום ("go IN peace") — ואתה תבוא אל אבתיך בשלום ("you shall
  COME TO YOUR FATHERS IN PEACE," 15:15): the covenant vision's
  own promise clause as the parting formula's source.
- 64a:11 — context (from synagogue to study house — the chapter's
  close).

### Ketubot 8b:9-11, 50a:2-4 (6)
- 8b:9 — context (never open a mouth to the accuser).
- 8b:10 — MATERIAL (docket). The mourners' consolation formula on
  its feet: אחינו גומלי חסדים בני גומלי חסדים המחזיקים בבריתו של
  אברהם אבינו ("our brothers, DOERS OF KINDNESS, sons of doers of
  kindness, who HOLD THE COVENANT of Abraham our father") — the
  proof: כי ידעתיו למען אשר יצוה את בניו ("for I have KNOWN him,
  that he will COMMAND his children," 18:19): the kindness
  lineage clause inside the liturgy's own words.
- 8b:11 — context (the next formula — against all Israel).
- 50a:2 — MATERIAL. באושא התקינו ("at USHA they ordained"): the
  spender may not spend MORE THAN A FIFTH lest he need the
  creatures — with the case of the one R. Yeshevav (or R. Akiva)
  stopped.
- 50a:3 — MATERIAL (docket). Rav Nachman (or Rav Acha bar
  Yaakov): מאי קרא ("what is the VERSE?") — וכל אשר תתן לי עשר
  אעשרנו לך ("all You give me I will surely TITHE-TITHE to You,"
  28:22): Jacob's vow verb DOUBLED — two tenths, the fifth.
- 50a:4 — MATERIAL. The arithmetic pressed: the second tenth of
  the remainder is smaller! Rav Ashi: אעשרנו לבתרא כי קמא ("I
  will tithe the SECOND like the FIRST") — the doubled verb's own
  grammar carries the equal-tenths reading; the cap is exactly a
  fifth.

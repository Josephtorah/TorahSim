## Sifra, Emor — Section 1 (16 rows)
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 1 — MATERIAL. THE ADDRESSEE CENSUS at
  21:1: "sons of Aaron" — the SONS do not defile for the dead, the
  DAUGHTERS of Aaron do; "the priests" excludes the חללים (chalalim,
  the profaned); "sons of Aaron" INCLUDES the blemished and the
  minors. Four populations settled by two tokens.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 2 — MATERIAL. "For a soul" — not only the
  corpse: BLOOD included, by "for the blood is the soul"
  (Deut 12:23) — a quarter-log defiles.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 3 — MATERIAL. "And say to them" — the
  impurities that DEPART from the dead included; "among his people"
  — when his people are there he does not defile, but he DOES
  defile for a מת מצוה (met mitzvah, an abandoned corpse): the
  override built from the "among his people" condition.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 4 — MATERIAL. THE RELATIVE TABLE opens:
  שְׁאֵרוֹ (she'ero, "his flesh") = HIS WIFE (from "she is your
  father's flesh"); "the near" excludes the BETROTHED; "to him"
  excludes the DIVORCED. Mother and father both written — the
  mother's line profanable, the father's by PRESUMPTION only
  — חזקה (chazakah, presumption): father written to cover presumption.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 5 — MATERIAL. The mirror argument: father
  written alone would not cover a PROFANED mother; "for his mother"
  covers her. Both tokens load-bearing, neither derivable.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 6 — MATERIAL. Son and daughter vs mother
  and father: the parents' honor-duty would have derived the
  children — but then STILLBORNS (נפלים) would enter; parents'
  tokens teach: children must be VIABLE — בני קיימא (those who endure).
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 7 — MATERIAL. Why son AND daughter: the
  father's duties to a SON (circumcise, redeem, teach Torah, teach
  a craft, marry him off) vs his rights in a DAUGHTER (her finds,
  her handiwork, annulling her vows) — the two obligation sets
  censused; neither token spare.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 8 — MATERIAL. Brother and sister vs son
  and daughter: the children teach — siblings who INHERIT him
  (paternal); MATERNAL siblings excluded ("as his son and daughter
  whom he inherits").
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 9 — MATERIAL. "His virgin sister" — the
  MINOR sister, not the adult (the two tokens brother/sister each
  needed for the age carve).
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 10 — MATERIAL. The mirror: "his brother"
  — minor or adult alike.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 11 — MATERIAL. "The virgin" excludes the
  raped and the seduced; "who has not BEEN to a man" keeps the one
  injured by wood (מוכת עץ): "her being was by a man" — the
  agent-of-loss test.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 12 — MATERIAL. "The near" INCLUDES the
  betrothed sister; "to him" includes the adult (בוגרת); "for her
  he shall defile" = A COMMAND: refusing, they defile him BY FORCE
  — the case of Yosef the priest whose wife died on Passover eve
  and the Sages pushed him and defiled him against his will.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 13 — MATERIAL. "For her" — for the
  CERTAIN not the doubtful; not for others alongside her ("since
  I am defiled, let me gather so-and-so's bones"); not for her
  LIMBS — no one defiles for a limb from a living parent; but for
  a barley-grain bone of his father — R. Yosei: not even that.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 14 — CONTEXT. The story of Yosef ben
  Paksas: the surgeon told to leave a hair's breadth; the son sent
  away at the cut — "no one defiles for a limb of a living father";
  the Sages: "the righteous perishes in his righteousness."
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 15 — MATERIAL. "A husband shall not
  defile among his people" — there IS a husband who defiles and
  one who does not: for a FIT wife he defiles, for an UNFIT wife
  (a forbidden marriage) he does not.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 1 16 — MATERIAL. "Among his people" — while
  they act as his people, not when he has separated from the
  community's ways; "to profane himself" — WHILE OCCUPIED with the
  dead he is profane; he withdraws — back in his holiness: a
  status that toggles with the act, not a permanent stain.

## Sifra, Emor — Chapter 1 (18 rows)
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 1 — MATERIAL. "Baldness" — liable PER
  BALD SPOT (five spots, five liabilities).
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 2 — MATERIAL. "On their head" — the whole

- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 3 — MATERIAL. THE PRIEST-ISRAEL TRANSFER:
  the verbal analogy "baldness"-"baldness" carries per-spot and
  whole-head from the priests to Israel, and FOR-THE-DEAD-ONLY
  from Israel to the priests — a two-way verbal analogy settling
  both ledgers at once.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 4 — MATERIAL. "Gashing" — per gash.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 5 — MATERIAL. The same two-way transfer on
  "gash"-"gash" (Lev 19:28): per-gash to Israel, for-the-dead to
  the priests.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 6 — MATERIAL. "Holy to their God" — BY

  offer" — not the Levites; "they shall be holy" — INCLUDES THE
  BLEMISHED (holiness is not service-conditioned).
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 7 — MATERIAL. זונה (zonah) DEFINED — the

  of bearing); the Sages — the convert, the freed slavewoman, and
  one who had illicit intercourse; R. Eliezer — even an unmarried
  man with an unmarried woman not for marriage.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 8 — MATERIAL. חללה (chalalah) = one BORN
  of any of the disqualified unions.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 9 — MATERIAL. The חלוצה (chalutzah, the
  released widow) — the a-fortiori from the divorcee (permitted to
  her divorcer yet barred from priesthood; the chalutzah barred
  from her releaser — surely barred).
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 10 — MATERIAL. THE RIVAL REFUTES: the
  צרה (rival wife) is barred from the releaser yet FIT for
  priesthood; the distinction — divorcee and chalutzah had a DEED
  done to them, the rival none; and the fallback: "and a woman"
  amplifies the chalutzah; the rival excluded by "divorced FROM
  HER HUSBAND" — whose divorce is in her husband's hand.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 11 — MATERIAL. R. Elazar ben Matya's
  case: husband abroad, reported dead, she betrothed another, the
  husband returns — the second's bill of divorce does NOT bar her
  from priesthood: "divorced from her HUSBAND" — not from one who
  is not her husband. The bill's validity keyed to the giver's
  status.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 12 — MATERIAL. "They shall not take...
  shall not take" (doubled) — THE WOMAN IS WARNED THROUGH THE MAN:
  the prohibition binds both parties from one verb.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 13 — MATERIAL. "You shall sanctify him"
  — by force (if he refuses, BEAT HIM); "the bread of your God he
  offers" — tells what caused it; "holy to you" — includes the
  blemished; "I the LORD sanctify you" — warns THE COURT: the
  enforcement addressed to the court.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 14 — MATERIAL. The priest's daughter:
  "when she profanes herself BY WHORING" — not by another
  profanation (desecrating the Sabbath is not this clause).
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 15 — MATERIAL. The unmarried excluded:
  "her father" here and "her father" at Deut 22:21 — whoring WITH A
  HUSBAND'S BOND; the "all the land" objection answered by "she
  profanes" already covering it.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 16 — MATERIAL. All ages and states:
  betrothed girl, married girl, betrothed adult, married adult,
  even the OLD — "daughter of a priest" in any case.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 17 — MATERIAL. Married to a Levite, an
  Israelite, even a Netin or a mamzer — "daughter of a MAN priest"
  even though she is no longer a priestess.
- Sifra, Emor, Chapter 1 18 — MATERIAL. "Her father she profanes"
  — R. Eliezer: with her father BURNING, with her father-in-law
  STONING; "SHE by fire" — she burns, her partner does not; the
  perjured witnesses — זוממין (the scheming witnesses) — do not burn.

## Sifra, Emor — Section 2 (7 rows)
- Sifra, Emor, Section 2 1 — MATERIAL. "The priest GREATER than his
  brothers" — greater in beauty, wealth, strength, wisdom, and
  appearance; lacking wealth — MAKE HIM GREAT from his brothers'
  means (Pinchas of Chavata found quarrying, his quarry filled with
  gold dinars; R. Chananya ben Gamliel: he was plowing, twelve

- Sifra, Emor, Section 2 2 — MATERIAL. THE ONE-HOUR RULE: "on whose
  head the anointing oil was POURED" — anointed seven days and
  invested seven, or seven and one, or one and seven, or one and
  one, EVEN ONE HOUR: the office attaches at the pouring, not at
  the week (Exod 29:30's seven-day clause bounded).
- Sifra, Emor, Section 2 3 — MATERIAL. "His head he shall not let
  grow wild, his garments not rend" — R. Yehuda: not at all; R.
  Meir: not FOR HIS DEAD as people do; the tearing geometry — the
  high priest rends BELOW, the commoner ABOVE.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 2 4 — MATERIAL. "All souls of the dead" — R.
  Akiva: a quarter-log of blood FROM TWO CORPSES defiles ("two
  souls and one dead"); "shall not come... shall not defile" — TWO
  prohibitions, and the commoner priest carries both by the verbal
  analogy "not defile"-"not defile"; "for father and mother" — yet
  he DOES defile for an abandoned corpse (met mitzvah).
- Sifra, Emor, Section 2 5 — MATERIAL. "From the sanctuary he shall
  not go out" — WITH them no, AFTER them yes: they hidden, he
  revealed, alternating, up to the CITY GATE (R. Meir); R. Yehuda:
  he does not go out AT ALL. A recorded two-arm dispute on the
  funeral geometry.
- Sifra, Emor, Section 2 6 — MATERIAL. THE ONEN SPLIT: the high
  priest who served as an אונן (onen, on the death day) — his
  service VALID ("shall not profane" — he does not); the commoner
  onen's service INVALID. "The anointing oil upon him" — the
  many-garmented priest included by "the CROWN of the oil."
- Sifra, Emor, Section 2 7 — MATERIAL. "He" — not the KING, not the
  NAZIRITE; the war-anointed priest INCLUDED; "a wife in her
  virginity" excludes the בוגרת (adult) whose virginity has ended;
  R. Eliezer and R. Shimon permit the adult.

## The finds (this ledger's crowns)
- THE RELATIVE TABLE BY TOKEN PAIRS (Section 1 4-10): every
  relative in 21:2-3 is proven NON-DERIVABLE from its neighbor by a
  mirrored pair of arguments — the mother covers the profaned line,
  the father covers presumption, the children must be viable, the
  siblings paternal, the sister minor; the table is built from
  redundancy, one pair per row.
- THE FORCED DEFILEMENT (Section 1 12): "for her he shall defile" is
  a COMMAND enforced against the priest's will — Yosef the priest
  on Passover eve — an obligation with an effect the court executes.
- THE TOGGLING PROFANATION (Section 1 16): profane WHILE occupied,
  holy on withdrawal — a status flag, not a stain.
- THE TWO-WAY TRANSFER (Chapter 1 3, 5): one verbal analogy moving
  laws in BOTH directions between priests and Israel.
- ZONAH DEFINED THREE WAYS (Chapter 1 7) with Onkelos sitting on the

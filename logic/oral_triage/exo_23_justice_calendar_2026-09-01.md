## Mekhilta of Rabbi Yishmael on Exodus 23:1-19 — read IN FULL, 40 ¶

- Mekhilta on Exod 23:1 (2¶) — MATERIAL. LO TISA as THREE azharot:
  the receiver of slander; the JUDGE not hearing one litigant before
  the other stands ("the case of BOTH comes before the judges"); the
  LITIGANT not arguing to the judge alone (Deut's "the two men shall
  stand"); Abba Chanan: the judge's oath requires AMEN — without it,
  a vain oath. "Set not your hand with the wicked": refusing the
  split-a-false-witness offer (the mane/mane story); the refined of
  Jerusalem who signed nothing without knowing the co-signer;
  CHAMSANIM AND GAZLANIM DISQUALIFIED as witnesses (R. Natan).
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:2 (1¶) — MATERIAL. MAJORITY RULES, asymmetric:
  "you are not with them for evil but you ARE with them for good" —
  12 acquit vs 11 convict → acquitted; 13 convict vs 10 acquit →
  convicted; 11-vs-12 conviction margin of ONE does not convict:
  "execute by witnesses, execute by TILTERS — as witnesses are two,
  the tilters are two"; the I-don't-know judge; the closing azharah:
  the judge tilts only toward ACQUITTAL.
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:3 (1¶) — MATERIAL. No GLORIFYING the poor in
  judgment (paired against Lev 19's no-favoring-the-poor — each
  verse takes a referent); Abba Chanan in R. Elazar's name: the
  gleanings-forgotten-sheaf-corner context.
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:4 (5¶) — MATERIAL. WHO IS "YOUR ENEMY": the
  idolater (R. Yoshiyah, with the camp-verses), the relapsed convert
  (R. Eliezer), the Israelite TRANSGRESSOR (R. Yitzchak), or R.
  Natan — your own son after a quarrel: AN ENEMY FOR THE HOUR.
  "Straying" = outside the boundary (Gen 37:15's straying Joseph as
  the witness — our gen_60 ink cited); ox and donkey each its own
  return-duty; the return ladder (near, far, unknown — announce,
  examine the claimant for fraud); a find that eats works-and-eats,
  not one that wastes.
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:5 (5¶) — MATERIAL. SEE/ENCOUNTER reconciled by
  MEASURE: the sages measured ONE IN SEVEN-AND-A-HALF OF A MIL — the
  RIS — as the duty-distance (both verses kept, a number minted
  between them); "lying" — not a habitual lier-down; "under its
  load" — not more than its load; UNLOADING AND LOADING both Torah
  (R. Yoshiyah both-in-perikah (both verses in the unloading) + the a-fortiori; R. Yehudah b.
  Beteira splits the verses; R. Yishmael: as unloading is Torah so
  loading is Torah); PRIORITY CONFLICTS RECORDED: Israel's donkey
  under a gentile's load — unload; gentile's donkey under Israel's
  load — "you shall refrain"; the father who says "do not unload, do
  not return" — DO NOT LISTEN: parents are themselves bound by the
  commandments; the graveyard-defilement case (positive does not
  simply override); "azov ta'azov" also read: leave WHAT IS IN YOUR
  HEART against him — the grudge released with the load ("with him"
  — only if he shares the work; an idle owner exempts you).
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:6-8 (3¶) — MATERIAL. "Your needy IN HIS CAUSE"
  — the WICKED-and-worthy litigant pair: do not say "he is wicked, I
  will tilt against him" — he is "needy IN COMMANDMENTS," judgment
  stays straight. DISTANCE FROM FALSEHOOD: no ignorant colleague on
  the bench, no advocates (sanegorin) beside the judge, R. Natan:
  the azharah against MINUT (heresy). SINGLE WITNESSES DO NOT JOIN
  ACROSS ACTS: one saw sun-worship, one moon-worship — no
  combination. THE PURSUER-WARNING PASSAGE: the formula quotes Gen
  9:6 (the Noahide statute spoken as the operative law — our
  G21-13's own Mekhilta seat); the averted-eyes case (knife
  dripping, no witnesses = NO EXECUTION — circumstantial proof
  refused) with the Shimon b. Shetach / Yehudah b. Tabbai narratives
  and the snake that executed what the court could not; THE
  ASYMMETRY: left court GUILTY and merit found later — RETRIED;
  left court ACQUITTED and guilt found later — NOT retried ("as he
  left your court [acquitted]... 'for I will not acquit the
  wicked'" — Heaven's court reserved); BRIBERY absolute — even to
  judge truly; the taker's three-fold blindness ladder (R. Natan:
  Torah-confusion, dependence on people, loss of eyesight).
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:10-11 (shemitah, 5¶) — MATERIAL. Six-years vs
  FOUR-shemitot-per-week homily (obedience economics, R. Eliezer*);
  produce that CROSSES the year-line is seventh-year produce both
  ways (entering and leaving, R. Yoshiyah's common-side argument; R.
  Yonatan from the beasts' remainder); THE ABANDONMENT IS LAWLESS BY
  DESIGN: "do not say 'I will gather it and distribute to the
  poor'" — the Torah says BREACH IT WITH BREACHES; the sages fenced
  it for tikkun olam; rich/poor phases (all eat while plenty; after
  the removal-hour the poor only — R. Yehudah b. Beteira); the
  beast-hekesh: as the beast eats untithed of the seventh, so may
  the man — the seventh year suspends tithing.
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:12 (Shabbat, 3¶) — MATERIAL. Shabbat restated
  INSIDE the shemitah passage "so the Shabbat of creation is not
  displaced from its place" by the year-laws; ANIMAL REST DEFINED AS
  GRAZING FREEDOM: confinement "is not rest but SUFFERING" — the
  rest-clause grants the tearing-and-eating liberty; בן אמתך = the
  UNCIRCUMCISED SLAVE, והגר = the RESIDENT SOJOURNER (ger toshav);
  the status table: resident sojourner on Shabbat = as Israel on
  festival day; uncircumcised slaves = as Israel on the festival
  weekdays (R. Yoshiyah; R. Akiva switches the pair).
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:13 (3¶) — MATERIAL. "In ALL that I said to you
  take heed" — the catch-all: the passage's fine details (R. Eliezer
  b. Yaakov), positive-and-negative both (R. Eliezer*), Torah-words
  as obligation (R. Meir), and the SHEVUT hook: acts barred as REST
  (not labor) ride this clause. Idol names: no swearing BY them, no
  making them a landmark/meeting-word; R. NATAN'S BABEL ANALOGY:
  shem-shem joins our clause to Gen 11's tower ("let us make us a
  NAME") — the tower read as idolatry by verbal analogy (gen_25's
  ink on the far side); the naming decorum table (idols by shame-
  names, the Place by praise-names).
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:14-17 (pilgrimage, 4¶) — MATERIAL. Three
  REGALIM fixed to the three named feasts; THE EXEMPTION TABLE read
  word by word: yera'eh — the blind; regalim — the lame; zekhurkha —
  women; kol zekhurkha — tumtum and androgynos; the Deut recital —
  the deaf; ve-samachta — the sick and the old; before the LORD —
  the impure: "ALL are obligated in appearing except..." (the
  chagigah-table in the making); not-empty-handed = OFFERINGS
  (burnt-offerings of appearing — argued rejoicing-rejoicing; "your
  table full and your Master's empty?"); the aviv-month anchor; "the
  MASTER, the LORD" — on Israel He set His name especially (the
  Shema/Jeremiah parallels).
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:18 (2¶) — MATERIAL. No slaughtering the
  PASSOVER while leaven stands (R. Yishmael; R. Akiva adds the
  THROWING of the blood; R. Yehudah: the daily offering — "the
  offering wholly Mine"); the FATS: disqualified by lodging OFF the
  altar-pavement, NOT disqualified lodging ON the altar-pyre — the
  two-way dialectic between fats and limbs closed both directions.
- Mekhilta on Exod 23:19 (4¶) — MATERIAL. BIKKURIM: liquids brought
  (from "you shall bring") though not read over; the BRING-BUT-NOT-
  READ classes (sharecroppers, tenants, the violent seizer, women,
  tumtum, androgynos — "your land" excludes, "you shall bring"
  re-includes); MEAT-IN-MILK, the full engine: WHY THREE TOKENS —
  seven recorded answers (three covenants; beast/wild/fowl; large
  cattle/goats/sheep; eating/benefit/cooking; in-land/abroad,
  Temple-present/absent; R. Akiva's three exclusions — wild, fowl,
  impure; R. Yose ha-Gelili's fowl-has-no-mother's-milk); THE EATING
  BAN DERIVED (the pesach → sinew → carcass → fat-and-blood
  refutation ladder closing at "lo tokhlenu"; Issi's two further
  routes); THE BENEFIT BAN DERIVED (orlah → chametz → mixed-vineyard
  ladder; Rebbi from "sell-or-cook": when you sell it do not cook-
  and-sell); scope extensions by a-fortiori (the sister's milk, the
  animal's own milk, goats' milk in sheep — "the Torah spoke of the
  KID because its mother's milk is abundant" = the ordinary case);
  and the ¶4 closer: cooking banned only for MEAT-IN-MILK among all
  Torah prohibitions (the two-permitted-things architecture), with
  the hekdesh extension from "the house of the LORD your God."

## Onkelos Exodus 23:1-19 — read IN FULL, 19 verses

- Onkelos Exod 23:2 — MATERIAL. The crux-verse EXPANDED into three
  rulings: "do not refrain from TEACHING what you see about the
  judgment; AFTER THE MANY THE JUDGMENT IS COMPLETED (בָּתַר סַגִיאֵי
  שְׁלַם דִינָא)" — majority-rule and the duty to speak your view,
  rendered as the verse itself.
- Onkelos Exod 23:5 — MATERIAL. "You shall SURELY LEAVE WHAT IS IN
  YOUR HEART AGAINST HIM (מָא דִבְלִבָּךְ עֲלוֹהִי) and unload with him"
  — the grudge-release reading carried INSIDE the translation,
  beside the physical unloading: both laws in one rendered verse.
- Onkelos Exod 23:7 — MATERIAL. "One who went out ACQUITTED from the
  court (דִי נְפַק דְכֵי מִן דִינָא) you shall not kill" — the
  no-retrial-after-acquittal law rendered as the verse's own text.
- Onkelos Exod 23:18 — MATERIAL. זבחי rendered פִּסְחִי ("my
  PASSOVER"), חגי rendered "the fats of MY FESTIVAL OFFERING" — the
  Mekhilta's identifications decided in translation.
- Onkelos Exod 23:19 — MATERIAL. THE CROWN RENDERING: לא תבשל גדי
  בחלב אמו → לָא תֵיכְלוּן בְּשַׂר בַּחֲלָב ("you shall not EAT MEAT IN
  MILK") — the kid-sentence translated as the general eating ban:
  the whole derivation chain compressed into the received text; the
  bikkurim clause pointed at בֵּית מִקְדְשָׁא ("the SANCTUARY house").
- Onkelos Exod 23:3 (לא תרחם, no PITY in judgment), 23:13 ("shall
  not be HEARD upon your mouth"), 23:17 (רִבּוֹן עָלְמָא, "Master of
  the World") — noted; carried with their steps.
- Onkelos Exod 23:1, 23:4, 23:6, 23:8-12, 23:14-16 — context (close
  renderings).

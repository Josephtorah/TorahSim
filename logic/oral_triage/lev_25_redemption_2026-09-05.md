## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 4 (rows 8-9 of 9; rows 1-7 in the
## shemittah ledger)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 4 8 — MATERIAL. "The land shall not be sold
  לִצְמִתֻת (litzmitut, 'in perpetuity')" — IRREVOCABLY; "for the
  land is Mine" — do not look on it grudgingly; "for you are
  strangers and residents" — do not make yourselves the principal
  (David and the Chronicler quoted); "with Me" — enough for the
  servant to be like his master: when it is Mine it is yours.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 4 9 — MATERIAL. "In all... you shall give
  redemption" — could it include slaves, documents, movables? "the
  land of your holding" — no; then why "in all"? to INCLUDE HOUSES
  AND THE HEBREW SLAVE. The redemption domain: land, houses, the
  Hebrew slave.

## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 5 (7 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 5 1 — MATERIAL. THE SALE GATE: no one may
  sell his field to keep the money in his purse, buy a beast, tools,
  or a house — only WHEN HE HAS BECOME POOR ("when your brother
  becomes poor and sells"); nor sell ALL at once — "of his holding,"
  not all his holding; R. Elazar b. Azariah: if even to Heaven one
  may not devote all his property, how much more must a man spare
  his property. "His redeemer near him comes" — the NEAREST FIRST;
  "redeems his brother's sale" — the gift recipient included.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 5 2 — MATERIAL. "A man who has no redeemer"
  — is there an Israelite with no redeemer? one who CAN redeem;
  "his hand attains" — his OWN hand; "and finds" — not sell far to
  redeem near, nor a bad field to redeem a good, nor BORROW to
  redeem; "sufficient for its redemption" — no HALF redemptions.
  The owner-redemption's four constraints.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 5 3 — MATERIAL. "He shall reckon the years
  of his sale" — YEARS, not months; but if he wishes to make months
  a year he may ("reckon"); "return the surplus to the man he sold
  to" — sold to the first for a maneh, the first to a second for
  two hundred: reckons with the FIRST ("to whom he sold"); sold for
  two hundred, resold for a maneh: reckons with the LAST — "to the
  MAN," the one now in it, says Rabbi. The reckoning counterpart
  chosen by which price favors the seller.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 5 4 — MATERIAL. R. Dostai b. Yehuda: sold at
  a maneh and it IMPROVED to two hundred — reckons at the maneh
  ("the surplus" in his hand); sold at two hundred and it FELL to a
  maneh — reckons at the maneh ("return the surplus" — of the
  land). The seller pays the lesser figure either way. "Return to
  his holding" — the holding's owner, not a buyer reselling.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 5 5 — MATERIAL. "If his hand has not found"
  — if it does not bear on the field's owner (already taught), GIVE
  IT to the REDEEMER, that he redeem by this same order — the
  freed-token reassignment (M-18) again: no borrowing, no half.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 5 6 — MATERIAL. "His sale shall be in the
  buyer's hand" — NOT the sanctuary's: from the sanctuary one DOES
  sell far to redeem near, bad to redeem good, borrow, and redeem by
  halves — the four constraints inverted for consecrated land.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 5 7 — MATERIAL. R. Shimon's reason: a
  layman's field unredeemed at the Jubilee returns to the OWNER; the
  sanctuary's unredeemed at the Jubilee goes to the PRIESTS. "Until
  the Jubilee year" — the year itself not entered; so FIELDS AND
  MONEY RELEASE TOGETHER: the Jubilee frees at its START, the
  seventh releases at its END. "Return to his holding" — the woman
  included.

## Sifra, Behar — Section 4 (10 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 1 — MATERIAL. THE WALLED-CITY ROSTER: "a
  dwelling house in a walled city" — walled FROM THE DAYS OF JOSHUA,
  not walled afterward; what counts — three courtyards of two houses
  each walled since Joshua: the old fort of Sepphoris, the citadel of
  Gush Chalav, old Yodfat, Gamla in Galilee; Gadod in Transjordan;
  Chadid, Ono, Jerusalem in Judah; R. Yishmael b. R. Yosei: only
  those the returning exiles re-sanctified; the first sanctity
  lapsed with the land. A gazetteer as a rule's input.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 2 — MATERIAL. "Its redemption shall be" —
  AT ONCE; "until the end of the year of its sale" — its own year,
  not the calendar's; "days shall be its redemption" — Rabbi: days
  are no fewer than TWO.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 3 — MATERIAL. Sold to a first who resold
  to a second — whose year? "until a full year is completed FOR
  HIM" — the year runs from the FIRST sale.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 4 — MATERIAL. "A full year" — Rabbi: 365
  days, the solar count; the Sages: twelve months day for day —
  so an intercalated year is intercalated FOR HIM. A parameter's
  two settings.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 5 — MATERIAL. "The house shall stand" —
  houses only? oil-presses, cisterns, caves, bathhouses, dovecotes,
  towers by "which is in the city"; FIELDS? "house" — a dwelling,
  fields are no dwelling: excluded (R. Yehuda).
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 6 — MATERIAL. R. Meir: fields INCLUDED by
  "which is in the city." The dispute over fields inside the wall.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 7 — MATERIAL. "Which has a wall" — R.
  Yehuda: excludes a house BUILT INTO the wall; R. Shimon: its outer
  wall is its wall; R. Elazar b. R. Yosei: "which has" — even if
  it has none now but had one before.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 8 — MATERIAL. "In perpetuity" —
  irrevocably; also the gift recipient included. HILLEL'S
  ORDINANCE: at first the buyer would HIDE on the last day of the
  twelfth month so the house became his; Hillel the Elder ordained
  the seller may deposit his money in the chamber, break the door,
  and enter; the buyer takes his money when he pleases. A recorded
  exploit and its repair.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 9 — MATERIAL. "To the buyer" — him alone?
  "to his generations"; his generations and not him? "to the
  buyer" — until both verses are said (the two-verse pin).
- Sifra, Behar, Section 4 10 — MATERIAL. "In perpetuity to the buyer
  to his generations, it does not go out in the Jubilee" — EXCLUDES
  the sanctuary, which has no generations.

## Sifra, Behar — Chapter 6 (9 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 6 1 — MATERIAL. "Houses of the open villages
  which have no wall" — even if they have one, they are SEEN as
  without; how many? "houses" — two; "villages" — two: two
  courtyards of two houses each; THREE of two each — walled-city
  status. The threshold between village and city in house-counts.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 6 2 — MATERIAL. "Reckoned as the field of
  the land" — as the field goes out in the Jubilee and by deduction
  of money, so these; but not the field's two-year floor: "it shall
  have redemption" — AT ONCE. The village house takes the best of
  both.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 6 3 — MATERIAL. Given the strength of both,
  might it NOT go out at the Jubilee? "in the Jubilee it goes out."
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 6 4 — MATERIAL. "The Levites' cities, the
  houses of their holding" — the perpetual redemption not for
  slaves, documents, movables; "perpetual redemption for the
  Levites" — against the two-year floor of "by the number of years."
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 6 5 — MATERIAL. Against the walled-city
  year-limit and against the field's forfeit to the priests — for
  the Levites, "perpetual redemption" each time. Three exemptions
  from one clause.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 6 6 — MATERIAL. "And what one redeems from
  the Levites" — a Levite redeems from a LEVITE too, not only an
  Israelite from a Levite.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 6 7 — MATERIAL. "From the Levites" — not all:
  excludes the Levite who is a Netin's or a mamzer's son; "the sale
  goes out" — slaves, documents, movables? "house," "city,"
  "holding"; then why "sale"? his SALE goes out in the Jubilee, his
  CONSECRATION does not — only by redemption.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 6 8 — MATERIAL. An Israelite who inherited
  his Levite maternal grandfather — redeems by this order? "what one
  redeems from the Levites"; a Levite who inherited an Israelite
  grandfather? "the houses of the Levites' cities" — until he is a
  LEVITE and IN THE LEVITES' CITIES (Rabbi); the Sages: only in the
  Levites' cities; "among the children of Israel" — tells what
  caused it.
- Sifra, Behar, Chapter 6 9 — MATERIAL. THE ZONING RULE: no field
  made pasture nor pasture field, no pasture city nor city pasture
  ("the field of the pasture of their cities"); R. Eliezer: in the
  Levites' cities; in Israel's cities a field may become pasture
  and pasture a city, never the reverse — so Israel's cities are
  not ruined; "shall not be sold" — the TREASURER'S sale, not a
  permanent sale ("a perpetual holding for them").

## Sifra, Behar — Section 5 (4 rows)
- Sifra, Behar, Section 5 1 — MATERIAL. THE FALLING LOAD: "when your
  brother becomes poor and his hand falters with you" — do not let
  him FALL: like a load on a donkey — while still in place one
  holds it up; fallen to the ground, five cannot raise it; supported
  four or five times, support again ("strengthen him"); may you
  drive him to bad ways? "with you"; "stranger" — the righteous
  convert; "resident" — the convert who eats carcasses; "that he
  live with you" — YOUR LIFE COMES FIRST.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 5 2 — MATERIAL. THE INTEREST DEFINITIONS:
  "take from him no נֶשֶׁךְ (neshekh, 'bite') and no תַּרְבִּית
  (tarbit, 'increase')" — from him you take none, but you may be
  his GUARANTOR; bite — lending a sela for five dinars, two seah of
  wheat for three: it bites; increase — increasing in produce: he
  bought wheat at a gold dinar the kor at market price, wheat rose
  to three dinars, "give me my wheat to sell for wine" — "your wheat
  is reckoned at three dinars and you have wine with me" — and he
  has no wine: forbidden; if he has it, he must give it. The two
  terms defined by two worked cases.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 5 3 — MATERIAL. THE ONE FLASK: "that your
  brother live with you" — ben Petura: two in the desert with one
  flask, one drinks and reaches habitation, both drink and both die
  — let both drink and die; R. Akiva: "that your brother live WITH
  you" — YOUR LIFE COMES FIRST. "Your money" — not others'; "your
  food" — not others'; tithe money and animal food by Deut 23:20's
  "interest of money, interest of food." "I am the LORD" — who
  accepts the yoke of interest accepts Heaven's yoke; "who brought
  you out" — ON CONDITION that you accept the interest ban; who
  admits it admits the Exodus, who denies it denies the Exodus.
- Sifra, Behar, Section 5 4 — MATERIAL. "To give you the land of
  Canaan, to be your God" — who dwells in the land accepts Heaven's
  kingship; who leaves it is as one who serves idols (David, 1 Sam
  26:19 expounded). The land clause as a status statement.

## Onkelos Lev 25:23-38 (16 verses, read whole)
- Onkelos Lev 25:23 — MATERIAL. לַחֲלוּטִין (irrevocably) for "in
  perpetuity" — Onkelos IS the Sifra's gloss (Chapter 4 8, Section 4
  8: "irrevocably"); דַיָּרִין וְתוֹתָבִין אַתּוּן קֳדָמַי (you are
  dwellers and residents BEFORE Me).
- Onkelos Lev 25:24 — MATERIAL. פֻּרְקָנָא תִּתְּנוּן לְאַרְעָא
  (redemption you shall give to the land) — the redemption noun that
  runs the chapter.
- Onkelos Lev 25:25 — MATERIAL. פָרִיקֵיהּ דְּקָרִיב לֵיהּ (his
  redeemer who is near him) — nearness kept as the redeemer's mark
  (Chapter 5 1's nearest-first).
- Onkelos Lev 25:26 — MATERIAL. וְתַדְבֵּק יְדֵהּ (and his hand
  attains) — the attainment verb; כְּמִסַּת פֻּרְקָנֵהּ (sufficient
  for his redemption) — the sufficiency token behind no-halves.
- Onkelos Lev 25:27 — MATERIAL. וִיחַשֵּׁב יָת שְׁנֵי זְבִינוֹהִי (he
  shall reckon the years of his sale); מוֹתָרָא (the surplus) — the
  surplus noun both of Chapter 5 3-4's readings run on.
- Onkelos Lev 25:28 — CONTEXT. Stays in the buyer's hand until the
  Jubilee, goes out; plain.
- Onkelos Lev 25:29 — MATERIAL. קַרְתָּא מַקְּפָא שׁוּר (a city
  surrounded by a wall); עִדָּן בְּעִדָּן (time by time) for "days"
  — a span rather than a count; Rabbi's two days is the Sifra's,
  not Onkelos's.
- Onkelos Lev 25:30 — MATERIAL. שַׁתָּא שְׁלֶמְתָּא (a whole year);
  לַחֲלוּטִין (irrevocably) again; לְדָרוֹהִי (to his generations).
- Onkelos Lev 25:31 — MATERIAL. בָּתֵּי פַצְחַיָּא (the houses of
  the open villages); סְחוֹר סְחוֹר (round about); פֻּרְקָנָא תְּהֵי
  לֵיהּ (redemption it shall have).
- Onkelos Lev 25:32 — MATERIAL. פֻּרְקַן עָלָם (perpetual redemption)
  for the Levites — the clause the three exemptions hang on.
- Onkelos Lev 25:33 — CONTEXT. The Levite's redeemed sale goes out
  in the Jubilee; plain.
- Onkelos Lev 25:34 — MATERIAL. חֲקַל רְוַח קִרְוֵיהוֹן (the field of
  the OPEN SPACE of their cities) for "pasture" — the zoning rule's
  middle band named as open space; אַחֲסָנַת עָלָם (a perpetual
  holding).
- Onkelos Lev 25:35 — MATERIAL. וּתְמוּט יְדֵיהּ (and his hand
  totters) — the falling verb of Section 5 1's donkey-load;
  וְתַתְקֵף בֵּיהּ (and you shall strengthen him); דַּיַּר וְתוֹתָב
  (dweller and resident).
- Onkelos Lev 25:36 — MATERIAL. חִבּוּלְיָא וְרִבִּיתָא (bite-interest
  and increase) — the two terms translated as two, matching the
  Sifra's two definitions.
- Onkelos Lev 25:37 — MATERIAL. Money in bite-interest, food in
  increase — the pairing kept as the verse pairs them.
- Onkelos Lev 25:38 — CONTEXT. The Exodus clause and Canaan; plain.

## Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 — limb from the living (11:24-25)
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 1 — MATERIAL. The class narrowed to
  the BEAST by staged tokens: fish out ('behemah'), locusts out
  ('mafreset'), birds staged ('parsah') — the limb-from-the-living
  impurity holds where the kind has carcass-impurity.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 2 — MATERIAL. WHICH limb: not their
  death (stated), not the dead's limb (stated below) — THE LIMB
  FROM THE LIVING is this verse's subject.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 3 — MATERIAL. The argument from the
  human's limb opened.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 4 — MATERIAL. And from the swarmer —
  each a-fortiori met.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 5 — MATERIAL. The limb-from-the-dead
  comparison (Noahide-scope argument) met by the flesh-counter;
  the verse resolves.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 6 — MATERIAL. FLESH SEPARATED FROM
  THE LIVING IS PURE — 'nevelah' pairs the limb: R. Yosei
  HaGelili — what has no replacement; R. Akiva — sinews and
  bones; Rabbi — as its creation: flesh, sinews, bones.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 7 — MATERIAL. THE PARADOX PAIR, side
  one: the TOUCHER is impure until evening but does NOT
  contaminate garments — the a-fortiori from carrying DEFEATED by
  the written clause.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 8 — MATERIAL. Side two: the CARRIER
  contaminates garments — the a-fortiori from touch defeated by
  the written clause; the lattice held by ink at both corners.
- Sifra, Shemini, Section 4 9 — MATERIAL. 'Garment' expanded
  (large, small, white, dyed, the hairnet caps and wrappings);
  other vessels made like garments; person and earthenware
  excluded.

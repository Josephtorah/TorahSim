Eighth prospective middot ("measures" — interpretive rules) run. Survey tier

## A. Corpus-side structural finds (DB-verified 2026-08-01, pre-derivation)

1. **The corpus's first IM ("if") — both tokens in one speech (4:7):**
   the law's branch particle debuts as moral counsel to Cain (*im
   teitiv ... ve-im lo teitiv* — the two-way conditional; third corpus
   token only at 13:9). The lev_13 watch-list called this verse "the
   corpus's first im-branch" from the far end of its se'et join; the
   source is now derived.
2. **chattat ("sin") debuts crouching at a door (4:7)** — first of 142
   corpus tokens, the overwhelming majority being the SIN-OFFERING of
   the law (the DB's own gloss for the lemma is "sin-offering"): the
   word for sin is born one verse before the first murder, and its
   career is the sacrifice that answers it. Token #2: 18:20 (Sodom).
3. **The offering vocabulary debuts as a sibling pair:** minchah
   ("offering/grain-offering", 113 tokens — Lev 2's term) x3 in this
   story first; Abel's portion carries BEKHOROT ("firstlings" — the
   firstborn laws ahead) and CHELEV ("fat" — Exod 23:18; Lev 3:16
   "all fat is the LORD's"): two altar institutions in embryo, one
   verse apart. sha'ah ("regard/gaze") — 3 corpus tokens: 4:4, 4:5,
   Exod 5:9. The REJECTION carries no stated reason [OPEN].
4. **The 3:16 pair lands verbatim (4:7):** *ve-elekha teshukato
   ve-atah timshol-bo* — teshukah ("longing") + mashal ("rule"), the
   woman's sentence grammar re-aimed at SIN personified (teshukah's
   only two Torah tokens; DB-verified at gen_11).
5. **se'et (4:7) and mi-neso (4:13) bracket the story with one root:**
   the nasa ("lift/bear") root offered as UPLIFT if he does well,
   returned as a burden too great to BEAR after the murder — the
   lev_13 A-join (se'et as the intake's lead mark-name) now has its
   source derived.
6. **harag ("kill") — the whole debut is this story:** tokens 1-3 =
   4:8 (the deed), 4:14 (the fear), 4:15 (the protection); 42 corpus
   tokens. The first DEATH in the corpus is fratricide — the armed
   handler's word (mot) still unexecuted by heaven; the machine's
   handler-delta (gen_11) gains its sharpest edge: the first actual
   death is man-made.
7. **The 4:8 EMPTY QUOTE:** *va-yomer Kayin el-Hevel achiv* — and no
   content follows (straight to "and when they were in the FIELD" —
   2:5's word as murder scene): the corpus's first speech report
   with no speech [OPEN — the versions supply content; the letter
   does not; chain dossier BR 22:16 named-only].
8. **The interrogation formula recurs:** *ei Hevel achikha* (4:9 —
   ayeka's second token, verified at gen_10) answered by the first
   LIE (*lo yadati* — the KNOW verb: the father knew his wife, 4:1;
   the son knows nothing, 4:9) and the guard-word thrown back:
   *ha-SHOMER achi anokhi* ("am I my brother's KEEPER?") — 2:15's
   office word, 3:24's cherub duty, now a murderer's rhetorical
   question. *meh asita* (4:10) = 3:13's question verbatim.
9. **The curse reaches a human (4:11):** *arur ATAH min-ha-adamah* —
   token #3 of the curse chain (gen_11's census), and the ground
   that carried the man's curse now has a MOUTH (*patztah et-piha*)
   that drank the brother's BLOODS (demei — plural [OPEN: the
   chain's worlds-doctrine; see Onkelos below]). 4:12 escalates
   3:17: for Cain the ground won't yield strength AT ALL — the
   ground-worker (4:2, oved adamah — 3:23's job inherited) loses
   his medium.
10. **The close is built from Eden's exile vocabulary:** gerashta
    (4:14 — garash #2, first person, answering 3:24); the FACE
    thread (hidden from Your face — satar's debut); shivatayim
    ("SEVENFOLD" — the week's number as vengeance arithmetic); OT
    ("sign" — day 4's word turned personal: the mark of Cain; token
    #2-3 = the RAINBOW, 9:12,17 — personal protection precedes
    cosmic covenant); le-vilti ("not-to" — 3:11's form) hakot; and
    *va-yeshev be-eretz NOD* — he settles in "WANDERING" (the nad
    of his own sentence fossilized as a place-name), KIDMAT-EDEN —
    east of Eden: the exile geography continues in the guards'
    direction.

## C. Tier-A Onkelos findings (read at derive time, FETCHLOG 2026-08-01)

1. **4:7 REBUILT as a theology of repentance (flagged, the largest
   expansion in our spans):** "if you make your DEEDS good, it will
   be FORGIVEN you (yishtevek lakh); if not — FOR THE DAY OF
   JUDGMENT your sin is KEPT (netir), destined to be exacted from
   you IF YOU DO NOT REPENT; and if you REPENT, it will be
   forgiven." se'et resolved as FORGIVENESS (the nasa root as
   bearing-away); the crouching beast replaced by eschatological
   bookkeeping; teshuvah inserted twice; the teshukah/mashal clause
   absorbed entirely.
2. **4:13 -> "my sin is too great TO BE FORGIVEN" (mi-lemishbak):**
   the same shevak ("forgive") verb as 4:7 — the Aramaic brackets
   the story in forgiveness vocabulary where the Hebrew brackets it
   in nasa; Cain's line becomes despair (or, in chain readings, a
   question — named-only).
3. **4:10 -> "the voice of the blood of the DESCENDANTS (zar'in)
   destined to issue from your brother" (flagged):** the plural
   demei read as GENERATIONS — Mishnah Sanhedrin 4:5's
   worlds-doctrine inside the translation; cries "before ME"
   (kodamai — buffered).
4. **4:14 -> "from before You it is NOT POSSIBLE to hide"
   (flagged):** Cain's I-shall-be-hidden corrected to an
   impossibility — theology overruling the speaker's grammar.
5. 4:15 -> "to SEVEN GENERATIONS (le-shava darin) it will be
   exacted" — the multiplier read as a genealogy clock (the Lamech
   line ahead); 4:16 -> Nod dissolved into "exiled and wandering"
   — the place-name returned to sentence-vocabulary (the letter's
   irony made explicit), with another mi-lekadmin ("from of old").
6. 4:1 kaniti ... et-YHWH -> "from BEFORE the LORD" (the
   acquisition buffered); 4:3-4 minchah -> tikruvta/KORBANA (the
   korban vocabulary); sha'ah -> "there was FAVOR (ra'ava) from
   before the LORD" / "there was NOT favor" — gaze become favor,
   presence buffered; 4:9 ha-shomer -> ha-NATER — the same
   natar/keep verb the Aramaic gave 3:15's grudge.

## D. Tier-A Onkelos findings (read at derive time, FETCHLOG 2026-08-01)

1. *arum* ("cunning") -> **CHAKIM ("wise")** — the SAME word the
   translation gave the knowledge-tree's eaters (2:9/2:17 chakimin):
   in Aramaic the serpent already IS what the tree makes you. (The
   2:25/3:1 naked/cunning pun levels away: artila'in / chakim.)
2. The serpent's name-drop LEVELED: Onkelos's serpent says the divine
   name (amar YHWH) where the Hebrew's says bare Elohim — the
   Hebrew's name census is invisible in translation (dual-track:
   the letter's finding stands on the Hebrew).
3. *ke-Elohim* ("like God") -> **ke-RAVREVIN ("like the great ones")**
   — the promise demoted; *yodea Elohim* ("God knows") -> **galei
   kodam YHWH ("it is REVEALED BEFORE the LORD")** — the standard
   buffering, both flagged in the edition.
4. **The MEMRA debuts (3:8, 3:10):** "the voice of the WORD
   (meimra) of the LORD" walks in the garden; the human heard "the
   voice of YOUR WORD." And *le-ruach ha-yom* ("at the breeze of the
   day") -> **limnach yoma ("at the REST of the day")** — the nuach
   rest-root again.
5. The fence's *tigu* ("touch") -> **tekarvun ("come NEAR")** — the
   sacrificial bring-near root; *pen* ("lest") -> dilma ("perhaps").
6. 3:6's criteria rewritten: *taavah la-einayim* -> **assi hu
   le-aynin ("a HEALING for the eyes")**; *le-haskil* ("to make
   wise") -> le-istakala ("to LOOK AT") — the wisdom claim stripped
   from the woman's reasoning, left only in the serpent's promise.
7. 3:11 *mi higid lekha* ("who TOLD you") -> man CHAVI lakh — the
   telling verb is the chava root (the name the woman receives at
   3:20: the next unit's watch item).

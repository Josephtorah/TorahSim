1. **The waters LIFT the ark (7:17):** VA-YISU ("and they LIFTED/CARRIED")
   — the nasa bear-verb's TOKEN 2 in the Torah (census: 168 Torah tokens;
   token 1 = 4:13 mi-neso, Cain's "my punishment is too great to BEAR").
   The verb is ACTIVE, plural, with THE WATERS as agent — the judgment

   the letter gives the waters the deed]. And VA-TARAM ("and it ROSE
   high") — the rum root ("be high/rise"), its FIRST TOKEN of 46 in the
   Torah: the rise-verb debuts on the ark; career: 14:22 (Abram's
   raised-hand oath), Exod 7:20 (the staff raised over the Nile),
   Exod 14:8 (Israel leaving Egypt "with a HIGH hand").
2. **The ark WALKS where the spirit hovered (7:18):** VA-TELEKH ("and it
   WENT/WALKED") — the go-verb stem's TOKEN 2 (census, Strong 3212: 229
   Torah tokens; token 1 = 3:14, the serpent's belly-crawl sentence "you
   shall GO on your belly") — applied to a VESSEL, AL-PNEI HA-MAYIM ("on
   the FACE OF THE WATERS"): that phrase has exactly THREE Torah tokens
   (census): 1:2 (the spirit of God hovering on the face of the waters),
   7:18 (the ark walking there), Exod 32:20 (the golden calf's dust
   scattered there). The ark's builder walked with God (hithalekh, "walked
   about", 6:9 — the other walk-stem); now his build walks the deep.
3. **The prevail-verb is BORN as a flood cluster (7:18-24):** GAVAR
   ("prevail / be strong") — census: exactly 7 Torah tokens, and TOKENS
   1-4 ARE ALL IN THIS SPAN (7:18, 7:19, 7:20, 7:24): the
   strengthen-verb's debut is four beats of rising water. Tokens 5-7:
   49:26 (the blessings of your father PREVAILED) and Exod 17:11 x2
   (Israel and Amalek prevailing by Moses' hands). Morphology splits the
   four: 7:18 and 7:24 are narrative and-then forms (va-yigberu, "and
   they prevailed"); 7:19 and 7:20 are perfects (gavru, "they had
   prevailed") — two events framing two standing states.
4. **The mountains enter the corpus under water (7:19-20):** HAR
   ("mountain") — FIRST TOKENS of 150 in the Torah are 7:19 and 7:20,
   both drowned; token 3 = 8:4 (the ark RESTS on the mountains of
   Ararat). GAVOHA ("high/lofty") — exactly 3 Torah tokens, TOKEN 1 here
   (7:19, "the HIGH mountains"; the others: Deut 3:5 and 28:52 — high

   tokens (of 47) are these two agentless passives (va-yekhusu, "and
   they WERE COVERED" — pual, the intensive passive); token 3 = 9:23,
   the sons COVERING their father's nakedness: the verb debuts burying
   mountains and next appears as filial mercy.
5. **Fifteen cubits, the blueprint's own unit (7:20):** AMAH ("cubit")
   TOKEN 5 (census: 73 Torah tokens; tokens 1-4 are ALL the ark's

   the rescue's own unit. Token 6 opens Exod 25:10 — the dimensions of

6. **Three death-orders, none the same (7:21 / 7:23 / 6:7):** the 7:21
   dying-list runs bird -> livestock -> beast -> swarmer -> ALL

8. **The wipe executed TWICE in one verse (7:23):** MACHAH ("wipe") —

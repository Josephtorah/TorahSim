2. **"Sevenfold" exists exactly TWICE in the whole Torah (7659):**

## C. Tier-A Onkelos findings (read at derive time, FETCHLOG 2026-08-01)

1. **4:23 — THE BOAST NEGATED (flagged, the sharpest inversion in
   our spans):** "I have NOT (la) killed a man that I should bear
   sins (savil chovin) for him, NOR wounded a youth that my seed
   (zar'i) be destroyed." The Aramaic inserts a negation the letter
   does not have: confession -> protest of innocence. And its
   vocabulary is loaded: savil = BEAR (the nasa/burden thread) and
   "my seed destroyed" echoes the descendants-doctrine the same
   translator put into 4:10.
2. **4:24 — vengeance becomes mercy arithmetic:** "seven GENERATIONS
   were suspended (it'liu) for Cain — shall not (ha-la) Lamech his
   son [get] seventy-seven?" Consistent with Onkelos 4:15
   (seven-generations clock); the boast becomes an a-fortiori PLEA,
   and "his son" compresses the genealogy.
3. **4:26 — the translation VOTES on the fork:** "then in his days
   the sons of man CEASED/PROFANED (chalu) from praying (letzala'ah)
   in the Name of the LORD." huchal's dark sense chosen; "calling"
   upgraded to "praying"; the subject supplied (sons of man). The
   letter stays [OPEN]; the vote is dual-track.
4. **4:20-22 — avi ("father-of") -> RABBHON ("guild-master-of"):**
   the office formula institutionalized — and Onkelos GRANTS
   Tuval-Kayin the master-title the Hebrew withholds (the letter's
   broken pattern leveled). 4:20's tent rendered mashkenin — the
   Tabernacle root for a herdsman's dwelling.
5. **4:25 — the divine-name switch ERASED:** Hebrew Elohim -> Aramaic
   YY ("the LORD") — both of the mother's birth-speeches now use one
   name; and shat ("set") flattened to yehav ("gave"): the 3:15
   ashit root-echo deleted in translation (dual-track, flagged).
6. 4:17 city = karta, the building participle preserved; 4:19 "took
   two wives" preserved without comment; 4:21 pipe -> abuva, with
   "knowers of song" added; 4:22 the she-spelling leveled to plain
   "she too."

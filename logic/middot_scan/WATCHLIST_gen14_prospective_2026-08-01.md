   garden (alone, at the wind of the day) -> 5:22 + 5:24 Chanokh

   Solitary walk -> accompanied walk -> commanded walk.

7. **The Noah-speech quotes the curse dossier (5:29):** ITZAVON

   our spans):** "and he was not, for the LORD (LA — not?) CAUSED

   likeness, WHO RESEMBLED HIM (de-damei leh)" — the Aramaic
   refuses to pass tzelem ("image") father-to-son, exactly where
   the letter dares it (flagged).

   created adam, in the likeness of GOD (Elohim) he made him" —
   the Hebrew has Elohim in both slots; the translation gives the
   creating to YY and leaves the likeness with Elohim.

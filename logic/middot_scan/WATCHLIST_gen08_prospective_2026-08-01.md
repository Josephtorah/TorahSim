## A. Corpus-side structural finds (DB-verified 2026-08-01, pre-derivation)

1. **YHWH debuts at Gen 2:4** (lemma 3068 first token), and the BARE
   compound *YHWH Elohim* ("the LORD God" — personal name + the week's
   generic name, no suffix) occurs in the whole Torah ONLY in Gen
   2:4-3:23 (20 tokens) + **Exod 9:30** (1) — the Eden narrative's
   exclusive signature. Solo YHWH first appears at Gen 4:1, after the
   expulsion. Inside the span the compound is unbroken; in the serpent
   dialogue (3:1-5, next units) the name drops to bare Elohim — the
   serpent never says the personal name.
2. **Gen 2:16 *akhol tokhel* ("eating you may eat") is the corpus's
   FIRST infinitive-absolute doubling** — the emphatic device debuts in
   the first rule's PERMISSION clause. Tokens 2-4 of the device: 2:17
   *mot tamut* ("dying you shall die" — the penalty), 3:4 *lo mot
   temutun* (the serpent's negated quote), 3:16 *harbah arbeh*
   ("multiplying I will multiply" — the sentence).
3. **Gen 2:17 is the corpus's first prohibition** (first *lo* + 2ms
   imperfect: *lo tokhal*, "you shall not eat"). Second token: Gen 3:17
   — the sentencing verse QUOTING this rule back verbatim.
4. **The death-doubling census (31 members):** 2:17's *mot tamut* is the
   first of a family that becomes the law codes' standing capital
   formula *mot yumat* ("he shall surely be put to death") — Exod 21:12,
   15, 16, 17; 22:18; 31:14-15; Lev 20 ×9; 24:16-17; Num 35 ×5; etc.
   The Eden penalty clause institutionalizes.
5. ***va-yetzav* ("and He commanded") at 2:16 is the first token of
   tzavah (6680)** — 252 occurrences Torah-wide; root of the *ka'asher
   tzivah* ("as He commanded") receipt system measured corpus-wide.
6. **toledot ("generations") series confirmed:** 13 tokens in Genesis
   (2:4, 5:1, 6:9, 10:1, 10:32, 11:10, 11:27, 25:12, 25:13, 25:19, 36:1,
   36:9, 37:2) — 2:4 opens the series: the book's own section-header
   device, first applied to HEAVEN AND EARTH, not a person.
7. **Letter minimal pair inside the span:** 2:7 *va-YIItzer* ("and He
   formed", wayyiqtol) written with DOUBLE yod; 2:8 *yatzar* (perfect)
   with single yod — he_plain DB-verified. (The two-formations /
   two-inclinations chain discussion is Berakhot 61a + Bereshit Rabbah
   14 — named-only, not cached.) The formation verb's only non-Eden
   Torah token is **Exod 32:4 — Aaron forms the CALF** (join table,
   A-cross-book).
8. **Ktiv-qere in the span:** 2:12 *ha-hiv* — 3fs pronoun written as
   *hu* (morph HTd/Pp3fs against written he_plain ההוא), the Torah's
   pervasive written-he/read-she oddity, present in the river registry.
9. **Weqatal ("and-he-shall-do" form) in NARRATIVE:** 2:6 *ve-hishqah*
   ("and it would water") and 2:10 *ve-hayah* ("and it would become") —
   the law-genre spine form (TIR-029) used here as past-HABITUAL /
   geographic present. Genre control: the form is not law-exclusive;
   what marks law (lev_13) is the unbroken CHAIN of them.
10. **Negative-precondition block 2:5-6 = the 1:2 pattern:** *terem*
    ("not-yet") ×2 + imperfects, *lo himtir* ("had not caused rain"),
    *adam ayin* ("no human") — the lack-list names the JOB (*la-avod
    et-ha-adamah*, "to work the ground") before the worker exists.
    *terem* count: 8 in Torah — and one of them is **Exod 9:30, the
    same verse that carries the only non-Eden bare compound**: both
    Eden signatures co-occur there (discovered join, record).

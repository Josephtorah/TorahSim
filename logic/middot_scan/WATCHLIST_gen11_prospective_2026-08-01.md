## A. Corpus-side structural finds (DB-verified 2026-08-01, pre-derivation)

1. **The cunning/cursed pun brackets the serpent's arc:** 3:1 *arum
   mi-kol chayat ha-sadeh* ("CUNNING more than every beast of the
   field") -> 3:14 *arur ... mi-kol ... chayat ha-sadeh* ("CURSED more
   than every beast of the field") — one letter apart (mem/resh), the
   same comparative frame: the credential returns as the sentence.
2. **arur ("cursed") — the corpus's first curse, and its chain is
   explicit (lemma 779, exact):** 3:14 (serpent) -> 3:17 (THE GROUND,
   not the man — *ba-avurekha*, "because of you") -> **4:11 (Cain:
   "cursed are YOU from the ground" — the curse reaches a human one
   cycle later, and the ground is its instrument)** -> 5:29 (Noah
   NAMED as consolation "from the ground WHICH YHWH CURSED" — the
   3:17 curse cited verbatim) -> 9:25 (Canaan). The negative status
   axis opens opposite 2:3's kadosh ("holy") — both written by the
   same ASSIGN device.
3. **eivah ("enmity") has THREE Torah tokens:** 3:15 -> Num 35:21, 22
   — the MANSLAYER law's intent criterion ("in enmity" = murder;
   "without enmity" = manslaughter): the sentence's word becomes the
   law's mens-rea test. The enmity is SET *bein ... u-vein* x4 — day
   1's partition vocabulary (havdil bein) turned hostile. *shuf* (the
   head/heel verb) has exactly TWO corpus tokens, both in 3:15 —
   a reciprocal that exists nowhere else.
4. **teshukah ("longing") + mashal ("rule") repeat as a PAIR:** 3:16
   (to the woman) -> **4:7 (to CAIN: "its longing is for you, and you
   shall rule over it" — sin at the door gets the woman's sentence
   grammar verbatim)** — teshukah's only two Torah tokens.
5. **itzavon ("toil") x3:** 3:16 (the woman's) + 3:17 (the man's —
   the SAME word shared across both sentences) + 5:29 (Noah named as
   comfort "from the ITZAVON of our hands"). *harbah arbeh*
   ("multiplying I will multiply", 3:16) = infinitive-absolute
   doubling #4 (first hiphil member; census at gen_08).
6. **The rule is quoted a FOURTH time (3:17) — verbatim at last:**

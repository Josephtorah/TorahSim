# Day one re-derived under the readings — delta vs frozen v1

DRAFT · 2026-08-20 comparison exercise (owner order). Baseline: the five
`boot_steps` of frozen `gen_01_creation_boot.yaml` (STEP_Gn_1_1..1_5).
Sources: the 16 material verdicts of the 2026-07-30 triage ledger that
anchor to Gen 1:1-5 (claims C1-C11 in claims_manifest.json).

## What the readings CHANGED

1. **1:1 is an utterance, not "narrated not commanded" (C1).** v1's
   comment says: "No directive operator: verse 1 is direct
   initialization, narrated not commanded." Three independent chain
   witnesses (Bereshit Rabbah 17:1, Megillah 21b, Rosh Hashanah 32a)
   count 1:1 among the ten utterances via Ps 33:6 — the absence of
   וַיֹּאמֶר (va-yomer, "and He said") is surface form, not class. v2
   gives e1 an UTTERANCES register entry; day one ends at 2 of the ten.

2. **Bulk-create architecture (C2).** New from Rosh Hashanah 32a: the
   bereshit-utterance creates all matter at once; the later fiats
   differentiate. v2 seeds `matter_bulk` at 1:1 and models 1:3's light
   as differentiation, not a second ex-nihilo creation.

3. **The 1:2 presupposed-materials flag becomes a two-witness dispute
   (C5).** v1 flags choshekh/tehom/mayim/ruach as "READ without prior
   install (uninitialized entities)" — a flag, not a fix. The readings
   supply the chain's own two answers: Bereshit Rabbah 1:9 (found
   materials — the philosopher's challenge, answered with creation
   verses elsewhere) vs Chagigah 12a:6 (the ten things created ON day
   one, including tohu, vohu, darkness, wind, water). v2 runs BOTH
   readings and asserts convergence of observable post-1:5 state;
   under reading B the uninitialized-read dissolves and 1:4's divide
   operates on an installed entity — v1's step-4 comment ("divide
   operates on choshekh — an entity never installed") holds only
   under reading A.

4. **The yom collision resolves by polysemy split (C8).** v1 writes
   `name(or) := yom` and then uses yom as the ledger's day unit —
   one word, two jobs, uncommented. Onkelos splits it formally:
   יְמָמָא (yemama, "daytime") for the registry label, יוֹמָא (yoma,
   "day") for the calendar unit. v2 carries two senses:
   `or -> yom[daytime]`, ledger unit `yom[calendar]`.

5. **The cardinal flag closes (C9).** v1: "echad = CARDINAL 'one' —
   marked form, flagged not resolved … [OPEN]". Bereshit Rabbah 3:9
   asks the mixed-series question itself and answers it; Onkelos
   preserves יוֹמָא חַד (yoma chad, "ONE day"). The anomaly is
   chain-witnessed as intentional: [OPEN] → CHAIN-WITNESSED.

## What the readings CONFIRMED (now chain-grounded, was grammar-only)

- **TIME_ANCHOR** (C10): Onkelos בְּקַדְמִין (be-kadmin, "at first").
- **Theme-marker et** (C3): Chagigah 12a:20 — without et, שָׁמַיִם
  (shamayim, "heavens") could read as the Creator's name; Onkelos
  renders et as the accusative marker. TIR-014/015's reading now has
  the Bavli and the authorized translation behind it. Plus R. Akiva's
  inclusions: the Themes are inventories (sun/moon/constellations;
  trees/grasses/garden).
- **Latency-0 RESULT** (C6): Bereshit Rabbah 3:2 derives
  instantaneous-complete from וַיְהִי (va-yehi, "and it was") vs
  וְהָיָה (ve-haya, "and it shall be") — the chain's own derivation,
  not just our mood-reading.
- **Registry semantics** (C7): Bereshit Rabbah 3:6 asks "is the light
  the day?!" — label ≠ entity, exactly v1's "registry write, not a
  truth claim."
- **The cycle commit** (C11): seder zemanim ("order of times") from
  the evening-morning close — cited since the pilot, re-read in full.

## Genuinely NEW

- **Theme order carries no temporal claim (C4)**: Mekhilta Pischa 1:4
  rules surface order ≠ creation order (via Gen 2:4 "earth and
  heaven"). v2's Theme list is an unordered set. v1 was silent.

  ha-ganuz, "the hidden light") — the primordial light hidden for the
  righteous, טוֹב (tov, "good") read as the righteous (Bereshit Rabbah
  3:6 + Chagigah 12a:10). Carried as ORAL WITNESS on the claim record,
  deliberately NOT machine state — aggadic, not operative.

## Post-comparison fix (2026-08-20)

The blind comparison against the TorahSim derivation caught one miss on
this side, now fixed as C12: the ten-utterances census carries its own
recorded dissent — R. Yaakov ben Kurshai counts the רוּחַ (ruach,
"wind/spirit") of 1:2 as an utterance, Menachem bar Yosei swaps it for
Gen 2:18 — so "utterances after day one" is itself disputed (2 vs 3).
The machine now carries it as a dispute register, never deciding it.
The mirror-image miss (Bereshit Rabbah 3:7's seder zemanim citation,
absent from the TorahSim claims) was sent back to that side and fixed.

The hidden-light divergence was RULED by the owner 2026-08-20: TYPED
state — the hidden light enters the world witness-grounded, its
evidence tier distinct from every text-grounded fact, so Ps 97:11
("light is sown for the righteous") finds it mechanically when Psalms
is derived, while the world's claims stay tiered honestly. Neither the
flat-state nor the witness-only draft position survived unchanged;
the ruling is the synthesis.

## Bottom line

The five verses' operator skeleton survives re-derivation intact —
TIME_ANCHOR, EVENT, PRECONDITION, DECLARE/TRIPLE/RESULT,
TEST/PARTITION, NAME/COMMIT all stand. What the readings change is the
CLASSIFICATION layer above it: 1:1 joins the utterance census, creation
becomes bulk-then-differentiate, the 1:2 flag becomes the chain's own
recorded dispute (run both ways, converging), and two of v1's honest
[OPEN] flags close with chain witnesses. Nothing in the readings
contradicts a v1 operator; three v1 self-doubts turn out to be
questions the chain asked itself — and answered.

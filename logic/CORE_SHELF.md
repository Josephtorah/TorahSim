## Foundation — both tracks, every span

1. **The Hebrew text itself** with cantillation trees + morphology
   (Steps 1-2, done whole Bible — Data/*.xml, the parse DB). The source.
2. **Onkelos** — the received translation (chain_primary). The minimal
   resolver: read Tier-A at derive time on every span; where it smooths
   the Hebrew (plural for singular, a counting verb made explicit), the
   roughness it smooths is evidence.
3. **Rashi** — the chain's first-pass compiled commentary (11th c.).
   Not itself the authority: the POINTER LAYER — names the chain source
   or middah for nearly every crux; what Rashi transmits, verify at its
   primary.
4. **The middot catalogs** — the 13 of Rabbi Ishmael (law) + the 32 of
   Rabbi Eliezer (narrative): logic/MIDDOT.md. The licensed inference
   forms; Step 5's `middah:` field.
5. **The masorah apparatus** (Minchat Shai as gateway) — the letter
   layer: defective/full spellings, ketiv/qere ("written"/"read" —
   where the received text is written one way and read another),
   crowns. Where our
   letter-visible flags meet the tradition's own letter bookkeeping
   (the gen_04 orthography dossier came from here).

## The Law track — compiling a legal span

6. **The verse-order legal midrash on the span's book** — the token
   decoder, tannaitic, walks the scroll (chain_primary):
   Exodus → **Mekhilta** (both recensions local) ·
   Leviticus → **Sifra** ·
   Numbers → **Sifrei Numbers** ·
   Deuteronomy → **Sifrei Deuteronomy**. (Genesis has none — narrative.)
7. **Mishnah** — the case tables: input→output, no reasons
   (chain_primary; 63 tractates local; topic map logic/MISHNAH_TOPICS.md).
   Step-5 claims + THE Step-9 test suite.
8. **Tosefta** — the supplement cases, same topic order
   (chain_primary; 61 tractates local).
9. **Babylonian Talmud** — the derivation record: read the WHOLE
   discussion on each implementing mishnah unit (chain_primary; 37
   tractates local).
10. **Jerusalem Talmud** — independent derivation witness, and the ONLY
    Talmud on most of Seeds and Purities (chain_primary; 39 local).
11. **Mishneh Torah (Maimonides)** — the compiled reference
    implementation. Register class halakhic_application: NEVER a claims
    source — its role is the deployed-code cross-check: our machine's
    outputs should be explainable against the code the tradition
    actually shipped.

## Explicitly NOT minimum (depth, when wanted)

- **Anthologies/locators** (Yalkut Shimoni, Lekach Tov, Sekhel Tov,
  Ein Yaakov, Torah Temimah): resolve to the primary they quote.
- **The Rishonim bench beyond Rashi** (Ibn Ezra, Ramban, Chizkuni,
  Radak, Sforno…): read in TOP10 depth passes, not the minimum.
- **Supercommentary on Rashi** (Mizrachi, Gur Aryeh, Siftei Chakhamim)
  and **Acharonim** (Malbim, Hirsch…): depth strata.
- **Later codes** (Tur, Shulchan Arukh): second deployed-code mirrors
  after Mishneh Torah.
- **outside_chain_academic**: observation only, never authority.

shorthand; decode:

| Shorthand | Means | Is |
|-----------|-------|----|
| **Bavli** ("Babylonian") | the **Babylonian Talmud** | Mishnah + Babylonian commentary (the default "the Talmud") |
| **Yerushalmi** ("Jerusalemite") | the **Jerusalem Talmud** | Mishnah + Land-of-Israel commentary |
| **Gemara** ("the learning") | the commentary layer inside either Talmud | Talmud = Mishnah + Gemara |
| **Shas** (initials of "six orders") | the whole Talmud | as in Mesorat haShas |
| **BR / Bereshit Rabbah** | **Midrash Rabbah, Genesis** | narrative track book 1 |
| **PDRK** | **Pesikta DeRav Kahana** | early homiletic cycle |
| **PRE** | **Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer** | narrative compilation |
| **Rambam** ("R. Moshe ben Maimon") | **Maimonides** | author of the Mishneh Torah |
| **MT** | the **Mishneh Torah** | the compiled code, not the Mishnah! |
| **JT / TJ** | Jerusalem Talmud / Targum Jonathan | context decides — spell them out |

contains it whole and adds its own discussion.

**AN UNANTICIPATED RETURN, found in the first derived parashah**
(2026-08-30; the workshop found the instances, both windows read them
the same way — a finding, not a ruling, recorded because it would
otherwise live only in chat). The spine default reduces the declared
shelf to TWO members, the book's own spine and Onkelos. That makes a
new class of evidence legible: when the two members disagree, converge,
or divide internally, it is the WHOLE DECLARED SHELF doing it, with no
third voice to drown the signal. Parashat Vayera produced four such
instances — at Genesis 18:3 the spine and the translation split on
whether Abraham's address is sacred or profane; at 19:18 that same
split appears INSIDE Onkelos's own printed editions; and at 18:21,
19:27 and 20:3 the two converge INDEPENDENTLY, the last of them on a
prophecy grade, where the midrash states the rule outright while the
translation encodes the identical distinction structurally, routing
the gentile king's revelation through the Word where Abraham's comes
direct — neither citing the other. Independent convergence is worth
more than agreement by dependence. The reading was made thinner to go
faster; it also made the shelf's own agreement measurable. No display
class has been built for this: four instances from one parashah is
thin evidence, and the standing method here is to tag the instances
and let the count decide rather than argue.

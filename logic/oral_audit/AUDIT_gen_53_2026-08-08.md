## CROWNS (chain-attested + DB-verified)

- **G53-07 the one full fathers**: אבותיך ("RETURN to the land of
  YOUR FATHERS," 31:3 — the command that turns the unit) is the
  Torah's ONE fathers-of-yours written full with both vav and
  yod; the other fifteen tokens all run vav-lean. The word
  written whole exactly once — at the command to go back to it.
- **G53-11 the one full and-they-said**: ותאמרנה ("and Rachel and
  Leah answered AND SAID," 31:14) — the Torah's ONE feminine
  group-speech written full with its closing he; its two bare
  sisters are the midwives before Pharaoh (Exod 1:19) and the
  well-daughters of Midian (Exod 2:19): three companies of women
  speak in the Torah, and only the wives consenting to the return
  speak the word written whole.
- **G53-03 the glory written like the liver**: הכבד ("all this
  WEALTH," 31:1) — the glory-word written LEAN against the
  fullness of every other glory-word in the Torah whose kaf
  carries qamatz (the long a), and the
  lean skeleton is exactly the skeleton of the LIVER: the other
  eleven tokens of these letters are all the liver-lobe of the
  altar (Exodus/Leviticus, bet in tzere — the long e — builder-checked
  one by one). The
  begrudged wealth written one vav short, into the letters of the
  organ burned whole on the fire. Second skeleton collision of
  the run (after the remaining flock of gen_52).
- **G53-04/05 yesterday whole at the seeing, bare at the telling**:
  כתמול שלשום ("as yesterday and the day before") — at 31:2 (the
  narrator: Jacob SAW Laban's face) both words full; at 31:5 (Jacob
  TELLING his wives) the identical idiom drops both vavs — exactly
  as the Hilleli, the precise books, and the Ramah's lean-list
  rule. What Jacob saw was whole; what he could say of it had
  already thinned.
- **G53-08 the pronoun among the give-verbs** (Kitzur): ואתנה
  ("AND YOU know," 31:6) — nine tokens of these letters in the
  Torah, eight the verb "and I will give"; only here the feminine
  pronoun (the tagged form distinguishes them) — and it is the
  pronoun the Masorah's three-list counts, both companions in
  Ezekiel (the pasture-flock that is Israel; the hailstones).
- **G53-12 the dream-lift's ruled music**: ואשא עיני ("and I
  LIFTED MY EYES," 31:10) — merkha (the Sephardic maarich) on the
  lift-verb, TEVIR on the eyes-word against the zaqef qatan (the small
  two-dot pause) camp:
  the accent-pair carried mark for mark.
- **G53-09/10 the mock and the change**: התל ("your father has
  MOCKED me," 31:7) — he in tzere, tav soft with its doubling dot
  resting unwritten (Minchat Shai dismissing the printer by the
  verse's own verb: "a deceived heart misled him"); והחלף ("and
  he CHANGED my wage") — het in hataf segol (the compound
  half-vowel), lean of its yod with the Ramah.
- **G53-01/02/13/14 as ruled**: וישמע under revia (31:1); את of

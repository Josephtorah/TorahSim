  FULL with its yod exactly twice — the sweat-bread sentence (3:19)
  and "by the breath of Your nostrils the waters piled up" (Exod
  15:8). The Masorah's (the received text tradition's) own mnemonic:
  man's livelihood is as hard as the splitting of the Sea of Reeds.
  Check discriminates 2 of 4 tokens (the defective "your anger"

- **G11-02/03 the priestly garments**: וילבשם ("and He clothed them")
  appears twice per the Masorah — Adam here (3:21), Aaron's sons at
  Lev 8:13 — the tradition's teaching that God made the first man

  the eight garments of the priesthood (Kitzur). The frozen unit's
  own skin-word import chain to lev_13 now carries the tradition's
  priestly arrow beside it. (The Kitzur's English translation says
  "eight LETTERS" — the Hebrew says תיבות, "words": paraphrase-loss

  two Torah tokens — the serpent's strike-target (3:15) and Gad's

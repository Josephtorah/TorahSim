## Batch 1 digest — Torah Temimah on Exod 21:12-22 (55 listings)

Span: TT 21:12:1 → 21:22:8. The verse-indexed chain digest opens block 2
exactly as it opened block 1 — Torah Temimah as the map of the sugya-space.

**21:12 מכה איש ומת ("one who strikes a man and he dies").**
(1) Victim scope: "ish" (man) can't limit the victim — woman and minor
included from Lev 24:17 ואיש כי יכה כל נפש אדם ("a man who strikes ANY human
soul"); but נפלים (non-viable births) excluded — victim must be "fit to live
and become a man." Day-old viability threshold noted from Sanhedrin 84b.
(2) Striker scope: woman-striker liable (makkeh unqualified); MINOR-striker
excluded from Lev 24's "a MAN who strikes." TT asks why exclusion is needed
at all and answers from Rambam's civilization-defense theory of homicide
(kippah confinement): bloodshed "destroys the settlement of the world" — one
might extend ben-sorer logic (kill the future menace) to the killer-child;
the exclusion teaches the child matures OUT of cruelty. Developmental mens
rea, argued inside a footnote.
(3) Causation: ומת ("and he dies") — liable only if THIS blow took the whole
soul: ten men with ten sticks → ALL exempt (Sanhedrin 78a); a slap that
finishes an already-beaten victim isn't capital. כל נפש ("the whole soul")
as a totality-of-causation requirement.

**21:13 ואשר לא צדה ("and he who did not lie in wait") + refuge.**
(1) Near-willful excluded from refuge: intended to throw two cubits, threw
four → refuge city does NOT absorb him (karov le-mezid, near-willful), and
the blood-avenger who kills him goes free (Makkot 7b). Refuge is calibrated
to PURE accident only.
(2) Reish Lakish's inn (Makkot 10b) — see marquee. God as scheduler: הקב"ה
מזמינן לפונדק אחד ("the Holy One appoints them to one inn").
(3) ושמתי לך מקום ("I will appoint YOU a place") — לך read three ways: in
your lifetime (Moses separates three cities, Deut 4:41); from your place
(the Levite cities = refuge; Moses is a Levite); and desert-era exile = the
Levite CAMP. Levite who kills: exiled district-to-district (pelekh el
pelekh); TT untangles Tosafot vs his own reading of "his district absorbs
him" (killed outside Levite cities → flees to his own city).
(4) Temporary refuge (desert) ↔ permanent (Levite cities) welded by gezerah
shavah (verbal analogy), the earlier "from your place" demoted to mere
asmakhta (supporting allusion) — the system self-annotates which derivations
carry weight.

**21:14 וכי יזד ... מעם מזבחי תקחנו למות ("if a man schemes ... from My
altar you shall take him to die").**
(1) Hatraah from the imperfect — see marquee.
(2) The yazid/nazid pun (seed/cooking): "when does he become an ish? when
his seed cooks" — puberty definition for ben sorer windows (JT Sanhedrin).
(3) בערמה ("with guile"): warning must name WHICH death he'll die
(yaarimuhu — "make him wise to it"); and guile-capacity excludes deaf-mute
and insane (no daat, no guile), PLUS the authority exemptions: physician who
erred, court flogger, father/teacher chastising — exempt from death, but
Tosefta: liable to EXILE (not careful to strike gently) — and today, money.
(4) The altar stack — see marquee. Extra: תקחנו למות ("take him TO DIE") —
only for death; for money-judgments, flogging, exile the altar DOES shelter.
(5) Executions require the standing Temple ("if you have an altar you may
execute") — Ramban citing a Mekhilta lost from our text; TT connects the
40-years-before-destruction cessation of capital cases.

**21:15 ומכה אביו ואמו מות יומת ("one who strikes his father or his mother
shall surely die").**
(1) The rov/chazakah proof — see marquee.
(2) Wound requirement: strike-parent capital only with חבורה (wound) —
derived from the animal-striker parallel (Lev 24: pays only for wound);
healing exempted (son may bleed his father medically — but prefer another
physician, Yoreh Deah 241); liable only while the parent LIVES.
(3) Father alone / mother alone each suffice (analogy from the curser).
(4) Death = חנק (strangulation), the unmarked default: "every unqualified
mot yumat in the Torah is strangulation" — because Heaven's death leaves no
mark, court-default death leaves no mark (Sanhedrin 52b).

**21:16 וגונב איש ומכרו ("one who steals a man and sells him").**
Woman-kidnapper liable (unqualified וגונב); victim must be viable (bar
kayama); ומכרו — sold WHOLE, not half (half-slave victim excluded);
ונמצא בידו ("and he is found in his hand"): "found" = by two witnesses
(binyan av from Sifrei); "hand" = possession (he must bring victim into his
domain); his own SON excluded — מצוי ("regularly present") can't be
"found." TT then quotes the Saadia-Gaon structural theory (via Ibn
Ezra/Ramban): why does kidnap interrupt strike-parent → curse-parent?
Because kidnap-victims grow up not knowing their parents AND COME TO CURSE
THEM — the verse order encodes causation. His own pshat alternative: a
descending-severity ladder (killer > parent-striker > kidnapper > curser,
act > word) where each stays capital — לא זו אף זו ("not only this, even
this").

**21:17 ומקלל אביו ואמו ("one who curses his father or his mother").**
Curse-of-parent equated to curse-of-God (Kiddushin 30b — the honor triad's
dark twin); each parent separately (the two attachments of קלל in Exod vs
Lev 20:9); death = סקילה (STONING) via דמיו בו ("his blood is on him")
gezerah shavah from the ov/yidoni necromancers. TT's puzzle: stoning >
strangulation, yet cursing is word and striking is act — why is the WORD
harsher? His answer: the curse is equated to cursing GOD, and that fixes
stoning; striking has no divine analogue, so it falls to the default chenek.

**21:18-19 The injury engine opens: וכי יריבן אנשים ("if men quarrel").**
(1) באבן או באגרוף ("with stone or with fist") — R. Natan's two-way hekesh:
instrument must be death-capable AND witness-assessable; stone lost before
witnesses saw it → exempt (the assessment doctrine, omed la-nezakin —
"assessment applies to damages": a small pebble that can't injure → boshet,
humiliation-payment, only). R. Akiva: "delivered to witnesses."
(2) ולא ימות ("and he does not die") + ונקה המכה ("the striker is cleared")
— the CUSTODY doctrine (Ketubot 33b): assessed-for-death → jail him; victim
dies → execute; victim rises → pay idleness + healing. See marquee.
(3) והתהלך בחוץ על משענתו ("and walks outside on his support") — "outside,
not in the house"; "HIS support" = his own body's strength (על בוריו, "on
his soundness"), excluding the wasting walker (mitnavneh).
(4) רק שבתו יתן ורפא ירפא ("only his idleness he shall give, and heal he
shall heal") — the healing-liability cluster: victim defies doctor, eats
honey, proud flesh (garguteni) grows → striker exempt (רק minimizes);
relapse from the ORIGINAL wound (tzemachim sprout, wound reopens) → liable
again, from the doubled verb (like השב תשיבם "return, you shall return them
— even a hundred times"); physician's license — see marquee; and rippui is
owed BESIDES nezek (damage), not only in its absence.

**21:20-21 Slave homicide: וכי יכה איש את עבדו ("if a man strikes his
slave").**
(1) Canaanite slave only — כי כספו הוא ("for he is his money") = permanent
acquisition; Hebrew slave (six-year term) excluded.
(2) בשבט ("with a rod") — Rambam (Rotzeach 2:14): the two-day exemption
applies ONLY to disciplinary instruments (rod, strap); knife/sword/stone/
fist assessed-lethal → executed even if death comes a year later. The verse
licenses discipline, never execution-grade force.
(3) נקם ינקם ("avenged shall be avenged") = death by SWORD (from חרב נקמת
"an avenging sword", Lev 26:25) — TT: the נקם wording tells you the slave's
blood demands vengeance like any man's, despite the money-frame.
(4) יום או יומים ("a day or two days") — reconciled as "a day that is like
two days": 24 hours me-et le-et (from time to time).
(5) כי כספו הוא excludes: sold-with-30-day-service-reservation (NEITHER
seller nor buyer exempt — one lacks ownership, the other lacks service),
partnership slaves, half-slaves (Rambam end Rotzeach 2).

**21:22 וכי ינצו אנשים ונגפו אשה הרה ("if men brawl and strike a pregnant
woman").**
(1) R. Elazar: the brawl is INTENDED-killing (the ason clause proves it) —
so transferred intent is liable: meant one man, killed another (Ketubot
33a); the וכי יריבון / וכי ינצו pairing teaches it BOTH directions (JT
Sanhedrin 9:3), including partial transfer: didn't mean to sever the hand →
still pays the hand.
(2) אנשים ולא שוורים ("men, not oxen") — ox goring a pregnant woman → owner
EXEMPT from fetus-value (Bava Kamma 42a).
(3) אשה הרה ("a pregnant woman") — "hara" seemingly redundant (her children
went out!) → two readings: shevach heryon, the pregnancy PREMIUM assessed
to the husband (how much more is a pregnant woman worth in the market); or
R. Eliezer b. Yaakov via Rashbag: blow must land "opposite the womb" — Rav
Papa: wherever heat reaches = torso, excluding hand/foot blows. TT flags
Rambam's omission of the torso rule as צ"ע (needs study).
(4) ויצאו ילדיה ("and her children go out") — plural minimum two, אחד from
"hara" — even one; plural teaches twins are no defense.
(5) ולא יהיה אסון ("and there is no calamity") — Abaye welds man's ason to
Heaven's ason via Jacob's פן יקראנו אסון ("lest calamity befall him," Gen
42:38 — a GENESIS import): capital liability by Heaven (e.g. Yom Kippur
karet) ALSO cancels co-liable payment, like court-capital does — kim leih
bederabbah mineih ("he already stands in the greater one"). And the
one-penalty rule: משום רשעה אחת אתה מחייבו ("for ONE wickedness you charge
him") — death+money never stack; TT tours why multiple lashes-counts CAN
stack (same penalty-kind ≠ two wickednesses).

## Batch 2 digest — TT 21:22-27 + stragglers + Mekhilta DeRabbi Shimon 21:12-25 (38 listings)

Span: TT 21:22:9 → 21:27:1, TT 21:28:14 + 21:32:7 (goring-ox rows anchored
into this span), TT Lev 24:17:1 + Num 6:2:5 (cross-book TT rows), then

midrash walking the same verses, with real deltas from the Mekhilta of R.

**TT 21:22:9-14 — the payment clause ענוש יענש ונתן בפלילים ("punished he
shall be punished ... he shall give by the pelilim").**
(1) ענוש = MONEY, gezerah shavah from וענשו אתו מאה כסף ("they shall fine
him a hundred silver," Deut 22:19) — body-injury compensated in coin is the
imported precedent.
(2) כאשר ישית עליו בעל האשה ("as the woman's husband imposes on him") is
CAPPED by ונתן בפלילים — pelilim = JUDGES, proven from ואויבינו פלילים
("our enemies are judges," Deut 32:31) and ופללו אלהים ("God shall judge
him," 1 Sam 2:25). Husband's role: he must CLAIM (if he doesn't demand,
the court doesn't collect — Rashi's "when he sues"); the amount is the
court's.
(3) Rav Papa: fetus-value goes to the "husband" even from a non-marital
union; the Jerusalem Talmud gate: one "not fit to be CALLED a husband"
(incest unions — his mother, his sister) gets nothing — בעל read as a
status-qualification, with TT untangling R. Ukva vs the Rashba's R. Akiva
text via whether kiddushin (betrothal) takes hold in prohibited unions.

**TT 21:23 — ונתתה נפש תחת נפש ("you shall give life for life").**
Ason = death, from Jacob's וקראהו אסון ("and calamity befall him," Gen
42:38) — flagged only זכר לדבר (a "reminder," not proof; Jacob may have
feared lesser harm too). The tannaitic fork: the sages — LIFE literally
(they hold transferred intent liable → real execution); Rebbi — MONEY. And
the system's own consistency proof: why is נפש literal but עין ("eye")
money? Because Num 35:31 says no ransom (kofer) for a murderer's LIFE —
"but you DO take ransom for limbs" (Bava Kamma 83b). The ransom-exclusion
verse is the type-boundary between the two clauses.

**TT 21:24 — עין תחת עין ("eye for eye") = money.**
(1) Tanna d'vei Chizkiyah: "eye for eye — not eye-AND-life for eye":
literal blinding can kill; retaliation cannot guarantee equal magnitude →
must be payment. An overshoot-impossibility proof.
(2) Rav Ashi's gezerah shavah: תחת here ↔ תחת אשר ענה ("because he

**TT 21:26-27 — the slave's eye and tooth.**

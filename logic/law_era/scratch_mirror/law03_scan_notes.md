  "atonement like kodashim," and KODASHIM HIDES are permitted after
  the blood is thrown. Which is exactly why the stoned ox needed את
  בשרו to catch its hide: one derivation-net binding corpse, eglah
  arufah, sacrificial hides, and the ox's את.
- (B20) MAN'S LUCK BLOCKS THE TRANSFER (Hirsch, 21:35): אדם אית ליה
  מזלא — man stands under special heavenly protection, so a goring
  that would kill a beast "need not necessarily" kill a man; hence
  mu'ad-for-beasts is NOT mu'ad-for-man (three ox-kills leave it tam
  toward men), though the reverse holds. The two נגח/נגף verbs split
  as act-word vs effect-word (מגפה = sudden death) on the same page.
- (B19) HIRSCH'S DIGNITY RATIONALE FOR THE FLAT 30 (crown of the
  series): human life has NO market price; the free man's cofer uses
  his hypothetical slave-price as a mere TOKEN — harmless, since for
  a free man it is obviously not his real value. But pricing a SLAVE
  by his ACTUAL market value would make the valuation real — degrading,
  against the law's own point. So the Torah FIXES the sum, at the
  HIGHEST Arakhin value of the Jewish woman (30), whose mitzvah-
  station the slave shares. The flat tariff protects "the godlike
  value of every human life." (After Chizkuni's frequency, Bekhor
  Shor's quarrels, Netziv's viciousness-fine, Bartenura's average,
  Maharal's metaphysics — the dignity reading.)
- (B19) THE STONING AS DELEGATED DIVINE JUDGMENT (Hirsch, 21:28):
  the ownerless ox is still executed — so the stoning is neither
  owner-punishment nor police safety; it is Gen 9:5 מיד כל חיה
  אדרשנו ("from every beast I will demand it") DELEGATED to human
  courts, "to keep the godlike dignity of human life vividly
  conscious." Same ownerless-ox datum Meshekh Chokhmah used (B16),
  turned into moral philosophy.
- (B19) NO COFER FOR THE PIT-DEATH BECAUSE MONEY CANNOT REACH IT
  (Hirsch, 21:33): human life is not replaceable property — no
  compensation category exists for it; the ox-owner's cofer atones,
  but the pit-maker's guilt for a human death "remains before the
  Presence of God" under ולא תשים דמים בביתך ("harbour no blood-guilt
  in thy home"). The MISSING payment read as the GREATER culpability.
- (B19) HIRSCH'S קרקע עולם CAUSATION: the beast that stumbled over
  the pit and fell OUTSIDE it — the world's ground hurt it, exempt;
  fell INSIDE — the same world-ground at the pit's bottom "received
  its dangerous properties through him," becoming HIS instrument.
  Plus the resting-hazard אב (glass, roof-objects blown down that
  damage after coming to rest) and the threatened class defined:
  those who do not watch the ground — men, young beasts, old ones
  after dark. And the גרמא remainder: legally exempt indirect
  causation is still "a grave crime for which God holds us
  responsible" (BK 56a) — the law's edge is not morality's edge.
- (B19) RASHBAM'S OPEN PESHAT/SAGES LEDGER: והמת יהיה לו — peshat:
  to the DAMAGER ("since he pays the full value, justice is that the
  dead be his"); "but the sages interpreted: to the victim." Stated
  side by side without anxiety — the same split the Gra ruled on
  (B11). And his working-digger realism on כי יכרה: the digger who
  doesn't cover each evening "because tomorrow he must enter and dig
  again, and covering it daily is a bother — nevertheless liable."
- (B19) כרה = TO PREPARE, NOT ONLY TO DIG (Hirsch): the verb is
  chosen over חפר because it also means arrange/adapt — exact for
  ALTERING an existing pit; the repeated איש = exclusive liability of
  the one whose act changed the danger's FORM (9→10 handbreadths:
  the whole pit is his). A fourth, independent philology of the
  digger-after-digger clause (after Mecklenburg B13, Netziv B14,
  Malbim B15 — theirs shallow-vs-complete, his modify-vs-create).
- (B18) RALBAG'S ANTI-SPOILAGE ECONOMICS of והמת יהיה לו: if the
  carcass belonged to the DAMAGER, he would refuse to touch it until
  court ruled (possession = admission) and the victim would not tend
  what is not his — the carcass would ROT while both wait. So "the
  Torah's yosher" vests it in the VICTIM at the moment of death, so
  someone sells it in time — and the pit-owner still pays the cost
  of hauling it out of his pit. The verse's purpose read as incentive
  design against dead-weight loss.
- (B18) STRUCTURE AS LAW (Ralbag, shoresh 3): the Torah INTERRUPTED
  the ox-kills-man exposition with the pit clause before finishing
  with ox-kills-ox — and the interruption omits any man's death; so
  a man who dies in a pit is excluded by the passage's ARCHITECTURE,
  not by a word: the pit has no self-starting motion, so the ox's
  death-law cannot cross to it. (Injury liability survives — money
  lost is money restored, man or beast alike.)
- (B18) THE VICTIM BECOMES CO-DEFENDANT (Ralbag): since a tam pays
  from its body (ומכרו את השור החי), the victim becomes PARTNER in
  the goring ox — and if it gores again, the victim-partner shares
  liability for the new goring in proportion to his share. The
  remedy converts the plaintiff into part-owner of the hazard.
- (B18) THE BURGLAR IS KILLED AS RESCUE, NOT PUNISHMENT (Ralbag,
  shorashim 25-26): therefore a MINOR or FEMALE burglar is equally
  killable — no age-of-punishment threshold applies, "the soul of
  the female or the minor is not dearer than the adult's"; and the
  father tunneling into his SON'S house is presumed non-lethal —
  proven from David's order to spare Absalom even while Absalom
  hunted his life.
- (B18) LOST-OPTION-VALUE IN THEFT (Ralbag, shoresh 18): a stolen
  thing whose way is to APPRECIATE at a set season — the thief pays
  double/four-five at COURT-STANDING value, "for the owners would
  have waited to sell at the dear time"; if it declines, he pays at
  theft-time value (no one waits for a falling market). Market-
  timing economics inside a 14th-century damages code. Beside it:
  the shekel weighed at 384 BARLEY GRAINS of pure silver.
- (B18) WIFE-AND-SLAVE DAMAGES DEFERRED (Ralbag): the owner is
  exempt for what his slave or wife damages — "else the slave could
  destroy his master's whole estate, and this is not the Torah's
  yosher" — but the liability WAITS: they pay when he is freed, when
  she is divorced. Judgment-proof today, collectible tomorrow.
- (B17) TIFERET SHLOMO'S DEATH-SPLITTING SEGULAH (via Pardes Yosef):
  for one under the signs of death — give charity and split the word:
  insert חצי ("half") into מת ("dead") and it becomes מחצית ("the
  half-shekel"). וחצו את כספו ("split its silver" — to charity) →
  וגם את המת יחצון ("the dead one too they shall halve") = the death
  decree itself is halved. Chassidic word-surgery on the tam clause.
- (B17) OR HACHAIM: THE AVENGER WHO KILLS THE OX-OWNER IS EXECUTED —
  the blood-avenger's heat-of-heart license (יחם לבבו) covers only a
  hands-on killer; where "his ox killed," the go'el hadam who strikes
  first is a murderer. And והועד בבעליו in the PLURAL: an ox of two
  partners → BOTH owners heaven-liable, and in cofer (money) both
  pay full ransom (Rambam: a whole cofer each, stringent in doubt).
- (B17) OR HACHAIM'S STADIUM-OX THEODICY (Gen 8:21 crossref): "man's
  heart is evil from his youth" = the gladiator ox — כי יגח ולא
  שיגיחוהו ("when it gores, not when they make it gore," BK 39b):
  evil TRAINED from the womb mitigates God's wrath ("he has a cause
  for his wickedness") but not accountability — man's advantage over
  the trained ox is that he can discern and refuse.
- (B17) A SECOND SPELLING-MACHINE ON THE PIT (R. Shlomo Cohen via
  Pardes Yosef): full בור = the VAPOR-pit (stuffed with wool, no
  impact — liability rides hevel, needs the full ten handbreadths);
  defective בר = the IMPACT-pit (chavata — six suffice, for "the
  ox's belly rides four handbreadths off the ground," BK 51a). The
  hevel-vs-chavata physics of BK 50b encoded in plene/defective,
  independent of the Gra's who-fills-pays run (B13).
- (B17) VERDICT AS ISSUR-GENERATOR (Imrei HaTzvi via Pardes Yosef):
  even those who permit benefit from a gentile's corpse concede that
  one EXECUTED BY COURT-VERDICT is like the stoned ox — forbidden to
  all; the death-sentence itself generates the prohibition (the
  Keritot verdict-carries-the-issur principle extended to persons).
  Beside it the whole corpse-benefit net: Philistine foreskins "worth
  a perutah to dogs," human skin permitted mid'oraita (Tosafot Niddah).
- (B17) RAMBAM'S CONFLICT-OF-LAWS APOLOGIA (via Pardes Yosef): the
  gentile-ox asymmetry defended — "we judge them by THEIR OWN laws,
  for it cannot be that Israel's law should serve them better than
  their own" (Nizkei Mamon 5:5, from the Yerushalmi); nations that
  keep the seven laws are judged as Israel; "and with this falls the
  complaint of the accusers." The medieval anti-defamation defense
  inside the inversion.
- (B17) THE MU'AD TEMPLATE ENTERS PENITENTIAL THEOLOGY (Tosafot Yom
  HaKippurim via Pardes Yosef): sins are forgiven THREE times (Yoma
  86b) because through three the sinner is NOT YET MU'AD — the
  fourth finds him forewarned. Fourth system-isomorphism, after
  plague (B10), insanity (Chagigah), and menstrual vesting (B12).
- (B16) MESHEKH CHOKHMAH: THE MISSING ומת — every damage clause in
  the block writes "and it dies" EXCEPT the pit: ונפל שמה שור או
  חמור jumps straight to והמת יהיה לו with no ומת before it. So there
  EXISTS a pit liable for damages without death — and WHICH pit (nine
  handbreadths vs ten) was "handed over to the sages" (נמסר לחכמים,
  קים להו לרבנן): the oral nine/ten datum pinned to a missing word.
  "This is the depth of the peshat our rabbis revealed."
- (B16) TWO EVIDENTIARY REGIMES IN TWO WORDS (Meshekh Chokhmah):
  ox-kills-MAN says והועד בבעליו — capital-style process (no
  circumstantial inference, testimony in the owner's presence, since
  the ox's death is hekesh'd to the owner's); ox-kills-OX says only
  או נודע — money-style process: R. Acha's camel-among-camels
  inference (אומדנא) and absent-party testimony suffice. And the
  missing איש in 21:28: an OWNERLESS or hekdesh ox is also stoned, so
  ובעל השור נקי must address whoever ACQUIRES it after the goring —
  proving the benefit-prohibition binds everyone, not just the owner.
- (B16) MIZRACHI: שור ולא אדם IS DECREE, NOT WISDOM — the common
  rationale ("a man has sense, let him look where he walks") fails:
  children who lack sense are excluded from pit-liability too (BK,
  mishnah), and so is a blind man, and a man falling at NIGHT —
  so the exclusion is גזרת הכתוב. Beside it, Rav Giddel's fiction-
  pair: the public-domain pit and chametz after midday — "two things
  NOT in a man's possession that Scripture made his" for liability.
- (B16) SECOND BAR-NAFLI WITNESS (Megalleh Amukkot, Sukkot homily):
  quotes ונפל שמה שור — דא משיח בן יוסף ("an ox fell there — this is
  Messiah son of Joseph") beside Sanhedrin 96b-97a's "when will BAR
  NAFLI come?"; gematria ב"ר נפל"י = בן ישי; David's falling sukkah
  propped daily by prayer; the four species = Abraham, Esau's
  defeat, Jacob, and the Josephide Messiah as willow-of-the-brook.
- (B16) TARGUM CRITICISM INSIDE THE SCAN (Netinah LaGer on Onkelos):
  the reading או לבר ישראל יגח תורא — the word ישראל is a STUDENT'S
  CORRUPTION (the Mekhilta derives minors-as-adults, not Israelites);
  יושת rendered ישוון, PLURAL = the court sets the cofer; and וגם את
  המת יחצון rendered ואף ית דמי מיתא ("the VALUE of the dead") = the
  tam pays only from its body, encoded in the Aramaic's added word.
- (B16) OR HACHAIM OPENS WITH TRANSFERRED INTENT: את איש (21:28) —
  aimed at Shimon, killed Reuven: liable (Sanhedrin 79a); but an ox
  that aimed at a BEAST and killed a man is exempt — the particle את
  carries the intent-transfer boundary into the goring law.
- (B15) THE TWO MESSIAHS FALL INTO OUR PIT (Abarbanel, Mashmia
  Yeshuah, quoting Zohar Vayechi): Messiah son of David = the DONKEY
  ("poor and riding a donkey," Zech 9:9), Messiah son of Joseph = the
  OX ("his firstborn ox," Deut 33:17) — and ונפל שמה שור או חמור
  ("an ox or a donkey falls in," 21:33) IS their fall into the great
  abyss; the Messiah is named BAR NAFLI ("son of the fallen") from
  it; והמת יהיה לו ("the dead one shall be his") = Messiah b. Joseph
  destined to be killed; and בעל הבור ישלם — the Pit's OWNER repays.
  The block's pit-clause runs the whole two-Messiah eschatology.
- (B15) MALBIM RESCUES RAMBAM'S "WONDROUS" RULING FROM THE DOUBLED
  ולא ישמרנו (21:29 + 21:36): owners stay liable even when their
  guard guarded PERFECTLY (Laws of Property Damage 4:4 — attacked by
  Raavad and Tur, half-retracted to the sages of Lunel, deleted by
  Bach and Sma) — Malbim: מקורו טהור מן המכילתא, "its source is pure,
  from the Mekhilta": the second ולא ישמרנו בעליו pins liability on
  the OWNER in person, whoever else was guarding.
- (B15) THE COFER IS A SACRIFICE, NOT A DEBT (Ramban via Malbim, 21:30):
  because the ransom is a KAPPARAH "like the offerings," the court
  does not compel it — whether they may even take a pledge is left
  unresolved (BK 40a; Rambam rules they distrain). Atonement money
  sits outside ordinary debt procedure.
- (B15) THREE PHILOLOGISTS, ONE VERB: Malbim joins Mecklenburg (B13)
  and Netziv (B14) — כרה = to BEGIN digging, חפר = to finish (proof
  Gen 26: Isaac's servants ויכרו a well, and only at אשר חפרו do they
  report "we found water") — so כי יכרה איש בר (defective) IS the
  nine-handbreadth pit and digger-after-digger is peshat. Independent
  19th-century convergence on the oral law's reading.
- (B15) MALBIM'S ETHOLOGY OF THE EQUAL OXEN (21:35): the verse
  pictures two 200-zuz oxen because that is how oxen FIGHT — "the
  gorer is usually not weaker than the gored, for it defeats it by
  strength, and the weak one hides itself from the strong"; the
  paradigm case is drawn from animal behavior, and his מחצית-vs-חצי
  semantics (like Mecklenburg's, independently) lets וחצו cover the
  unequal cases.
- (B15) THE WIFE TAKES THE THIRTY (Malbim, 21:32): for a melog-slave
  (the wife's property, the husband's usufruct) gored to death, the
  30 shekels go TO HER — לאדוניו ("to his master") means the BODY's
  owner, and ownership-of-body beats ownership-of-fruits (BK 89b-90,
  R. Yehudah). Marital property law hanging on one possessive noun.
- (B14) NETZIV: THE UNGUARDED MU'AD-OWNER IS A MURDERER-IN-FORMATION
  — וגם בעליו יומת ("and its owner too shall die," 21:29) kept ALIVE
  as peshat: who won't guard a known killer shows bloodshed is light
  to him, and he will END by killing a man himself — Heaven will
  steer it to his hand (והאלהים אנה לידו) and he dies by avenger or
  court. And the cofer needs TESHUVAH on top of the money: ונתן פדיון
  נפשו = "he must also accept repentance, fasting, charity."
- (B14) THE SLAVE'S 30 = A SHEPHERD'S WAGE (Hadar Zekenim): the gored
  slave is the herd's SHEPHERD, and a shepherd's hire is thirty —
  from Zechariah 11:12 וישקלו את שכרי שלשים כסף ("they weighed out my
  wage: thirty silver"). The tariff and Zechariah's thirty joined
  from inside the tradition.
- (B14) MALBIM'S GRAMMAR RESCUE of לא יֵאָכֵל את בשרו (21:28): an
  accusative marker את after a PASSIVE verb is ungrammatical — "the
  grammarians were forced"; only the sages' reading parses: את = WITH
  (the hide, הנאת עורו). The oral law as the verse's syntax repairman.
- (B14) NETZIV'S THIRD ECONOMICS OF THE SLAVE TARIFF (the superfluous
  השור, 21:32): slaves live and work WITH the oxen daily — an ox that
  gores even a familiar slave is proven hyper-vicious, and the owner
  who neither guarded nor slaughtered it is FINED the flat 30 (vs the
  Chizkuni/Bekhor Shor frequency arguments, B10-11). And his peshat of
  בן/בת (21:31): CHILDREN TEASE mad oxen — contributory negligence
  weighed (BK 24b) and overridden: כמשפט הזה יעשה לו anyway.
- (B14) NO FORCED PARTNERSHIP WITH YOUR DAMAGER (Netziv, 21:35): the
  verse orders the live ox SOLD and the money split — rather than
  co-ownership — because "he never agreed to be his partner and it is
  not pleasant for him to be partnered with him"; the sale protects
  the victim from co-holding property with the man whose ox killed his.
- (B14) THE TARIFF IN MEDIEVAL EUROPEAN COIN (Hadar Zekenim + Levush):
  the shekel priced in esterlins and Cologne half-ounces; Rabbeinu
  Tam's gold-weighs-double theory, proven from Araunah's floor (50
  GOLD shekels = 600 silver, harmonizing Samuel vs Chronicles) and
  firstborn-redemption change-making (Bekhorot). 13th-century currency
  metrology riding the 30-shekel clause.
- (B13) MECKLENBURG READS THE MISHNAH OUT OF ONE WORD: וגם את המת
  יחצון (21:35) — וגם here is not "and also" but a NOUN meaning LOSS
  (root גמם "to lop/cut," his German gloss: Verlust), citing Hebrew
  enantiosemy (אור = both light and dark); so the verse SAYS "the
  LOSS on the dead one they shall halve" = פחת שפחתה מיתה מחצין בחי
  verbatim. And וחצו = UNEQUAL division (ויחץ את הילדים — Jacob split
  the children unequally) while יחצון's extra ן = exact halves. The
  half-damage law exits the verse philologically, no forcing.
- (B13) THE GRA'S PIT-SPELLING PUNCHLINE (via HaKtav VeHaKabbalah):
  יפתח…בור spelled FULL = a pit of full 10 handbreadths; יכרה…בר
  spelled DEFECTIVE = the 9-handbreadth pit; and בעל הבור written
  FULL again = מי שהוא ממלא משלם — "HE WHO FILLS [the last handbreadth]
  PAYS." Digger-after-digger liability read from plene/defective
  alone; Mecklenburg adds: כרה is shallower than חפר, so the verb
  itself signals the not-yet-finished pit.
- (B13) THE SUFFIX-THEORY OF GUARDING (ולא ישמרנו, 21:36): a BOUND
  suffix (ישמרנו) means the GENERAL guarding all oxen get (reduced),
  a free pronoun (ישמור אותו) would mean this ox's SPECIAL guarding —
  so the MU'AD escapes with reduced guarding (R. Yehudah, BK 45b)
  because קלא אית ליה, "it has a voice": the notorious ox is avoided,
  so the TAM needs stricter guarding than the mu'ad. Same grammar
  runs Rambam's oath law (אוכלנה = the whole loaf, אוכל אותה = an
  olive's worth) and Gen 1:27 בראם (Adam-and-Eve bound as one).
- (B13) MONETARY TALION FROM WORD ORDER (Lev 24:19, via the block):
  כן יעשה לו with the verb BEFORE the pronoun = compensation flows TO
  the victim; כן לו יעשה would have meant bodily retaliation; תחת =
  "replacement-of" (Yerushalmi: תשלומי תברא); and the verse says
  עשיה not ישלם because nezek alone is NOT full payment — four more
  damage-heads remain. Proof-text: כמשפט הזה יעשה לו (21:31), where
  עשיה is undeniably payment.
- (B13) OWNERSHIP MUST PERSIST GORING→JUDGMENT: the superfluous
  בעליו (21:36) teaches an ox made OWNERLESS after goring is exempt
  from damages (Rosh) though still stoned for killing; R. Akiva
  Eiger's paradox — the capital verdict itself makes it ownerless,
  so how re-judge for money? — answered from the Shitah Mekubetzet:
  collect from the condemned ox's own PLOW-EARNINGS while it waits.
- (B13) THE GOADED OX GOES FREE (Gur Aryeh, Vayikra crossref): כי
  יגח = "when it gores OF ITS OWN ACCORD" — an ox others goaded into
  goring (מגיחין אותו) is not stoned (BK 39a); and the un-stoned
  killer is still barred from the ALTAR (like the beast of bestiality
  on owner's-word: no capital process, but Heaven won't take it).
- (B12) CHATAM SOFER'S MU'AD-VESET ISOMORPHISM: the ox that gores on
  the 15th of one month, 16th of the next, 17th of the third (BK 37b)
  IS the Rav-vs-Shmuel template for a woman's LEAPING MENSTRUAL CYCLE
  (Niddah 64a) — does a skipping pattern vest in 3 events or 4? His
  resolution: the systems point OPPOSITE ways because the presumptions
  differ — oxen sit in a presumption of being guarded (חזקת שימור),
  women in a presumption of seeing. Third system-isomorphism for the
  mu'ad template after plague (Taanit, B10) and insanity (Chagigah).
- (B12) THE DREAM-AND-PRAYER LAYER (Berakhot): one who sees an ox IN
  A DREAM must rise and say "his firstborn ox, majesty is his" (Deut
  33:17) BEFORE the verse כי יגח שור ("when an ox gores") reaches him
  (56b) — the tariff verse as an omen to be outrun; and one who sees
  REAL oxen while praying INTERRUPTS: keep 50 cubits from a tam, a
  full eyeshot (מלא עיניו) from a mu'ad (33a) — the tam/mu'ad line
  drawn in liturgy. Chida adds R. Elazar of Worms (ms.): dreaming a
  goring ox = "sons who gore in halakhah."
- (B12) EM LAMIKRA CITES DRACO OF ATHENS (in French, via Eusebius):
  Draco's law BANISHED BEYOND THE BORDER inanimate objects that fell
  and killed a man — set against סקול יסקל השור ("the ox shall surely
  be stoned"): 19th-century comparative jurisprudence inside the
  inversion; the killer-object as defendant is a shared ancient form.
- (B12) TWO MORE RATIONALES FOR THE FLAT 30: Bartenura — 30 is the
  MIDDLE Arakhin valuation (ערך הממוצע), the statutory average man;
  MAHARAL (Derekh Chayyim 2:9) — 30 is the ADULT-WOMAN's Arakhin
  value and the slave shares the woman's matter-leaning station ("a
  people like the donkey"); and GUR ARYEH adds: the 30 can EXCEED the
  slave's worth because it is KAPPARAH, not damage-pricing — "for
  killing a soul one pays more than the damage." Da'at Zekenim's
  gematria seal: Canaan's curse עבד עבדים יהיה לאחיו — יהיה = 30.
- (B12) BERESHIT RABBATI NAMES THE BROTHERS FROM THE TAM-SPLIT: בעלי
  חצים ("masters of arrows/halves," Gen 49:23) = they DIVIDED Joseph's
  20 silver among themselves, read from וחצו את כספו ("they shall
  halve its money," 21:35) — the ox-vs-ox split clause supplies the
  Joseph-sale its accounting term.
- (B12) GUR ARYEH'S SUBSTANCE-AND-ACCIDENT את: the particle את in
  את בשרו ("its flesh," 21:28) includes whatever ATTACHES to the
  substance (the hide) — because "no substance is without accidents"
  (עצם/מקרה, Aristotelian scaffolding for the ribbui); same machine
  that makes "et the Lord your God" include Torah scholars — the
  verse Shimon HaAmsoni retreated on (B5) now given its metaphysics.
- (B11) THE GRA: "THE HALAKHAH UPROOTS THE VERSE" (הלכה עוקרת את
  המקרא) on יומת ("he shall be put to death", 21:29) — his radical
  formula for oral-law override (death-by-heaven displaces the written

## What the Grok note measures vs what our test measures

Two different axes on the same material — that is the whole value:

- **The Grok note's axis (CORE / EXTENSION / DISPUTE / NEW_MODULE):**
  does the oral layer's *output* stay inside the verse-named answer
  alphabet? Its finding on 48 rows of Exodus 21: NEW_MODULE zero —
  the Mishnah never mints a new kind of verdict.
- **Our axis (K/I/M/C/D/G/O/R/E):** where does the law's *content*
  come from — the kernel's ink, an analogy, a case table, a decree,
  an override?

Each lens hides what the other catches. Their scheme scored
eye-for-eye-equals-money as EXTENSION (money is in the chapter's
alphabet) — hiding the override mechanism our O tier flags. Our scheme
classifies provenance but never asked whether the output *vocabulary*
is closed — their central invariant. Both are now run.

## Test A — the output-alphabet test (coded: output_alphabet.py, MILL)

The block's verse-named alphabet has eleven letters (stone, no-benefit,
clear, owner-death, ransom, thirty, split, full-pay, carcass,
restitution, tariff). All 35 witnessed claims categorized: 23 VERDICT
rows (face the alphabet), 2 STATUS gates, 6 PROCEDURE rows, 4
no-output rows.

**Result: NEW KINDS MINTED — ZERO.** The Grok note's invariant holds
on our independently derived material. The live machine's outputs were
harvested in the same run — status, stoning, ransom, the thirty, the
benefit ban, the tam's clearance — every one maps to an alphabet
letter.

**Three strains, all inside the alphabet but stretching it — and they
sit exactly on the tiers our scheme built for them:**

1. **L29-04 (tier O, the override):** the owner-death letter kept, the
   executor reassigned — the court's death becomes Heaven's. Same
   letter, different jurisdiction.
2. **L35-02 (tier D, dispute-parameter):** the half-damage letter's own
   *type* is what the teachers dispute — fine or compensation — with
   real downstream behavior (admission exempts from fines).
3. **L35-03 (tier C, case-table):** the split remedy re-typed as
   partnership in the ox — the mechanism deepened, the letter kept.

**Sharpened joint finding:** the oral expansion preserves the verse's
output alphabet AND the places where the alphabet audibly creaks are
precisely the override and dispute tiers. The two schemes corroborate
each other from opposite directions.

## Test C — the gap roster (their 12-paragraph ox exam × our machine)

What the block-3 machine can already run: the vesting battery (2:4),
context-limited forewarning (4:2), the Temple/gentile asymmetries
(4:3), person-victim liability with ransom and the thirty (4:5), the
intent gate (4:6), ownerless-ox continuity (4:7), the benefit ban at
sentencing (4:8), graded guarding (4:9), and the half/full payment
split (1:4, via the ox-vs-ox ops).

**The backlog — cases the machine cannot yet face:**

1. **The collection queue (First Gate 4:1):** one ordinary ox gores
   several before judgment — who collects first, and the last victim's
   priority. Needs a multi-victim ledger on the ox's body value.
2. **The stadium ox (4:4):** a trained/forced goring is not stoned —
   needs a coercion input on the stoning verdict.
3. **The guardian procedure (4:4):** court-appointed guardians for the
   orphan's ox — ownership exists in our census; the procedure does not.
4. **THE DOUBT LAYER (5:1) — the headline gap:** gored cow, dead calf,
   cause unknown. Verdict under uncertainty: our machine has no burden
   of proof, no doubt-resolution, no verdict-with-uncertainty output.
   And the earlier finding that the tradition keys doubt policy to
   provenance tier (strict for Torah-tier, lenient for rabbinic-tier)
   says this is not an edge case — it is a missing subsystem, and
   First Gate 5:1 is its entry test.

Adopted from the note for the case-file design (no code yet): the
hook-not-fence discovery rule (find where the gemara cites the verse;
the Mishnah atop that page is the exam; the pericope is one program),
and the caution that oral texts carry no biblical cantillation — the
Step-6 tree machinery cannot parse a Mishnah paragraph; the case-file
side needs its own conventions.

## The combined one-breath finding

Two independent classification schemes, built blind of each other on
the same chapter, agree at row grain wherever they overlap; the Grok
note's closed-alphabet invariant survives our 35 claims (zero new
kinds, three strains exactly on our O and D tiers); each lens catches
what the other hides (their alphabet-closure, our override/dispute
mechanisms); and the join produced a concrete backlog — the collection
queue, the stadium input, the guardian procedure, and above all the
doubt layer, which is the first missing *subsystem* the case files
will demand.

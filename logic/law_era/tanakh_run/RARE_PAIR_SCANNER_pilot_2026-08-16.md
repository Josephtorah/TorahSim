# THE RARE-PAIR SCANNER — first run
2026-08-16 · the exclusive-join method (the class that found mot tamut,
"dying you shall die," and the two deep sleeps) industrialized: every
unpointed phrase occurring EXACTLY TWICE in the whole Tanakh, in two
different books, mined mechanically. Tool:
logic/law_era/scratch_mirror/rare_pair_scan.py · full result files in
the session scratchpad (rare_pairs_3.txt, rare_pairs_4.txt).

## The counts

- 3-word phrases: 224,428 distinct; 3,235 exclusive cross-book pairs
  (a further 3,597 fell inside synoptic parallel blocks, auto-detected
  and set aside: Kings/Chronicles, Psalm 18 / 2 Samuel 22, and kin).
- 4-word phrases: 220,058 distinct; 1,233 exclusive cross-book pairs
  (3,021 more in parallel blocks).

Every pair is a candidate deliberate channel between two books: a
phrase that exists at exactly two addresses in all of Scripture.
Ranking is by combined rarity of the component words; the list below
is the hand-curated first fruits of the top of the 4-gram table.

## First fruits

1. THE TWO SIXTY-CUBIT SPECIFICATIONS. Daniel 3:1 and Ezra 6:3 share,
   exclusively, the Aramaic dimensions formula אמין שתין פתיה אמין
   ("cubits sixty, its breadth cubits..."), twice over (two
   overlapping phrases at the top of the table). The only two
   buildings in Scripture specified in this formula: Nebuchadnezzar's
   golden image (height sixty cubits), and the Temple as decreed in
   CYRUS'S FILED MEMORANDUM (height sixty cubits). The counterfeit
   colossus and the authorized house, written in the same
   administrative Aramaic, joined by a channel that exists nowhere
   else. The scanner's first screen handed the archive-report a
   companion it did not know it had.
2. DANIEL'S PRAYER QUOTING THE COVENANT. Daniel 9:4 and Deuteronomy
   7:9 exclusively share שמר הברית והחסד לאהביו ("keeping the covenant
   and the mercy for them that love Him"). The report on the
   Daniel-Jeremiah chain argued that Daniel executes the covenant's
   own protocol; the scanner now shows his prayer's opening formula is
   an exclusive verbatim join to the covenant text itself. The claim
   acquired a mechanical receipt.
3. HANNAH'S SONG AND THE PSALTER. 1 Samuel 2:8 and Psalm 113:7
   exclusively share מעפר דל מאשפת ירים ("He raises the poor from the
   dust, the needy from the ash heap"), again twice over with the
   overlapping phrase. A prayer in the histories and a psalm in the
   liturgy, joined word for word and nowhere else.
4. THE TWO DECALOGUES' MERCY CLAUSE. Exodus 20:6 and Deuteronomy 5:10
   share their form of the thousands-mercy clause exclusively: the two
   givings of the Ten Words joined by their own wording, the pair the
   method predicts and finds.
5. THE COVENANT BOUNDARY QUOTED. Genesis 15:18 and Deuteronomy 1:7
   exclusively share הנהר הגדל נהר פרת ("the great river, the river
   Euphrates") as the land's far border: the covenant grant and the
   marching order, the deed's terms read back at possession time.
6. THE SIGN THAT BECAME THE PROMISE. 2 Kings 19:29 and Isaiah 65:21
   exclusively share ונטעו כרמים ואכלו פרים ("and they shall plant
   vineyards and eat their fruit"): Hezekiah's recovery sign reused,
   once and only once more, inside the new-creation promise.
7. THE SPOIL FORMULA. Ezekiel 38:12 and Isaiah 10:6 exclusively share
   לשלל שלל ולבז בז ("to take spoil and to seize prey"): the
   commission of Assyria and the ambition of Gog, the same
   plunder-license wording at the two ends of the prophetic corpus.
8. GENEALOGICAL JOINS. Several top pairs are the Chronicler reading
   the Torah's registries verbatim (1 Chronicles 1:38 with Genesis
   36:20; 1 Chronicles 5:3 with Exodus 6:14; 1 Chronicles 5:27 with
   Genesis 46:11): the registry copied by exact key, which is what a
   registry is for.
9. THE ALLOTMENT CROSS-REFERENCES. Joshua's land grants join Numbers'
   boundary commands exclusively (Josh 15:3 with Num 34:4; Josh 14:2
   with Num 34:13; Josh 18:7 with Num 34:15): the execution reading
   the specification, clause by clause.

## Caveats from the first run

The parallel-block filter (three or more shared exclusive phrases
between one verse-pair) catches the great synoptic duplicates but not
every short overlap: the Zedekiah verses (2 Kings 25:7 with Jeremiah
52:11) slipped through as gems though Jeremiah 52 is a parallel
appendix. Curation therefore remains a human step, as it should. And
rarity-ranking favors name-lists; the middle of the table, not only
the top, will hold theological gems (the known two-sleeps and
dying-you-shall-die pairs are 1-grams and 2-grams, below this run's
n; a 2-gram run with a stronger commonness filter is the natural next
sweep).

## What the pilot establishes

The exclusive-join channel is real, abundant, and minable: over four
thousand cross-book pairs await curation, and the first screen of the
first run already delivered one find of the first rank (the two
sixty-cubit specifications) and one mechanical confirmation of an
existing claim (the prayer's covenant join). The method that the
sages named gezerah shavah ("equal decree") and that this project has
used by hand now has an industrial form.

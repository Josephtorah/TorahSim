## A. Pre-Sinai cases (the mode-flag block) — 4
1. Gen 7:2, 8 — Noah takes CLEAN beasts by sevens: the clean/unclean
   partition operating before Sinai. The era-table question in one verse.
2. Gen 8:20 — Noah sacrifices of every CLEAN beast and fowl.
3. Gen 34:5, 13, 27 — Dinah "defiled" (טמא): the moral-defilement register
   of the same root, pre-law.
4. Gen 35:2 — Jacob's household order: "purify yourselves (הטהרו) and
   change your garments" before Bethel. A purification protocol with no
   statute yet in existence.

## B. The law executed inside the Torah's own narrative — 7

8. Num 9:6-13 — THE second-Passover men: "we are unclean by the dead body

   the Disclosure docs; it is a PURITY case at its root.)
9. Num 12:10-15 — Miriam: the disease, the diagnosis glance ("behold, she
   was leprous"), the seven days outside the camp, the nation waiting.
10. Num 31:19-24 — the war purification: seven days outside the camp,
    third-day and seventh-day sprinkling, spoils through fire or water.
    Numbers 19 executed at army scale.
11. Num 35:34 — "you shall not defile the land... for blood defiles the
    land": the bridge verse where the HOMICIDE machine and the purity
    machine share state (land-defilement as a ledger both laws write).

## C. Monarchy-era cases (the histories as event stream) — 17
12. 1 Sam 20:26 — Saul's silent inference on David's empty seat: "something
    has befallen him, he is NOT CLEAN" (the Deut 23 camp-purity class
    applied as everyday probability). A layman running the law in his head.
13. 1 Sam 21:5-7 — David and the showbread: "the young men have kept
    themselves from women... the vessels are holy." Purity status traded
    against hunger; the case Ahimelech actually adjudicates.
14. 2 Sam 3:29 — David's curse on Joab's house: "let there never fail one
    with a DISCHARGE (זב) or the SKIN DISEASE (מצרע, "one afflicted with it")..." — purity statuses
    invoked as hereditary judgment.
15. 2 Sam 11:4 — Bathsheba "purifying herself from her uncleanness": the
    monthly-cycle law (Lev 15) as narrative timestamp, load-bearing for
    the paternity plot.
16. 2 Sam 11:11 — Uriah's refusal: "the ark and Israel abide in booths...
    shall I go in to my wife?" War-camp discipline observed by the man
    David is trying to compromise.
17. 1 Kgs 13:22-30 — the man of God's CARCASS (נבלה used of a human
    corpse, six hits): thrown on the road, lion standing guard, buried by
    the old prophet. Corpse-handling scene end to end.
18. 1 Kgs 17:21 — Elijah stretches himself on the dead child. Deliberate
    corpse contact by a prophet, to save life.
19. 2 Kgs 4:34-35 — Elisha lies mouth to mouth on the corpse. Same class.
20. 2 Kgs 13:21 — the dead man revives on touching Elisha's BONES: a
    corpse-contact scene inverted.
21. 2 Kgs 5:1-14 — NAAMAN: the foreign general with the disease; "wash
    seven times"; flesh restored. The diagnosis-and-cleansing frame run on
    a foreigner (jurisdiction question built in: the king of Israel: "am I
    God... that this man sends to me a man to cure him of his disease?").
22. 2 Kgs 5:27 — Gehazi: the disease TRANSFERRED as judgment, "to your
    seed forever." The Joab-curse class, executed on the page.
23. 2 Kgs 7:3-10 — the four men with the disease at the gate: quarantined
    OUTSIDE the besieged city (the Lev 13 exclusion holding even in
    famine), and therefore the first to find the deliverance.
24. 2 Kgs 15:5 + 2 Chr 26:16-23 — UZZIAH: the king struck with the
    disease at the altar, "and they thrust him out quickly"; he dwells in
    the separate house "unto the day of his death"; his son judges the
    people; buried in the FIELD of the kings' burial, "for they said: he
    is a metzora ('one afflicted')." The purity law running on a KING:
    lifetime quarantine, forced co-regency, even the grave relocated. The
    single richest institutional case in the corpus.
25. 2 Kgs 9:37 — Jezebel's carcass "as dung upon the field": corpse-status
    as prophecy fulfilled.
26. 2 Kgs 23:8-16 — JOSIAH WEAPONIZES DEFILEMENT: he "defiled" (טמא) the
    high places, Topheth, the altars, and burned dead men's BONES on them
    to unfit them forever. The purity mechanics used as a demolition tool:
    the king writes tumah INTO objects on purpose.
27. 2 Chr 23:19 — Jehoiada's gatekeepers "that none UNCLEAN in anything
    should enter": access control at the Temple gates as standing policy.
28. 2 Chr 29:4-19 — Hezekiah's cleansing: the priests carry the tumah OUT
    of the sanctuary to the brook Kidron, eight days of purification,
    inventory reported. A decontamination operation with a log.
29. 2 Chr 30:17-20 — HEZEKIAH'S PASSOVER: "a multitude had NOT purified
    themselves, yet they ate the Passover OTHERWISE THAN IT IS WRITTEN
    (בלא ככתוב, "not as it is written"). For Hezekiah prayed: may the good LORD pardon every one
    who sets his heart to seek God, though not according to the purity of
    the sanctuary. And the LORD hearkened, and HEALED the people." Mass
    violation + royal intercession + divine override, all on the record,
    with the narrator citing the written law he knows is being broken.
    The jewel of the harvest for the disputes-as-data method.
30. 2 Chr 36:14 — the closing indictment: priests and people "polluted
    (טמא) the house of the LORD": the tumah ledger as the stated cause of
    the exile itself.

## D. The prophets as console (indictments that read the purity ledger) — 12
31. Isa 6:5-7 — "a man of UNCLEAN lips": the coal ritual as purification;
    the prophet must be cleansed before transmitting.
32. Amos 7:17 — "you shall die in an UNCLEAN land": exile framed as a
    purity status of geography.
33. Hos 9:3-4 — "they shall eat unclean things in Assyria... their bread

34. Ezek 4:13-14 — Ezekiel's protest: "my soul has NOT been polluted; from
    my youth I have not eaten what died of itself (נבלה)": a priest citing
    his own compliance record against a divine command.
35. Ezek 22:10, 26 — the indictment: "in you they humbled her that was
    unclean in her impurity (נדה)... her priests have not distinguished
    between the unclean and the clean": the priestly job description (Lev
    10:10) cited as the count of the charge.
36. Ezek 24:11-13 — Jerusalem the pot whose FILTH (טמאה, "impurity") will not leave
    except by fire: the purity vocabulary as the siege verdict.
37. Ezek 36:17, 25 — "their way was before Me as the uncleanness of the
    NIDDAH... I will sprinkle CLEAN WATER upon you and you shall be
    clean": the Numbers 19 sprinkling promised at national scale.
38. Ezek 43:7-9 — the carcasses of kings defiling the Temple precinct:
    royal burial practice as standing contamination.
39. Ezek 44:23-31 — the priests' operating manual restated: teach the
    difference between unclean and clean; the corpse-mourning exception
    (only father, mother, son, daughter, brother, unmarried sister) =
    Leviticus 21 running in the restoration blueprint; no carcass-meat.
40. Hag 2:11-14 — THE RECORDED CONSULTATION: "Ask now the priests
    torah ('a ruling'): if one carries holy flesh in his garment-fold and
    it touches bread... does it become holy? The priests answered: No. If
    one UNCLEAN BY A CORPSE touches any of these, is it unclean? The
    priests answered: It is unclean." A live oral-law examination on
    transmission mechanics, question and answer preserved verbatim, and
    the priests' answers match the later codified law: holiness does not
    transmit at second remove; corpse-impurity does. A gradeable exchange
    the future purity machine can CONFIRM.
41. Zech 3:3-5 — Joshua the high priest in FILTHY garments, re-clothed:
    the investiture purity of the priesthood as vision.
42. Lam 4:14-15 — the exiles in the streets: "Depart! UNCLEAN! they cried
    at them; depart, depart, touch not!" — the metzora's own outcry (Lev
    13:45) turned on the whole nation.

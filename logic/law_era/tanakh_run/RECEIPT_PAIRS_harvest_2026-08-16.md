# THE RECEIPT-PAIRS HARVEST — the corpus's own fulfillment table
2026-08-16 · owner ask: generalize the Daniel-Jeremiah chain by resolving
every "according to the word of the LORD" receipt to its source oracle.
Census verified against tanakh.sqlite.

METHOD. The receipt formula כדבר יהוה ("according to the word of the
LORD") occurs at exactly 26 sites. Beside it stands the fulfill-family
(למלאות דבר יהוה, "to fulfill the word of the LORD") and the
establish-family (הקים את דברו, "He established His word"). Each receipt
below is paired with its emission. Where the pairing is short-range
(command and execution in one scene) it is listed summarily; the
long-range pairs are the harvest's substance.

## B. The special classes

1. RECEIPT WITHOUT RECORDED EMISSION. 2 Kgs 14:25: Jeroboam II restores
   the border "according to the word of the LORD... which He spoke by
   the hand of His servant JONAH son of Amittai." The oracle itself
   appears nowhere in the corpus. The log cites a message that was
   never logged: proof that the receipt system indexes more than the
   canon preserves.
2. THE RECEIPT INSIDE THE READ. Daniel 9:12, inside the prayer: "He has
   ESTABLISHED His words which He spoke against us... by bringing upon
   us a great evil." Daniel, mid-confession, issues a receipt for the
   covenant CURSE as faithfully executed. The reader of one message
   receipts another while reading.
3. OBEDIENCE RECEIPTS. Jonah 3:3 ("Jonah arose and went to Nineveh
   according to the word of the LORD"); Jer 13:2 (the waistband);
   1 Kgs 17:5 (Elijah to Cherith); Josh 8:8, 27 (the Ai stratagem and
   spoil). The formula also stamps compliance, not only fulfillment.
4. THE ESCROW JOIN. Jer 32:8: "Hanamel came... according to the word
   of the LORD" — the receipt that opens the sealed-deed scene,
   joining this system to the seal subsystem (see the companion
   report).
5. LAW RECEIPTS AT FESTIVALS. 2 Chr 35:6: Josiah's Passover "according
   to the word of the LORD by the hand of Moses": the statute
   receipted at national scale.

## C. The exception handler, and the one strained receipt

The corpus states its own override rule: "At one instant I may speak
concerning a nation... to pluck up and to break down... but if that
nation TURN from their evil, I will REPENT of the evil that I thought
to do" (Jeremiah 18:7-10, with the mirror clause for promised good).
The rule is exercised on the record: Nineveh (Jonah 3:10, "God
repented of the evil"), Hezekiah's fifteen added years (Isaiah
38:1-5, a delivered death sentence revised upon prayer, with the
revision itself receipted), and Ahab's deferral (1 Kgs 21:29, whose
receipt above lands on the amended terms). Oracles here are standing
orders subject to a published amendment clause, and the receipts
track the amendments.

And one receipt is strained, and is printed at full size as this
project's method requires: Huldah promises Josiah "you shall be
gathered to your grave IN PEACE" (2 Kgs 22:20), and Josiah dies of
battle wounds at Megiddo (23:29-30). The traditions reconcile it (he
died before the catastrophe he was spared from seeing; he was
gathered to his fathers in peace though he died by the sword); the
tension stands on the page either way. One strained receipt among
dozens clean is the texture of a real record.

## The system reading

The Deuteronomy 18:21-22 validation rule (the word must come to pass)
is not left as doctrine; the historian operationalizes it, stamping
fulfillments with a standard formula that names the channel ("by the
hand of X"). The corpus therefore contains its own regression log:
twenty-six formula receipts plus the fulfill and establish families,
spanning single scenes to five books, covering commands, curses,
forecasts, laws, and amended sentences, with an exception handler
published and exercised, one aggregate receipt, one receipt for an
unlogged message, and one strained case. The Daniel-Jeremiah chain is
not an isolated wonder; it is the largest member of a documented
class.

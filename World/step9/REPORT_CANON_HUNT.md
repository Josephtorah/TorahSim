  enter on the Sabbath day") — an enforcement log of the compiled

## THE FINDING

Five functions hunted, five found, every link recorded by the
tradition, one rediscovered by machine before consulting the record.
The owner's hypothesis is CONFIRMED and sharpened into a taxonomy:

**The Torah demonstrates by SPEC; the Prophets and Writings
demonstrate by RUN.** The rest of the 24 books is the functions'
RUNTIME LOG — holding at least seven distinct record types:
1. RE-STATEMENT (a prophet re-issuing the rule, citing its seat —
   Jeremiah 17).
2. ENFORCEMENT LOG (the rule run with police powers — Nehemiah 13).
3. REPEATED CALL (the same function invoked generations apart,
   near-verbatim — II Kings 12 and 22).
4. RUNTIME TRACE (the protocol executing step by step — Ezra 8).
5. PARAMETER DRIFT (the constant floating while the invariant
   holds — Nehemiah 10's third-shekel).
6. CORRUPT-RUN EDGE CASE (valid forms, false content — Naboth).
7. PRECEDENT CITATION and INSTRUMENT CHARTER (the court quoting a
   prior ruling — Jeremiah 26; the targum installed — Nehemiah 8).

Consequence for the build: when pass 1 reaches the Prophets and
Writings, it is not reading new LAW — it is reading the TEST LOG of
law already seated, which means those books join the machine mostly
as runtime evidence for functions the Torah pass already compiled:
exactly the cheap-to-derive, high-confirmation layer the two-pass
plan wants. And the piece-together method works TODAY, before any
derivation: the recorded links + the lemma scanner assemble a
function's full canon career in one sitting.

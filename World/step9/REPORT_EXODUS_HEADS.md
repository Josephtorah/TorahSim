## THE READING

**X1 THE MAIDSERVANT (21:7-11).** וְכִי יִמְכֹּר אִישׁ אֶת בִּתּוֹ לְאָמָה ("and when a man sells his daughter as a maidservant",
21:7): a MINOR (Mekhilta Nezikin 7 1 — the signs of puberty take her out of her father's hand, all the more before a
sale); the FATHER sells, the mother does not, the daughter is not sold for her theft nor pierced (7 2-4; Mishnah Sotah
3:8); she does not go out by the tooth and the eye "as the male slaves go out" but does go out by the years and the
jubilee (7 8, from Deuteronomy 15:12) and, beyond him, by the SIGNS (Mishnah Kiddushin 1:2; Kiddushin 18a:5 — and she
is redeemed against his will and not sold twice). 21:8 אִם רָעָה בְּעֵינֵי אֲדֹנֶיהָ אֲשֶׁר לֹא יְעָדָהּ וְהֶפְדָּהּ ("if she is evil in
the eyes of her master who did not designate her, he shall let her be redeemed"): designation before redemption (8 1,
citing Mishnah Bekhorot 1:6); לְעַם נָכְרִי לֹא יִמְשֹׁל לְמָכְרָהּ ("to a foreign people he has no power to sell her") a warning
to the court (8 3). 21:9 וְאִם לִבְנוֹ יִיעָדֶנָּה ("and if he designates her for his son"): the son, not the brother (9 1);
כְּמִשְׁפַּט הַבָּנוֹת ("according to the law of the daughters") — the clause "comes to teach and is taught": the three of
21:10 hold for every daughter of Israel (9 2, Rabbi Yoshiya); designation is BETROTHAL (Kiddushin 18b:3-4), the
first money given for it, and only with her knowledge (Kiddushin 19a:7 — Rabbi Yannai: no designation except of an
adult, because none except with consent). 21:10 שְׁאֵרָהּ כְּסוּתָהּ וְעֹנָתָהּ לֹא יִגְרָע ("her food, her clothing and her conjugal
right he shall not diminish"): the three named — food, clothing, conjugal right — with the recorded dispute on which
noun is which (Mekhilta 10 2: Rabbi Yoshiya — שְׁאֵר ("her flesh") is food and עוֹנָה ("her season") the conjugal right; Rabbi
the reverse; Ketubot 47b:10-11 the same two arms; Rabbi Yonatan and Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov read "clothing FITTING her state and her season", Ketubot
48a:1); the quantities are the answer sheet's data (Mishnah Ketubot 5:6 the conjugal schedule, 5:8-9 the food and the
clothing). 21:11 וְאִם שְׁלָשׁ אֵלֶּה לֹא יַעֲשֶׂה לָהּ וְיָצְאָה חִנָּם אֵין כָּסֶף ("and if these three he does not do for her, she
shall go out free, without money"): "these three" = designate her for himself, for his son, or redeem her (11 1); free of
the money, not free of the bill of divorce (11 2); THE TWO EXITS IN ONE CLAUSE — "free" the days of maturity, "without
money" the days of youth (11 3-4; Kiddushin 4a:3, 4a:11 — "let this come and teach that"), and "no money for THIS master
but money for another, the father" (Kiddushin 3b:5, 4a:4 — the father's right to her betrothal money).

**X2 THE CAPITAL CLAUSES (21:13-15, 21:17).** 21:13 וַאֲשֶׁר לֹא צָדָה ("and he who did not lie in wait"): the unintentional
killer FLEES — the Mishnah's descent rule (Makkot 2:1: rolling down, lowering, descending a ladder — exile; pulling up,
drawing up, ascending — no exile; Mekhilta Nezikin 13 1 the same rows); the place: the Levites' camps for the hour, the
Levites' cities for the generations (13 2); the father goes into exile for the son and the son for the father (Makkot
2:3), but not the father disciplining his son, the teacher, the court's agent (2:2 Abba Shaul). 21:14 וְכִי יָזִד אִישׁ עַל
רֵעֵהוּ לְהָרְגוֹ בְעָרְמָה ("and when a man acts presumptuously against his fellow to kill him with guile"): the deliberate
killer alone — "with guile" excludes the deaf-mute and the minor, the physician who killed, the court's agent, the
father and the teacher (14 1-3); Chizkiyah's school: "with guile — that they WARNED him and he is still deliberate"
(Sanhedrin 41a:2 — the forewarning read off the word); מֵעִם מִזְבְּחִי תִּקָּחֶנּוּ לָמוּת ("from My altar you shall take him to
die"): they void the service from his hand and he goes out to be killed (14 4); FROM BESIDE My altar, not from UPON it —
Rabbi Yochanan: that was said to put to death, but to save life even from upon it (Yoma 85a:15-85b:1; Sanhedrin
35b:5, 36a:4 the daily offering); "to die — not to be judged, lashed or exiled" (14 7); the Sanhedrin sits beside the
altar (14 8); the mode: the murderer among THE BEHEADED (Mishnah Sanhedrin 9:1) — the sword derived by a taught
verbal analogy from OUR 21:20, נָקֹם יִנָּקֵם with Leviticus 26:25 חֶרֶב נֹקֶמֶת נְקַם בְּרִית ("a sword avenging the covenant's
vengeance"), Sanhedrin 52b:13 = Mekhilta Nezikin 20 6 (Rabbi Natan). 21:15 וּמַכֵּה אָבִיו וְאִמּוֹ מוֹת יוּמָת ("and he
who strikes his father and his mother shall surely be put to death"): a blow with a WOUND (15 3; Mishnah Sanhedrin
11:1), either parent (15 2), the mode STRANGLING — the unspecified death (15 4; Mishnah Sanhedrin 11:1 lists him among
the strangled), the striker after death exempt where the curser is liable (11:1; Sanhedrin 85b:3). 21:17 וּמְקַלֵּל אָבִיו
וְאִמּוֹ מוֹת יוּמָת ("and he who curses his father and his mother shall surely be put to death"): THE SECOND SEAT of
Leviticus 20:9 — the woman, the tumtum and the androgynos included from this seat's "and he who curses" (17 1), either
parent, after death too, by the explicit Name (17 2; Mishnah Sanhedrin 7:8), the mode STONING by the blood-formula's
analogy דָּמָיו בּוֹ / דְּמֵיהֶם בָּם (17 3 — Leviticus 20:9 with 20:27), the warning built from the judge, the prince and
the deaf (17 3). The mode and the readings are already compiled at the Leviticus seat (cold_run_sanctions.py
`curser`); this seat CALLS them — a REFERENCE (the same clause at two seats; the shared content lemmas "curse",
"father", "mother") — and its own ink adds the inclusion of the woman.

**X3 THE SLAVE STRUCK (21:20-21).** וְכִי יַכֶּה אִישׁ אֶת עַבְדּוֹ אוֹ אֶת אֲמָתוֹ בַּשֵּׁבֶט וּמֵת תַּחַת יָדוֹ נָקֹם יִנָּקֵם ("and when a man
strikes his slave or his maidservant with the rod and he dies under his hand, he shall surely be avenged"): the
Canaanite slave (20 3 — Rabbi Eliezer; Rabbi Yishmael from "for he is his money": a permanent acquisition wholly in his
domain — the Hebrew slave, the jointly owned and the half-free EXCLUDED, they are under the general clause); "with the
rod" — a thing that can kill, at a place that can kill (20 4); "under his hand" — the blow and the death in his domain
(20 5: struck him, sold him, and he died — exempt); "avenged" — DEATH, by the sword (20 6, Rabbi Natan; Sanhedrin
52b:13). 21:21 אַךְ אִם יוֹם אוֹ יוֹמַיִם יַעֲמֹד לֹא יֻקַּם כִּי כַסְפּוֹ הוּא ("but if a day or two days he stands, he shall not
be avenged, for he is his money"): "a day that is like two days, two days that are like a day" — FROM TIME TO TIME,
twenty-four hours (21 1); "his money" — the recorded four-way dispute on the slave sold with thirty days' service
reserved (Bava Kamma 90a:5-8: Rabbi Meir the first master, Rabbi Yehuda the second, Rabbi Yose both — capital doubt
lenient, Rabbi Eliezer neither — "his money, wholly his", Rava at 90a:9).

**X4 THE BURGLAR (22:1-2).** אִם בַּמַּחְתֶּרֶת יִמָּצֵא הַגַּנָּב וְהֻכָּה וָמֵת אֵין לוֹ דָמִים ("if in the tunnel the thief is found and
is struck and dies, he has no blood"): the doubt — he came to steal or to kill — and the presumption that a man does
not stand by while his money is taken (Mekhilta continuation 1 1; Sanhedrin 72a:4 Rava: "the Torah said, if he comes
to kill you, rise early and kill him"); "and is struck" — by ANY person; "and dies" — by any death (72b:6-8); THE SUN A
FIGURE: "did the sun shine on him alone? — if it is clear to you as the sun that he is at peace with you, do not kill
him" (continuation 2 1, Rabbi Yishmael; the two baraitot at Sanhedrin 72a:15-16 resolved at the FATHER breaking into
the son's house — presumed merciful, 72b:1-2), day and night not divided; Mishnah Sanhedrin 8:6: הַבָּא בַּמַּחְתֶּרֶת נִדּוֹן
עַל שֵׁם סוֹפוֹ ("the one who comes by tunneling is judged by his end") — broke a barrel: with blood liable, without blood
exempt (the greater penalty absorbs — 72a:8-10); on the Sabbath the same, and the pile is cleared from him (72b:3-5).
22:2 שַׁלֵּם יְשַׁלֵּם אִם אֵין לוֹ וְנִמְכַּר בִּגְנֵבָתוֹ ("he shall surely pay; if he has nothing, he is sold for his theft"): sold
for SIX YEARS, from 21:2 (continuation 2 3 — a reference to the Hebrew slave's term); for the principal, never the
double (Kiddushin 18a:8); not for less than the theft's worth (2 4 — Rabbi Yehuda / Rabbi Eliezer on more); THE MAN IS
SOLD, THE WOMAN IS NOT — בִּגְנֵבָתוֹ "for HIS theft" and not for hers (Sotah 23b:12; Mishnah Sotah 3:8; Mekhilta 7 4).

**X5 THE FATHER'S REFUSAL (22:16).** אִם מָאֵן יְמָאֵן אָבִיהָ לְתִתָּהּ לוֹ כֶּסֶף יִשְׁקֹל כְּמֹהַר הַבְּתוּלֹת ("if her father utterly
refuses to give her to him, he shall weigh silver like the dowry of the virgins"): the fine stands though the marriage
is refused; "I have only the case where she has a father — when she has no father? 'if he utterly refuses' — in any
case" (Mekhilta continuation 16 1, Rabbi Yose HaGelili: she herself refuses); the amount by the far seat, "silver"
here and "silver" there, fifty (16 2-3 — the pointer the runner already holds as FETCH-50); the seducer sends her away
if he wishes where the rapist "drinks from his vessel" (Mishnah Ketubot 3:4-5 — the marriage of 22:15 לוֹ לְאִשָּׁה "to
him as a wife" is written when no one refuses).

## FINDINGS

1. **ONE CLAUSE TEACHES ANOTHER'S PENALTY.** The deliberate murderer's death mode is absent from 21:12-14; the
   tradition derives THE SWORD from the slave-killer's 'he shall surely be avenged' (21:20) by a verbal analogy with
   Leviticus 26:25's 'sword avenging' (Sanhedrin 52b:13; the Mekhilta's Rabbi Natan) — a taught transfer inside the
   span, and the effect `beheaded` carries it with its teacher named. The machine held no mode for the murderer until
   the slave's clause was compiled.
2. **THE BURGLAR'S VERDICT IS A STATUS THE MISHNAH NAMES.** 'He has blood' / 'he has no blood' (Sanhedrin 8:6's own
   phrase, 22:1-2's own noun) is the state on the thief that decides the householder's guilt AND the broken barrel's
   liability; the sun is clarity, the father presumed merciful — the effect `has_blood` with the arm's reason as its
   value.
3. **TWO EXITS IN ONE CLAUSE.** 'She shall go out free, without money' (21:11) is read at Kiddushin 4a as two exits —
   'free' the days of maturity, 'without money' the days of youth — and 'no money for THIS master, but money for the
   father'; the cell holds both, the scene fires each on its own row.
4. **THE HAND-MODEL MISSED THE LIBRARY'S FORM, ONCE.** The tuple prediction assumed a timer effect is written at its
   fire; the standing library writes the term at once and carries the freedom as the timer. Forty-one slots right on
   the first run, one wrong for a reason the scene then taught; the prediction stands as printed, the miss beside it.
5. **THE SECOND SEAT IS A CALL, NOT A COPY.** 21:17's curser fetches the mode, either parent, after death and by the
   Name from the Leviticus 20:9 seat by live call (a REFERENCE — the same clause at two seats), and adds only what its
   own ink adds: the woman, the tumtum and the androgynos (Mekhilta Nezikin 17 1). The daemon that wraps it is the
   Leviticus seat's.

The block's charter: THE_LOOP.md "The tent as the run's interrupt" (2026-09-09) and the decisions D4-D6, D11. Four times in the
run a case arises that no standing law decides; the run HALTS on it, the case is carried to the tent, and what comes out is part
VERDICT on the instance and part NEW RULE written in the case's name (Sanhedrin 8a:4-5, 78b:4-7, 80b:5; Bava Batra 110b:4, 119b:10;

the mouth of the LORD" (24:12); (2) THE UNCLEAN MEN AT PASSOVER, Num 9:1-14 — "stand, and I will hear what the LORD commands
concerning you" (9:8); (3) THE WOOD-GATHERER, Num 15:32-36 — "because it had not been declared what should be done to him" (15:34);
(4) THE DAUGHTERS OF ZELOPHEHAD, Num 27:1-11 and 36:1-12 — "and Moses brought their judgment before the LORD" (27:5). This file is
the block's design and record, one section per case; each case's sitting writes its design BEFORE its types, its declaration

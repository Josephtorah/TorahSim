--- 136:4 (head (16, 9))
HE: יָכוֹל יִקְצוֹר וְיָבִיא, וְיִסְפּוֹר כָּל זְמַן שֶׁיִּרְצֶה? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: מֵהָחֵל חֶרְמֵשׁ בַּקָּמָה תָּחֵל לִסְפֹּר.
EN: Is it possible to say thathe shall harvest itand count the days whenever he wants?The Teaching states:“From when the scythe is first set to the standing grain, you shall begin to count” (Dt.16:9)—[the counting must begin immediately at the harvest].
--- 136:5 (head (16, 9))
HE: יָכוֹל יִקְצוֹר וְיִסְפּוֹר, וְיָבִיא כָּל זְמַן שֶׁיִּרְצֶה? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר (ויקרא כג טו): ״מִיּוֹם הֲבִיאֲכֶם תִּסְפְּרוּ״.
EN: Is it possible to say that he shall harvest it, count the days, and then bring it whenever he wants?The Teaching states:“From the day that you bring it” (Lv.23:15)—[the days must be counted in sequence].
--- 136:6 (head (16, 9))
HE: יָכוֹל יִקְצוֹר בַּיּוֹם וְיִסְפּוֹר בַּיּוֹם וְיָבִיא בַּיּוֹם? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר (ויקרא כג טו): ״שֶׁבַע שַׁבָּתוֹת תְּמִימוֹת תִּהְיֶינָה״. אֵימָתַי הֵן תְּמִימוֹת? מִשֶּׁאַתָּה מַתְחִיל מִבָּעֶרֶב.
EN: Is it possible to say thathe should harvest by day,and count by day,and bring it [to the presiding Priest] by day?The Teaching states:“There shall be seven complete weeks” (Lv.23:15).When are they deemed complete?When you start counting the days from the evening.
--- 136:7 (head (16, 9))
HE: יָכוֹל יִקְצוֹר בַּלַּיְלָה וְיִסְפּוֹר בַּלַּיְלָה וְיָבִיא בַּלַּיְלָה? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר (ויקרא כג טו): ״מִיּוֹם הֲבִיאֲכֶם תִּסְפְּרוּ״, אֵין הֲבָאָה אֶלָּא בַּיּוֹם.
EN: Is it possible to say thathe should harvest by night,and count by night,and bring it by night?The Teaching states:“From the day that you bring it” (Lv.23:15)—this bringing must occur by day.

--- 129:1 (head (16, 2))
HE: (דברים טז ב) וְזָבַחְתָּ פֶּסַח – שֶׁתְּהֵא שְׁחִיטָתוֹ לְשֵׁם פֶּסַח, שֶׁאִם שְׁחָטוֹ שֶׁלֹּא לִשְׁמוֹ – פָּסוּל.
EN: Pisqa’ 129416H: 173-174; JN1: 315-316.1. “And you shall slaughter the Paschal lamb” (Dt.16:2)—so that its slaughtering should be entirelyfor the sake of the Paschal lamb.For if he slaughtered it under a different name,it is disqualified.
--- 129:2 (head (16, 2))
HE: אֵין לִי אֶלָּא שְׁחִיטָתוֹ, מִנַּיִן לְרַבּוֹת קִבּוּל דָּמוֹ וּזְרִיקַת דָּמוֹ? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר (דברים טז א): ״וְעָשִׂיתָ״.
EN: I might infer only thatone who [intended to] slaughter it under a different name[disqualifies the Paschal lamb from being eaten].On what basis do I know to include[a change of intention while]receiving its blood and sprinkling its blood?417Cf. M. Pes. 5:2.The Teaching states:“To celebrate” (`asita;418Cf. the translation of `asita at Pisqa’ 128.1 Dt.16:1)—[to perform the rite with full mindfulness].
--- 129:3 (head (16, 2))
HE: יָכוֹל שֶׁאֲנִי מְרַבֶּה אֶת הֶקְטֵר חֲלָבָיו? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: וְזָבַחְתָּ.
EN: Is it possible that I should include [as a disqualifications]the burning of its organ-fats [under a different name]?The Teaching states:“You shall slaughter” (Dt.16:2)—[only acts intrinsic to sacrifice disqualify the offeringif they are performed under a different name].
--- 129:4 (head (16, 2))
HE: זְבִיחָה בִּכְלָל הָיְתָה, וְלָמָּה יָצְאָה? לְהַקִּישׁ אֵלֶיהָ: מַה זְבִיחָה מְיֻחֶדֶת, שֶׁמְּעַכֶּבֶת אֶת הַכַּפָּרָה – אַף כָּל שֶׁמְּעַכֵּב אֶת הַכַּפָּרָה; יָצְאָה הַקְטָרָה שֶׁאֵינָהּ מְעַכֶּבֶת אֶת הַכַּפָּרָה.
EN: Now, slaughtering [the lamb] could have been incorporated[under the rule of other sacrificial rites].So why is it singled out for special mention?To use it for comparison:Seeing that slaughter is distinctive,in that [improper intention during the slaughter] hinders expiation—so, too, whatever hinders expiation is comparable to slaughter.This excludes burning the organ-fats,for they do not hinder expiation [when offered with improper intention].
--- 129:5 (head (16, 2))
HE: לַה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ – לַשֵּׁם הַמְיֻחָד.
EN: “For HASHEM your God” (Dt.16:1)—[only the acts intrinsic to slaughter must be done]for the sake of the Unique Name!419Cf. Sifre Nu., 143 and Pisqa’ 128.1.
--- 129:6 (head (16, 2))
HE: צֹאן וּבָקָר, וַהֲלֹא אֵין פֶּסַח בָּא אֶלָּא מִן הַכְּבָשִׂים וּמִן הָעִזִּים! אִם כֵּן לָמָּה נֶאֱמַר צֹאן וּבָקָר? צֹאן לַפֶּסַח וּבָקָר לַחֲגִיגָה. דָּבָר אַחֵר: לְהַקִּישׁ כָּל הַבָּא מִן הַצֹּאן וּמִן הַבָּקָר לַפֶּסַח: מַה פֶּסַח שֶׁהוּא בָּא חוֹבָה, אֵינוֹ בָּא אֶלָּא מִן הַחֻלִּין – כָּךְ כָּל דָּבָר שֶׁהוּא בָּא חוֹבָה אֵינוֹ בָּא אֶלָּא מִן הַחֻלִּין.

--- 129:7 (head (16, 2))
HE: בַּמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר יִבְחַר ה׳ לְשַׁכֵּן שְׁמוֹ שָׁם – זוֹ שִׁילֹה וּבֵית עוֹלָמִים.
EN: “In the place at which—there— HASHEM will settle His name” (Dt.16:2)—[the redundancy of place and there]implies that one refers to the sanctuary at Shiloh,and the other refers to the Eternal Abode.422Cf. Pisqa’ 74.2.

Lichtenstein’s Zer`a Avraham: “The symbolism may be decoded as follows: tzel`a (“ridge”) also means “rib”, and thus points towards David’s sin with Bathsheba. Despite this sin, “the mountain stood opposite him”, that is, the future deed of building the Abode on Zion cancels out David’s sin with Bathsheba. Through the merit of Solomon, who actually built the Abode, David’s sin in regard to Shimei b. Gera was eventually absolved.” Cf.

Baroqa:I heard the following at the Vineyard of Yavneh—he who has but one testicle is considered a “daylight castrate”230Heb: saris khamah. “A eunuch from the time of seeing the sun” (Jastrow:476, s.v. khamah).[whose condition is congenital]. 231//T. --- 247:4 (head (23, 2))
HE: בִּקְהַל ה׳, רַבִּי יְהוּדָה אוֹמֵר: אַרְבַּע קְהִלּוֹת הֵן: קְהַל כֹּהֲנִים קְהַל לְוִיִּם קְהַל יִשְׂרָאֵל קְהַל גֵּרִים.
EN: “The Community of HASHEM” (Dt.23:2).R. Judah says:There are four such communities—the community of the Priests;the community of the Levites;the community of Israel,and the community of Proselytes.

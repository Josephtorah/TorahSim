--- 157:2 (head (17, 15))
HE: מֶלֶךְ – וְלֹא מַלְכָּה.
EN: “A king” (Dt.17:15)—but not a queen.
--- 157:3 (head (17, 15))
HE: אֲשֶׁר יִבְחַר ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ בּוֹ – עַל פִּי נָבִיא.
EN: “Whom HASHEM your God shall choose” (Dt.17:15)—according to the guidance of a prophet.
--- 157:4 (head (17, 15))
HE: מִקֶּרֶב – וְלֹא מֵחוּצָה לָאָרֶץ.
EN: “From among your brothers” (Dt.17:15)—but not an Israelite from beyond the Land.
--- 157:5 (head (17, 15))
HE: אַחֶיךָ – וְלֹא מֵאֲחֵרִים.
EN: “From among your brothers” (Dt.17:15)—but not from [gentile] outsiders.83A literal rendering of the Hebrew: ‘akheirim. But, see Pisqa’ 43.18- 30, for word-plays which exploit the assonance of ‘akheirim with ‘elohim ‘akheirim, yielding “gods of the others” as well as “other gods.”

--- 157:7 (head (17, 15))
HE: דָּבָר אַחֵר: שׂוֹם תָּשִׂים עָלֶיךָ מֶלֶךְ – מִצְוַת עֲשֵׂה.
EN: 3. Another word:“You should indeed place over yourself a king” (Dt.17:15)—this is a prescriptive commandment.
--- 157:8 (head (17, 15))
HE: לֹא תוּכַל לָתֵת עָלֶיךָ אִישׁ נָכְרִי – מִצְוַת לֹא תַעֲשֶׂה.
EN: “You will not be permitted to place over yourself a foreign man” (Dt.17:15)—this is a proscriptive commandment.
--- 157:9 (head (17, 15))
HE: אִישׁ נָכְרִי – מִכָּן אָמְרוּ: הָאִישׁ מְמַנִּים פַּרְנָס עַל הַצִּבּוּר, וְאֵין מְמַנִּים הָאִשָּׁה פַּרְנֶסֶת עַל הַצִּבּוּר.
EN: “A foreign man” (Dt.17:15)—On this basis they taught:A man may be appointedto provide for the community,but they may not appoint a womanto provide for the community.

--- 123:1 (head (15, 18))
HE: (דברים טו יח) לֹא יִקְשֶׁה בְעֵינֶיךָ בְּשַׁלֵּחֲךָ, מִכָּן אַתָּה אוֹמֵר: שָׂכִיר עוֹבֵד בַּיּוֹם; זֶה – בַּיּוֹם וּבַלַּיְלָה.
EN: Pisqa’ 123370H:168; JN1:303-304.1. “You shall not consider it a hardship when you set him free from among you,for he has given you twice the service of a hired hand for six years” (Dt.15:18).On this basis you teach:A hired hand works by day,while this one [the slave] works by day and night.371Cf. Mechilta Ishmael, neziqin, 1. The parallel in Mechlta makes exactly the opposite point from Sifre: “just as a hired hand works by day but not by night, the Hebrew slave, too, works by day but not by night.” Cf. Hammer:442, pisqa 123, n. 1.
--- 123:2 (head (15, 18))
HE: וּבֵרַכְךָ ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ, יָכוֹל בָּטֵל? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר בְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר תַּעֲשֶׂה.
EN: “And HASHEM your God shall bless you” (Dt.15:18).Is it possible to say thatbeing idle [you will prosper]?The Teaching states:“In all that you do” (Dt.15:18)—[that is, your labor causes you to prosper].
--- 123:3 (head (15, 18))
HE: וּבֵרַכְךָ ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ, כָּל מָקוֹם שֶׁהַכָּתוּב עוֹנֵשׁ מָמוֹן – כָּתוּב בְּרָכָה, וּבְיוֹתֵר מָקוֹם שֶׁיֵּשׁ בּוֹ חֶסְרוֹן כִּיס.
EN: “And HASHEM your God shall bless you” (Dt.15:18)—whenever a verse mentions financial misfortuneit writes [also] of blessing.And especially regarding loss of income[as in the owner’s cost in liberating a slave].372//Sifra, tzav, par. 1:1.

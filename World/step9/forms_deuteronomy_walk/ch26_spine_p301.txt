EN: “Which you have given me, HASHEM” (Dt.26:10).On this basis they taught:Administrators [of property],[Hebrew] slaves,court-appointed messengers,44The first three listed are temporarily landless as a condition of fulfilling certain social roles and therefore cannot truthfully acknowledge the Land as theirs.women,persons with ill-defined genitalia,45Heb: tumtum; “one whose sex is obscure.”and those with both sets of genitalia,46Gr: ‘androgynos;” “androgyne.” This second group of three, because they are women or inconclusively male, are precluded by biological destiny from uttering the confession that includes acknowledgement of the Land as theirs.bring the First-fruits, but do not declaim—for they are unable to say:“Which you have given me, HASHEM” (Dt.26:10) 47=M.Bik.1:5.[since their status prevents them from owning land].

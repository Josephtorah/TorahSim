--- 331:3 (head (32, 41))
HE: דָּבָר אַחֵר אָשִׁיב נָקָם לְצָרָי אֵלּוּ כּוּתִיִּים, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (עזרא ד א): ״וַיִּשְׁמְעוּ צָרֵי יְהוּדָה וּבִנְיָמִן״.
EN: 2. Another word:“I will return vengeance to My tormentors” (Dt.32:41)—this refers to the Samaritans,187Heb: kutim. A second-temple religious-ethnic community, based in Samaria, with a long history of conflict with Judean-rabbinic Jews, who regarded them as practicing a debased form of quasi-Judaism (e.g., B. Qiddushin 75b). The first-century CE incorporation of Samaritan territories into the Roman province of Judea, exacerbated tensions between Judeans and Samaritans, which are reflected in rabbinic traditions such as the present one, as well as the gospel tradition (e.g., Luke 10:25-37).As it is said:“And the tormentors of Judah and Benjamin heard” (Ezra.4:1).

--- 100:1 (head (14, 4))
HE: (דברים יד ד-ה) זֹאת הַבְּהֵמָה אֲשֶׁר תֹּאכֵלוּ, שׁוֹר שֵׂה כְשָׂבִים וְשֵׂה עִזִּים; אַיָּל וּצְבִי וְיַחְמוּר, מְלַמֵּד שֶׁחַיָּה בִּכְלַל בְּהֵמָה.
EN: Pisqa’ 100222H:146-147; JN1:257.1. “This is the cattle (behemah) which you shall eat:the ox, the lamb of the sheep, and the lamb of the goats,the ram, the deer, the gazelle, the wild goat, the ibex,the antelope, the mountain sheep” (Dt.14:4-5).This teaches thatwild cattle (khayah) [such as rams] share a common rubricwith [domesticated] cattle (behemah).
--- 100:2 (head (14, 4))
HE: מִנַּיִן שֶׁאַף בְּהֵמָה בִּכְלַל חַיָּה? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר (ויקרא יא ב): ״זֹאת הַחַיָּה״ וְגוֹ׳. מְלַמֵּד שֶׁחַיָּה קְרוּיָה בְּהֵמָה וּבְהֵמָה קְרוּיָה חַיָּה, וּמְלַמֵּד שֶׁבְּהֵמָה טְמֵאָה מְרֻבָּה מִבְּהֵמָה טְהוֹרָה. בְּכָל מָקוֹם הַכָּתוּב פּוֹרֵט אֶת הַמּוּעָט, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר זֹאת הַבְּהֵמָה אֲשֶׁר תֹּאכֵלוּ וְגוֹ׳ אַיָּל וּצְבִי וְיַחְמוּר וְגוֹ׳.
EN: On what basis do we know that[domesticated] cattle share a common rubricwith wild cattle?The Teaching states:“This is the wild cattle which you shall eat among all the cattle which are on the earth” (Lv.11:2).This teaches thatwild cattle may be called cattleand [domesticated] cattle may be called wild cattle.223Sifra, shemini, par. 2:8.This teaches [also] that[the species of] unclean cattle out-number [those of] clean cattle.For in all cases,Scripture enumerates the smaller[category before enumerating the larger],224Cf. Pisqa’ 103.4as it is said:“This is the cattle you shall eat: the ox . . the ram, . . . the antelope” (Dt.14:4-5).

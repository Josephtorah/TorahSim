--- 241:1 (head (22, 22))
HE: (דברים כב כב) כִּי יִמָּצֵא אִישׁ – בְּעֵדִים. שֹׁכֵב עִם אִשָּׁה – כָּל שְׁכִיבָה. בְעֻלַת בַּעַל, לְהָבִיא אֶת שֶׁנִּבְעֲלָה בְּבֵית אָבִיהָ וַעֲדַיִן הִיא אֲרוּסָה.
EN: Pisqa’ 241197H:247-248;JN2:160.1. “If a man is found” (Dt.22:22)—as reported by witnesses.“Bedding a married woman” (Dt.22:22)—that is, any kind of bedding.“Married” (Dt.22:22)—to include a woman who was already mountedin her father’s house, while still betrothed.
--- 241:2 (head (22, 22))
HE: וּמֵתוּ – בִּסְתָם מִיתָה הָאֲמוּרָה בַּתּוֹרָה, בְּחֶנֶק.
EN: “Then, indeed both of them shall die” (Dt.22:22)—where the mode of execution mandated by the Torah is unspecified,the sentence is assumed to be strangulation.198Cf. Pisqa’ 155.1.
--- 241:3 (head (22, 22))
HE: שְׁנֵיהֶם, וְלֹא הָעוֹשִׂים מַעֲשֵׂה הִירוֹדָס,
EN: “Both of them” (Dt.22:22)—but not if they are engaged in the Deed of Herod,199Heb: ma`aseh herodes; i.e. necrophilia. RH’s test has: ma` aseh khidudim. rendered by Jastrow as “unnatural gratification.” The reference is explained at B.BB.3b according to a tradition that Herod had his Hasmonean wife embalmed in honey and preserved for his own sexual needs.[which requires that the female partner already have died].200See F:271, n.13.
--- 241:4 (head (22, 22))
HE: כְּשֶׁהוּא אוֹמֵר גַּם שְׁנֵיהֶם לְרַבּוֹת הַבָּאִים מֵאַחֲרֵיהֶם.
EN: When He says:“Both of them (Dt.22:22)—this includes anyone who happens along after them[and also beds the woman].
--- 241:5 (head (22, 22))
HE: הָאִישׁ הַשֹּׁכֵב עִם הָאִשָּׁה, וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁהִיא קְטַנָּה.
EN: “The man who beds the woman” (Dt.22:22)—even if she is still a minor.
--- 241:6 (head (22, 22))
HE: וְהָאִשָּׁה, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁנִּבְעֲלָה לְקָטָן.
EN: “And the woman” (Dt.22:22)—even though she was mounted by a minor.
--- 241:7 (head (22, 22))
HE: וּבִעַרְתָּ הָרָע, בַּעֵר עוֹשֵׂי הָרָעוֹת מִיִּשְׂרָאֵל!
EN: “And you shall expunge the evil from Israel” (Dt.22:22)—expunge evil-doers from Israel.201Cf. Pisqa’ot 155.1, 240.1, 241.1, 242.1.

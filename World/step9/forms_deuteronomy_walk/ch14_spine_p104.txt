--- 104:1 (head (14, 21))
HE: (דברים יד כא) לֹא תֹאכְלוּ כָל נְבֵלָה, אֵין לִי אֶלָּא נְבֵלָה, טְרֵפָה מִנַּיִן? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: ״נְבֵלָה״ – ״כָל נְבֵלָה״.
EN: Pisqa’ 104240H:149-150; JN1:265-266.1. “Do not eat any carcass” (Dt.14:21).I might infer only that[the rule applies to] a carcass found dead.On what basis do I know that[it applies] to what is mauled [and about to die]?The teaching states:“Any carcass” (Dt.14:21)—[this includes] anything found dead[including a mauled animal that will not survive].

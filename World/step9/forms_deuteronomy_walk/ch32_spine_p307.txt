Sealed in my treasury?” (Dt.32:34)?So, when does each of them take away [what they have earned]?“For all His ways are just” (Dt.32:4)—in the future, when He sits on the Throne of Judgment,He dispenses judgment to each and all,providing him with what is appropriate.

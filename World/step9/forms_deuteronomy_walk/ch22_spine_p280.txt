--- 280:1 (head (24, 16))
HE: (דברים כד טז) לֹא יוּמְתוּ אָבוֹת עַל בָּנִים, וְכִי מָה בָּא הַכָּתוּב לְלַמְּדֵנוּ שֶׁלֹּא יוּמְתוּ אָבוֹת עַל יְדֵי בָנִים וְלֹא בָנִים עַל יְדֵי אָבוֹת, וַהֲלֹא כְּבָר נֶאֱמַר אִישׁ בְּחֶטְאוֹ יוּמָתוּ אֶלָּא שֶׁלֹּא יוּמְתוּ אָבוֹת בְּעֵדוּת בָּנִים וְלֹא בָנִים בְּעֵדוּת אָבוֹת.
EN: Pisqa’ 280395H:272;JN2:228.1. “Fathers shall not be put to death by reason of the sons” (Dt.24:16).Now, what is the verse trying to teach us—that father’s shall not be put to deathbecause of the [deeds of their] sons,nor sons because of their fathers?But isn’t it already stated [in this very verse]:“A person shall die for his own sin” (Dt.24:16)?Actually, [the point us that]fathers shall not be put to deathon the testimony of their sons,nor sons on the testimony of their fathers.
--- 280:2 (head (24, 16))

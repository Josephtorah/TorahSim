--- 152:1 (head (17, 8))
HE: (דברים יז ח) כִּי יִפָּלֵא – מְלַמֵּד שֶׁבְּמֻפְלָא הַכָּתוּב מְדַבֵּר.
EN: Pisqa’ 15258H:189-190; JN2:17-181. “If you are bedazzled”59Heb: yipalei; literally: “extraordinary,” “baffling.” Following is the JPS rendering of the first clause of Dt.17:8: “If a case is too baffling for you to decide, be it a controversy over homicide, civil law or assault—matters of dispute in your courts.” The midrashic reading of the verse, presented in my own rendering of Sifre’s text, takes pains to re-signify the verse to make room for characteristic institutions associated with the oral Torah’s institutions of jurisprudence. Cf. Fraade, p.84-85. (Dt.17:8).This teaches thatthe verse speaks [even] of a judicial appointeeof dazzling intellect.60Heb: mufla’. Tannaitic memory recognizes the mufla’ shel bet din as an appointed office (e.g., M. Hor.1:4, T. San.7:1) that should go to the most brilliant judge in the Sanhedrin.
--- 152:2 (head (17, 8))
HE: מִמְּךָ – זוֹ עֵצָה.
EN: “Beyond your own [capacities]” (Dt.17:8)—of deliberation.
--- 152:3 (head (17, 8))
HE: דָּבָר – זוֹ הֲלָכָה.
EN: “By an issue” (Dt.17:8)—concerning the oral legal tradition.
--- 152:4 (head (17, 8))
HE: לַמִּשְׁפָּט – זֶה הַדִּין.
EN: “Of case-law” (Dt.17:8)—that requires adjudication.
--- 152:5 (head (17, 8))
HE: בֵּין דָּם לְדָם – בֵּין דַּם נִדָּה לְדַם יוֹלֶדֶת לְדַם זִיבָה.
EN: “In distinguishing between blood and blood” (Dt.17:8)—whether menstrual blood,parturient blood,or a bloody venereal discharge.
--- 152:6 (head (17, 8))
HE: בֵּין דִּין לְדִין – בֵּין דִּינֵי מָמוֹנוֹת לְדִינֵי נְפָשׁוֹת לְדִינֵי מַכּוֹת.
EN: “Between judgment and judgment” (Dt.17:8)—whether monetary judgments,capital judgments,or judgments [regarding judicial] lashings.61Heb: dinei makot. I follow RH and Fraade, p. 84 in rendering this phrase. Cf. H:189, “bodily injury law” and JN2:17. “assault.”
--- 152:7 (head (17, 8))
HE: בֵּין נֶגַע לָנֶגַע – בֵּין נִגְעֵי אָדָם לְנִגְעֵי בְגָדִים לְנִגְעֵי בָתִּים.
EN: “Between one plague-sign and another” (Dt.17:8)—whether those affecting human bodies, clothing, or homes.
--- 152:8 (head (17, 8))
HE: דִּבְרֵי – אֵלּוּ עֲרָכִים וַחֲרָמִים וְהֶקְדֵּשׁוֹת.
EN: “And words” (Dt.17:8)—[declarations of vows]concerning the monetary valueof persons, devoted objects, or consecrated gifts (Lv.27:1-34).62`Arachim, kheremim, and heqdeshot denote vows to offer the monetary value of specific persons or objects to the upkeep of the Sanctuary.
--- 152:9 (head (17, 8))
HE: רִיבוֹת – זוֹ הַשְׁקָיַת סוֹטָה וַעֲרִיפַת עֶגְלָה וְטָהֳרַת מְצוֹרָע.
EN: “Of conflict” (Dt.17:8)—whether the ordeal of the accused adulteress (Nu.5:12ff.),the rite of the beheaded calf (Dt.21:1-9),or the purification of victims of Scale-disease (Lv.14:1ff.).
--- 152:10 (head (17, 8))
HE: בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָ – זֶה לֶקֶט שִׁכְחָה וּפֵאָה.
EN: “Within your gates (Dt.17:8)—whether Gleanings, the Forgotten Sheaf, or Corner-offerings.63These are agricultural offerings that remain in the fields unharvested for the exclusive benefit of the poor (see Lv.19:19, Dt.24:19, Dt. 26:12).

EN: “Arise” (Dt.17:8)—in the court.
--- 152:13 (head (17, 8))
HE: מִכָּן אָמְרוּ (סנהדרין פי״א מ״ב): שְׁלֹשָׁה בָּתֵּי דִינִים הָיוּ שָׁם, אֶחָד עַל פֶּתַח הַר הַבַּיִת, וְאֶחָד עַל פֶּתַח הָעֲזָרָה, וְאֶחָד בְּלִשְׁכַּת הַגָּזִית.
EN: On this basis they taught:Three courts were there:one at the entrance to the Mount of the Abode;and one at the entrance to the Courtyard;and one in the Chamber of Carved Stone.
--- 152:14 (head (17, 8))
HE: בָּאִים לָזֶה שֶׁל שַׁעַר הַר הַבַּיִת וְאוֹמֵר: ״כָּךְ דָּרַשְׁתִּי וְכָךְ דָּרְשׁוּ חֲבֵרַי, כָּךְ לִמַּדְתִּי וְכָךְ לִמְּדוּ חֲבֵרַי״. אִם שָׁמְעוּ, אָמְרוּ לָהֶם, וְאִם לָאו – בָּאִים לָזֶה שֶׁעַל פֶּתַח הָעֲזָרָה וְאוֹמֵר: ״כָּךְ דָּרַשְׁתִּי וְכָךְ דָּרְשׁוּ חֲבֵרַי, כָּךְ לִמַּדְתִּי וְכָךְ לִמְּדוּ חֲבֵרַי״. אִם שָׁמְעוּ, אָמְרוּ לָהֶם, וְאִם לָאו – אֵלּוּ וָאֵלּוּ בָּאִים לְבֵית דִּין הַגָּדוֹל שֶׁבְּלִשְׁכַּת הַגָּזִית, שֶׁמִּשָּׁם תּוֹרָה יוֹצְאָה לְכָל יִשְׂרָאֵל, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: מִן הַמָּקוֹם הַהוּא אֲשֶׁר יִבְחַר ה׳.

EN: Pisqa’ 127398H:172; JN1:313.1. “Take care to observe the month of Aviv” (Dt.16:1)—observe [the moon] for a month prior to Aviv,so that Aviv falls at its proper time [in the spring]. 399The biblical month of Aviv is “the first month” of the year (Ex. 12:2), and corresponds to the rabbinic month of Nisan, which in the rabbinic liturgical calendar constitutes the seventh month. Cf. Mechilta Ishmael, paskha’, 2.

EN: Is it possible to say thatif a [lunar] year is short [of the solar year] by fourteen or fifteen days.you may add [to the month preceding Aviv]fourteen or fifteen days[enabling calendrical calculation to match the season]?The Teaching states:“The month” (Dt.16:1)—not a day more or less [than a month].

--- 164:1 (head (18, 2))
HE: (דברים יח ב) וְנַחֲלָה – זוֹ נַחֲלַת שְׁלֹשָׁה.
EN: Pisqa’ 164125H:195; JN2:35.1. “An inheritance he shall not have” (Dt.18:2)—within the inheritance of the three[trans-Jordanian tribes, Gad, Reuben, and Manasseh].
--- 164:2 (head (18, 2))
HE: לֹא יִהְיֶה לּוֹ בְּקֶרֶב אֶחָיו – זוֹ נַחֲלַת חֲמִשָּׁה.
EN: “In the midst of his brothers” (Dt.18:2)—nor within the inheritance of the five[Canaanite nations, the Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites,Hivites, and Jebusites (e.g. Dt.20:17)].
--- 164:3 (head (18, 2))
HE: ה׳ הוּא נַחֲלָתוֹ כַּאֲשֶׁר דִּבֶּר לוֹ – לְהַגִּיד מֶה גָרַם.
EN: “HASHEM is his inheritance, as He has promised him” (Dt.18:2)—this explains the rationale behind[Levite landlessness].126Cf. SZ, qorakh, 24.

EN: “Whom You have redeemed” (Dt.21:8)—referring to the dead.This explains thateven the dead need absolution.We turn out to learn thatThe blood-spiller’s sin evenaffects those who left Egypt!

EN: “Whom you have redeemed” (Dt.21:8)—for this reason did You redeem us,so that there not be among us anyoneguilty of spilling innocent blood!Another word:For this reason did You redeem us,so that when we sin, You can absolve us!

## Sifrei 190:16 EN
R. Yose the Galilean says:on what basis do I know thata man does not go off to warunless he has hands, feet, eyes and teeth?The Teaching states:“You shall show no pity, . . . hand for hand, foot for foot (Dt.19:21),when you go off to war” (Dt.20:1)—[a soldier must be physically whole in preparation for combat].285The point is made by reading Dt.20:1’s “going to war,” as a continuation of Dt.19:21, contrary to the masoretic punctuation.

## Sifrei 190:20 (head (19, 17)) HE

## Sifrei 190:20 EN
“Should you see horse and chariot” (Dt.20:1)—just as they attack you with horse and chariot,you, too, must attack them with horse and chariot.Just as they attack you with a large force,you, too, must attack them with a large force.

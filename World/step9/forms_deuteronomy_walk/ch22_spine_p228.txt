--- 228:1 (head (22, 7))
HE: (דברים כב ז) שַׁלֵּחַ תְּשַׁלַּח – מִצְוַת עֲשֵׂה. שִׁלְּחָהּ וְחָזְרָה, שִׁלְּחָהּ וְחָזְרָה, אֲפִלּוּ חָמֵשׁ פְּעָמִים – חַיָּב לְשַׁלֵּחַ, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: שַׁלֵּחַ תְּשַׁלַּח.

--- 228:2 (head (22, 7))
HE: הָאַוָּזִים וְתַרְנְגוֹלִים שֶׁמָּרְדוּ וְשֶׁקִּנְּנוּ בַּפַּרְדֵּס – חַיָּב לְשַׁלֵּחַ; בַּבַּיִת – פָּטוּר מִלְּשַׁלֵּחַ.
EN: 2. Ducks or chickens that got looseand nested in an orchard—one is under obligation to send the dam away.If the birds are nesting in the house—he need not send the dam away.
--- 228:3 (head (22, 7))
HE: (דברים כב ו) אֶפְרוֹחִים אוֹ בֵיצִים, מָה אֶפְרוֹחִים שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּם צוֹרֶךְ – יָצְאוּ מֵתִים שֶׁאֵין בָּהֶם צוֹרֶךְ, אַף בֵּיצִים שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּהֶם צוֹרֶךְ – יָצְאוּ בֵּיצִים מוּזָרוֹת שֶׁאֵין בָּהֶם צוֹרֶךְ. מִעוּט אֶפְרוֹחִים שְׁנַיִם, מִעוּט בֵּיצִים שְׁנַיִם, וַאֲפִלּוּ אֵין שָׁם אֶלָּא בֵּיצָה אַחַת אוֹ אֶפְרוֹחַ אֶחָד – חַיָּב לְשַׁלֵּחַ. דָּבָר אַחֵר: כִּי יִקָּרֵא קַן צִפּוֹר לְפָנֶיךָ מִכָּל מָקוֹם.
EN: “The fledglings or the eggs” (Dt.22:6)—fledglings are useful as food.Therefore we exclude from the ruledead fledglings, which are useless.Similarly, eggs are useful as food.Therefore we exclude from the ruleabnormal eggs, which are useless.The minimum number intendedby the plural, fledglings, is two.and the minimum of eggs is two.105Cf. Pisqa’ 227.2.Even if there is but one fledgling or one egg,he is obliged to send off the dam.3. Another word:“If you happen across a bird’s nest” (Dt.22:6)—any place.
--- 228:4 (head (22, 7))
HE: בַּדֶּרֶךְ בְּכָל עֵץ אוֹ עַל הָאָרֶץ, מַה דֶּרֶךְ רְשׁוּת – יָצְאוּ מְקֻשָּׁרִים שֶׁהֵן בִּרְשׁוּת אַחֵר.
EN: “In the path, in any tree, or upon the ground” (Dt.22:6)—just as the term, in the path,implies that the birds are free-ranging,we exclude from the rulebirds that are tied together,for they obviously have limited range.

EN: Another word:“You must definitely send away the dam” (Dt.22:7)—the verse discusses a clean bird.Or, [perhaps,] is it also[discussing] an unclean bird?The Teaching states:“Every clean bird shall you eat” (Dt.14:11).This permits us to recognizea family resemblance among scriptural verses,106Heb: binyan ‘ av. Josiah.Indeed, if he sent her away and returned to his journey,he is under no obligation to repeatedly send her away.

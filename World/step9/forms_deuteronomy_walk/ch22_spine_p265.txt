--- 265:1 (head (23, 23))
HE: (דברים כג כג) וְכִי תֶחְדַּל לִנְדֹּר, רַבִּי מֵאִיר אוֹמֵר (קהלת ה ד): ״טוֹב אֲשֶׁר לֹא תִדֹּר מִשֶּׁתִּדֹּר וְלֹא תְשַׁלֵּם״, טוֹב מִזֶּה וּמִזֶּה שֶׁלֹּא תִדֹּר כָּל עִקָּר. רַבִּי יְהוּדָה אוֹמֵר: ״טוֹב אֲשֶׁר לֹא תִדֹּר״, טוֹב מִזֶּה וּמִזֶּה נוֹדֵר וּמְשַׁלֵּם.
EN: Pisqa’ 265324H:261;JN2:199-200.1. “But if you restrain yourself from uttering a vow” (Dt.23:23).R. Meir says:“It is far better not to utter a vow at all, than to vow and not fulfill it” (Ecc.5:4). Better than either is to avoid even the hint of a vow.R. Judah says:“It is far better not to utter a vow at all” (Ecc.5:4)—Better than either is to utter a vow and pay it!325//T. Hul. 2:17.
--- 265:2 (head (23, 23))
HE: (דברים כג כד) מוֹצָא שְׂפָתֶיךָ, מִצְוַת עֲשֵׂה.
EN: 2. “As you utter with your lips” (Dt.23:24)—this is a prescriptive commandment
--- 265:3 (head (23, 23))
HE: תִּשְׁמֹר, מִצְוַת לֹא תַעֲשֶׂה.
EN: [to make the avowal out loud].“Pay attention to your words” (Dt.23:24)—this is a proscriptive commandment[not to take the avowal lightly].
--- 265:4 (head (23, 23))
HE: וְעָשִׂיתָ, הֲרֵי זוֹ אַזְהָרָה לְבֵית דִּין שֶׁיְּעַשּׂוּךָ.
EN: “So that you do” (Dt.23:24)—this is a warning to the court,to enforce upon you [fulfillment of the vow].
--- 265:5 (head (23, 23))
HE: כַּאֲשֶׁר נָדַרְתָּ, זֶה נֵדֶר.
EN: “Exactly what you have vowed” (Dt.23:24)—this refers to the [value of the] avowed object.
--- 265:6 (head (23, 23))
HE: לַה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ, אֵלּוּ עֲרָכִים וַחֲרָמִים וְהֶקְדֵּשׁוֹת.
EN: “To HASHEM your God” (Dt.23:24)—this applies to [vows based upon]the value of persons (Lv.27:1-8);dedications made to the priesthood (Lv:27:28);and consecrations to the Abode (Lv.27:9).326Cf. Pisqa’ 264.2
--- 265:7 (head (23, 23))
HE: נְדָבָה, זוֹ נְדָבָה.
EN: “Voluntarily” (Dt.23:24)—this is the Voluntary-offering[that is paired with the Vow-offering].327Discussed at Pisqa’ 264.1.
--- 265:8 (head (23, 23))
HE: אֲשֶׁר דִּבַּרְתָּ, זֶה הֶקְדֵּשׁ בֶּדֶק הַבַּיִת.
EN: “Which you have uttered” (Dt.23:24)—this refers to consecrations formaintaining the Abode.328So Pisqa’ 264.2
--- 265:9 (head (23, 23))
HE: בְּפִיךָ, זוֹ צְדָקָה.
EN: “With your mouth” (Dt.23:24)—this refers to [pledging] alms[for the destitute].

Pisqa’ 306.15..Is it possible to say thatjust as you enter them under general rubrics,you should disburse them [to your disciples] under general rubrics?The Teaching states:“May my teaching drip like rain” (Dt.32:2).Now, dripping is actually a term of commerce.9Heb: leshon kena`ani.

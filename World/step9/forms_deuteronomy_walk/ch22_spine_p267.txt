--- 267:1 (head (23, 26))
HE: (דברים כג כו) כִּי תָבֹא בְּקָמַת רֵעֶךָ, יָכוֹל לְעוֹלָם? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: וְחֶרְמֵשׁ לֹא תָנִיף, בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁאַתָּה מֵנִיף חֶרְמֵשׁ עַל קָמַת בַּעַל הַבַּיִת.
EN: Pisqa’ 267334H:262;JN2:203.1. “When you come upon your kinsman’s standing grain,you may snatch kernels by hand” (Dt.23:26)—is it possible to say thatthe rule applies any time[you enter the standing grain]?The Teaching states:“But you may not raise a sickle” (Dt.23:26)—not when you are raising the sickleon behalf of the boss [while working].
--- 267:2 (head (23, 26))

EN: “Your kinsman” (Dt.23:26)—this excludes [gentile] outsiders335Heb:‘akherim; so F:287, l.3, Ish-Shalom and Pardo. RH and TA have: bavliyim (“Babylonians”). In his commentary, RH emends to ‘akherim.from the rule.“Your kinsman” (Dt.23:26)—this excludes [from the rule produce consecrated to] the Supernal One.
--- 267:3 (head (23, 26))
HE: וְקָטַפְתָּ מְלִילֹת בְּיָדֶךָ, שֶׁלֹּא תְהֵא קוֹצֵר בְּמַגָּל.
EN: 2. “You may snatch kernels by hand” (Dt.23:26)—that is, you may not reap with a tool.

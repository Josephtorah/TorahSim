My translation follows his guidance.In one there was written:“And he sent the youngsters (za`atutei) of the Israelites” (Ex.24:5) and“But he did not send to the youngsters (za`atutei) of the Israelites” (Ex.24:11).And in two there was written:“And he sent the lads (na`arei) of the Israelites” (Ex.24:5) and“But he did not send to the young nobles (‘atzilei) of the Israelites” (Ex.24:11).Sages suppressed the one textand preserved the other two.

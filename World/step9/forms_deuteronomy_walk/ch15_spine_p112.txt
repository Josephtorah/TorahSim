--- 112:2 (head (15, 2))
HE: [וְזֶה דְּבַר הַשְּׁמִטָּה], שְׁמִטָּה מְשַׁמֶּטֶת מִלְוָה וְאֵין יוֹבֵל מְשַׁמֵּט מִלְוָה.
EN: 2. The Release-year releases a loan,but the Jubilee-year does not release a loan.
--- 112:3 (head (15, 2))
HE: שֶׁהָיָה בַּדִּין: מָה שְׁמִטָּה, שֶׁאֵין מוֹצִיאָה עֲבָדִים, מְשַׁמֶּטֶת מִלְוָה, יוֹבֵל, שֶׁמּוֹצִיא עֲבָדִים, אֵינוֹ דִין שֶׁיְּשַׁמֵּט מִלְוָה? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: וְזֶה דְּבַר הַשְּׁמִטָּה – שְׁמִטָּה מְשַׁמֶּטֶת מִלְוָה וְאֵין יוֹבֵל מְשַׁמֵּט מִלְוָה.
EN: Now, one might have reasoned otherwise:Just as the Release-year,which does not liberate slaves[with fewer than seven years’ service], does release a loan,so, too, the Jubilee year,which does liberate slaves (Lv.25:40)—isn’t it only logical thatit should release a loan?The Teaching states:“Now, this is the procedure for the release:every creditor shall release the debt” (Dt.15:2)—[therefore,] the Release-year releases a loan,while the Jubilee year does not release a loan.
--- 112:4 (head (15, 2))
HE: קַל וָחוֹמֶר לַשְּׁמִטָּה שֶׁתּוֹצִיא עֲבָדִים: וּמַה יּוֹבֵל, שֶׁאֵין מְשַׁמֵּט מִלְוָה, מוֹצִיא עֲבָדִים שְׁבִיעִית, שֶׁמְּשַׁמֶּטֶת מִלְוָה, אֵינוֹ דִין שֶׁתּוֹצִיא עֲבָדִים? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר (ויקרא כה יג): ״בִּשְׁנַת הַיּוֹבֵל הַזֹּאת״ – שְׁבִיעִית מְשַׁמֶּטֶת מִלְוָה וְיוֹבֵל מוֹצִיא עֲבָדִים.
EN: Here is a logical argument proving thatthe Release-year should liberate slaves:Just as the Jubilee year,which does not release a loan,nevertheless liberates slaves (Lv.25:40)—isn’t it only logical thatthe Seventh year,which does release a loan,should also liberate slaves?The Teaching states:“In this year of the Jubilee, shall a man return to his property” (Lv.25:13)—[with no mention of the release of debts, therefore]:the Seventh year releases a loan,while the Jubilee year liberates slaves.310=Sifra, bahar, per. 3:6.
--- 112:5 (head (15, 2))
HE: שָׁמוֹט כָּל בַּעַל, יָכוֹל אַף בִּגְזֵלָה וּבְפִקָּדוֹן? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: מַשֵּׁה יָדוֹ.
EN: 3. “Every creditor shall release the debt owed him by his kinsman (Dt.15:2).Is it possible to say thateven stolen property or collateral [must be released]?The Teaching states:“the debt owed him” (Dt.15:2)—[this excludes obligation to release stolen or pledged objects].
--- 112:6 (head (15, 2))
HE: אוֹ מַשֵּׁה יָדוֹ, יָכוֹל שְׂכַר שָׂכִיר וְהַקָּפַת הַחֲנוּת? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: אֲשֶׁר יַשֶּׁה בְּרֵעֵהוּ.
EN: Or [we might reason as follows]:“The debt owed him” (Dt.15:2)—is it possible to say that[an employer must release] a worker’s back-payor a shop-keeper [must release] an extension of credit?The Teaching states:“Owed him by his kinsman” (Dt.15:2).
--- 112:7 (head (15, 2))
HE: אִם סוֹפֵנוּ לְרַבּוֹת כֻּלָּם, מַה תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר מַשֵּׁה יָדוֹ? מַה מַשֵּׁה יָדוֹ בְּזָקוּף, אַף כֻּלָּם זְקוּפִים.
EN: Now, if we are ultimately going to include all of these[under the rule of the Release-year],311Cf. M. Shev.10:1; T. Shev.8:3.what does the Teaching mean by:“Owed him” (Dt.15:2)?Just as owed him [implies that the debtor has]a standing obligation [to pay the creditor],so, too, all of these are standing obligations [rather than loans].
--- 112:8 (head (15, 2))
HE: לֹא יִגֹּשׂ – לִתֵּן עָלָיו בְּלֹא תַעֲשֶׂה.
EN: “He shall not dun” (Dt.15:2)—this verse places [a dunning creditor] in violation of a proscription.
--- 112:9 (head (15, 2))
HE: אֶת רֵעֵהוּ – פְּרָט לַאֲחֵרִים.
EN: “His kinsman” (Dt.15:2)—excluding outsiders [from protection from dunning].
--- 112:10 (head (15, 2))
HE: וְאֶת אָחִיו – פְּרָט לְגֵר תּוֹשָׁב.
EN: “Or his brother” (Dt.15:2)—excluding the resident alien [from protection from dunning]
--- 112:11 (head (15, 2))
HE: כִּי קָרָא שְׁמִטָּה לַה׳ – בֵּין בָּאָרֶץ בֵּין בְּחוּצָה לָאָרֶץ.
EN: “For you have declared a Release-year from HASHEM” (Dt.15:2)—[the release of debts may be declared wherever you may be,] whether in the Land or beyond the Land.

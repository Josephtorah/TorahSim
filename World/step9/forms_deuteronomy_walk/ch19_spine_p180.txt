--- 180:1 (head (19, 2))
HE: (דברים יט ב) שָׁלֹשׁ עָרִים, עָרִים וְלֹא טִירִים, עָרִים וְלֹא כְּרַכִּים, עָרִים וְלֹא כְּפָרִים.
EN: Pisq’a 180206H: 204; JN2: 60.1. You shall designate for yourself . . . three cities” (Dt.19:2)—cities, but not fortresses;cities, but not capitols;cities, but not towns.207//T. Mak.3:8; cf. Sifre Num, 159.
--- 180:2 (head (19, 2))
HE: תַּבְדִּיל לָךְ – וְלֹא לַאֲחֵרִים.
EN: “Designate for yourself” (Dt.19:2)—but not for [gentile] others.208Cf. Pisqa’ 116.2
--- 180:3 (head (19, 2))
HE: בְּתוֹךְ אַרְצְךָ – וְלֹא בַּסְּפָר.
EN: “Within your Land” (Dt.19:2)—but not along the border.209Cf. Sifre Num, 160.
--- 180:4 (head (19, 2))
HE: אֲשֶׁר ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ נוֹתֵן לְךָ לְרִשְׁתָּהּ, מַה שֶּׁתִּירַשׁ – תִּכְבֹּשׁ.
EN: “Which HASHEM your God is giving you to inherit” (Dt.19:2)—you will have to conquer whatever you inherit.
--- 180:5 (head (19, 2))
HE: (דברים יט ג) תָּכִין לְךָ הַדֶּרֶךְ, תָּכִין לְךָ סְטְרָטְיָאוֹת שֶׁיְּהוּ מְפֹרָשׁוֹת לְתוֹכָהּ.
EN: 2. “Prepare for yourself the way” (Dt.19:3)—prepare for yourself highways thatlead straight to it.
--- 180:6 (head (19, 2))
HE: וְשִׁלַּשְׁתָּ אֶת גְּבוּל אַרְצְךָ, שֶׁלֹּא יִהְיוּ מְפֻזָּרוֹת אֶלָּא יִהְיוּ מְכֻוָּנוֹת, כִּשְׁתֵּי שׁוּרוֹת שֶׁבַּכֶּרֶם.
EN: “And tri-sect the boundary of your Land” (Dt.19:3)—so that the [three cities] are not scattered,but are aligned like two rows of a vineyard.210Cf. T. Mak. 2:3, M. Mak.2:5.
--- 180:7 (head (19, 2))
HE: אֲשֶׁר יַנְחִילְךָ ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ – לְרַבּוֹת עֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן.
EN: “Which HASHEM your God is bequeathing to you” (Dt.19:3)—this includes the trans-Jordanian territories[of Gad, Reuben, and Menasseh].211Cf. Sifre Nu, 160 and Pisqa’ 164.1
--- 180:8 (head (19, 2))
HE: וְהָיָה לָנוּס שָׁמָּה כָּל רוֹצֵחַ, שֶׁלֹּא יִהְיֶה גוֹלֶה מֵעִיר לְעִיר.
EN: “So that any one who unwittingly kills [an innocent party]might seek asylum there” (Dt.19:3)—so that he needn’t be expelled from city to city.212Cf. Pisqa’ 183.2

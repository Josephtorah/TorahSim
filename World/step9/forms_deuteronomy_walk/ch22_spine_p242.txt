EN: 3. “The young lady, for the reason of not calling out” (Dt.22:24).When He says, for the reason of205Heb: `al davar; literally, “on the word/matter of.” Dt.22:24),[it implies that she ignored] “an oral warning.”206Heb: `al pi hatraya; literally, “according to a warning.” For a similar exegesis of Dt. 22:24, see Pisqa’ 250.1.Thus the rule applies to a woman who—warned by onlookers [of the Torah’s severity in this matter]—brazenly ignores their warnings.
--- 242:7 (head (22, 23))
HE: וְאֶת הָאִישׁ עַל דְּבַר אֲשֶׁר עִנָּה אֶת אֵשֶׁת רֵעֵהוּ, כְּשֶׁהוּא אוֹמֵר עַל דְּבַר – עַל פִּי הַתְרָיָה.

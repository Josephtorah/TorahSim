--- 115:1 (head (15, 5))
HE: (דברים טו ה) רַק אִם שָׁמוֹעַ תִּשְׁמַע בְּקוֹל ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ, מִכָּן אָמְרוּ: שָׁמַע אָדָם קִמְעָה – מַשְׁמִיעִים אוֹתוֹ הַרְבֵּה, שָׁמַע אָדָם דִּבְרֵי תוֹרָה – מַשְׁמִיעִים אוֹתוֹ דִּבְרֵי סוֹפְרִים.
EN: Pisqa’ 115319H:161; JN1:288.1. “Only if you listen closely (shamo`a tishm`a) to the voiceof HASHEM your God” (Dt.15:5).On this basis they taught:If a person has heard (sham`a) only a little [Torah],they enable him to hear (mashmi`in lo) much.If a person has heard words of [written] Torahthey enable him to hear [the oral] teachings of scribes.
--- 115:2 (head (15, 5))
HE: לִשְׁמֹר לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת כָּל הַמִּצְוָה הַזֹּאת – שֶׁתְּהֵא מִצְוָה קַלָּה חֲבִיבָה עָלֶיךָ כְּמִצְוָה חֲמוּרָה.
EN: “To preserve and to enact all of this commandment” (Dt.15:5)—may the simplest commandmentbe as dear to youas the most arduous commandment.320Cf. M. Avot 2:1. See also Pisqa’ 79.1 and note thereto.

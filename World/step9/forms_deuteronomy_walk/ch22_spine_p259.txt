--- 259:1 (head (23, 16))
HE: (דברים כג טז) לֹא תַסְגִּיר עֶבֶד אֶל אֲדֹנָיו, מִכָּן אָמְרוּ: הַמּוֹכֵר עַבְדּוֹ לְגוֹיִם אוֹ לְחוּצָה לָאָרֶץ יָצָא בֶן חוֹרִים.
EN: Pisqa’ 259287H:257-258;JN2:188.1. “Do not return a slave to his master” (Dt.23:16).On this basis, they taught:one who sells his [Hebrew] slave to gentilesor [to an owner] beyond the Land—the slave goes forth a free man.
--- 259:2 (head (23, 16))
HE: אֲשֶׁר יִנָּצֵל אֵלֶיךָ מֵעִם אֲדֹנָיו, לְרַבּוֹת גֵּר תּוֹשָׁב.
EN: “When he has sought refuge with you from his master” (Dt .23:16)—this includes as well a resident alien.
--- 259:3 (head (23, 16))
HE: (דברים כג יז) עִמְּךָ יֵשֵׁב, וְלֹא בָּעִיר עַצְמוֹ. בְּקִרְבְּךָ, וְלֹא בַּסְּפָר.
EN: “He may live with you” (Dt.23:17)—but not in the city itself.“In your midst” (Dt.23:17)—but not on the border lands.
--- 259:4 (head (23, 16))
HE: בַּמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר יִבְחָר, בַּמָּקוֹם שֶׁפַּרְנָסָתוֹ יוֹצְאָה.
EN: “In the place he shall choose” Dt.23:17)—in the place where he earns his living.
--- 259:5 (head (23, 16))
HE: בְּאַחַד שְׁעָרֶיךָ, בִּשְׁעָרֶיךָ וְלֹא בִּירוּשָׁלִַם. כְּשֶׁהוּא אוֹמֵר בְּאַחַד שְׁעָרֶיךָ שֶׁלֹּא יְהֵא גּוֹלֶה מֵעִיר לְעִיר.
EN: “In one of your gates” (Dt.23:17)—he may live in [any] of your gates,but not in Jerusalem,[which falls outside the tribal inheritances]. 288Cf. T. Neg. 6:2 in re: the incorrigible son (Dt. 21:18ff., Pisqa’ 218.4), whose trial and execution may occur “anywhere but Jerusalem, to publicize that it belongs to all Israel [and not to any one tribe].”2. When He says:“In one of your gates” (Dt.23:17)—this implies that he shouldn’t wander from city to city.
--- 259:6 (head (23, 16))
HE: בַּטּוֹב לוֹ, מִנָּוֶה הָרָע לְנָוֶה הַיָּפֶה.
EN: “If he is doing well” (Dt.23:17)—that is, if he moves from a poor area to a fine area.
--- 259:7 (head (23, 16))
HE: לֹא תּוֹנֶנּוּ, זוֹ הוֹנָאַת דְּבָרִים.
EN: 3. “Do not oppress him” (Dt.23:17)—with verbal oppression, in particular.289Cf. M. BM. 4:10, in re: verbal oppression of the resident alien and the child of converts: “A person shall not say to him: Recall the deeds of your ancestors!” See also T. BM. 3:25 and Mechilta Ishmael, neziqin, 18.
--- 259:8 (head (23, 16))
HE: דָּבָר אַחֵר: לֹא תַסְגִּיר עֶבֶד אֶל אֲדֹנָיו, בְּגוֹי שֶׁנִּצַּל מֵעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה הַכָּתוּב מְדַבֵּר.
EN: Another word:“Do not return a slave to his master” (Dt.23:16)—this verse concerns a gentile escaping enslavement to a foreign cult.

--- 155:1 (head (17, 12))
HE: (דברים יז יב) וְהָאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר יַעֲשֶׂה בְזָדוֹן, אֲשֶׁר לֹא שָׁמַע מִפִּי בֵּית דִּין וּמוֹרֶה.
EN: Pisqa’ 15571H:190; JN2:21-221. “As for the man who acts in defiance” (Dt.17:12).This refers to one whodoes not submit to the courtand insists upon his teaching.
--- 155:2 (head (17, 12))
HE: אֲשֶׁר יַעֲשֶׂה – עַל מַעֲשֶׂה הוּא חַיָּב וְאֵינוֹ חַיָּב עַל הוֹרָיָה.
EN: “Who acts” (Dt.17;12)—he is held liable for an actual act,but he is not held liable for merely teaching.
--- 155:3 (head (17, 12))
HE: בְזָדוֹן – עַל זָדוֹן הוּא חַיָּב וְאֵינוֹ חַיָּב עַל שְׁגָגָה.
EN: “Who acts defiantly” (Dt.17:12)—he is held liable for teaching defiantly,but he is not held liable for inadvertent error.
--- 155:4 (head (17, 12))
HE: לְבִלְתִּי שְׁמֹעַ – וְלֹא שׁוֹמֵעַ מִפִּי שׁוֹמֵעַ.
EN: “By not listening” (Dt.17:12)—but not one who violates a teaching heard at second-hand.
--- 155:5 (head (17, 12))
HE: אֶל הַכֹּהֵן הָעֹמֵד לְשָׁרֵת, מַגִּיד שֶׁאֵין שֵׁרוּת כָּשֵׁר אֶלָּא מֵעוֹמֵד. הָא אִם יָשַׁב וְעָבַד – עֲבוֹדָתוֹ פְּסוּלָה.
EN: “To the Priest who stands there in service” (Dt.17:12)—this indicates that proper serviceis performed only while standing.Therefore, if he served while seated,his service is disqualified. 72Sifre Nu. 39; cf. Pisqa’ 167.1.
--- 155:6 (head (17, 12))
HE: אוֹ אֶל הַשּׁוֹפֵט – זֶהוּ שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ: אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵין בּוֹ כֹּהֲנִים וּלְוִיִּם כָּשֵׁר.
EN: “Or to the justice” (Dt.17:12)—as we have taught:73At Pisqa’ 153.1.even though a court has no Priests or Levites,it is qualified.
--- 155:7 (head (17, 12))
HE: וּמֵת הָאִישׁ – בִּסְתָם מִיתָה הָאֲמוּרָה בַּתּוֹרָה, בְּחֶנֶק.
EN: “Then that man shall die” (Dt.17:12)—Where the mode of execution is unspecified in the Torah,we assume strangulation.74//Mech ISH, neziqin, 5, s.v., khol mitah ha’amurah stam. Cf, Pisqa’ 273.1.
--- 155:8 (head (17, 12))
HE: הַהוּא – לֹא אָנוּס וְלֹא שׁוֹגֵג וְלֹא מֻטְעֶה.
EN: “That man” (Dt.17:12)—but not if he is coerced  [into apostasy by a threat to his life],nor one who acted unintentionally,nor one who was misled [out of ignorance].75Cf. Pisqa’ 149.4.
--- 155:9 (head (17, 12))
HE: וּבִעַרְתָּ הָרָע מִיִּשְׂרָאֵל – בַּעֵר עוֹשֵׂי הָרָעוֹת מִיִּשְׂרָאֵל!
EN: “And you must expunge this evil from Israel” (Dt.17:12)—that is, expunge the evil-doers from Israel.76This mandate for expunging evil is appears commonly in Sifre, attached to several verses in Deuteronomy. Cf. Pisqa’ot 140.1 (Dt.22:1) and Dt.22:2 (141.1).

--- 264:3 (head (23, 22))
HE: לֹא תְאַחֵר לְשַׁלְּמוֹ, הוּא וְלֹא חֲלוּפָיו.
EN: “Ýou must not delay to pay it” (Dt.23:22)—[you must offer] the avowed animal, and not a substitute (Lv.27:10).
--- 264:4 (head (23, 22))
HE: כִּי דָרֹשׁ יִדְרְשֶׁנּוּ, אֵלּוּ חַטָּאוֹת וַאֲשָׁמוֹת.
EN: “For HASHEM your God will surely seek it out from you” (Dt.23:22)—It refers to Purification-offerings and Guilt-offerings[offered in atonement for a delay in the Vow-offering].

--- 264:6 (head (23, 22))
HE: מֵעִמָּךְ, זֶה לֶקֶט שִׁכְחָה וּפֵאָה.
EN: “From you” (Dt.23:22)—this refers to Gleanings, the Forgotten-sheaf, and the Corner–offering,[all of which come from privately-owned fields].
--- 264:7 (head (23, 22))
HE: וְהָיָה בְךָ חֵטְא, בְּךָ חֵטְא וְלֹא בְּקָרְבָּנְךָ חֵטְא.
EN: “And the sin will be yours” (Dt.23:22)—the sin will be yours, but there will be no flaw in your sacrifice,[even though it is delayed].

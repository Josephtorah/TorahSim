--- 97:1 (head (14, 2))
HE: (דברים יד ב) כִּי עַם קָדוֹשׁ אַתָּה לַה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ, קַדֵּשׁ אֶת עַצְמְךָ. כִּי עַם קָדוֹשׁ אַתָּה לַה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ – קְדֻשָּׁה שֶׁהִיא עָלֶיךָ, הִיא גָּרְמָה לָךְ.
EN: Pisqa’ 97214H:145; JN1:255.1. “For you are a people consecrated (qadosh) to HASHEM your God” (Dt.14:2)—[therefore,] consecrate (qadesh) yourself.“For you are a people consecrated” (Dt.14:2)—the holiness (qedushah) upon youhas caused [your consecration].
--- 97:2 (head (14, 2))
HE: דָּבָר אַחֵר: לֹא תִגְרֹם לְעַם אַחֵר לִהְיוֹת קָדוֹשׁ כַּיּוֹצֵא בְּךָ.
EN: Another word:[For you are a people consecrated” (Dt.14:2)—]do not cause another peopleto be consecrated like yourself!
--- 97:3 (head (14, 2))
HE: וּבְךָ בָּחַר ה׳, (תהלים קלה ד) ״כִּי יַעֲקֹב בָּחַר לוֹ יָהּ״. לִהְיוֹת לוֹ לְעַם סְגֻלָּה – (תהלים קלה ד) ״יִשְׂרָאֵל לִסְגֻלָּתוֹ״.
EN: “And HASHEM has chosen you” (Dt.14:2),[as He says:]“For Jacob was chosen by HASHEM” (Ps.135:4)—to be His cherished people,[as He says:]“Israel, as His cherished people” (Ps.135:4).215F:158, n. 14 consider this material a marginal insertion.
--- 97:4 (head (14, 2))
HE: וּבְךָ בָּחַר ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ לִהְיוֹת לוֹ לְעַם סְגֻלָּה מִכֹּל הָעַמִּים, מְלַמֵּד שֶׁכָּל אֶחָד וְאֶחָד חָבִיב לִפְנֵי הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא מִכָּל אֻמּוֹת הָעוֹלָם.
EN: “And HASHEM has chosen you to be His cherished people from among all the peoples” (Dt.14:2)this you [in the singular (bekhah)] teaches that each and every [Israelite]is more beloved before the Holy One,than all the nations of the world.
--- 97:5 (head (14, 2))
HE: יָכוֹל אַף מֵאָבוֹת הָרִאשׁוֹנִים? תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: מִכֹּל הָעַמִּים אֲשֶׁר עַל פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה, אֱמֹר מֵעַתָּה: מִשֶּׁלְּפָנָיו וּמִשֶּׁלְּאַחֲרָיו, וְלֹא מֵאָבוֹת הָרִאשׁוֹנִים!
EN: Is it possible to say that[each Israelite] is even more [beloved]than the founding Patriarchs [of Israel]?The Teaching states:“From among all the peoples who inhabit the earth” (Dt.14:2)—from this verse, you may conclude that[Israel is] most [beloved] among all its predecessorsand most [beloved] among all its successors,but not more than the founding Patriarchs!

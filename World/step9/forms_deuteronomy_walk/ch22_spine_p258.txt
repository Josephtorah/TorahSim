--- 258:1 (head (23, 15))
HE: (דברים כג טו) כִּי ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ מִתְהַלֵּךְ בְּקֶרֶב מַחֲנֶיךָ, מִכָּן אָמְרוּ לֹא יִקְרָא אָדָם אֶת שְׁמַע בְּצַד מִשְׁרָה שֶׁל כּוֹבְסִים, וְלֹא יִכָּנֵס לֹא לַמֶּרְחָץ וְלֹא לַבֻּרְסְקִי וּסְפָרִים וּתְפִלִּין בְּיָדוֹ.
EN: Pisqa’ 258280H:257;JN2:187.1. “Because HASHEM your God moves about in the midst of your camp” (Dt.23:15).On this basis they taught:A person should not recite the “Listen, Israel” (Dt.6:4 ff.)next to the soaking-vat of launderers[because of the smell].281Cf. M. Ber. 3:5.And: one should not enter a bath-house or a tannerywith scriptural scrolls or prayer-amulets in his hands.
--- 258:2 (head (23, 15))
HE: לְהַצִּילְךָ וְלָתֵת אוֹיְבֶיךָ לְפָנֶיךָ, אִם עָשִׂיתָ כָּל הָאָמוּר בָּעִנְיָן לְסוֹף שֶׁהוּא מַצִּילְךָ וְנוֹתֵן אֶת אוֹיְבֶיךָ לְפָנֶיךָ.
EN: 2. “To spare you and to place your enemies before you” (Dt.23:15).If you have done allthat is stated in this matter,He will in the end spare youand place your enemies before you.282This trope appears as well at Pisqa’ 200.3.
--- 258:3 (head (23, 15))

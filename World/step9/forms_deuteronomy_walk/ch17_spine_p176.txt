--- 176:1 (head (18, 16))
HE: (דברים יח טז) כְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר שָׁאַלְתָּ מֵעִם ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ בְּחֹרֵב בָּזוֹ זָכוּ שֶׁיַּעַמְדוּ לָהֶם נְבִיאִים: לֹא אוֹסִיף לִשְׁמֹעַ אֶת קוֹל ה׳ אֱלֹהַי.
EN: Pisqa’ 176194H:202; JN2:54.1. “According to all you asked from HASHEM my God at Horeb” (Dt.18:16)—with this requestthey became worthy of having prophets arise for them,[as it is said:]“I wish no longer to hear the voice of HASHEM my God!” (Dt.18:16)—[but, rather, His message through prophecy]!
--- 176:2 (head (18, 16))
HE: (דברים יח יז) וַיֹּאמֶר ה׳ אֵלָי הֵיטִיבוּ אֲשֶׁר דִּבֵּרוּ – כִּוְּנוּ לְדַעְתִּי, נָבִיא אָקִים לָהֶם. הָא לָמַדְתָּ שֶׁבִּשְׂכַר יִרְאָה שֶׁיָּרְאוּ – זָכוּ שֶׁיַּעַמְדוּ לָהֶם נְבִיאִים.
EN: 2. “Then HASHEM said to me: their words are indeed well-chosen” (Dt.18:17)—sharing, [as they do,] My opinion!“I will raise up for them a prophet” (Dt.18:18)—indeed, you learn from this that,as a reward for their awe,they became worthy of having prophets arise for them.195//Mechilta Ishmael, bakhodesh, 9. Cf. Mechilta Shimon-Nelson, bakhodesh, p.253.
--- 176:3 (head (18, 16))
HE: (דברים יח יח) וְנָתַתִּי דְבָרַי בְּפִיו – דְּבָרַי בְּפִיו אֲנִי נוֹתֵן, אֲבָל אֵינִי מְדַבֵּר עִמּוֹ פָּנִים בְּפָנִים. דָּבָר אַחֵר: וְנָתַתִּי דְבָרַי בְּפִיו, מִכָּן וָאֵילָךְ הֱוֵי יוֹדֵעַ הֵיאַךְ רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ נִתֶּנֶת בְּפִי הַנְּבִיאִים.
EN: “And I shall place my words in his mouth” (Dt.18:18)—My words will I place in his mouth,but I will not speak to him face to face[as I do with Moses].196This material, lacking in most sources for Sifre, has a loose parallel at SZ, beha`alotecha, 11:8.
--- 176:4 (head (18, 16))
HE: וְדִבֶּר אֲלֵיהֶם – שֶׁלֹּא לְהוֹשִׁיב אֶת הַתּוּרְגְּמָן.
EN: 3. Another word:“And I shall place my words in his mouth” (Dt.18:18)—from now on, be aware thatthe Spirit of the Holy One is placed in the mouths of the prophets.“And he will declare to them” (Dt.18:18)—with no need to appoint a translator.197Heb: turgeman.
--- 176:5 (head (18, 16))
HE: אֵת כָּל אֲשֶׁר אֲצַוֶּנּוּ – אוֹמֵר עַל רִאשׁוֹן רִאשׁוֹן וְעַל אַחֲרוֹן אַחֲרוֹן.
EN: “Whatever I shall command him” (Dt.18:18)—he will explain primary points first,And secondary points later.198//M. Av.5:7. The prophet will anticipate the method of the rabbinic disciple.

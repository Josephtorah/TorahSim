EN: 3. “He guarded him, as the pupil of His eye” (Dt.32:10)—even had the Blessed Holy Onerequested Abraham’s very eyeball,he would have given it to Him!And not only his eyeball—but even his soul, which he loved most of all.As it is stated:“Take, now, your son,your special one (yakhid)—namely, Isaac” (Gn.22:2).Now, don’t we all know that Isaacwas his special son?Rather, this refers to the soul,as it is said:“Spare my soul (nafshi)from the sword,My special one (yekhidati) from the clutches of the dog” (Ps.22:21).

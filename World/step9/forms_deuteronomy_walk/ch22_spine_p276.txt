EN: Pisqa’ 276381H:269;JN2:221.1. “Should you ever lend on pledge to your kinsman” (Dt.24:10).I might infer only that[the subject here is] a lender.On what basis do I include under this rule[other kinds of loans,]such as a worker’s back-wages,or a shop-keeper’s extension of credit [to a customer]?The Teaching states:“A loan secured by a pledge of any kind” (Dt.24:10).

--- 211:1 (head (21, 10))
HE: (דברים כא י) כִּי תֵצֵא לַמִּלְחָמָה – בְּמִלְחֶמֶת הָרְשׁוּת הַכָּתוּב מְדַבֵּר.
EN: Pisqa’ 2111H:224; JN2:111-112.1. “When you go out to battle” (Dt.21:10)—the verse speaks of an optional war.2For the various terms for divinely sanctioned war, see Pisqa’ot 190.4, 198.2
--- 211:2 (head (21, 10))
HE: עַל אֹיְבֶיךָ – כְּנֶגֶד אוֹיְבֶיךָ.
EN: “Upon your enemies” (Dt.21:10)—that is, against your enemies,[but not in an alliance with them].
--- 211:3 (head (21, 10))
HE: וּנְתָנוֹ ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ בְּיָדֶךָ, אִם עָשִׂיתָ כָּל הָאָמוּר בָּעִנְיָן – סוֹף שֶׁה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ נוֹתְנוֹ בְּיָדֶךָ.
EN: “And HASHEM your God has delivered him into your hands” (Dt.21:10)—if you have performed all that is stated here,in the end, HASHEM your God will deliver himinto your hands!
--- 211:4 (head (21, 10))
HE: וְשָׁבִיתָ שִׁבְיוֹ – לְרַבּוֹת כְּנַעֲנִים שֶׁבְּתוֹכָהּ.
EN: 2. “And you take him captive” (Dt.21:10)—including any Canaanites that might be within.
--- 211:5 (head (21, 10))
HE: (דברים כא יא) וְרָאִיתָ בַּשִּׁבְיָה – בִּשְׁעַת שִׁבְיָה.
EN: “And you see among the captured” (Dt.21:11)—that is, during her captivity.
--- 211:6 (head (21, 10))
HE: אֵשֶׁת – וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁהִיא אֵשֶׁת אִישׁ.
EN: “A woman” (Dt.21:11)—even though she is a married woman.
--- 211:7 (head (21, 10))
HE: יְפַת תֹּאַר, אֵין לִי אֶלָּא בִּזְמַן שֶׁהִיא נָאָה, מִנַּיִן אֲפִילּוּ הִיא כְּעוּרָה? – תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: וְחָשַׁקְתָּ בָהּ, אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵינָהּ יְפַת תֹּאַר.
EN: “In fine shape” (Dt.21:11)—I infer from this only thatshe may be taken [as a war-bride]only as long as she is a beauty.How do I know thatshe may be taken even if she is ugly?The Teaching states:“And you desire her” (Dt.21:11)—even though she’s not in fine shape.
--- 211:8 (head (21, 10))
HE: וְלָקַחְתָּ לְךָ לְאִשָּׁה, שֶׁלֹּא תֹּאמַר ׳הֲרֵי זוֹ לְאַבָּה׳, ׳הֲרֵי זוֹ לְאָחִי׳.
EN: “Then you may take her as your wife” (Dt.21:11)—lest you say:this one’s for my father, and that one’s for my brother!

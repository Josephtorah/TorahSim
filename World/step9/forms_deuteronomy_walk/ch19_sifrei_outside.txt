## Sifrei 80:3 EN
“And you shall disinherit them and settle in their land” (Dt.12:29)—inferring from what is stated elsewhere:“And you shall disinherit them and settle in their cities and houses” (Dt.19:1),is it possible to say thatyou may not add to the [existing] structures?The Teaching states:“And you shall disinherit them and settle in their land” (Dt.12:29)—any place you wish to build, build!

## Sifrei 293:2 EN

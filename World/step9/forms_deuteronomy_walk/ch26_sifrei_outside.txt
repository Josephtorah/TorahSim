## Sifrei 303:17 EN
“Nor have I offered any of it to the dead” (Dt.26:14)—I didn’t purchase with [its proceeds] a coffin or shrouds for a corpse:69//M.MS.5:12.Words of R. Eliezer.Said to him R.

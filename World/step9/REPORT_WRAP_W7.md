## The headline: THE SEVENTEENTH OF TAMMUZ COMPUTED ON THE TAPE

The first ascent is submitted on day 7 (24:16: the cloud six days, the call on the seventh) and sets the tablets on a TIMER of
forty days (24:18). The timer fires on day 47. Taanit 28b computes the day of the breaking from these two numbers — the seventh
of Sivan plus forty is the seventeenth of Tammuz — and Mishnah Taanit 4:6 lists the breaking first among the five things of that
day. The tape's timer fired on the day the tradition names, from the ink's own two numbers, and the breaking (32:19) is
submitted on that day as an honest silence: the daemon writes nothing on it, because the breaking is the history's act and its
ratification ("may your strength be firm that you broke," Yevamot 62a) is a verdict on Moses, not a ledger entry. The second
ascent (day 48) sets the second tablets and the radiant face on the same forty-day timer; both fire on day 88. This is the first
calendar date the simulator computed rather than received — the design thread's clock specification (ARCHITECTURE/THE_CLOCK.md)
would make such a boundary a calendar tick on an institution; here it is still a timer in days, and named as such.

- **THE DEATH TIERS AS A VERDICT TABLE.** The calf chapter's three death-verbs — the sword (32:27), the plague (32:35), the

  ordinances engine; the court that exempted the bower bears sin (Horayot 1:3).

  by the vow-annulment law (Berakhot 32a, Rava).

## FINDINGS

1. **THE PROMISES ARE LEDGER ENTRIES.** The five expressions of 6:6-8 are five HEAVEN entries opened at one verse and
   closed one by one where the ink itself says each was kept — 12:51, 14:30, 15:13, 19:8 — with the fifth open at the
   three books' end beside Amalek's blotting, the healer's condition, the treasure's offer and "now you will see": the
   ledger holds the prophecies against the run, which is the effects law's stated target.
2. **THE TRADITION'S CHRONOLOGY IS DATA THE ENGINE GRADES.** The shelf's day-tables (Kiddushin 38a, Sotah 12b, Shabbat
   86b-87b) are calendar rows with their arms; the engine's weekday from day one of creation reproduces the shelf's
   SPACING (Nisan full, Iyar deficient — CS2) and the giving on the tape's own ascent day (CS3), and DIVERGES on the
   ANCHOR (CS1, CS4: the exodus on the second day of the week against the shelf's fifth) — twenty-four centuries of
   modeled intercalation are not the tradition's, said as a checkpoint. The ark falls a day past the shelf's sixth of
   Sivan under the Calendar's month key, and the 88-day reading lands on the same seventh: the shelf's "three months"
   from the seventh of Adar is not the Calendar's three months (CS0).
3. **THE COVENANT'S DAEMON RUNS ON EXODUS BIRTHS.** Moses' and Gershom's births fire Genesis 17's eighth-day timer by the
   statute's own recorded run; both debits stay OPEN — Moses' with Sotah 12a:17's "born circumcised" beside it,
   Gershom's because 4:25 names "her son" and the registry holds the identity uncertain (O10's sitting). Nedarim
   31b-32a's argument over Moses' laxity is an open debit on a ledger.
4. **THE PLUNDER IS THE LABOR'S WAGE.** 12:36's emptying of Egypt is a TRANSFER whose ground the tradition states as a
   ledger claim (Sanhedrin 91a:12 — the wage of six hundred thousand for four hundred and thirty years, both numbers the
   ink's own: 12:37, 12:40), and Berakhot 9a-b reads the "please" of 11:2 as the closing of Genesis 15:14's open promise —
   S2's write, closed here.
5. **TEN STRUCK, FOUR REMOVED, SIX OPEN.** The plagues on Egypt's ledger by name (Avot 5:4's count graded on the entries,
   CS8), closed only where the ink narrates a removal — the four Pharaoh entreated for; the sea's fifty derived by the
   finger and the hand (Mekhilta Beshalach ch.31 row 2), one act on the tape; the hardening's thirteen narrative seats
   with the agent read off each clause, the first divine hand at 9:12.
6. **THE REGISTER TEST HAS A BLIND SPOT.** "Then he SANG" (15:1) is a yiqtol with "then" — the narrative past in another
   form — and the wayyiqtol window stays inside the chapter, so the song's act was cited at Miriam's answer (15:21) to
   ride the tape; 14:2's "turn back and camp" (a command inside a speech) is off the tape by the same test. Both are the
   test's own shape, not the ink's silence — named for the stitcher's next design pass.
7. **A HAND-MODEL MUST KNOW THE OP CLASS OF A REUSED EFFECT.** The one scene miss (16 against 17 open entries) came from
   `cry_heard`'s HEAVEN op, not consulted when the model counted the opens — O7's write-time lesson extended.
8. **THE PARSER'S TABLE LACKED THE CONSTRUCT NUMERALS.** The stitcher's ink check found "seven of days" and "three of
   days" unparsed and, once the forms were added, read "the TEN words" at 34:28 into a standing row — the table is the
   ink's, and the check works as written.

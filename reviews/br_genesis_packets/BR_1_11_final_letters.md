## Plain English summary

Five Hebrew letters have special **final shapes** when they end a word:  
**mem, nun, tsadi, peh, kaf** — remembered as **man-tsepakh** / *man-tse-PAKH*.

One view: the **seers / prophets** (*tzofim*) said these forms are **halakhah to Moses from Sinai** (received law).

Story: on a rainy day sages did not come to the study house; children ran a session and asked why those letters are doubled (medial + final forms). Their midrash: the pairs mean **handoff**—from God’s **utterance** to Moses’ utterance, from the **Faithful** God to faithful Moses, from **Righteous** to righteous, from **mouth** to mouth, from **palm** (hand) to palm.

Later those children grew into great sages (some say Eliezer, Yehoshua, Akiva).

## Plain English summary

Why does God tell Israel **what was created on day one, day two,** and so on?

So that **idolaters** (who serve stars and signs of the sky) cannot taunt Israel: “You are only a nation of thieves (of land).”  
Israel can answer: **you** hold land by conquest too—and the whole world belongs to God. When He wills, He gives it and takes it. Revealing the creation order shows the world is **His**, and the land gift is His to assign.

## 1. Retell

**Transmitter:** Rabbi Yehoshua of Sikhnin in the name of Rabbi Levi.

Opens with Psalm 111:6.  
**Question:** Why reveal day-by-day creation?  
**Answer:** Because of idolaters who would mock Israel as land-thieves. Israel answers: you also hold seized land; the world is God’s; He gives and takes. That is “to give them the heritage of nations”—and He told them the works across the generations.

## Plain English summary (read this first)

**Rabbi Berekhiah** opens in the name of **Rabbi Yehudah bar Simon** with Psalm 33:6:

> “By the word of the Lord the heavens were made…”

Teaching:

1. **How God creates:** Not by **toil**, not by **hard labor** — the Holy One created His world **by the word of the Lord**. The heavens were already “made” that way (Psalm).

2. **How the light line is written:** Scripture does **not** say a form that would sound like “and light **will be**…” — it says **“and there was light”** (*va-yehi or*). Read here as: **it already was** (instant / already accomplished by the word).

**One idea:** Creation (and light) is **speech-effect**, not sweat-effect; the verb form backs **already-done**, not long struggle.

## 1. Retell (plain English)

**Opener (petihah):** Rabbi Berekhiah in the name of Rabbi Yehudah bar Simon → Psalm 33:6.

**Claim A — Mode of creation**  
God did **not** create the world by human-style **toil** or **labor**.  
He created it **by the word of the Lord**.  
Proof-texture: Psalm already says the heavens **were made** by that word.

**Claim B — Light verb**  
On Genesis 1:3’s outcome line:  
- Not written: something like **“and light will be”** (*ve-hayah or*)  
- Written: **“and there was light”** (*va-yehi or*)  
- Midrash punchline: **it already was** (*kevar hayah*).

So the same unit binds: **speech-only creation** + **instant/already light**.

## Plain English summary (read this first)

A **long multi-voice unit** on naming and separating light.

### A — Primordial light not the sun; stored for the future
Wonder: God called light “Day”—are light and day simply the same?  
Teaching: The light made in the six days **cannot** simply serve as ordinary daytime light (it would outshine/dim the sun’s wheel) and was **not** made to light the night.  
**Where is it?** **Stored away** (*nignazah*), prepared for the **righteous in the time to come** (Isaiah 30:26—multiplied future light).

### B — “Seven days” of light (Isaiah puzzle)
Isaiah’s “light of the seven days”—but luminaries appear on day four (only three “light days” before)?  
- Like a man who says he will provide for **seven days of a wedding feast** (whole festival span).  
- **Rabbi Nehemiah:** the **seven days of mourning for Methuselah** the righteous, when God poured light for them.

### C — Blessing on a lamp
**Rabbi Ze’ira** (son of Abbahu) in Caesarea: One only blesses over a lamp when one can **benefit from its light**—derived from **saw** then **separated** (use/recognize before ritual).

### D — What was “separated”?
- **Yehudah bar Simon:** Separated it **for Himself**.  
- **Rabbis:** Separated it for the **righteous in the future**—like a king who sets aside a fine portion for his son.  
- **Yohanan & Resh Lakish** (via Berekhiah): **Real domain split**—king has two commanders fighting over day rule; king assigns: you rule day, you rule night. So light’s territory = day; darkness’s = night (Job 38:12—do you know the dawn’s place?).

### E — Peace after assign; Name only on good
- **Tanhuma:** Isaiah 45:7 forms light, creates darkness, **makes peace**—once created, peace is made between them.  
- **Eleazar:** God never attaches **His name** to evil, only to good:  
  “**God** called the light Day” — but for darkness it is **not** written “**God** called the night,” only “He called the night…”

**One idea:** Primordial light is **reserved capital** for the future righteous; separation is **jurisdiction**; divine naming prefers **good** side of the polarity.

1. Primordial light ≠ ordinary day/night utility → **stored for future righteous**.

3. Halakhic midrash: bless lamp only when light is usable (*saw* → *separated*).  
4. Separation targets: **Himself** / **future righteous** / **day-night jurisdictions**.  
5. After split: **peace** between light and dark.  
6. Naming asymmetry: **Elohim** ties to light/day, not written on darkness/night the same way.

## Plain English summary

From the start of creation, God’s **first word is truth**.  
Genesis opens “In the beginning **God created**” (singular verb)—and Jeremiah calls the Lord “God of truth.”  
Creatures justify God’s decrees and accept them in faith.  
No one can say **two powers** made the world: the text does **not** say “gods spoke / said / created” in the plural for the create-verb pattern—it says **God** (with singular verbs) **created / said**.

## 1. Retell

**Rabbi Yitzhak** opens Psalm 119:160: from the beginning of the world’s creation, “Your first word is truth”—linked to Genesis 1:1 and Jeremiah 10:10.

Judgments God decrees: creatures justify them and accept in faith.

Grammar polemic: not “gods spoke / said / created” as multi-agent openers—**God said**, **God created** (singular).

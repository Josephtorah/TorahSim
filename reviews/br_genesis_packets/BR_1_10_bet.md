## Plain English summary (read this first)

This midrash asks: **why does the Torah start creation with the letter B (Bet), not A (Alef)?**

Main answer: the letter Bet is shaped like a box **open only forward**. So when you study creation, you have **no right** to demand answers about what is **above, below, before, or behind** the created world — only from creation **forward**.

Other rabbis add: two worlds; blessing vs curse; the letter points to God; the letter A waits until Sinai for “I am the Lord your God.”

## 1. Retell (plain English)

**Who is speaking (transmission chain):**  
Rabbi Yonah teaches **in the name of** Rabbi Levi (main line). Then Bar Kappara; Rabbi Yehudah ben Pazi; more “another interpretation”s; Rabbi Eleazar bar Hanina in the name of Rabbi Aha; Rabbi Hoshayah.

**Main line (Levi, via Yonah):**  
Why was the world created with **Bet**?  
Because Bet is **closed on all sides and open in front**.  
So you have **no permission** to say: what is **below**, **above**, **before**, **behind** — only from the day the world was created **and onward**.

**Bar Kappara** quotes Deuteronomy 4:32 (free gloss):  
Ask about the former days, from the day God created humans on the earth…  
— You may seek **from that day**; you may **not** seek earlier.  
From one end of heaven to the other you may inquire; **not** beyond.

**Yehudah ben Pazi** (on Work of Creation, with Bar Kappara): why Bet? To teach **two worlds** — this world and the world to come.

**Other readings of why Bet / not Alef:**

- Bet sounds like **blessing**; Alef can be linked to **curse** language — don’t open the world on curse.  
- Don’t give scoffers a line: “How can a world stand if it was created with curse-words?” God chooses **blessing** language so the world may stand.  
- Bet has **two little points** (like thorns): one points up → “the One above made me”; one points back → “the Lord is His name.”

**Alef’s complaint:** For **twenty-six generations** Alef argues before God’s throne: I am first of the letters — why didn’t You create with me?  
God answers: the world was created only for the sake of **Torah** (Proverbs: by wisdom God founded the earth). Tomorrow at Sinai I will begin **with you** — *Anokhi*, “I am the Lord your God.”

**Hoshayah:** Alef’s name is linked to “a thousand” — a word commanded for a thousand generations (play on Psalm 105:8).

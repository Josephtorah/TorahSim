## Plain English summary (read this first)

Rabbi Shimon ben Lakish reads Genesis 1:2 as a map of **Israel’s exiles** (forced dispersions under foreign empires)—not only of early people (that was 2:3).

| Creation phrase (free English) | Midrash: “this is…” |
|--------------------------------|---------------------|
| Earth was *tohu* (formless waste) | **Exile of Babylon** |
| And *vohu* (void) | **Exile of Media** (Persia; wordplay with “hurried / alarmed”) |
| Darkness | **Exile of Greece** — darkened Israel’s eyes with decrees (write on the ox’s horn: you have no share in the God of Israel) |
| On the face of the deep | **Exile of the wicked empire** (Rome / “evil kingdom”) — unfathomable like the deep |
| Spirit of God hovering | **Spirit of the King Messiah** (Isaiah 11:2) |
| Hovering on the waters | Comes in the merit of **repentance**, which is compared to **water** (Lamentations 2:19: pour out your heart like water) |

Then a famous side story: Ben Zoma gazes into the Work of Creation—upper and lower waters only two or three finger-widths apart; “hovering” like a bird whose wings touch and do not touch. Rabbi Yehoshua says Ben Zoma is already “gone”; soon Ben Zoma dies.

**One idea:** Same Written line can be a **history of empires and hope**, not only of Adam→Abraham or earth’s complaint.

## 1. Retell (plain English)

### Main map — Rabbi Shimon ben Lakish

He “solves” Genesis 1:2 **as exiles**:

1. ***Tohu*** → Babylonian exile (links a Jeremiah vision of the land as waste).  
2. ***Vohu*** → Median exile — midrash ties to Esther 6:14 language of **hurrying / alarming** (*bahalu* / they hurried to bring Haman)—sound-play near *vohu*.  
3. ***Darkness*** → Greek exile: decrees that darken Israel’s eyes; force them to write that they have no portion in the God of Israel.  
4. ***Face of the deep*** → wicked empire’s exile: no bottom to their cruelty, like the unfathomable deep.  
5. ***Spirit of God hovering*** → spirit of the **King Messiah** (Isaiah 11:2).  
6. **On what merit does it approach?** On the waters = on the merit of **repentance**, compared to water (pour out your heart like water).

### Coda — Ben Zoma and the hovering spirit

Rabbi Haggai in the name of Rabbi Pedat: a covenant with water (even in heat a breeze sweetens it).  

Story: Ben Zoma sits dazed. Rabbi Yehoshua greets him; no answer, then a rushed answer. “Where are you coming from?” — “From gazing.” He was contemplating the Work of Creation: between upper and lower waters only two or three fingers; Scripture says not “blowing” but **hovering**—like a bird fluttering, wings touching and not touching.  

Rabbi Yehoshua tells his students: Ben Zoma is gone. Within days, Ben Zoma is dead.

**Warning tone:** deep creation inquiry can unmoor the mind (fits chapter 1 honor / Bet caution).

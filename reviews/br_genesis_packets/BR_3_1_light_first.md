## Plain English summary (read this first)

Chapter 3 opens the next Written words after the long chapter-2 readings of 1:2:  
**“And God said, Let there be light.”**

Rabbi Yitzhak opens with Psalm 119:130: “The opening of Your words gives light; it gives understanding to the simple.”

Then a debate:

- **Rabbi Yehudah:** **Light was created first.**  
  Like a king who wants to build a palace in a dark place: he first lights lamps so he can set the foundations. So light first, then the build.

- **Rabbi Nehemiah:** **The world was created first.**  
  Like a king who builds a palace and **then** crowns it with lamps. So structure first, light as adornment.

Later voices return to the Psalm: the **opening of Your mouth** was light for us — and that is “God said, Let there be light.”

**One idea:** Light is either **construction lighting** (tool for the build) or **finishing illumination** (crown on the finished palace)—same verse, two build-order models.

## 1. Retell (plain English)

**Opener:** Rabbi Yitzhak on Psalm 119:130.

**Rabbi Yehudah:** Light first.  
King wants a palace; site is dark → lights lamps to know how to set the foundations.  
So **the light was created first**.

**Rabbi Nehemiah:** World first.  
King builds the palace, then adorns it with lamps.  
So **the world was created first** (light as crown, not bootstrap tool).

**Further teachers** (Pinchas, Yehudah bar Simon, Hanun in the name of Samuel bar Isaac):  
“The opening of Your words gives light to the simple” → the **opening of Your mouth** was light for us — and that is Genesis 1:3.

So the verse is also: **divine speech itself as illumination**.

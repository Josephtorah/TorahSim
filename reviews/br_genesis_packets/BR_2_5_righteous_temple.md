## Plain English summary (read this first)

Two teachers give two more maps of the same creation lines.

**Rabbi Abbahu:** From the start of the world, God **foresaw** deeds of the **righteous** and of the **wicked**.  
- Earth was *tohu va-vohu* → **deeds of the wicked**  
- “Let there be light” → **deeds of the righteous**  
Which does God prefer? When Scripture says He saw the light **that it was good**, that shows He desires the deeds of the righteous, not of the wicked. (Psalm 1:6: God knows the way of the righteous; the way of the wicked perishes.)

**Rabbi Hiyya the Great:** From the start, God foresaw the **Temple**:  
- “In the beginning God created” → Temple **built**  
- Earth was *tohu va-vohu* → Temple **destroyed** (same chaos phrase as in Jeremiah’s ruin vision)  
- “Let there be light” → Temple **built and finished in the future** (Isaiah: “Arise, shine, for your light has come…”)

**Chapter 2 closes** with moral and Temple timelines on the same few words—after people (2:3) and exiles (2:4).

## 1. Retell (plain English)

### A — Rabbi Abbahu (moral map)

From the beginning of the world’s creation, God foresaw deeds of righteous and wicked (Psalm 1:6).

| Creation line | = |
|---------------|---|
| Earth was *tohu va-vohu* | deeds of the **wicked** |
| “Let there be light” | deeds of the **righteous** |

Uncertainty: which does He want?  
Resolved by “He saw the light **that it was good**” → He wants righteous deeds, not wicked ones.

### B — Rabbi Hiyya Rabbah (Temple map)

From the beginning, God foresaw the Temple **built, destroyed, and built again**.

| Creation line | = |
|---------------|---|
| “In the beginning God created…” | Temple **built** (linked to Isaiah 51:16 planting heavens / founding earth / saying to Zion “you are My people”) |
| Earth was *tohu va-vohu* | Temple **destroyed** (Jeremiah 4:23 chaos vision) |
| “Let there be light” | Temple **built and completed in the future** (Isaiah 60:1, 60:20 — light on Zion while darkness covers the earth) |

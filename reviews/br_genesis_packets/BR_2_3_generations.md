## Plain English summary (read this first)

Rabbi Yehudah bar Simon **maps the creation lines onto people and generations**—not only onto sky and ground.

| Written phrase (free English) | Midrash: “this is…” |
|-------------------------------|---------------------|
| Earth was *tohu* (formless waste) | **Adam** the first human — was “for what? / as nothing” |
| And *vohu* (void) | **Cain** — who tried to push the world back to chaos |
| Darkness | Generation of **Enosh** — their deeds in the dark |
| On the face of the deep | Generation of the **Flood** — deeps burst open |
| Spirit of God hovering on the waters | God sends a **wind/spirit** over the earth after the Flood |
| “Let there be light” | **Abraham** — who **lights** from the east (wordplay on a verse) |
| Light called Day | **Jacob** |
| Darkness called Night | **Esau** |
| Evening / morning | Esau’s evening, Jacob’s morning |
| “One day” | A day that is the Lord’s (Zechariah) — or **Yom Kippur** (Day of Atonement) |

**One idea:** Creation language is also a **timeline of humanity** from Adam through the Flood to the fathers—and a turn from darkness to Abraham’s light.

## 1. Retell (plain English)

**Who speaks:** Rabbi Yehudah bar Simon.

He walks Genesis 1:2–5 phrase by phrase:

1. ***Tohu*** → Adam: “for what / as nothing” (early human emptiness).  
2. ***Vohu*** → Cain: tried to return the world to chaos (murder / undoing).  
3. ***Darkness*** → generation of Enosh — linked to Isaiah 29:15 (deeds in darkness: “who sees us?”).  
4. ***Face of the deep*** → Flood generation — Genesis 7:11, deeps burst open.  
5. ***Spirit of God hovering on the waters*** → after the Flood, Genesis 8:1, God passes a wind/spirit over the earth.  
6. God: “How long will the world run in gloom? Let light come.”  
   ***Let there be light*** → **Abraham**.  
   Isaiah 41:2: “who aroused righteousness from the east…” — midrash: do not read only “aroused” (*he’ir*) but **“lit / shone”** (*he’ir* wordplay) → Abraham as light.  
7. Light called Day → **Jacob**; darkness called Night → **Esau**.  
8. Evening → Esau; morning → Jacob (again).  
9. “One day” → Zechariah 14:7 (a day known to the Lord, not day/night as usual) — **or** the **Day of Atonement** given as a special “one day.”

## 2. The link map (Written → people)

```text
Gen 1:2  tohu          →  Adam
         vohu          →  Cain
         darkness      →  Enosh's generation
         deep          →  Flood generation
         spirit/waters →  wind after Flood (Gen 8:1)

Gen 1:3  "let there be light"  →  Abraham (lights from the east)

Gen 1:5  light = Day   →  Jacob
         dark = Night  →  Esau
         evening/morning → Esau / Jacob
         "one day"     →  Lord's day / Yom Kippur
```

**How linking works here:**  
Not “same phrase in Jeremiah” (that was 2:1).  
Here: **זה X** / *zeh X* / “**this** is X” — identification of each creation word with a **person or generation**, often with a proof verse.

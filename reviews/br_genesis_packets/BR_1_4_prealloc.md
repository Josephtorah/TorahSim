## Plain English summary (read this first)

Genesis 1:1 starts the world’s **runtime**.  
This midrash asks: **what was already there before that runtime?**

Answer: **six things** preceded creation of the world.  
They split into two tiers:

1. **Actually created** before the world: **Torah** and the **Throne of Glory**  
2. **Rose in thought** to be created later: the **Patriarchs**, **Israel**, the **Temple**, and the **name of the Messiah**

Others add **repentance** (*teshuvah*), argue whether Torah or Throne is older, claim **Israel’s thought** is first of all, or re-read *reishit* as **hallah / tithes / firstfruits** — so the world exists for those gifts.

**One line:** Creation week is not blank-slate; a **prior inventory** (document + destiny objects) is already staged.

## 1. Retell (plain English)

**Hook:** *Bereshit bara Elohim…*

**Main inventory — six things preceded creation of the world:**

| # | Item | Tier | Proof (free gloss of how midrash hooks it) |
|---|------|------|-----------------------------------------------|
| 1 | **Torah** | Created | Prov 8:22 — “the Lord acquired me as *reishit* of His way” |
| 2 | **Throne of Glory** | Created | Ps 93:2 — “Your throne is established from of old” |
| 3 | **Patriarchs** | In thought | Hos 9:10 — “like grapes in the wilderness…” (early love of the fathers) |
| 4 | **Israel** | In thought | Ps 74:2 — “Remember Your congregation, which You acquired of old” |
| 5 | **Temple** | In thought | Jer 17:12 — “Throne of glory, on high from the beginning…” |
| 6 | **Messiah’s name** | In thought | Ps 72:17 — “May his name endure forever…” |

**Rabbi Ahava son of Rabbi Zeira:**  
**Also repentance** — Ps 90:2–3: before mountains were born… “You return man to dust” / “return, children of man” (read as *teshuvah* staged early).

**Order debate (Torah vs Throne):**  
“I don’t know which preceded which.”  
**Rabbi Abba bar Kahana:** **Torah before Throne** — Prov 8:22 *reishit* comes before the “throne from of old” verse.

**Israel’s thought first (strong rival model):**  
**Rabbis Huna and Jeremiah in the name of Samuel bar Isaac:**  
**The thought of Israel preceded everything.**

**Parable:** A king married to a matron with no son yet. In the market he says: “Take ink and pens **for my son**.” People laugh — he has no son! Then: the king is a great astrologer; he would not order school supplies unless he **foresaw** a son.

**So:** if God had not foreseen that after **twenty-six generations** Israel would receive the Torah, He would not have written in the Torah “command the children of Israel / speak to the children of Israel.”

**Why was the world created at all? (competing merits)**

| Voice | For whose merit / for what |
|-------|----------------------------|
| **Rabbi Banai** | For the sake of **Torah** (Prov 3:19 — by wisdom God founded the earth) |
| **Rabbi Berekhiah** | For the sake of **Moses** (Deut 33:21 — “he saw a *reishit* for himself”) |
| **Rav Huna in the name of Rav Mattenah** | For three things: **ḥallah**, **tithes**, **firstfruits** — each called *reishit* in Torah (Num 15:20; Deut 18:4; Exod 23:19) |

So *Bereshit bara Elohim* can be midrashically read as: the world exists for those **first-portion** commandments.

## Plain English summary (read this first)

House of Shammai and House of Hillel disagree:

- **Shammai:** The **heavens** were created first, then the **earth**.  
- **Hillel:** The **earth** was created first, then the **heavens**.

Each side brings a parable and a Bible verse.

Later teachers do not only pick a winner. They offer softer solutions:

- One order for **first creating**, another for **finishing / fitting out**.  
- Or both were made **together**, like a pot and its lid.  
- Or when the wording swaps order, that teaches the two are **of equal weight** (like other name-pairs that sometimes swap).

**What we learn for the interface:** Genesis 1:1’s order “heavens… and earth” is **not** the only order Scripture uses. BR treats order as a **real debate** with several legal-style resolutions—not one silent default.

## 1. Retell (plain English)

### The disagreement

**House of Shammai:** Heavens first, then earth.  
**House of Hillel:** Earth first, then heavens.  
Each side brings a reason.

### Shammai’s parable and verse

Like a king who first makes himself a **throne**, and only afterward makes the **footstool**.  
So God says (Isaiah 66:1, free English): “The **heavens** are My throne and the **earth** is My footstool…”

→ Throne before footstool → heavens before earth.

### Hillel’s parable and verse

Like a king who builds a **palace**: first the **lower** stories, then the **upper** ones.  
So Scripture says (Genesis 2:4, free English): “in the day the Lord God made **earth and heavens**.”

→ Lower before upper → earth before heavens.

**Rabbi Yehudah bar Ilai** adds another verse for Hillel (Psalm 102:26, free English):  
“Of old You founded the **earth**,” and afterward “the heavens are the work of Your hands.”

### Pushback and a two-stage model

**Rabbi Hanin:** The place that seems to help Shammai is removed by Hillel’s camp.  
Genesis 1:2, free English: “And the earth **was**…” — midrash: it **already was** (so earth is already on stage when the story continues).

**Rabbi Yochanan in the name of the Sages:**  
- For **creation** (*beri’ah* / first bringing-into-being): **heavens came first**.  
- For **completion / finishing** (*shikhlul*): **earth came first**.

**Rabbi Tanhuma** gives verses for that split:  
- Creation-first for heavens: Genesis 1:1 (“In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth” — heavens named first).  
- Finishing-first for earth: Genesis 2:4 (“earth and heavens”).

### Together, or equal weight

**Rabbi Shimon ben Yochai:** I am amazed the great houses split on this. I say they were only created **together**, like a **pot and its lid**.  
Support (Isaiah 48:13, free English): “I call to them—they stand **together**.”

**Rabbi Eleazar son of Rabbi Shimon:** If they are equal (as Father said), why does Scripture sometimes put earth first and sometimes heavens first?  
To teach: **both are of equal weight**.

He stacks other equal-weight swaps:

| Usually first | Sometimes swapped | Lesson |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| Abraham, then Isaac, then Jacob | Jacob named first in one remembrance verse (Leviticus 26:42) | The three are equal in weight |
| Moses, then Aaron | “Aaron and Moses” once (Exodus 6:26) | Equal weight |
| Joshua, then Caleb | Caleb named first once (Numbers 14:30) | Equal weight |
| Turtledoves before young pigeons (offerings) | Young pigeon first once (Leviticus 12:6) | Equal weight |
| Honor father before mother | Mother named first once (Leviticus 19:3) | Equal weight (though sages still say father has priority for a separate reason) |
| Creation of heavens before earth | Earth before heavens in Genesis 2:4 | **Equal weight** |

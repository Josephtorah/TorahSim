## Plain English summary

A builder needs **six** things: water, earth/dust, wood, stones, reeds, and iron.  
Even a rich man needs a **measuring reed** (Ezekiel’s measuring rod).

So too: **Torah preceded** with six “before / from of old” style words in Proverbs 8 (pairs of priority language, plus “the Lord acquired me…”).

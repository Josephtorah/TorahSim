## Plain English summary

Two big claims:

1. **Human body marvel:** a skin bag (waterskin) leaks if it has a needle hole—but a human is full of openings and still holds the breath of life. Who does that? “You alone are God.”

2. **Angels were not partners on day one.** Some say angels on day **two**, some on day **five**—but **all agree** nothing angelic was created on day **one**, so no one can say Michael stretched the south of the sky and Gabriel the north while God measured the middle. Isaiah: “I, the Lord, make all… I stretch the heavens **alone**.” God alone created, alone is praised.

## 1. Retell

**Rabbi Tanhuma** opens with Psalm 86:10; waterskin vs human body → God alone.

**When angels?**  
- **Rabbi Yochanan:** day **two** (Psalm 104:3–4 links waters above and “makes His angels winds”).  
- **Rabbi Hanina:** day **five** (Genesis 1:20 “let birds fly” + Isaiah 6:2 angels with wings).  
- **Rabbi Luliana bar Tavrin in name of Rabbi Yitzhak:** either way, **not day one**—so no dual-architect myth (Michael south, Gabriel north, God middle). Isaiah 44:24: alone; read “who was with Me?” as “who was My partner?”

**Another reading:** A human king is praised with his nobles who share the load; God has **no co-builders**—He alone creates, alone is praised, alone is glorified.

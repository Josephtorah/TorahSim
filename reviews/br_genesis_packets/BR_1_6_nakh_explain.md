## Plain English summary (read this first)

This paragraph does two jobs.

**A. Petihah on Daniel 2:22**  
“He reveals deep and hidden things…”  
Midrash maps that line to Gehenna / Eden, deeds of the wicked / righteous, and the Messiah as light.

**B. Method claim about Genesis (the systems-relevant part)**  
From the **start of the world’s creation**, “He reveals deep and hidden things”:

| Genesis says | Midrash note | Where BR says it is **explained** |
|--------------|--------------|-----------------------------------|
| *Bereshit bara Elohim…* (heavens side) | **Did not detail** (*lo parash*) | **Isaiah 40:22** — stretches heavens like gauze |
| *ve-et ha-aretz* | **Did not detail** | **Job 37:6 / 38:38** — earth / dust cast |
| *Yehi or* (“let there be light”) | **Did not detail** | **Psalm 104:2** — wraps light like a garment |

**One line:** Genesis 1 is a **compressed header**; later biblical books are treated as **detail files** for the same creation topics.

That is a **cross-reference expand codec** — not the same as *et* = include sun/moon in BR 1:14.

## 1. Retell (plain English)

**Who is speaking:**  
**Rabbi Yehudah bar Simon** opens (and returns in the creation-method half).  
**Rabbi Abba Serungaya** on Messiah-light.  
Note “in the Pesikta” for the creation-detail half.

### Part A — Daniel 2:22 maps

| Phrase in Daniel | Midrash maps to | Hook (free gloss) |
|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Reveals the **deep** | **Gehenna** | Prov 9:18 shades; Isa 30:33 “deep and wide” |
| And the **hidden** | **Garden of Eden** | Isa 4:6 shelter; Ps 31:21 hide them in the secret of Your face |
| **Another reading:** deep + hidden | **Deeds of the wicked** | Isa 29:15 — hide counsel deep from the Lord; works in dark |
| Knows what is in **darkness** | Wicked deeds | same Isa 29:15 |
| **Light** dwells with Him | **Deeds of the righteous** | Prov 4:18 path of righteous like light; Ps 97:11 light sown for the righteous |
| Light dwells with Him (alt.) | **King Messiah** | Isa 60:1 “Arise, shine…” |

So the petihah is moral/eschatological: deep/hidden/dark/light as **afterlife + ethics + messiah**, not only cosmology.

### Part B — Creation headers not detailed

Same teacher (Yehudah bar Simon), applied to Gen 1:

> From the **beginning of the creation of the world**, “He reveals deep and hidden things”:  
> *Bereshit bara Elohim…* — **and He did not detail**.  
> **Where did He detail later?** …

| Compact (Genesis) | Detail locus (Nakh) | Free gloss of detail verse |
|-------------------|---------------------|----------------------------|
| Heavens (in 1:1) | Isa 40:22 | He who stretches the heavens like fine cloth |
| *ve-et ha-aretz* | Job 37:6; 38:38 | Snow/earth speech; dust poured into a mold |
| *Yehi or* (1:3) | Ps 104:2 | Wrapping light like a cloak |

**Job of this half:** authorize reading **later Scripture as expansion of Gen 1’s short lines**.

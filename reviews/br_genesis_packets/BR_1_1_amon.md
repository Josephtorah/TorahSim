## Plain English summary (read this first)

Bereshit Rabbah does **not** start by retelling day 1.  
It opens with a **petihah** (proem): a verse from **Proverbs 8** about wisdom as God’s **amon**.

That word *amon* is read several ways (tutor, covered, hidden, great city) — then as **craftsman / master builder**.

**Main claim:**  
A human king does not build a palace from raw whim. He uses a **craftsman**, and the craftsman uses **plans and notebooks**.  
So too: the Holy One **looked into the Torah and created the world**.  
Torah says of itself: *Bereshit* — and *reishit* means **Torah** (Prov 8:22: “the Lord acquired me as the **beginning** of His way”).

So Gen 1:1 is not only “God started creating.”  
Dual-track: it is also “creation ran from a **prior plan document** — Torah.”

## 1. Retell (plain English)

**Who is speaking:**  
**Rabbi Hoshayah Rabbah** opens (the flagship first voice of BR).

**Opens with Proverbs 8:30:**  
“I was beside Him as an *amon*…”

**Four (plus) readings of *amon*:**

| Sense | Hook verse (free gloss) | Plain English |
|-------|-------------------------|---------------|
| **Tutor / fosterer** (*pedagog*) | Num 11:12 — “as the nurse carries the nursing child” | Wisdom/Torah as caregiver beside God |
| **Covered** | Lam 4:5 — “those raised on scarlet…” (*ha-emunim*) | Hidden / covered aspect |
| **Hidden / stored** | Esth 2:7 — “he was foster-father (*omen*) to Hadassah” | Treasured, kept close |
| **Great** (*rabbata*) | Nah 3:8 — “Are you better than No-Amon?” (Thebes); Targum: great Alexandria | “Great one” / great city name-play |
| **Craftsman** (*uman*) — **main load** | (homily, not a fifth prooftext in the same way) | Torah = God’s building kit |

**Main parable (craftsman):**  
In the world, a flesh-and-blood king builds a palace.  
He does **not** build it only from his own head — he uses a **craftsman**.  
The craftsman does **not** build only from *his* own head — he has **plans and notebooks** so he knows how to make rooms and little doorways.

**So:** the Holy One would **look into the Torah and create the world**.  
And Torah says: *Bereshit bara Elohim…*  
And there is no *reishit* except **Torah** — as Prov 8:22: “the Lord acquired me as *reishit* of His way.”

## Plain English summary

A philosopher tells Rabban Gamliel: your God is a great artist—but He found good **ready pigments** that helped Him: formlessness, void, darkness, wind, water, and deeps.

Gamliel answers harshly: all of those are written as **created** things, not pre-owned paint. Each has a “create / form / command” verse (Isaiah, Psalms, Amos, Proverbs).

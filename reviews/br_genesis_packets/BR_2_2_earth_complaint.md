## Plain English summary (read this first)

Genesis 1:2 says the earth **was** *tohu va-vohu* (formless and void).  
BR 2:1 said that mark shows **early** and returns **late**.  

**BR 2:2 asks why the earth sits that way** and gives **three parables**. In each, the earth (or a stand-in) is upset because **upper** and **lower** were made together but get **unequal lots**:

1. **Labor:** Uppers feed on the glow of God’s Presence; lowers only eat if they **work**.  
2. **Death:** Uppers **live**; lowers **die**.  
3. **Future curse:** Like a nurse who frets because the prince will one day take what is under her care—the earth already “sees” it will be **cursed for man’s sake** (Genesis 3:17).

So *tohu va-vohu* is read as the earth’s **bewilderment / complaint**, not only as empty scenery.

## 1. Retell (plain English)

### Voice A — Rabbi Abbahu (two slaves)

A king buys **two slaves** on the **same contract and same price**.  
On one he decrees: fed from the treasury.  
On the other: **work, or you don’t eat.**

The second sits **bewildered**: we cost the same—why does he eat free while I must labor?

**Applied to the earth:**  
Upper and lower were created **together**.  
Uppers are nourished from the **radiance of the Divine Presence**.  
Lowers, if they do not labor, do not eat.  
So the earth sits *tohu va-vohu*.

### Voice B — Rabbi Yehudah bar Simon (two maidservants)

A king buys **two maidservants** on the same contract and price.  
One must never leave the palace.  
The other is sentenced to **expulsion / hard displacement**.

The second sits bewildered: same purchase—why is she fixed in the palace while I am driven out?

**Applied to the earth:**  
Uppers and lowers created together.  
Uppers **live**; lowers **die**.  
Therefore: “And the earth was formless and void.”

### Voice C — Rabbi Tanhuma (nurse and the prince)

A prince sleeps in a cradle; his **wet-nurse** sits bewildered—because she knows that one day what is under her care will be taken from her hands.

**Applied to the earth:**  
The earth foresaw that what is “under” it would be taken from under man’s hand—i.e. it would be **cursed for your sake** (Genesis 3:17).  
Therefore it was already *tohu va-vohu*.

## Plain English summary (read this first)

Two related teachings about **before** this world’s settled form.

### A — Rabbi Yehudah bar Simon (time order already)
It is **not** written “**let there be** evening” (*yehi erev*), but “**and there was** evening” (*va-yehi erev*).  
**From here:** there was already an **order of times** before this.

### B — Rabbi Abbahu (worlds tried and scrapped)
This teaches that God was **creating worlds and destroying them** until He created **these** (this world / this arrangement).  
He said (Aramaic flavor in the midrash): **“These please me; those do not please me.”**

**Rabbi Pinchas** gives Abbahu’s scriptural support: Genesis 1:31 — “God saw **all** that He had made, and behold, it was **very good**” →  
**“These please Me; those do not please Me.”**  
(“Very good” as acceptance of **this** total, against discarded priors.)

**One idea:** Creation week sits on **prior temporal order** and (in Abbahu’s view) **prior world-attempts**—this cosmos is the one that **passed review**.

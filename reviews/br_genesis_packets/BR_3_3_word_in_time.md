## Plain English summary (read this first)

**Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai** opens with Proverbs 15:23:

> “Joy to a man in the answer of his mouth, and a word in its time—how good.”

He maps the proverb onto creation speech:

| Proverb piece | = | Genesis / proof |
|---------------|---|-----------------|
| “Joy to a man” | the Holy One | Exodus 15:3: “The Lord is a **man** of war…” |
| “in the answer of his mouth” | “And God said, Let there be light” | Gen 1:3 speech |
| “a word in its time—how good” | “God saw the light, that it was **good**” | Gen 1:4 |

**One idea:** Creation speech is **timely word** that brings **joy** to the Speaker and is judged **good**—not only physics of light, but **right word, right time**.

## 1. Retell (plain English)

Petihah: Prov 15:23 → bind to Gen 1:3–4.

1. **Who is the “man” who has joy?** HKBH — via Exod 15:3 (*ish*).  
2. **What is the mouth’s answer?** The creation command: *yehi or*.  
3. **What is the timely word that is good?** The evaluation: light is *tov*.

So “good” on light is already prepared by the proverb’s “how good” on a word in its time.

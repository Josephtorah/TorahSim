## Plain English summary (read this first)

**Rabbi Simon:** The word for light (*orah* / light language) appears **five times** in this stretch—**corresponding to** (*keneged*) the **five books of the Torah**.

| Creation phrase | = Torah book | Why (plain) |
|-----------------|--------------|-------------|
| “God said, Let there be light” | **Genesis** | God engaged and **created His world** |
| “And there was light” | **Exodus** | Israel went from **darkness to light** (leaving Egypt) |
| “God saw the light, that it was good” | **Leviticus** | Full of **many laws** (*halakhot*) |
| “God separated light and darkness” | **Numbers** | Separates those who **left Egypt** from those who **enter the land** |
| “God called the light Day” | **Deuteronomy** (*Mishneh Torah*) | Full of **many laws** |

**Pushback:** Colleagues: “But Leviticus is also full of many laws!” (Why give that reason mainly to Deut?)  
**Reply:** He (or “it”) also **taught something** in that book—both law-heavy books get standing.

**One idea:** Primordial light language is a **five-slot index** into the whole Torah library—creation light pre-maps the Written corpus.

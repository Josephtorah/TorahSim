## Plain English summary (read this first)

**Scene:** Rabbi Shimon ben Yehotzadak asks Rabbi Shmuel bar Nahman (because he is a master of *aggadah*):

> “From where was the light created?”

**Answer 1 (whispered):** The Holy One **wrapped Himself in it like a garment**, and the **radiance of His glory** shone from one end of the world to the other.  
Proof: Psalm 104:2 — “He wraps Himself in light as a garment.”

Why whisper? The asker is surprised: “It’s a full open verse—why whisper?”  
Reply: **“As I heard it in a whisper, so I said it to you in a whisper.”**

**Transmission note (Berekhiah):** If Rabbi Yitzhak had not expounded it **in public**, it would not have been possible to say. (Public teaching unlocks the whisper-track.)

**Answer 2 (Berekhiah in the name of Yitzhak):** Light was created **from the place of the Temple**.  
- Ezekiel 43:2: glory of the God of Israel comes from the east  
- Glory = Temple (Jeremiah 17:12: throne of glory… place of our sanctuary)

**One idea:** Light’s source is either **God’s self-wrapping radiance** or **Temple-place glory**—origin question, two coordinates (Person / Place).

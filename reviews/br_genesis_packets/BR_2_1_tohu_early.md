## Plain English summary (read this first)

Genesis 1:2 says the earth **was** *tohu va-vohu* (formless and void / wild empty chaos).

Rabbi Berekhiah opens with a Proverbs line about a youth already showing character by deeds.  
His image: even while a fruit is still **unripe**, it already shows **marks / spots** of what it will be.

So too the earth: **while still “green / unripe,”** it already put out the sign of *tohu va-vohu*.  
And that same phrase is what a prophet will later see at the end (Jeremiah: “I saw the earth, and behold—*tohu va-vohu*”).

**One idea:** chaos language is not only “day-one scenery.” It is a **mark that appears early** and **returns late**—a thread across history.

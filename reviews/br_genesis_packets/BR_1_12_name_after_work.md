**Packet id:** `BR_1_12_name_after_work`

## Plain English summary

A human king is praised in a city **before** he has built its baths and public works—title first, projects later.

The Unique One of the world is the opposite: first He **acts / builds** the world’s needs, **then** His name is praised.

So Genesis says **“In the beginning He created”** first, and only then **“God.”**  
Shimon ben Azzai: a human lists name then titles; God creates the world’s needs first, then the name appears in the verse order.

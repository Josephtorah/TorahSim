## Plain English summary

**Part A (law speech):** Do not say “to the Lord, a burnt offering” in a way that puts the Name before the gift. Say “a burnt offering **to the Lord**” (gift first, then Name)—from “an offering to the Lord” (Leviticus 1:2). All the more: blasphemers and idolaters should vanish.

**Part B (creation):** A human builder expands upward if the build goes well, or widens the base if not. God is not like that: “**et** the heavens” = heavens that **rose in thought**; “**et** the earth” = earth that **rose in thought** (pre-planned fully).

**Part C:** Even the “new heavens” of Isaiah were already created in the six days of creation (reading Isaiah’s wording carefully).

## 1. Retell

**Sages:** human building vs God: heavens and earth fully present **in thought** with the *et* phrases.

**Rav Huna in name of Rabbi Eliezer son of Rabbi Yose the Galilean:** “new heavens” already from the six days.

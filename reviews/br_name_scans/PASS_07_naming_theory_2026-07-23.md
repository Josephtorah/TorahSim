**Date:** 2026-07-23 · **Confidence:** observed text claims

## Observed

- **Why was he named X?** — multiple sections (Yaktan, Ahaz, Og, Mamre, Laban the Aramean, letter Alef, etc.): names encode **deeds/character**.
- **On account of the name / named for** — dozens of sections.
- **Name of the Messiah** (*shem shel Mashiach*) among pre-creation thought-items (**BR 1:4**).
- **King Messiah** bound across BR: spirit hovering (2:4, also 8:1), light/east, donkey (Zechariah), Shiloh, staff, gathering exiles, study in the future, etc.

## Hypothesis

Naming is **semantic technology** in this corpus: human names carry compressed meaning; Messiah’s **name** is pre-allocated before the world. Person-name network may sit next to a **messianic name** node that is prior even to the patriarchal cast.

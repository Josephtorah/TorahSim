# Pass 9 — Blind spots

**Date:** 2026-07-23 · **Confidence:** observed

## Women and “this is…”

Using **זה** (masculine “this”) + Sarah/Rebecca/Rachel/Leah: **~0**.  
Using **זו** / *zu* / feminine “this is…”: Sarah ~4, Rachel ~2, Leah ~2, Rebecca ~1, Eve ~1, Hagar ~1+.

**Hypothesis:** Gendered pointer form (**זה** vs **זו**) is part of the binding grammar. Women are **not** absent from identity-binding; they use a **different demonstrative**. Our first catalogs under-counted them by only searching **זה**.

## Judah paradox

- Raw mentions: **very high** (~468)  
- **זה יהודה**: only ~9  

**Hypothesis:** Judah is a **narrative/tribal mass** more than a *zeh*-identification hub—or identification uses other formulas. Needs deeper pass.

## Under-read for sequential BR

Joseph (high *zeh* count), Moses, Noah, Sarah (via *zu*), Messiah-as-name, letter-name etymologies.

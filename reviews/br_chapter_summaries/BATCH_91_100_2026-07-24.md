# BR chapter summaries — Batch 10 (chapters 91–100) — FINAL

**Date:** 2026-07-24 · Level B · not binding law · [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)

**Span:** Brothers to Egypt → reveal → migration → blessings → death/burial (**Gen 42–50**). Includes mega-chapters **91, 98** and dense **99–100**.

---

## Chapter 91 — Grain in Egypt; brothers’ first descent
**Paras:** 11 · **Mass: heavy** · **Spine:** Gen 42  
Jacob sees there is *shever* (grain/hope wordplay) in Egypt; ten brothers go; Joseph knows them; spy accusation; prison three days; Simeon held; money in sacks; report to Jacob; “Joseph is not…”.  
**Themes:** Famine logistics; unrecognized brother; measure-for-measure fear.  
**Standout:** **אַשְׁרֵי שֶׁאֵל יַעֲקֹב בְּעֶזְרוֹ שִׂבְרוֹ עַל ה' / ashrei she-El Ya’akov be-ezro sivro al Hashem / “Happy is he whose help is Jacob’s God — whose hope is on the Lord”** (*shever/sever* grain-hope).  
**Confidence:** Solid overview.

---

## Chapter 92 — Second descent; Benjamin; feast
**Paras:** 9 · **Spine:** Gen 43  
Judah guarantor; gifts; “El Shaddai give you mercy”; money returned worry; Joseph sees Benjamin; private weep; banquet by birth order astonishment.  
**Themes:** Surety ethics; mercy plea; identity almost cracks.  
**Standout:** **וְאֵל שַׁדַּי יִתֵּן לָכֶם רַחֲמִים / ve-El Shaddai yiten lakhem rachamim / “And may El Shaddai give you mercy”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 93 — Cup; Judah’s speech; Joseph reveals
**Paras:** 12 · **Spine:** Gen 44–45:15  
Silver cup framed; Judah approaches: long plea, father will die, take me as slave. Joseph can no longer restrain; “I am Joseph”; send for father; weep; talk.  
**Themes:** Teshuvah drama; speech that redeems; recognition scene.  
**Standout:** **וַיִּגַּשׁ אֵלָיו יְהוּדָה / va-yiggash elav Yehudah / “Judah drew near to him”** — classic confrontation-as-repair.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 94 — News to Pharaoh; wagons; Jacob’s spirit lives
**Paras:** 9 · **Spine:** Gen 45:16–28  
Pharaoh pleased; wagons; Joseph’s message; Jacob’s heart numb then revived: “Joseph my son is still alive; I will go.”  
**Themes:** Royal endorsement; resurrection of hope in the father.  
**Standout:** **עוֹד יוֹסֵף בְּנִי חָי / od Yosef beni chai / “Joseph my son is still alive”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 95 — Judah sent ahead; Goshen
**Paras:** 4 · **Spine:** Gen 46:28–34  
Judah sent before to Joseph to show the way to Goshen; reunion weep; shepherds abominable to Egypt — strategy for separate land. Peaceable animal midrash openings.  
**Themes:** Judah as advance party; ethnic/economic separation plan.  
**Standout:** **וְאֶת יְהוּדָה שָׁלַח לְפָנָיו / ve-et Yehudah shalach lefanav / “He sent Judah before him”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid (short).

---

## Chapter 96 — Jacob lives in Egypt; closed parashah
**Paras:** 5 · **Spine:** Gen 47:28–31  
Seventeen years in Egypt; “why is this section closed?” — when Jacob dies, Egyptian bondage atmosphere begins. Burial oath (not Egypt).  
**Themes:** Sweet years before darkness; deathbed logistics.  
**Standout:** Closed *parashah* as literary signal of coming slavery.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 97 — Ephraim and Manasseh blessed
**Paras:** 6 · **Spine:** Gen 48  
Joseph brings sons; Jacob crosses hands; younger preferred; “by you Israel shall bless”; Redeemer angel; double portion. Faces shining midrash.  
**Themes:** Adoption of grandsons as tribes; left-hand preference pattern.  
**Standout:** **בְּךָ יְבָרֵךְ יִשְׂרָאֵל / bekha yevarekh Yisrael / “By you shall Israel bless”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 98 — Blessings of the tribes (part 1 / bulk)
**Paras:** 20 · **Mass: book’s heaviest chapter** · **Spine:** Gen 49:1–27 (main body)  
Jacob calls sons; prophetic character sketches: Reuben unstable; Simeon/Levi weapons; Judah scepter/Shiloh; seafaring Zebulun; Issachar burden; Dan serpent; Gad raids; Asher food; Naphtali words; Joseph fruitful vine / archers; Benjamin wolf. Festival openings (Rosh Hashanah/Yom Kippur) frame judgment lists.  
**Themes:** Tribal destinies; messianic Judah lines; Joseph as separate blessed spine.  
**Standout:** **לֹא יָסוּר שֵׁבֶט מִיהוּדָה / lo yasur shevet mi-Yehudah / “The scepter shall not depart from Judah”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid as map; each tribe’s midrash is its own world.

---

## Chapter 99 — More on the blessings; end of the words
**Paras:** 12 · **Spine:** Gen 49 continued / close  
Further tribal midrash (Dan, Benjamin, etc.); mountains jostling for Torah midrash openings; “all these are the tribes of Israel… he blessed them each according to his blessing.”  
**Themes:** Completion of oracle; particular blessings not one-size.  
**Standout:** **אִישׁ אֲשֶׁר כְּבִרְכָתוֹ בֵּרַךְ אֹתָם / ish asher ke-virkhato berakh otam / “each according to his own blessing he blessed them”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 100 — Death, burial, brothers’ fear, Joseph’s end
**Paras:** 13 · **Spine:** Gen 49:28–50:26  
Burial instructions; dies; embalmed; Egyptian mourning; Machpelah cortege; Goren ha-Atad. Brothers fear revenge; Joseph weeps: “Am I in place of God?”; intended for good. Joseph’s age; bones oath; dies; coffin in Egypt — handoff to Exodus.  
**Themes:** Mourning; full forgiveness speech; unfinished redemption (bones).  
**Standout:** **וְאַתֶּם חֲשַׁבְתֶּם עָלַי רָעָה אֱלֹהִים חֲשָׁבָהּ לְטֹבָה / ve-atem chashavtem alai ra’ah Elohim chashavah le-tovah / “You planned evil against me; God planned it for good”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

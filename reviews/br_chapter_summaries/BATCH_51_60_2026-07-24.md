# BR chapter summaries — Batch 6 (chapters 51–60)

**Date:** 2026-07-24 · Level B · not binding law · [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)

**Span:** Sodom destroyed → Abimelech → Isaac born → treaty → **Akedah** → Sarah’s death → servant seeks Rebecca (**Gen 19–24**).

---

## Chapter 51 — Fire on Sodom; Lot’s wife; cave
**Paras:** 11 · **Spine:** Gen 19:12–38  
Sulfur and fire; escape to Zoar; pillar of salt; Abraham sees smoke. Lot’s daughters and Moab/Ammon origin (handled as scandal-genealogy).  
**Themes:** Total city judgment; looking back; problematic after-survival.  
**Standout:** **וַה' הִמְטִיר… גָּפְרִית וָאֵשׁ / va-Hashem himtir… gofrit va-esh / “the Lord rained… sulfur and fire”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 52 — Gerar; Sarah and Abimelech
**Paras:** 13 · **Spine:** Gen 20  
Abraham migrates south; again “sister”; God warns Abimelech in dream; restoration; prayer heals household. Lot as fallen “mountain” foil in openings.  
**Themes:** Repeat matriarch danger; prophet-intercessor Abraham; fear of God among nations.  
**Standout:** Dream verdict: **הִנְּךָ מֵת עַל הָאִשָּׁה / hinekhah met al ha-ishah / “You are a dead man because of the woman”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 53 — Isaac born; Hagar/Ishmael cast out
**Paras:** 15 · **Spine:** Gen 21:1–21  
Lord visits Sarah as said; laughter of joy; circumcision day eight; weaning feast. Ishmael “playing”; cast out; well in desert; God hears the lad.  
**Themes:** Promise kept; rival-son crisis; God-with-the-outcast.  
**Standout:** **וַה' פָּקַד אֶת שָׂרָה / va-Hashem pakad et Sarah / “The Lord visited Sarah”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 54 — Covenant with Abimelech; well; tamarisk
**Paras:** 6 · **Spine:** Gen 21:22–34  
Peace treaty; well dispute; seven ewes; Beer-sheba; **אֵשֶׁל / eshel / tamarisk** planted; call on everlasting God.  
**Themes:** Political peace; naming wells; public monotheism.  
**Standout:** **בִּרְצוֹת ה' דַּרְכֵי אִישׁ גַּם אוֹיְבָיו יַשְׁלִם אִתּוֹ / birtsot Hashem darkhei ish gam oyevav yashlim ito / “When the Lord favors a man’s ways, even his enemies make peace with him”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 55 — The Binding begins (*Akedah* open)
**Paras:** 8 · **Spine:** Gen 22:1–3  
“After these things God tested Abraham.” Banner/miracle wordplay on *nes/nissah*. Take your son… Moriah. Early rise; saddle; split wood; go.  
**Themes:** Test after tests; love language that costs; readiness.  
**Standout:** **וְהָאֱלֹהִים נִסָּה אֶת אַבְרָהָם / ve-ha-Elohim nissah et Avraham / “God tested Abraham”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 56 — Third day; altar; ram; oath
**Paras:** 11 · **Spine:** Gen 22:4–19  
Sees the place; “we will return”; Isaac carries wood; “where is the lamb?”; bound; angel stops; ram in thicket; “now I know you fear God”; stars/sand oath; return to Beer-sheba.  
**Themes:** Third-day resurrection midrash links; substituted ram; merit for later Israel.  
**Standout:** **בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁלִישִׁי / ba-yom ha-shelishi / “On the third day”** — stacked with other third-day salvations.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 57 — News of Nahor’s children
**Paras:** 4 · **Spine:** Gen 22:20–24  
After Akedah, report of Milcah’s sons — Rebecca’s line staged. Envy/rot vs healing heart.  
**Themes:** Genealogy as next-plot seed (Isaac’s wife).  
**Standout:** Bridge notice: Rebecca is in the wings.  
**Confidence:** Solid (short).

---

## Chapter 58 — Sarah’s death and burial
**Paras:** 9 · **Spine:** Gen 23  
Years of Sarah (perfect years midrash). Kiryat Arba; eulogy; cave of Machpelah purchase from Ephron; full price; burial.  
**Themes:** First land deed in Canaan; honor of the dead; perfect lifespan rhetoric.  
**Standout:** **יוֹדֵעַ ה' יְמֵי תְמִימִים / yodea Hashem yemei temimim / “The Lord knows the days of the wholehearted”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 59 — Abraham old; oath of the servant
**Paras:** 12 · **Spine:** Gen 24:1–10  
Blessed in all; hand under thigh oath: no Canaanite wife; go to kindred; if she will not come… Camels and gifts set out.  
**Themes:** Old age crown; mission for the next generation’s marriage.  
**Standout:** **עֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאֶרֶת שֵׂיבָה / ateret tif’eret seivah / “Gray hair is a crown of glory”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 60 — Servant’s prayer; Rebecca at the well
**Paras:** 16 · **Spine:** Gen 24:12–61  
“Arrange it before me today.” Rebecca waters camels; jewelry; Bethuel’s house; Laban runs; retold story; consent “I will go”; blessing; departure.  
**Themes:** Providential match; chesed test at well; women’s agency in “elekh.”  
**Standout:** **הַקְרֵה נָא לְפָנַי הַיּוֹם / hakreh na lefanai ha-yom / “Please make it happen before me today”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

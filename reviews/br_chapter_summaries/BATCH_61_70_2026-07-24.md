# BR chapter summaries — Batch 7 (chapters 61–70)

**Date:** 2026-07-24 · Level B · not binding law · [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)

**Span:** Abraham’s end → Jacob/Esau birthright-blessing → Bethel → vow → Laban arrival (**Gen 25–29**). Includes mega-chapters **63, 65, 68, 70**.

---

## Chapter 61 — Keturah; gifts; Isaac heir
**Paras:** 7 · **Spine:** Gen 25:1–6  
Abraham takes Keturah (Hagar returned? multi-view); sons of concubines sent east with gifts; Isaac inherits all.  
**Themes:** Secondary lines cleared; single heir.  
**Standout:** Eastward send-off of rival branches.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 62 — Abraham’s death
**Paras:** 4 · **Spine:** Gen 25:7–11  
Full years; gathered to his people; Isaac and Ishmael bury him at Machpelah; God blesses Isaac.  
**Themes:** Good death; fraternal burial moment; transfer of blessing.  
**Standout:** **יוֹדֵעַ ה' יְמֵי תְמִימִים** again on Abraham’s complete days.  
**Confidence:** Solid (short).

---

## Chapter 63 — Isaac’s toledot; twins; birthright sold
**Paras:** 14 · **Spine:** Gen 25:19–34  
Prayer for Rebecca; two nations in womb; Esau red/hairy, Jacob smooth; parents’ split love; stew; birthright sold for red stuff.  
**Themes:** Intrauterine struggle; name destinies; cheapened firstborn right.  
**Standout:** **וְשֵׁנִי לְאֻמִּים מִמֵּעַיִךְ יִפָּרֵדוּ / u-shenei le’umim mi-me’ayikh yiparedu / “two peoples shall be separated from your womb”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 64 — Isaac’s famine; wells; treaty
**Paras:** 10 · **Spine:** Gen 26  
Famine; do not go to Egypt; sojourn; sister ruse with Abimelech; blessing; wells quarrel (Esek, Sitnah, Rehoboth); Beer-sheba; treaty.  
**Themes:** Isaac as re-runner of Abraham patterns; digging reopened wells = tradition recovery.  
**Standout:** **גוּר בָּאָרֶץ הַזֹּאת / gur ba-aretz ha-zot / “Sojourn in this land”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 65 — Esau’s wives; Isaac ages; the stolen blessing setup
**Paras:** 23 · **Mass: top-tier** · **Spine:** Gen 26:34–27:17  
Esau marries Hittites (bitterness). Isaac dims; favors Esau’s game. Rebecca overhears; Jacob dressed in Esau’s clothes; goat skins; food. Long moral/animal midrash (wild boar tearing vineyard = Rome/Esau type).  
**Themes:** Parental failure; disguise plot; Esau-as-empire typology.  
**Standout:** **יְכַרְסְמֶנָּה חֲזִיר מִיָּעַר / yekharsemenah chazir mi-ya’ar / “The boar from the forest ravages it”** — Esau/Rome reading.  
**Confidence:** Solid at overview; 23 paras = sketch depth on side paths.

---

## Chapter 66 — The blessing spoken
**Paras:** 7 · **Spine:** Gen 27:18–29  
Jacob enters; “Are you really my son Esau?”; dew of heaven, fat of earth; nations serve you; curse/bless reciprocity.  
**Themes:** Voice vs hands; irreversible spoken good.  
**Standout:** **וְיִתֶּן לְךָ הָאֱלֹהִים מִטַּל הַשָּׁמַיִם / ve-yiten lekha ha-Elohim mi-tal ha-shamayim / “May God give you of the dew of heaven”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 67 — Isaac trembles; Esau’s cry; hatred
**Paras:** 13 · **Spine:** Gen 27:30–41  
Esau returns; great trembling; “Bless me too!”; name Jacob = heeled/supplanted; residual blessing (fat places, sword, serve then break yoke). Esau hates; plans murder after mourning.  
**Themes:** Trauma of the stolen voice; limited leftover blessing; exile trigger.  
**Standout:** **וַיֶּחֱרַד יִצְחָק חֲרָדָה גְּדֹלָה / va-yecherad Yitzchak charadah gedolah / “Isaac trembled a very great trembling”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid. *(Gen 27:33 is a citation hub.)*

---

## Chapter 68 — Jacob flees; ladder dream
**Paras:** 14 · **Spine:** Gen 28:10–12  
Leaves Beer-sheba for Haran; night place; stones; ladder set earth to heaven; angels ascending/descending. Safe-road openings.  
**Themes:** Exile journey; vertical traffic at “the place”; portable Presence.  
**Standout:** **וְהִנֵּה סֻלָּם מֻצָּב אַרְצָה / ve-hineh sulam mutzav artzah / “Behold, a ladder set up on the earth”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid. *(Gen 28:11 hub.)*

---

## Chapter 69 — God stands above; land promise; awakening
**Paras:** 8 · **Spine:** Gen 28:13–17  
“I am the Lord, God of Abraham your father…” Seed as dust; I am with you. Jacob wakes: “Surely the Lord is in this place”; dread; house of God; gate of heaven.  
**Themes:** Promise re-issued to fugitive; sacred site discovery.  
**Standout:** **מַה נּוֹרָא הַמָּקוֹם הַזֶּה / mah nora ha-makom ha-zeh / “How awesome is this place”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 70 — Vow; arrival; Rachel at the well
**Paras:** 20 · **Spine:** Gen 28:18–29:14  
Stone pillar; oil; vow (if God with me… then the Lord shall be my God; tithe). Journey success; shepherds; Rachel with flock; stone rolled; kiss; Laban’s house month.  
**Themes:** Conditional vow debated; well as marriage theater (again); labor-for-love setup.  
**Standout:** **אִם יִהְיֶה אֱלֹהִים עִמָּדִי… וְנָתַן לִי לֶחֶם לֶאֱכֹל / im yihyeh Elohim immadi… ve-natan li lechem le’ekhol / “If God will be with me… and give me bread to eat”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

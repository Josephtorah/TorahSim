# BR chapter summaries — Batch 8 (chapters 71–80)

**Date:** 2026-07-24 · Level B · not binding law · [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)

**Span:** Leah/Rachel children → Laban flight → Esau approach → night wrestle → Shechem (**Gen 29–34**).

---

## Chapter 71 — Leah hated; sons named
**Paras:** 10 · **Spine:** Gen 29:31–35 (+30:1–)  
God sees Leah is hated; opens womb. Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah — name etymologies as prayer-states. God hears the poor/prisoners midrash frame.  
**Themes:** Divine preference for the unloved; children as theology.  
**Standout:** **וַיַּרְא ה' כִּי שְׂנוּאָה לֵאָה / va-yar Hashem ki senu’ah Leah / “The Lord saw that Leah was hated”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 72 — Mandrakes; more sons
**Paras:** 6 · **Spine:** Gen 30:14–21  
Reuben’s dudaim; Rachel bargains night; Issachar, Zebulun, Dinah. Train-a-child openings.  
**Themes:** Sister competition tech; fertility politics.  
**Standout:** Mandrake bargain as household law-of-the-jungle.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 73 — God remembers Rachel; Joseph; wages
**Paras:** 12 · **Spine:** Gen 30:22–43  
Rachel remembered; Joseph born; “gather my shame.” Jacob wants to leave; speckled wage deal; rods; flocks multiply; Laban’s sons grumble. Rosh Hashanah visitation midrash for Sarah/Rachel/Hannah.  
**Themes:** Memory as birth; cunning under oppression; Joseph as turning point.  
**Standout:** **וַיִּזְכֹּר אֱלֹהִים אֶת רָחֵל / va-yizkor Elohim et Rachel / “God remembered Rachel”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 74 — Flight from Laban; treaty
**Paras:** 17 · **Spine:** Gen 31  
“Return to fathers’ land.” Secret flight; teraphim stolen; chase; dream warning to Laban; heap Mizpah; covenant; “God watch between us.”  
**Themes:** Exit from exile-labor; idol theft crisis; border treaty.  
**Standout:** **שׁוּב אֶל אֶרֶץ אֲבוֹתֶיךָ / shuv el eretz avotekha / “Return to the land of your fathers”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 75 — Messengers to Esau
**Paras:** 13 · **Spine:** Gen 32:4–7  
Jacob sends malakhim (angels/messengers multi-read); “I stayed with Laban”; gift diplomacy; report: Esau comes with 400 men.  
**Themes:** Fear management; soft speech to the armed brother.  
**Standout:** **וַיִּשְׁלַח יַעֲקֹב מַלְאָכִים לְפָנָיו / va-yishlach Ya’akov malakhim lefanav / “Jacob sent messengers/angels before him”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid. *(Gen 32:4 is a top reused cite.)*

---

## Chapter 76 — Great fear; camp split; prayer
**Paras:** 9 · **Spine:** Gen 32:8–13  
Afraid and distressed; two camps; prayer recalls promises; “I am too small for all the kindness.” Gift waves prepared.  
**Themes:** Promise vs panic; humility before reunion.  
**Standout:** **וַיִּירָא יַעֲקֹב מְאֹד וַיֵּצֶר לוֹ / va-yira Ya’akov me’od va-yetzer lo / “Jacob was very afraid and distressed”** — two fears midrash (self / others).  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 77 — Wrestles alone
**Paras:** 3 (short) · **Spine:** Gen 32:25–26  
Left alone; man wrestles till dawn; hip struck; “Let me go, dawn has risen.”  
**Themes:** Night ordeal; body marked.  
**Standout:** **וַיִּוָּתֵר יַעֲקֹב לְבַדּוֹ / va-yivater Ya’akov levado / “Jacob was left alone”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 78 — Name Israel; limp; sun rises
**Paras:** 16 · **Spine:** Gen 32:27–33  
Name no longer Jacob but Israel; blessing; Peniel; sun rises as he passes Penuel limping; sinew custom. Morning mercies midrash.  
**Themes:** Rename after struggle; dietary memorial; face of God survived.  
**Standout:** **כִּי שָׂרִיתָ עִם אֱלֹהִים וְעִם אֲנָשִׁים וַתּוּכָל / ki sarita im Elohim ve-im anashim va-tukhal / “for you have striven with God and with humans and have prevailed”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 79 — Meeting Esau; Sukkot; Shechem “shalem”
**Paras:** 8 · **Spine:** Gen 33  
Bows; embrace; gifts pressed; “I have everything.” Esau to Seir; Jacob to Sukkot then **שָׁלֵם / shalem / “whole”** to Shechem; buys land; altar El-Elohei-Israel. Seven troubles motif.  
**Themes:** Reconciliation partial; wholeness claim; settled foothold.  
**Standout:** **וַיָּבֹא יַעֲקֹב שָׁלֵם / va-yavo Ya’akov shalem / “Jacob arrived whole/safe”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 80 — Dinah; Shechem; Simeon & Levi
**Paras:** 12 · **Spine:** Gen 34  
Dinah goes out; seized; Hamor negotiation; circumcision trick; slaughter; Jacob’s protest; brothers’ defense of sister.  
**Themes:** Sexual violence; deceptive covenant; zeal vs diplomacy.  
**Standout:** **וַתֵּצֵא דִינָה / va-tetze Dinah / “And Dinah went out”** — “like mother like daughter” mashal debates.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

# BR chapter summaries — Batch 4 (chapters 31–40)

**Date:** 2026-07-24  
**Depth:** Level B · not binding law  
**Index:** [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)

**Span:** Ark orders → Flood → exit → covenant/rainbow → vineyard → nations table → Babel → **Abram’s call** → Egypt famine (**Gen 6:13 → 12:13**).

---

## Chapter 31 — End of all flesh; build the ark

**Paras:** 14 · **Spine:** Gen 6:13–22

### Overview
Violence (*hamas*) fills the earth — end decreed. Ark specs as midrash world: rooms, pitch, window/light, stories. Food stores. Covenant language begins. Building in public as 120-year sermon.

### Themes
- Violence as terminal sin  
- Ark as designed micro-world  
- Obedience in detail  

### Standout
**קֵץ כָּל בָּשָׂר בָּא לְפָנַי / ketz kol basar ba lefanai / “The end of all flesh has come before Me”**.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

---

## Chapter 32 — Enter the ark; clean animals; Flood begins

**Paras:** 11 · **Spine:** Gen 7:1–16

### Overview
“Come into the ark” for righteousness. Clean sevens vs pairs. Seven days wait. Waters from windows of heaven and deeps. God shuts him in. Contrast liars/men of blood (Doeg/Ahitophel type openings).

### Themes
- Invitation to the righteous  
- Dual water sources  
- Divine sealing of the door  

### Standout
**בֹּא אַתָּה וְכָל בֵּיתְךָ אֶל הַתֵּבָה / bo attah ve-khol beitkha el ha-tevah / “Come, you and all your house, into the ark”**.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

---

## Chapter 33 — God remembered Noah

**Paras:** 7 · **Spine:** Gen 8:1–4 (landing)

### Overview
Remembrance as righteousness/justice balance (mountains vs deep). Wind over earth; fountains closed. Ark rests on Ararat. Animals included in memory. Ishmael/Akiva style readings of saving human and beast.

### Themes
- *Zakhor* (remember) mid-crisis  
- Mercy after judgment  
- Shared fate of creatures  

### Standout
**וַיִּזְכֹּר אֱלֹהִים אֶת נֹחַ / va-yizkor Elohim et Noach / “God remembered Noah”**.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

---

## Chapter 34 — Exit; offering; never again curse ground

**Paras:** 15 · **Spine:** Gen 8:15–22

### Overview
Leave the ark; multiply. Altar and pleasing aroma. God will not again curse the ground for man’s sake; yetzer from youth. Seedtime/harvest cycle guaranteed. Blood and image later seeded. Long moral aftercare of the Flood.

### Themes
- Worship after survival  
- Cosmic non-repeat pledge (pre-rainbow)  
- Yetzer realism  

### Standout
**לֹא אֹסִף לְקַלֵּל עוֹד אֶת הָאֲדָמָה / lo osif lekalel od et ha-adamah / “I will not again curse the ground any more”**.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

---

## Chapter 35 — Covenant with Noah and sons

**Paras:** 3 (short) · **Spine:** Gen 9:8–17

### Overview
“I establish My covenant.” Rainbow sign. Why Noah and sons get speech together (merit readings). Sign for God and humans when cloud covers.

### Themes
- Universal covenant  
- Sign technology (bow)  

### Standout
**אֶת קַשְׁתִּי נָתַתִּי בֶּעָנָן / et kashti natati be-anan / “My bow I have set in the cloud”**.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

---

## Chapter 36 — Sons of Noah; vineyard; Ham; Canaan cursed

**Paras:** 8 · **Spine:** Gen 9:18–27

### Overview
Leaving the ark into a quieted world. Noah the man of the soil plants a vineyard; wine and uncovering. Ham’s act; Shem and Japheth cover. Curse on Canaan; blessing Shem; Japheth in tents. Slavery motif planted.

### Themes
- Post-trauma agriculture/wine  
- Honor/shame of fathers  
- Geopolitical blessing-curse seed  

### Standout
**אָרוּר כְּנָעַן / arur Kena’an / “Cursed be Canaan”** — servitude oracle.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

---

## Chapter 37 — Table of nations (Japheth, Ham, Nimrod)

**Paras:** 8 · **Spine:** Gen 10

### Overview
Ethnic-geographic midrash: Gomer, Magog, etc. mapped to known lands (Africa, Germany, Media, Macedonia…). Nimrod as mighty hunter **before** the Lord — rebel king / tower context. Kingdoms of Babel, Erech, Akkad.

### Themes
- World registry after Flood  
- Empire and rebellion  
- Name → place dictionaries  

### Standout
**גִּבֹּר צַיִד לִפְנֵי ה' / gibbor tzayid lifnei Hashem / “a mighty hunter before the Lord”** (Nimrod) — not a compliment in classic midrash.

### Confidence
**Solid** on function; place-IDs are traditional guesses.

---

## Chapter 38 — Tower of Babel

**Paras:** 14 · **Spine:** Gen 11:1–9

### Overview
One language; plain in Shinar; bricks for stone. “Let us build a city and tower.” God’s descent; confuse speech; scatter. Why not kill them (memory of generations). Materials and motives (war on heaven / make a name / stay un-scattered). Generation of Division vs Flood generation compared.

### Themes
- Unity weaponized  
- Language as judgment tool  
- Name-making vs God’s name  

### Standout
**הָבָה נֵרְדָה וְנָבְלָה שָׁם שְׂפָתָם / havah nerdah ve-navlah sham sefatam / “Come, let us go down and confuse their language there”**.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

---

## Chapter 39 — Lekh lekha: Abram called

**Paras:** 16 · **Spine:** Gen 12:1–9

### Overview
Major patriarch install. Palace-on-fire mashal: Abram sees a world burning, asks who runs it; Owner appears. Leave land/birth/father. Blessing package (great nation, name, those who bless/curse). Journey to Canaan; altar building; calling on the Name. Age, companions, Terah backdrop.

### Themes
- Call as seeing + answer  
- Blessing as portable covenant start  
- Altar trail  

### Standout
1. **לֶךְ לְךָ / lekh lekha / “Go for yourself / Go to yourself”**.  
2. **בִּירָה דּוֹלֶקֶת / birah doleket / “a palace ablaze”** — mashal of the guided world (39:1).

### Confidence
**Solid** — classic BR peak chapter.

---

## Chapter 40 — Famine; down to Egypt; Sarai

**Paras:** 6 · **Spine:** Gen 12:10–20

### Overview
Famine tests the call. Descent to Egypt; “sister” plan; beauty; taken to Pharaoh; plagues; expulsion with wealth. Fearers of God and waiting for kindness. Pattern for later Israel descent.

### Themes
- Famine as trial  
- Matriarch in danger  
- Wealth out of Egypt prototype  

### Standout
**וַיְהִי רָעָב בָּאָרֶץ / va-yehi ra’av ba-aretz / “There was a famine in the land”** — first patriarch famine cycle.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

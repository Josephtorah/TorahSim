# BR chapter summaries — Batch 9 (chapters 81–90)

**Date:** 2026-07-24 · Level B · not binding law · [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)

**Span:** Bethel again → Rachel’s death → Edom kings → **Joseph sold** → Judah/Tamar → Egypt rise (**Gen 35–41**).

---

## Chapter 81 — Up to Bethel; purge foreign gods
**Paras:** 5 · **Spine:** Gen 35:1–5  
God: arise to Bethel. Jacob: remove foreign gods, purify, change clothes; terror of God on cities. Vow-debt warnings.  
**Themes:** House-cleaning before sanctuary; deferred vow risk.  
**Standout:** **הָסֵרוּ אֶת אֱלֹהֵי הַנֵּכָר / hasiru et elohei ha-nekhar / “Put away the foreign gods”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 82 — Bethel appearance; Benjamin; Rachel dies; Isaac dies
**Paras:** 15 · **Spine:** Gen 35:6–29  
Altar El-Bethel; rename Israel confirmed; nations/kings promise; Rachel dies birthing Benjamin (Ben-Oni/Ben-yamin); pillar on grave; Reuben bilhah episode; Isaac dies; Esau and Jacob bury.  
**Themes:** Full circle to Bethel; matriarch burial on the road; succession messy.  
**Standout:** **וַיֵּרָא אֱלֹהִים אֶל יַעֲקֹב… בְּבֹאוֹ מִפַּדַּן אֲרָם / va-yera Elohim el Ya’akov… / “God appeared to Jacob… when he came from Paddan-aram”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

## Chapter 83 — Edomite kings list
**Paras:** 5 · **Spine:** Gen 36  
Kings who reigned before any king in Israel; ship/oak midrash on nations.  
**Themes:** Esau’s political success recorded; contrast future Israel monarchy.  
**Standout:** **לִפְנֵי מְלָךְ מֶלֶךְ לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל / lifnei melokh melekh li-vnei Yisrael / “before any king reigned for the Israelites”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid (short registry chapter).

---

## Chapter 84 — Joseph story opens; dreams; sold
**Paras:** 22 · **Mass: late-book bulk** · **Spine:** Gen 37  
Jacob settles; Joseph 17, coats, evil reports; dreams (sheaves, stars); brothers hate; Shechem/Dothan; pit; Judah’s sale idea; Midianites/Ishmaelites; bloodied coat; Jacob mourns; Joseph to Egypt/Potiphar.  
**Themes:** Favorite son disaster; dreams as fate engine; descent begins.  
**Standout:** **וַיֵּשֶׁב יַעֲקֹב / va-yeshev Ya’akov / “Jacob settled”** — tried to settle; storm begins.  
**Confidence:** Solid overview; 22 paras dense.

## Chapter 85 — Judah and Tamar
**Paras:** 14 · **Spine:** Gen 38  
Judah separates; Er/Onan; Tamar’s wait; roadside disguise; pledge; “she is more righteous than I”; twins Perez/Zerah.  
**Themes:** Levirate failure; righteous boldness; Davidic seed line hidden in scandal.  
**Standout:** **צָדְקָה מִמֶּנִּי / tzedkah mimmeni / “She is more righteous than I”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

## Chapter 86 — Joseph in Potiphar’s house
**Paras:** 6 · **Spine:** Gen 39:1–6  
Joseph brought down; Lord with him; success; favor; all in his hand; handsome. Cords of man/love midrash (Israel drawn).  
**Themes:** Presence in slavery; administrative rise.  
**Standout:** **וַיְהִי ה' אֶת יוֹסֵף / va-yehi Hashem et Yosef / “The Lord was with Joseph”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

## Chapter 90 — Joseph ruler
**Paras:** 6 · **Spine:** Gen 41:38–57  
“Can we find such a one?” Spirit of God; ring, linen, gold chain, second chariot; Asenath; sons Manasseh/Ephraim; famine; all lands come to Joseph.  
**Themes:** Gentile recognition of ruach; food viceroy; famine magnet.  
**Standout:** **הֲנִמְצָא כָזֶה אִישׁ אֲשֶׁר רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים בּוֹ / ha-nimtza kazeh ish asher ruach Elohim bo / “Can we find a man like this, in whom is the spirit of God?”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

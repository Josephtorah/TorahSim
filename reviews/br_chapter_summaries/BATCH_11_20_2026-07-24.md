## Chapter 11 — Blessing the seventh day

**Paras:** 10 · **Written spine:** Gen 2:3 — וַיְבָרֶךְ… אֶת יוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי / *va-yevarekh… et yom ha-shevi’i* / “And God blessed… the seventh day”

### Overview
How Shabbat is **blessed and sanctified**: manna double portion / none on Shabbat; clothing and meal customs; blessing despite “loss” (exit of the extra soul / *neshamah yeteirah* themes); cold food for Antoninus still tastier because of Shabbat “spice.” Turnus Rufus vs Akiva: what makes the day different (king’s honor; Sambatyon rests). Circumcision vs creation-perfection (everything needs finishing work). Abraham inherits “by measure,” Jacob (Shabbat *techum*) without measure. Shabbat has no pair-day → Israel as spouse. God rests from world-building but still “works” reward and punishment.

### Themes
- Manna as lived proof of blessing/sanctity  
- Shabbat as royal honor and cosmic rest  
- Pairing: Shabbat ↔ Israel  
- Ongoing divine “work” of justice  

### Standout
1. **בֵּרְכוֹ בַּמָּן וְקִדְּשׁוֹ בַּמָּן / berakho ba-man ve-kidsho ba-man / “He blessed it with manna and sanctified it with manna”** (11:2).  
2. **כְּנֶסֶת יִשְׂרָאֵל הִיא בֶּן זוּגֵךְ / kenest Yisrael hi ben zugekh / “The community of Israel is your pair”** (to Shabbat) (11:8).

### Confidence
**Solid.**

## Chapter 12 — “These are the generations of heaven and earth”

**Paras:** 16 · **Written spine:** Gen 2:4 — אֵלֶּה תּוֹלְדוֹת / *eleh toledot* / “These are the generations…”

### Overview
Bridge from week-one to garden narrative. Thunder as tiny edge of God’s ways. **בְּהִבָּרְאָם / be-hibare’am** read as **בְּה"א בְּרָאָם / be-heh bera’am / “He created them with the letter *heh*”**. *Eleh* cancels prior *tohu*. Same-day creation vs timed appearance (fig-picker mashal). Core three + three generations each. Defective spelling of *toledot* (except Peretz and this verse) — damage after Adam. Cosmic measurements, light, rain, mountains. Human formed from dust of the whole earth / Temple dust traditions. Divine names stacked (YHWH Elohim).

### Themes
- Letter theology of creation  
- Full vs defective “generations”  
- Human as world-sample  
- Transition into second creation account  

### Standout
1. **בְּהִבָּרְאָם — בְּה"א בְּרָאָם / be-hibare’am — be-heh bera’am / “with the *heh* He created them”** (12:2).  
2. **אֵלֶּה פָּסַל אֶת הָרִאשׁוֹנִים… תֹּהוּ וָבֹהוּ / eleh pasal et ha-rishonim… tohu va-vohu / “*These* voids the earlier ones… chaos”** (12:3).

### Confidence
**Solid.**

## Chapter 13 — Mist, watering, service of earth

**Paras:** 17 · **Written spine:** Gen 2:5–6 — mist / no rain yet / man to work ground

### Overview
Why no rain before human? Service and recognition. Cloud/mist mechanics; how God waters the world. Prayer for rain; merit and land. Garden planting as royal park. Comparisons of upper and lower waters. Dense nature-midrash on climate and agriculture as divine system.

### Themes
- Rain ↔ human prayer/need  
- Earth awaits its worker  
- Water systems as providence  

### Standout
**וְאָדָם אַיִן… / ve-adam ayin… / “and there was no human to work the ground”** — rain held until there is one who will recognize the gift (chapter theme on 2:5–6).

### Confidence
**Solid** on theme; weather lore is sketch-level detail.

## Chapter 14 — Forming the human (dust + breath)

**Paras:** 10 · **Written spine:** Gen 2:7 — וַיִּיצֶר… עָפָר… וַיִּפַּח / *va-yitzer… afar… va-yipach* / formed dust, blew breath

### Overview
Double *yod* in *va-yitzer* (two formations / two worlds / good and evil inclinations). Dust from where (whole earth / altar site). Breath as soul; soul’s five names traditions. Body upright vs animals. Life as loan.

### Themes
- Dual formation  
- Soul taxonomy  
- Dust origin politics (Temple-centered readings)  

### Standout
**וַיִּיצֶר — שְׁתֵּי יְצִירוֹת / va-yitzer — shtei yetzirot / “He formed — two formations”** (double *yod* reading).

### Confidence
**Solid.**

## Chapter 15 — Garden of Eden planted

**Paras:** 7 · **Written spine:** Gen 2:8–14 — garden, trees, rivers

### Overview
God plants before or for the human; “east” / *mi-kedem* as primordial. Tree of life and knowledge identified with various species (traditions). Four rivers and world geography / empire hints. Gold of Havilah; precious stones.

### Themes
- Garden as prepared home  
- Tree identities (open multi-view)  
- Rivers as world map  

### Standout
**וַיִּטַּע ה' אֱלֹהִים גַּן בְּעֵדֶן מִקֶּדֶם / va-yita… gan be-Eden mi-kedem / “The Lord God planted a garden in Eden from the east / of old”**.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

## Chapter 16 — Command about the tree; rivers; work

**Paras:** 6 · **Written spine:** Gen 2:15–17 (into 2:10–14 links)

### Overview
Human placed **לְעָבְדָהּ וּלְשָׁמְרָהּ / le-ovdah u-le-shomrah / “to work it and to guard it”** — physical garden and/or Torah/mitzvot readings. Command not to eat; death warning. River going out to water. Authority and boundary as core gift.

### Themes
- Service + guard dual duty  
- Command as relationship  
- Death as stated consequence  

### Standout
**לְעָבְדָהּ וּלְשָׁמְרָהּ / le-ovdah u-le-shomrah / “to work it and to guard it”** — garden labor and/or covenant keep.

### Confidence
**Solid.**

## Chapter 19 — Serpent, dialogue, eating, hiding, blame

**Paras:** 12 · **Written spine:** Gen 3:1–13

### Overview
Serpent’s craft; what it said and why. Tree desire chain. Eyes opened; fig leaves. God walking in the garden; “Where are you?” Four who failed when “tested” (Adam, Cain, Balaam, Hezekiah) vs Ezekiel. Blame shift: man → woman → serpent. Job contrast (does not follow wife into blasphemy).

### Themes
- Speech crime (*lashon hara* on the Creator)  
- Responsibility dodging  
- Hiding from Presence  

### Standout
1. **אַיֶּכָּה / ayekka / “Where are you?”** — call that is already judgment opening.  
2. **הָאִשָּׁה אֲשֶׁר נָתַתָּה עִמָּדִי / ha-ishah asher natatah immadi / “The woman You gave to be with me”** — blame as theology fail (19:11–12).

### Confidence
**Solid.**

## Chapter 20 — Curses: serpent, woman, man; naming Eve; garments

**Paras:** 12 · **Written spine:** Gen 3:14–21

### Overview
Serpent judged first (small-to-large in calamity). Belly-walk; dust food; enmity. Woman: pain, desire, rule. Man: ground cursed; sweat; thorns; bread. Four “desires” (*teshukah*). Livelihood harder than childbirth. Eve named (life / serpent wordplay). **כָּתְנוֹת עוֹר / kotnot or / “garments of skin”** — R. Meir’s Torah: **אוֹר / or / “light”**.

### Themes
- Graded curse procedure  
- Desire typology  
- Even curse can hide blessing  
- Clothing as mercy  

### Standout
1. **עַל גְּחֹנְךָ תֵלֵךְ / al gechonecha telekh / “on your belly you shall go”** — angels cut limbs in one tradition (20:5).  
2. **כָּתְנוֹת אוֹר / kotnot or / “garments of light”** (Meir) vs skin (20:12).

### Confidence
**Solid.**

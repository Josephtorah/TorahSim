# BR chapter summaries — Batch 5 (chapters 41–50)

**Date:** 2026-07-24 · Level B · not binding law · [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)

**Span:** Abram/Lot → war of kings → covenant of pieces → Hagar → circumcision → Mamre guests → Sodom bargain → angels in Sodom (**Gen 12:17 → 19**).

---

## Chapter 41 — Plagues on Pharaoh; return; Lot separates
**Paras:** 10 · **Spine:** Gen 12:17–13:18  
Pharaoh struck for Sarai; Abram leaves rich. Lot’s flocks quarrel; separation — Lot chooses Jordan plain “like Egypt/Garden.” Abram promised whole land and seed as dust; walks the land; altar at Hebron.  
**Themes:** Matriarch protection; bad neighbor choice; land grant after separation.  
**Standout:** **צַדִּיק כַּתָּמָר יִפְרָח / tzaddik ka-tamar yifrach / “The righteous flourish like a palm”** applied to Abram’s straight growth.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 42 — War of the four/five kings
**Paras:** 8 · **Spine:** Gen 14:1–12  
Amraphel (often = Nimrod) era wars; wicked draw swords that turn on them. Sodom’s tar pits; Lot taken captive. Cosmic/historical overlay on local war.  
**Themes:** Empires collide; righteous collateral damage.  
**Standout:** **חַרְבָּם תָּבוֹא בְלִבָּם / charbam tavo be-libam / “their sword shall enter their own heart”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid. *(Heavy cite-load chapter in structure scans.)*

---

## Chapter 43 — Abram rescues Lot; Melchizedek
**Paras:** 9 · **Spine:** Gen 14:13–24  
Abram hears; armed trained men; night strike; recovers Lot. Melchizedek king of Salem brings bread/wine; blessing El Elyon; tithe. King of Sodom’s offer refused (“not a thread”).  
**Themes:** Brother-rescue; priest-king of Salem; refusing wicked wealth.  
**Standout:** **מִשְּׁמוּעָה רָעָה לֹא יִירָא / mi-shemu’ah ra’ah lo yira / “He will not fear bad news”** — heart fixed in trust.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 44 — Covenant between the pieces (*berit bein ha-betarim*)
**Paras:** 23 · **Mass: heaviest of early patriarch hub** · **Spine:** Gen 15  
Vision: “Fear not… shield… reward.” Childless complaint; seed as stars. Cut animals; deep sleep; smoking furnace / torch. Exile prophecy (400 years); Amorite iniquity not full. Land grant boundaries.  
**Themes:** Faith counted; historical exile scripted; multi-part covenant drama.  
**Standout:** **הָאֵל תָּמִים דַּרְכּוֹ… מָגֵן הוּא / ha-El tamim darko… magen hu / “God—His way is perfect… He is a shield”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid (Level B cannot exhaust 23 paras).

---

## Chapter 45 — Sarai, Hagar, Ishmael oracle
**Paras:** 10 · **Spine:** Gen 16  
Barren Sarai gives Hagar; conflict; flight; angel at the spring; “God sees”; Ishmael wild-ass man; well Beer-lahai-roi.  
**Themes:** Surrogacy stress; seeing God / being seen; destiny of Hagar’s son.  
**Standout:** **אֵשֶׁת חַיִל מִי יִמְצָא / eshet chayil mi yimtza / “A woman of valor who can find?”** hung on Sarai.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 46 — Circumcision covenant (age 99)
**Paras:** 13 · **Spine:** Gen 17:1–14  
El Shaddai; walk before Me and be whole. Name Abram→Abraham. Covenant in flesh; uncircumcised cut off. Nations/kings from him.  
**Themes:** Body as covenant media; name upgrade; universal fatherhood frame.  
**Standout:** **הִתְהַלֵּךְ לְפָנַי וֶהְיֵה תָמִים / hithalekh lefanai ve-heyeh tamim / “Walk before Me and be whole”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 47 — Sarai→Sarah; Isaac promised; laughter
**Paras:** 10 · **Spine:** Gen 17:15–27  
Sarah’s name; blessing her; Abraham laughs (age); Ishmael also blessed/heard; “but My covenant with Isaac.” Circumcision day implementation.  
**Themes:** Matriarch renamed; dual sons, single covenant line.  
**Standout:** **אֵשֶׁת חַיִל עֲטֶרֶת בַּעְלָהּ / eshet chayil ateret ba’alah / “A valorous wife is her husband’s crown”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 48 — Guests at Mamre
**Paras:** 20 · **Spine:** Gen 18:1–16  
Lord appears; three men; rushing hospitality (water, tree, bread, calf). Sarah’s tent laugh; “Is anything too wondrous?” Departure toward Sodom.  
**Themes:** Visiting the sick (post-circumcision); hachnasat orchim as peak virtue; promise timed.  
**Standout:** **וַיֵּרָא אֵלָיו ה'… וְהוּא יֹשֵׁב פֶּתַח הָאֹהֶל / va-yera elav Hashem… ve-hu yoshev petach ha-ohel / “The Lord appeared… as he sat at the tent door”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

---

## Chapter 49 — Shall I hide from Abraham? Bargaining for Sodom
**Paras:** 14 · **Spine:** Gen 18:17–33  
God discloses Sodom plan because Abraham will teach justice. Outcry of the city. Abraham bargains 50→10. “Judge of all the earth.”  
**Themes:** Prophetic friendship; justice pedagogy; minimum righteous threshold.  
**Standout:** **הֲשֹׁפֵט כָּל הָאָרֶץ לֹא יַעֲשֶׂה מִשְׁפָּט / ha-shofet kol ha-aretz lo ya’aseh mishpat / “Shall the Judge of all the earth not do justice?”**.  
**Confidence:** Solid. *(Gen 18:19 is a book-wide hub verse.)*

---

## Chapter 50 — Angels enter Sodom
**Paras:** 12 · **Spine:** Gen 19:1–11  
Two angels at evening; Lot’s hospitality vs city assault. “Where are the men?” Blindness plague on the mob. Lightning-fast angel movement midrash.  
**Themes:** Hospitality vs predatory city; threshold violence.  
**Standout:** **וְהַחַיּוֹת רָצוֹא וָשׁוֹב / ve-ha-chayot ratzo va-shov / “the living creatures running and returning”** — angel speed.  
**Confidence:** Solid.

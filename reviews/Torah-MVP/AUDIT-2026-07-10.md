## E. Cautious central claim (audit text)

The Masoretic Torah text, as digitized in the Leningrad-Codex-derived CSVs, is verifiably a four-layer character system (consonants, vowels, cantillation, paragraph punctuation) whose cantillation marks encode a binary constituency tree per verse — independently documented in computational-linguistics literature — and whose 669 paragraph markers segment it into 673 blocks; a compiler using only these native structures maps 97.9% of verses (block-weighted: 87.5% of blocks) onto eleven recurring structural shapes and emits code that executes without error. That these shapes exist and recur is **reproducible fact**; that they correspond to computational constructs (classification, state machines, scheduling) is an **interpretive mapping** resting on ~105 human-chosen keyword assignments; any claim of intent — that the text is a program — is **unproven** and not testable by this tooling.

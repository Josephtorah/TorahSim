## Discoveries worth keeping (as stated)

### 1. Four-layer binary structure (strongest structural claim)

Raw-byte scan, no Hebrew interpretation: **85 unique Unicode codepoints** in four layers:

| Layer | Count |
|-------|------:|
| Consonants | 27 |
| Vowels | 14 |
| Cantillation | 25 |
| Punctuation | 9 |

Strict transition grammar: consonant → vowel → cantillation → consonant.  
Numbers claimed verified in `reports/BINARY_STRUCTURE_ANALYSIS.md`.

### 2. Text ships with its own parser

- **Cantillation** → binary parse trees per verse (academic: Wu & Lowery LREC 2006). Etnahta median split ~0.47; Lev 13 observation\|diagnosis; Lev 11 condition\|verdict ~59%.
- **669** explicit `{פ}` / `{ס}` block markers = native code-block delimiters. Old heuristic matched markers in **1/106** cases — markers win.
- **Ketiv/qere** ≈ **38** inline patches (written vs read) — framed as embedded hotfix/VCS.

### 3. torahc2 validates native-structure approach

- 100% verse coverage (5,853/5,853)
- 97.9% compiled to real patterns
- 673 blocks, 0 errors, 573 tests
- Commit noted: `56c6b6b`

## 4. What this suggests (hypothesis)

1. **OSHB H216 is a clean absolute key** for noun “light.”  
2. BR **does not** tag most light-verses (~**1 in 4** of H216 verses).  
3. BR **does** hit the **Gen 1:3–5** package and several **known light operators** (Prov 4:18, Job 38:15, Isa 60…, Job cluster).  
4. **Gen 1:18** is a clean counterexample inside Gen 1: same lemma, **no BR tag** (Written still has the expand/rule job).  
5. So BR is a **sampling tutorial** over the light network, not a complete index of H216.

**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Question:** Of the **26** Tanakh verses that (a) have noun-light **H216** and (b) are **BR-tagged**, how many ride with the **Gen light install** (1:3–5) vs appear only as **free light talk** elsewhere in BR?  
**Data:** `_scan_h216_genlight_cocite_2026-07-27.json` · prior H216∩BR list  
**Method:** same-paragraph co-cite (primary); same BR *chapter* check (same split here)  
**Kind:** experiment · **not** binding law

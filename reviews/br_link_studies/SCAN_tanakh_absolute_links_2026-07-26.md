## 0. What “absolute” means here

| Absolute (keep) | Not absolute (drop) |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| Same Strong’s lemmas co-occur in few verses | “Both talk about light” |
| **n = 1** unique signature in all Tanakh | High-frequency single words |
| **n ≤ 3** tight pairs still usable as ports | Soft theme only |

**Limit:** Strong’s collapses some roots; cantillation structure not scored in this pass (lemma-only).  
**False positives possible** when one Strong’s number covers two senses (e.g. H1254 *bara* create vs fatten).

Under **Tanakh = code**:

```text
Job 38:15  = unique port  WITHHOLD + WICKED (+ light)
```

BR’s twin package is not free association. It is **calling two unique (or near-unique) code addresses**.

**Show work:** the create-week **partition** is locked to Genesis 1 (only two verses do full triple light+dark+divide).

### Split-by-people / camps (narrative absolute)

| Ref | Glue | Role |
|-----|------|------|
| **Exod 10:21–23** | Egypt dark · Israel light (same plague unit) | nation split |
| **Exod 14:20** | Egypt + Israel + dark + illuminate night | camp split |

Israel ∩ light (H3478 ∩ light): **8** verses — includes Exod 10:23, 14:20, menorah commands, Isa “light of Israel,” etc.  
Not unique alone, but **Exod 10** remains the unique **plague contrast**.

### Loose vs tight: light ∩ righteous / wicked

| Pair | n | Notes |
|------|--:|-------|
| light ∩ wicked | **3** | Job 18:5 (wicked’s light snuffed) · Job 38:15 (withheld) · Prov 13:9 (lamp of wicked dies) |
| light ∩ righteous | **10** | Prov 4:18, 13:9 · Ps 37:6, 97:11, 112:4 · Isa 42:6, 58:8, 59:9 · Mic 7:9 · Zeph 3:5 |

So “light + righteous” alone is **not** a unique absolute.  
**Path + light** or **path + nogah** *is*.

**Prov 13:9** is a strong sibling port:  
אוֹר צַדִּיקִים יִשְׂמָח וְנֵר רְשָׁעִים יִדְעָךְ / *or tzaddikim yismaḥ ve-ner reshaim yid’akh* /  
“light of righteous rejoices; lamp of wicked is extinguished” — **both** moral poles in one verse. BR 12:6 cites it as joy companion to Prov 4:18.

## 8. Bottom line

Searching the **whole Hebrew Bible** for absolute (lemma-tight) links shows:

1. **Prov 4:18** and **Job 38:15** are real **unique code ports**, not soft topics.  
2. **Gen 1:4/1:18** lock the **divide light|dark** operator.  
3. **Exod 10 / 14** lock **split light by people/camps**.  
4. BR is still best read as a **manual** that *calls* these ports — especially wiring create-light to withhold/path-light.  
5. Loose pairs (light+righteous alone) are **not** absolute; tight multi-lemma signatures **are**.

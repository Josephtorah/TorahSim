## 4. Per-locus notes (short)

### BR 1:6
- Open Dan 2:22 deep/hidden + light with Him.  
- Wicked half = dark/secret; righteous half = Prov 4:18 (+ Ps 97:11).  
- Then Messiah light (Isa 60:1); then Gen headers expanded in Nakh.  
- **No Job 38:15** — reserve side without explicit withhold twin.

### BR 11:2
- Classic **minayin** pair: Job 38:15 (stored/hidden from wicked) + Prov 4:18 (for righteous future).  
- 36 hours first light over Shabbat; then darkness; Adam; flint-fire blessing.

### BR 12:6
- Same store module; binds Gen 1:4 *ki tov* via Isa 3:10 (“righteous = good”).  
- Prov 4:18 = “from where stored for righteous”; Prov 13:9 joy.

### BR 42:3
- Portable into **grammar/theology** debate on *vayehi* / joy.  
- “And there was light” is not complete joy — world did not keep that light; Prov 4:18 in the store digression.

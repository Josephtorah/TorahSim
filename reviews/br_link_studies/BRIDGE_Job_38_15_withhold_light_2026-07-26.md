## 3. The twin package (how BR wires them)

In both hits, the midrash order is almost algorithmic:

```text
1. Day-one light: Adam could see world-end to world-end.
2. God foresaw corrupt generations
     (Flood / Dispersion; 12:6 also Dor Enosh).
3. He STORED the light (גנז / ganaz / "stored / hidden away").
4. minayin he stored / withheld?  →  Job 38:15
5. minayin prepared for righteous later? → Prov 4:18
6. (12:6 only) also: Gen 1:4 "ki tov" + Isa 3:10 (good = righteous)
7. Optional joy: Prov 13:9
8. Then narrative: 36-hour first light / Shabbat / Adam / darkness…
```

### Plain English

| Port | Verse | Job |
|------|--------|-----|
| **HIDE** | Job 38:15 | Light **blocked** from the wicked |
| **RESERVE** | Prov 4:18 | Light **path** for the righteous → full day |

Together they answer two *minayin* / “from where do we know?” questions that share one doctrine: **storage**, not destruction, of primordial light.

## 4. Locus A — BR 11:2 (detail)

**Near environment (tagged):**  
Ps 49:13 · Job 14:20 · Job 37:3 · **Job 38:15** · **Prov 4:18** · Ps 139:11 · Gen 3:15

**Argument spine (from local text):**

1. Light created day one; Adam sees end-to-end.  
2. God looks at Flood and Dispersion generations — deeds corrupt.  
3. He stores the light and prepares it for the righteous in the future.  
4. **From where stored?** Job 38:15 — *ve-yimana me-reshaim oram* / light withheld from wicked.  
5. **From where for the righteous later?** Prov 4:18 — path like increasing light.  
6. R. Levi in name of R. Zeira: light shone **36 hours** (12 Friday + 12 Friday night + 12 Shabbat).  
7. Motza’ei Shabbat: sun sets, darkness creeps; Adam fears.  
8. Ps 139:11 — “surely darkness will bruise me…” — linked to Gen 3:15 *yeshufkha* / snake bruise language.  
9. God provides two flints → fire; blessing *borei me’orei ha-esh*.

**Job’s job here:** pure **hide-proof** in a *minayin* pair with Prov.

**Note:** Partners Job 14:20, Ps 49:13 sit in the wider Adam/mortality/glory-fade discussion of the long section — not the same as the hide/reserve twin, but same **human after first light** drama.

## 5. Locus B — BR 12:6 (detail)

**Near environment:** larger set (22 unique tagged refs), including Gen 1:4, 2:3, 3:8, 3:15, 3:17, 3:19, 3:24, Ruth 4:18, Isa 3:10, Prov 13:9, Judg 5:31, Isa 30:26, 65:22, etc.

**Argument spine around the twin:**

1. Same day-one light; Adam sees end-to-end (R. Yehudah bar Simon).  
2. God sees Dor Enosh + Flood + Dispersion — corrupt.  
3. He stores it **from them** — **Job 38:15**.  
4. Why store? For the righteous in the future.  
5. Gen 1:4: God saw the light **כִּי טוֹב** / *ki tov* / “that it was good.”  
6. “Good” = righteous — Isa 3:10 *imru tzaddik ki tov*.  
7. **From where stored for the righteous?** Prov 4:18.  
8. Joy: Prov 13:9 *or tzaddikim yismaḥ* / light of righteous rejoices.  
9. Again 36 hours; honor to Shabbat; Adam’s sin and after-story (curses, expulsion, future light hopes).

**Job’s job here:** same hide-proof, but **12:6** inserts an extra **semantic bridge**:

```text
Job 38:15 (withhold)
    → Gen 1:4 (light was good)
    → Isa 3:10 (good = righteous)
    → Prov 4:18 (righteous light path)
```

So Job is not only twin to Prov; it is also the **first clamp** on a short chain into Genesis’s own “good light” line.

## 2. What each packet contains (standard shape)

**Owner is English-fluent, not Hebrew.** Every packet must be readable without Hebrew literacy.

0. **Plain English summary** — a few sentences before detail  
0b. **Mini-glossary** — Hebrew terms with how-to-say-it + plain meaning; free glosses marked `[EN-AID]`  
1. **Retell** — plain English of the BR paragraph (Hebrew only with transliteration + gloss)  
2. **Gates** — access / opening rules it installs (plain English in table)  
3. **Models** — competing structured readings it installs (plain English claims)  
4. **Ops** — tags from the ops catalog (`LIMIT_QUERY`, …) with short English gloss when helpful  
5. **Transmitters** — who speaks / in whose name  
6. **Written locus** — which Gen verse(s) it addresses, with free English  
7. **Modern measure** (when useful) — corpus counts, graphs, uniqueness  
8. **Fence** — does not rewrite Written boot  

Hebrew remains the derivation source for Torah logic; English is for reading.

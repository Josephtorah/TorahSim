### Read

- **Proof channel** (sheneemar, dikhtiv, hada hu) is the main highway.
- **Parable channel** (mashal + often kakh) is large and book-long, not only early.
- **Correspondence** (keneged) and **therefore** (lefikakh) are real but secondary.
- Classic talmud lomar appears (~51 secs)—shared midrash dialect with Talmud-era tools.

---

## Hypothesis

Linkage is dual-track: **(1) verse-to-verse proof** and **(2) mashal then thus then case**. BR is not only derash on words; it is an **application engine** (parable) plus a **citation engine** (proof).

**Confidence:** tested; kakh can be colloquial noise in places.

# Lev 1 area: Sifra → Talmud (linked list)

**Simple idea:** Where tradition links **Sifra on Leviticus 1** (burnt offering / opening of Vayikra) into the **Talmud**.

**Source:** Mesorat haShas edges (`sifra_talmud_links_2026-07-18.json`), filtered to Sifra section `Vayikra Dibbura d'Nedavah` units that treat **Lev 1** (olah / call), not yet minchah (Lev 2) or shelamim (Lev 3).

**Scope note:** Sifra does not always label “Lev 1:5” on every line. We took Nedavah units from **Chapter 1 through Chapter 9 / Section 7** (opening + cattle/flock/bird olah). Later Nedavah units (Section 8+) are mostly Lev 2–3 and are listed only as a count at the end.

## How to use

1. Pick a **Sifra unit** above (e.g. Section 3 on עולה / olah).
2. Open the **Talmud** addresses listed next to it (e.g. Zevachim daf).
3. Expect parallel midrash language (יכול…ת״ל, etc.), not necessarily the word “Sifra.”

## Limits

- Links = traditional parallels (Mesorat haShas), not full text proof.
- Unit → Lev 1 mapping is approximate (olah block of Nedavah).
- 122 more Nedavah→Talmud links exist for Lev 2–3 (minchah/shelamim); see full TSV.

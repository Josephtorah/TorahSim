## 0. Claim under test

```text
Gen create-week gives SHORT (weak / header) definitions of light ops.
Later instances (Exod history, Job/Prov policy, BR packages) EXPAND those ops:
  - add agents (people, camps, righteous/wicked)
```

**Weak definition** here = short, high-density, few parameters — not “incorrect.”

## 6. What “expand weak definitions” means as system design

```text
BOOT (Torah):
  LIGHT = create + divide_space + name_day_night
  // underspecified for people/moral/future

HISTORY instances:
  LIGHT += assign_by_people | camps | duration | barrier

WISDOM/POLICY instances:
  LIGHT += withhold(wicked) | path(righteous)→full_day | joy|snuff

LITURGY/RESTORE:
  LIGHT += sown | Zion rise | glory

ORAL (BR):
  packages instances into teaching units
  (store light; minayin pairs; land on Gen)
```

**Computer analogy (better than “call/return”):**

| Concept | Fit |
|---------|-----|
| **Interface / header** | Gen 1:3–5 |
| **Overloads / specializations** | Exod, Job, Prov… |
| **Re-entrant opcode** | same family, new call stack |
| **Linker docs** | BR chooses which overload to teach with Gen |

Not: later verses **replace** Gen.  
Yes: later verses **specialize** Gen’s thin API.

## 7. Do *all* BR-cited verses expand Gen?

**No — not all.**

| Kind of BR cite | Expands Gen light/create? |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| Light/create package (above) | **Usually yes** |
| *Amon* sense-table (Num, Esth, Lam, Nah) | Expands **amon**, not Gen light; lands via *reishit* |
| Random history proof in later BR chapters | Often **theme/root**, not Gen header expand |
| Particle school (1:14) | Expands **reading method**, not light physics |

So the expand thesis is **opcode-scoped**:

```text
IF Gen leaves opcode O underspecified
AND later verse is instance of O
THEN expect expansion of O’s parameters
ELSE cite may be root-play, story, or method — not expand-of-O
```

**Theory 6** is exactly the re-entrant-O case.  
**Expand-weak-def** is the **mechanism** that makes re-entrancy useful.

BR does not invent the expansions (they’re Written).

That is **documentation of overloads**, not random prooftexting.

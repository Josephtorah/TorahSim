Every step from “structured” to “self-describing program” (Genesis 1 = pipeline, 1+3+1 = software lifecycle) is post-hoc mapping that was revised whenever convenient and extracted zero executable rules. Believe the parser, keep the statistics, treat the compiler story as unproven.
